> ###### Fence
> **First appearance** Can't Shake You Loose #2
> **Appearances** 33

The man known as Fence is a broker of stolen goods in the Marvel U based out of New York City. He has advanced cybernetic components and a storehouse full of stolen goods - particularly exotic weaponry. Throughout Gambit's series, Fence serves as the man giving Remy equipment, intel, and friendly advice. 

It is established that the front for his illegal business is a cupcake shop in Brooklyn called Sugar Muffins. Fence takes pride in his baking.
