## History

Alexis along with Jasmine, Kayla Kurasawa, and the superhero [[Kate Bishop]] were all kidnapped by Count Griffon Walters a vampire who intended to enthrall them and make the group into his harem. While kidnapped the group had to face a mummy, a giant tentacle monster and finally Griffon himself before escaping his castle located somewhere in New Jersey. Griffon wasn't able to enthrall any of the group individually due to their strong beliefs. Alexis' personal motive was that she didn't believe in the way Griffon treated women and was able to fight his enthrallment because of it.
