## Brief History

Remaining as neutral as possible towards the political state of Genosha, Pipeline worked for the Genoshan Press Gang using his abilities as a means of transport for the team as well as Genoshan military. Pipeline provided his services during the X-Tinction Agenda and went on to participate in the Genoshan Civil War. It is unknown if Pipeline survived Genosha's destruction. If he is alive, it is unknown if he retained his abilities following M-Day.

## Creation

Pipeline was created by Chris Claremont and Rick Leonardi in 1988 and first appeared in Uncanny X-Men # 235.

## Powers & Abilities

Pipeline has the mutant ability to transport living matter through telephone and computer lines (a much more useful ability once land-lines became irrelevant). Those being transported were digitized and sent through the lines to a connected location.
