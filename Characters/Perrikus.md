> Perrikus is a warrior who joined the Dark Gods. He is fierce, strong, and almost indestructible.

## History

 

When the Dark Gods were on the world of Narcisson they met with an Asgardian named Lonkarr. Lonkarr then attacked Tserron who was one of the dark gods and he slayed her. Her spouse named Perrikus attacked Lonkarr but, the Dark Gods let him go so Perrikus would want revenge on the Asgardian. When they learned where the Asgardian lived which was Asgard, Perrikus and all of the Dark Gods attacked them. It showed that the Dark Gods were too much for the Asgardians to handle and they won the battle. Odin from Asgard had to have his son Thor give him a boost in his spirits so that Odin could fight on. Odin and the rest of the gods fought the Dark Gods again but this time they defeated them. Odin then banished them to the farthest reaches of the universe. Soon the Dark Gods and Perrikus would return for revenge. They took Asgard by surprise, took over the city and even tortured Odin in cruel ways. When Thor returned he himself could not defeat Perrikus in battle and was defeated. Thor then left and gathered an army of his own and attacked the Dark Gods and made them fall back far enough so he could free Odin. Now they would once and for all get rid of and destroy the Dark Gods. They believed they had defeated the Dark Gods forever. Later when Odin was slain by a demon named Surtur, Thor was given Odin powers and became lord of Asgard. Thor would try to help Earth by getting the Asgardians involved. But Zarrko the tomorrow man joined the government and on the hundredth day that the Asgardians had been on earth, Zarko attacked them. He pulled the Dark God Perrikus from the time stream and told Perrikus that if he could defeat Thor and put Asgard back to where it came from he would become the new ruler for Asgard. He agreed and as soon as he got started Perrikus wanted Thor first. Now Perrikus was as powerful as ever and didn't have as many gods to deal with after taking a lot of them out quietly. But, when Thor picked up the axe of the Executioner, Perrikus soon regretted his decision. Thor for a moment became almost evil and chopped off Perrikus's arm and threatened to kill him unless he told him who gave him the idea to do this and Perrikus revealed everything. It was not revealed what happened to Perrikus after that.

## Powers

 

 

 

Perrikus had super strength, endurance, and was nearly invulnerable. He is a skilled warrior. He also wields a deadly spear. He has destroyed Thor's hammer, Mjolnir, before with his scythe. He's also the God of unlimited and infinite energy
