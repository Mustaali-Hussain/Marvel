## Brief History

Joy Meachum has served as head of the Ward/Meachum Corporation. She is a daughter to [[Harold Meachum]] and niece of [[Ward Meachum]]. She initially blamed [[Iron Fist]] for the death of her father but has since then gotten over it and even maintained an on and off relationship with him. Joy has been last seen sleeping with Iron Fist in his apartment in his bed it appears Iron Fist may be having an open relationship with her or he is still dating [[Misty Knight]]. She is also Iron Fist's new secretary and works for Rand Corporation.
