> ###### Elisbeth Von Strucker
> **First appearance** Enemy Of The State, Part 4 #23
> **Appearances** 30

> [!abstract] In brief
> Elisbeth Von Strucker is the wife of Nazi Criminal, Baron Wolfgang Von Strucker. She is also the mother of the twin mutant team Fenris, A.K.A. Andrea & Andreas Von Strucker and Step-Mother to Werner Von Strucker of HYDRA. Currently the entire Von Strucker family, save Wolfgang, is deceased.

Elisbeth met and fell in love with Baron Wolfgang Von Strucker during WWI. They soon sired twins, whom were experimented on in-utero, and given superhuman powers. Having been born with their powers, Andrea & Andreas are classified as mutants, but can only access their powers when in contact with each other. Elisbeth is currently deceased.
