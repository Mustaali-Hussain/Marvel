> [!abstract] In brief
> Kwannon is a Japanese assassin for the Hand, who was body-swapped with the X-Man Betsy Braddock. Living in the body of Betsy, she assumed the codename "Revanche" and briefly joined the X-Men before dying of the Legacy Virus. She was later resurrected in her original body and is now the current Psylocke.

## Origin

*Trained to kill*

The young girl known as Kwannon was raised to be a deadly assassin by the organization known as The Hand. Taught that her gifts brought mercy to the people vying for power within modern civilization, she was ordered to kill as her master's weapon. It is implied that she had a name before Kwannon, but she has since forgotten it.

At some point she began a secret relationship with a boy and bore a daughter. Her master discovered this, killed the boy, and took Kwannon's daughter from her to teach her loss.

## Creation

Kwannon first appeared in X-Men #17 (Feb 1993) and was created by Fabian Nicieza and Andy Kubert, although Chris Claremont originally came up with the initial ideas of the character.

## Character Evolution

The character of Kwannon herself wasn't established until a while after the body swap. The character was introduced believing herself to be the real Elizabeth Braddock, travelling with the X-Men and Psylocke to find out what really happened. After some truths were realized, she went on to join the X-Men, even if it was briefly. Kwannon then contracted the mutant attacking Legacy Virus and died in her love's arms, thus finally settling the confusion behind the body swap.

Betsy's continuing to live in the body of a Japanese woman sparked debate in the decades since its introduction, and in 2019 she reverted to her original British body, allowing Kwannon to finally return as a character. Initially an aimless assassin, she took the Psylocke mantle from Betsy during The First Krakoan Age, and currently acts as a more heroic version of her former self as a member of the X-Men.

## Major Story Arcs

### The Body Swap

*Kwannon the Assassin*

Not much is known about Kwannon's life, not even her last name, She was however a mutant gifted with empathic and low level telepathic abilities. After years of training, Kwannon became the top assassin for Japanese crime Lord Nyoirin, and entered a working relationship with [[Matsuo Tsurayaba|Matsu'o Tsurayaba]], an assassin of the Hand. Kwannon and Matsu’o feared their line of work would put them on opposing sides but, respecting each other as warriors with honor, they vowed to fight to the death should they ever be forced to directly fight each other. When the interests of Nyoirin and the Hand came into conflict, Kwannon was honor-bound to face Matsu'o in combat. During the battle, she fell off a cliff suffering severe brain damage leaving her in a coma. Seeing this, Matsu’o abandoned his mission and retrieved Kwannon, bringing her to the Hand’s labs, promising to find a way to heal her.

The telepathic mutant Betsy Braddock (aka [[Betsy Braddock|Psylocke]]) was found washed up on the shore of the Hand's island base after passing through the Siege Perilous. [[Spiral]] convinced Matsu’o that she could use a combination of her tech and Psylocke’s mutant powers to save the life of his lover, Kwannon. Instead of using Psylocke’s telepathy to heal Kwannon’s shattered mind, Spiral betrayed Matsu’o and used her magic and alien technology to transfer Psylocke and Kwannon's minds and souls into each others body. Spiral also intermingled Kwannon and Psylocke's DNA. Possessing Betsy Braddock’s body and powers, Kwannon was led to believe she was the real Betsy Braddock and that Psylocke the was the imposter.

Kwannon took time to heal and re-trained to establish herself again as the master assassin. She took the name 'Revanche', a French term for the reclamation of lost territory, and made way to New York to visit the X-Men and reveal Psylocke as the 'true imposter'.

### Psylocke

*The Reveal*

Revanche made her way to America and infiltrated the Xavier Mansion. The computers in the mansion detected two mutants that registered as 'Betsy Braddock'. No X-Men paid attention to this, thinking it was the systems making a mistake. This meant Revanche could infiltrate the Danger Room where [[Jean Grey]] and Psylocke were fighting. After Psylocke knocked out Jean, Revanche attacked Psylocke wearing a version of Psylocke’s armor, the last costume that Betsy wore before the body swap. The X-Men came to Psylocke's aid, but Revanche pulled off her mask revealing her face and stated that she was the the real Betsy Braddock, and that Psylocke was, and always had been, an imposter.

### X-Men

*Fighting with the Avengers*

Since neither [[Wolverine]]’s enhanced senses nor [[Professor X|Xavier]]’s telepathy could tell which one was the real Betsy, the X-Men traveled to Japan, but only got conflicting stories and half-truths. After the dust from the confusion settled, Kwannon chose to stay with Psylocke's team, the X-Men, much to her doppelgänger‘s dismay. She officially joined the X-Men, joining Psylocke on the 'blue team' and gained a new, official costume. For her short time in the team, Revanche managed to get along with Psylocke and her X-Men team-mates. She was part of the team assembled to save the kidnapped [[Luna]], team up with the Avengers and fight [[Exodus]], Acolytes and [[Fabian Cortez]]. A mission even Wolverine wasn't part of. The mission was a success a Revanche had proven herself to be a useful and trustworthy member of the team.

### Death

*Revealing the Legacy Virus*

On one mission, Revanche and the X-Men discovered a clinic for mutants who suffer from the Legacy Virus. The team wanted to shut down the clinic but Revanche ordered a retreat, stating that the patients were there willingly and had to decide for themselves whether they wanted to be exploited or not. When asked why she felt so strongly about the issue, Revanche revealed that she too had contracted the Legacy virus.

Beast and Xavier attempted to help, trying to slow the process down, but nothing worked. The disease had spread too much and Revanche was terminal. Refusing to die in a lab, she choose to sort her past out once and for all. The virus caused her telepathic powers to flair up causing her to see clearly through the confusion in her mind, revealing that Psylocke was the true Betsy Braddock and that she was truly Kwannon. Before leaving the mansion, she left behind Elizabeth's bionic eyes, wanting to remove all signs of manipulation on her life.

*An Honourable Death*

Kwannon then set off for Japan one final time. She found the manipulative Nyoirin dead, killed by Matsu'o. He explained he did it for causing so much misery in their lives. She then made up with her old lover and asked him to kill her, not wanting to die of the Legacy virus, as she felt her end was coming. In order to spare his lover a dishonorable death, Matsu’o did as asked. He rammed a blade into her heart and, upon dying, Revanche released a wave of escalating telepathic energies, leaving him with an imprint of her.

Back at the institute, Psylocke felt Kwannon dying and travelled to Japan to sought out Matsu’o, who then used this telepathic imprint in the way Revanche had asked of him. Matsu’o released the energy on Betsy and her mental peace was restored too, as all fragments of Kwannon were taken from her soul, restoring her true sense of identity.

On her grave, Matsu'o and Psylocke chose to engrave as a tribute the epitaph "Kwannon, love that transcended body and soul". She was buried in Wolverine's private cemetery in Japan.

### Life After Death

*Destruction of the body*

Spiral continued to meddle with Kwannon even after her death.

Years after her demise at the hands of the Legacy Virus, Spiral and the Sisterhood dug up Kwannon's body under the orders of the mysterious and powerful Red Queen. The Red Queen, using the combined powers of the Sisterhood, reanimated the dead body and transferred the mind of Psylocke back into her original body. This has put Elisabeth back in her original form. However during the battle Psylocke’s original body was horribly disfigured and she transferred her mind once again into the Asian body. Kwannon still remains dead.

After this ordeal, Psylocke decided to bury her former body again. She travelled to Japan where she was attacked by ninjas. During the fight the ninjas used a spell to make the body disintegrate, all under the orders of Kwannon's former lover Matsu'o's orders. Matsu'o arranged this so Psylocke would kill him. Psylocke grants his request and eases his passing with the illusion of Kwannon giving him one final kiss before sending him plummeting off a cliff, ironically not unlike the fateful day when Kwannon plummeted and started the chain of events that led to the body swap.

### Return

*Kwannon Lives Again*

After Betsy Braddock's soul was ripped out of Kwannon's body and eaten by [[Sapphire Styx]], Betsy is able to use the stolen soul energies inside the vampiric villain and create a new body that was nearly identical to her original British body. With Elizabeth's soul no longer inside Kwannon's physical form, Kwannon came back to life, with her soul once again in her true body.

After the X-Men were disassembled following [[X-Man|Nate Grey's]] attack, Kwannon resurfaced when [[Joseph]] (posing as Magneto) reformed the Brotherhood of Mutants and attacked what remained of the X-Men. Before he and Cyclops could reach a compromise, Kwannon decapitates Joseph, and is taken into custody by the team. When [[Wolfsbane]] is murdered by bigots Wolverine frees Kwannon and they both seek vengeance on those who killed her, then team up again to seek retribution from [[Emma Frost]] and her Hellfire Club.

### Becoming Psylocke

During the First Krakoan Age Kwannon comes to the island and takes on the mantle of Psylocke, with Betsy becoming Captain Britain. She struggles to accept peace on the island, with memories of her deceased daughter preoccupying her thoughts, before a blinding vision warns her of a techno-organic threat known as [[Apoth]]. Kwannon strikes a deal with [[Mr. Sinister|Sinister]] to help keep her mission from the Quiet Council, and in turn she will bring Apoth to him before she kills it. She enlists [[X-23|Laura Kinney]] and a young [[Cable]] to her cause, stating that as soldiers and predators they do not belong in paradise. Together they go to Tokyo to investigate the connection between Apoth and a potent drug known as Overclock, which may connect to an incident involving a girl that reminds Kwannon of her lost daughter.

### Hellions

As part of her deal with Sinister, who promised to secretly resurrect her daughter, Kwannon was apointed as the filed leader of his Hellions group, a team of mutants who struggled to fit on Krakoa because of their disturbing behaviour or past traumas. On their first mission against the Goblin Queen, Psylocke proved herself as she asserted her dominance over the uncontrollable Wild Child. During her time with the Hellions, Kwannon started to attract fellow teammater Greycrow, feelings that she reciprocated and soon both started a romantic relationship.

Realising that Sinister would never hold his part of the deal, Kwannon turned against him and soon the Hellions were disbanded once Nanny and Orphan Maker were exiled to the pits of Krakoa and many of them grew tired and angry by Sinister's manipulations.

After her time with the Hellions, Kwannon was appointed as one of the War Captains of Krakoa.

### From The Ashes

*Joining Cyclops' X-Men*

After the fall of Krakoa, Cyclops invites Kwannon to join his X-Men team in the town of Merle, Alaska. As Psylocke, Kwannon acts as the psychic head of security and is Scott's second-in-command in the field. As part of an investigation into adults suddenly manifesting mutant abilities, Kwannon and [[Quentin Quire]] dive into the mind of the recently rescued [[Ben Liu]], where they discover the prescence of [[Cassandra Nova]] who has returned to the present and leads the organization 3K.

### Going Solo

In conjunction with Cyclops' X-Men, Kwannon has also become a mercenary for hire. After she rescues the daughter of an anti-mutant politician, she returns to Alaska only to be benched by Cyclops for exhausting herself working. Kwannon heads to an underground MGH auction hosted by A.I.M., who are trafficking children for their mutant genes. She interrupts the auction and saves the children, fighting [[Donald Pierce]] and [[Skullbuster]] before the latter takes MGH and mutates into a grotesque creature, giving Psylocke the excuse to kill him.

Once she rescued the abducted children, she took them to the X-men's base in Alaska, where they would be taken care. Then she tracked the source of the information about the MGH auction, who turned to be Shinobi Shaw, an old acquaintance of hers. Although he didn't want to directly intervene in shutting down the child trafficking, Kwannon succesfully convinced him to assist her. Both mutants teamed up and rescued all the kidnapped children.

After this mission, Psylocke was horrified to find the amputated arm of her lover Greycrow at her door.

## Powers and abilities

*Psychic Katana*

Kwannon was a superior assassin and skilled in many martial arts styles. Before the body swap she possessed a low-level empathic ability that allowed her to sense the emotions of others.

She had gained the power of telepathy from Psylocke after inhabiting her body, this meant she could read, control and confuse minds, read thoughts, communicate via thoughts, broadcast the thoughts of others and create illusions by altering perceptions. However, Revanche was extremely untrained in these psychic abilities, so she rarely used them.

Unlike Psylocke, Revanche manifested her psychic abilities by creating a psychic sword, not a dagger. She could also create a samurai sword made up of pure psionic energy. The blade was capable of causing physical and mental damage, something Betsy inherited after her death. Revanche could also use her psychic katana to force her way into an opponent's mind and reveal any thoughts that were originally shielded from her telepathic powers, including those of other telepaths.

Ever since having her body swapped with that of the powerful psychic Psylocke and ultimately returning to her own, she has displayed a vast array of powerful psychic (both telepathic and telekinetic) abilities she tends to employ during fights.
