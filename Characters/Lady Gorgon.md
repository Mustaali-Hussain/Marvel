## Brief History

A Hand operative, Lady Gorgon pretended to be Frank Castle's long dead wife to mess with his head. It was however later revealed that she was an assassin who Frank had once burned alive. She lost her high-ranking position within the organization when she failed to kill the Punisher.  She was later killed when the Punisher came after her in revenge, not by the Punisher's hand, though, but by Master Yoshiokya, a leader of the Hand.
