## Biography

In a future timeline, Charles Xavier II was born, the son of mutant civil rights leader Professor X, and the mutant terrorist Mystique. Xavier soon inherited his father's psychic abilities. Xavier witnessed the assassination of the United State's first mutant president, Alison Blaire, which radicalized him against humans. Xavier reformed the Brotherhood of Mutants alongside his half-brother Raze.

## Powers and Abilities

Xavier was a powerful mutant telepath, able to communicate telepathically, read minds, rewrite memories, and create psychic illusions. He had strong enough mind control powers to fully control several people at once, and could create powerful and seemingly autonomous psychic constructs that posessed a fascimile of sentience.
