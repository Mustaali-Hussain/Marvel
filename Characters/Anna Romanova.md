> Anna Romanova is the daughter of the Black Widow who assumed the mantle after her mother's demise.

## Origin

Daughter of the Black Widow, Anna Romanova is gifted with an extra pair of arms, from exposure to the Terrigen Mists. Anna adopted the Black Widow guise following the death of the original and began serving as a diplomat for Colossus in Russia. Despite being one of his most loyal followers, Colossus frequently incorrectly calls her by her mother's name, much to her annoyance.

## Creation

Black Widow was created by Alex Ross, Jim Krueger and John Paul Leon and first appeared in *Earth X* #8.
