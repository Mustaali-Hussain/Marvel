> ###### Bei the Blood Moon
> **First appearance** X of Swords, Chapter 11 #1

> [!abstract] In brief
> Arakko-born warrior who did not speak for the first fifty years of her life. When she did, she discovered she had a "Doom Note," a voice capable of destroying a mountain that desires to be understood but remains impossible to translate.

## Creation:

The Bei the Blood Moon was created by Jonathan Hickman and Pepe Larraz for the X of Swords event. Bei first appeared in X of Swords: Stasis #1 - Chapter 11.
