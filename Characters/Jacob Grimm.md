> ###### Jacob Grimm
> **Real name** Jacob Grimm
> **First appearance** Wendy's Friends #239
> **Appearances** 15
> **Also known as** Jacob Grimm, Dr. Jacob Grimm, Jake Grimm, Uncle Jake

Dr. Jacob "Jake" Grimm is a physician and the paternal uncle of the Thing. Following the death of his brother, Jacob receive custody of his nephew. He later moved from New York to Arizona. A car accident crippled his legs and killed his first wife. He was next married to Petunia Grimm, a much younger woman. Thing tends to be amused that his aunt is barely older than him.
