## Origin

Norton is the father of Hank McCoy a.k.a. The [[Beast]]. He is married to [[Edna McCoy]]; he was exposed to radiation during a meltdown in a power plant where he was working. Due to Norton´s exposure to radiation Hank was born with huge hands, feet, and incredibly high I.Q. During Hank´s childhood, they were supportive and kind to their boy.

## Creation

Norton McCoy was created by Stan Lee, Jack Kirby and Werner Roth and first appeared in (Uncanny) X-Men Vol.1 issue 15 (1965).

## Major Story Arcs

### Father of a Hero

*Proud of his son*

During Hank´s teen years, his powers manifested more readily, provoking jealousy and attention from others. One day Hank stopped three criminals using his mutant abilities, and this made it to the news. The villain known only as the [[El Conquistador|Conquistador]] decided that he could use Hank as a weapon after seeing this report. The Conquistador successfully managed to kidnap Hank, Edna, and Norton, and told Hank that he would release them when he stole an experimental nuclear device. Hank accepted but when he returned, Conquistador stated that he didn´t need Hank anymore; but he would keep Hank's parents indefinitely. At this moment [[Professor X]] with [[Cyclops]] and [[Iceman]] saved Hank and his parents, and offered Hank to join the X-Men, giving him codename Beast.

### The Dark Beast

Several years later, an alternate version of Hank ([[Dark Beast]]) arrived to the current time line and studied plenty of Hank´s closest friends and familiars in order to learn everything about Hank and supplant him. During his visit to Hank´s parents, Dark Beast almost killed them but he couldn´t, killing instead a pedestrian nearby.

## Powers and Abilities

Although the father of the X-man Beast, Norton is not a mutant and possesses no special powers, but is a very smart and educated man.
