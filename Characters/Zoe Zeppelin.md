> ###### Zoe Zeppelin
> **First appearance** #3
> **Appearances** 11

> [!abstract] In brief
> Leader of S.O.S., daughter of superhero pulp adventurer Professor Zeppelin.

Zoe Zeppelin is the leader of Special Operations Service (S.O.S.), a role she inherited from her superhero father, Professor. Zeppelin. She has demonstrated enhanced strength capable of inflicting damage even on [[Zack Overkill]], and some invulnerability. It is believed that her powers are derived from the same otherworldly crash site that gave The Black Death his own powers, with Zoe being a possible descendant of one of the original people that investigated the site. However, it is possible that she must consume a secret elixir created by her father to maintain her powers.
