## Origin

Percy Penguin was a comic character published by Timely Comics, Marvel Comic's predecessor, during the 'funny animal' craze in the 1950's. He most often teamed-up with his friends. After the funny animal craze had ended, Percy Penguin was hardly ever seen again.
