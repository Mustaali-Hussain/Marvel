> ###### Manacle
> **First appearance** Moscow Knights #383
> **Appearances** 40

> [!abstract] In brief
> Mutant agent of Ransome Sole and Elias Bogan.

The lumbering mutant Manacle worked with Revenant and attacked the X-Men when they invaded Ransome Sole's mutant club "The Slash" to try and rescue Major Debra Levin who had been investigating Sole. 

## Creation

Manacle was created by Chris Claremont and Adam Kubert in 2000 and first appeared in The Uncanny X-Men # 383.    

 

Later, as the Xtreme X-Men investigate the Marie D'ancanto terrorist trial, Manacle, again with Revenant, work for Elias Bogan.
