## Origin

After finally reclaiming his role as the God of Thunder, [[Thor]] began having an identity crisis which led to madness only made worse by the mysterious Valkyrie.

## Creation

Valkyrie was created by Jim Starlin, Ron Marz, Bruce Zick and Steve Montano and first appeared in The Mighty Thor #460.
