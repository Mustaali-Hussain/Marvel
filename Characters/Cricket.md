## Origin

It is not known who Mr. Cricket was before [[Madame Monstrosity]] spliced the scientist with a cricket making him her lab assistant. The transformed Mr. Cricket into a monster but he kept his intelligence and was a valuable asset to Madame research.

When Spider-Boy, with the help of [[Spider-Man]], [[Captain America]], [[Squirrel Girl]], [[Thor]] and The Toy Soldier, stopped Madame's operations Cricket helped make the machine to turn the Humanimals back to normal.

It is unknown why the process didn't work on Mr. Cricket but he stayed with [[Tabitha Briggs]] and [[Hellifino]] to help take care of the animals and the other Humanimals that could not be changed back.

## Creation 

Mr. Cricket was created by Dan Slott and Paco Medina for their series Spider-Boy.
