> Current Imperial Guard member and successor to the original Starbolt.

## Origin

The origin of this Starbolt is unknown except that he was recruited into the Imperial Guard as a member when the first [[Starbolt]] was killed during the events of the Realm of Kings.

## Creation

Starbolt was created by Dan Abnett, Andy Lanning and Kevin Walken and first appeared in Realm of Kings: Imperial Guard issue 5 (2009).

## Major Story Ars

### A Legacy to Fullfill

The new Starbolt had almost the same powers as the original Startbolt. This Starbolt however has a different appearance, with a skull-like face. More recently, Starbolt was part of the group of Imperial Guards that brought down Thanos.
