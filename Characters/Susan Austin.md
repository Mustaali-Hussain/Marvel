> ###### Susan Austin
> **First appearance** Quicksand Kills #392
> **Appearances** 61

> [!abstract] In brief
> A close friend to Eric Masterson whom sacrificed her own life to save that of Kevin and his mother.

Susan Austin was a close friend of [[Thunderstrike|Eric Masterson]]. During a battle between [[Thor]] and [[Loki]], Loki wanted to hurt Thor so he shot a bolt toward [[Kevin Masterson]] and his mother. The [[Enchantress]] appeared to Susan to warn her of the upcoming danger, and Susan pushed the mother and child away and took the brunt of the blast. She died a hero.
