> [!abstract] In brief
> Newton Destine is a human-genie hybrid.

Newton is a super genius who has a exceptional talent with machinery, chemistry, medicine and surgery. He is the one who created the ClanDestine's Relative Strangers Protocol, which hide the Destine's from society. He now lives on Etherea, which is an alternate earth in another dimesion. When moving around Etherea he lives in another body while his is in suspended animation. This body is more masculine than he is. He has built a watch that can teleport him across dimensions. He built a ship that could travel through dimensions and time. He has built a engine that he put in a car so his father could go into space.
