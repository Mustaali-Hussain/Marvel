### Marvel Future Fight

In Marvel's Future Fight, The Undead G Man is listed as a another costume for Johnny Blaze. His powers are similar to Blaze, but more similar to its own characters powers and origin. He can summon a 1930s style limousine. He uses a hellfire style 1930s tommy gun. The only thing similar but more in tuned to Danny's Ketchs Ghost Rider is a hellfire Chain he can used defensively.

Most of Undead References for this game is seemingly taken from Marvel Secret Wars Battle Wars. Undead G Man is a resistance fighter in the story. And in this story line and video game, there is no sign of his sidekick Knuckles O'Shaugnessy.

This version is from Earth-TRN012.
