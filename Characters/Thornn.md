## Creation

Thornn was created by Len Wein, George Perez and Joe Sinnott in 1977 and first appeared in Fantastic Four # 186. 

## Powers & Abilities

Thornn could magically assume a mutated form in which he could grow explosive power spines that he could shoot from his body. Even in human form he had limited abilities to wield magic.
