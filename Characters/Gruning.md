> [!abstract] In brief
> German member of the villainous Exiles who prefers to fight with a whip.

Gruning is a German who uses whips in combat. Initially he used normal ones, but later switched to electrified ones. He seems the most loyal to the [[Red Skull]] and may very well have served under the fellow German before.

## Creation

Gruning was created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby in 1968 and first appeared in *Captain America *#103.
