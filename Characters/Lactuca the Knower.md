> Lactuca is an Omega-level mutant from Arakko with the power to know the location of anything in the universe.

Lactuca is an Omega-level mutant from the island of Arakko and a member of the Great Ring. She has the power to know the location of anything in the universe, which she used in conjunction with Jean Grey and Exodus to calculate the exact position and trajectory necessary to transport Arakko to Mars.

## Powers

**Dimensional Manipulation: **Lactuca was able to close a dimensional rift created the The Eternal Uranos. She was able to intercepted Manifold teleportation and cloak him so Enigma couldn't detect him. 

**Sp****atial Awareness: **Lactuca knows the location of "everything" and can share her knowledge with others.

**Size Alterations: **Lactuca can alter her size at will

**Telepathy: **Lactuca can seemingly communicate with someone regardless of distance and create mental links to share knowledge. Her mental links can shield others from telepathic assaults as her mind is too expansive to comprehend.

**Teleport: **Can move herself and others throughout space.
