## Brief History

Lynn was the wife of Luther and lived in Maine in the 1940's. She and her husband first met Namor when they tried to stop his reign of terror. It was later that Namor's mother found Lynn suitable to become Namor's wife. This however never happened. They eventually parted as friends, but both Lynn's and Luthor's whereabouts are unknown.
