## Origin

*Young Jennifer*

Jennifer was born in Citrusville, Florida, one member of a large magic family (keepers of the Book of Zhered-na) and lived in the Florida swamp region. One day, young Jennifer stole a mystical text from her grandfather. She thought it was the Tome of Zhered-Na and learning to perform spells would be fun. Unfortunately for Jennifer, she had stolen a much more sinister text. Her "fun" little spell summoned [[Thog]], one of the Hell-Lords, to Earth. Fortunately for them, the creature known as the [[Man-Thing]] was nearby to fight the demon for them. After this, Jennifer and the [[Man-Thing]] developed a special bond, with the Man-Thing helping out Jennifer. The two became great allies. Jennifer tried to live a normal life as well as she could, but time and again she was drawn to magical battles.

## Creation

Jennifer Kale was created by Steve Gerber and Rich Buckler and first appeared in Adventure into Fear #11(1972).

Afterwards, she refused to release her possession of the family Tome and stayed together with Satana and Topaz as Witches to prevent other mystical threats from attacking earth.

## Powers and Abilities

Jennifer possesses vast magical powers. Taught by one of the most powerful human wizards, [[Dakimh]], she has followed the way of the ancient Atlantean magic. Presumably a dead type of magic, which only she has knowledge of practicing. Her magical powers have not been fleshed out much, but she is known to create illusions and has teleportation, locating, and blasting spells. Her most recent ability was to contain the zombie virus into Zombie [[Zombie|Simon Garth]], though this required the help from the voodoo magic of [[Black Talon]]. She is also in possession of the Tome of Zhered-Na, one of the most powerful magical artifacts in existence.

## In Other Media

### Agatha All Along (2024)

*Sasheer Zamata as Jennifer Kale*

In the Disney+ series actress Sasheer Zamata portrays Jennifer Kale, a holistic business owner whom [[Agatha Harkness]] recruits into her coven as the expert in potions magic. In the show Kale cut off ties with Harkness since the latter began possessing the Darkhold, and over the years Jennifer has been restricted in her use of magic and forced to open a holistic beauty product business. Facing numerous threats of legal action, Kale has no choice but to follow Agatha on The Witches' Road.

The coven - made up of Jen, Agatha, Lilia Culderu, [[Alice Gulliver|Alice Wu-Gulliver]], and Mrs Hart - alongside "Teen," walk the Witches' Road and are faced with five trials along the way. Each trial corresponds to the witch in the coven and their respective speciality. The first trial tests the coven's witch of potions, which falls to Kale after the coven is poisoned. Kale identifies the poison and assigns everyone specific ingredients to gather, including her Kale Kare products that are petroleum-based and the cause of her numerous legal issues. As the poison takes hold, Jennifer hallucinates a Doctor who berates her before attempting to drown her in the kitchen sink. The coven bring the ingredients to Kale and she manages to brew the antidote and save all but Mrs Hart, who they discover has died when they return to the Road.

Following the second trial, Jennifer reveals to the coven that she used to be a midwife before her magic was bound by the Doctor.

Following the Third Trial the sigil on "Teen" is broken, revealing him to be the soul of [[Wiccan|Billy Maximoff]] in the body of William Kaplan. On the Witches' Road, Billy - angered by Agatha's killing of Alice Wu - controls Jennifer and Lilia to throw Agatha from the road, before launching them off the road with a blast of magic. Jennifer awaken in the tunnelled undergrowth of the Road alongside Lilia, and both follow the path to the Fourth Trial. During this Lilia begins experiencing breaks in chronological perceptions of time, and Jennifer helps to guide her as they avoid the Salem Seven. As the two reach the trial and discover Agatha and Billy already attempting, and failing, the trial, Lilia takes position. She performs a tarot reading which divines the significance of the coven. For Jennifer, Lilia draws the High Priestess card. Upon completing the trial, Lilia pushes Jennifer through the exit, claiming she represents the coven's future. Jennifer protests but Lilia, telling her how much the coven has given her meaning, sacrifices herself to stop the Salem Seven.

*Jennifer escapes the Witches' Road*

During the Fifth and final Trial, Jen realizes she is blocking her own magic after an encounter with Agatha over a century ago. She performs an unbreaking incantation and restores her connection with magic, after which her prize is won and Jennifer ascends from the Road. She climbs out of the earth of a field outside Westview and, with her restored power, she flies away.
