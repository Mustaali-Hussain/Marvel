> ###### Michael Berengetti
> **Real name** Michael Francis Xavier Berengetti
> **First appearance** Crap Shoot #347
> **Appearances** 32
> **Also known as** Michael Francis Xavier Berengetti, Michael Berengetti, Mickey Berengetti, Mickey

> [!abstract] In brief
> A Highlty skilled businessman, with a deep knowledge of underworld politics. Skilled with firearms, and a talent for mathematics relating to games of chance.

He was a casino owner in Las Vegas with connections to organized crime. He was the employer of [[Hulk|Mr. Fixit]], the Grey Hulk. Berengetti, who called the Hulk "Joe Fixit" ensured that the Hulk had steady access to tailored suits and Las Vegas' more sensual pleasures. An honourable employer, Berengetti treated the loyal members of his staff as members of his own family. After the Hulk left Vegas, the android Frost (employed by the gangster Sam Striker) killed Berengetti.
