> ###### Pretty Boy
> **First appearance** Down Under #229
> **Appearances** 159

> [!abstract] In brief
> A member of the Reavers. He is a cyborg and has telepathic powers.

*"Other aspects of your character... are disctint liabilities. Inconvenient elements, such as a sense of morality, scruples, decency. So, I'm going to erase them. Replace them with an attitude more in keeping with your new life as a Reaver. Instant psychic re-programming. Like being reborn. No muss... No fuss, Presto Changeo... A whole new you! Isn't that nice?"* -- Pretty Boy.

Pretty Boy has bionic extendible arms and sharp fingers. Besides he has fiber-optics filaments which could combine with his telepathic powers using them for transform a person in a Reaver.

## Creation

Pretty Boy was created by Chris Claremont and Marc Silvestri in 1988 and first appeared in *Uncanny X-Men * #229.

## In Other Media
