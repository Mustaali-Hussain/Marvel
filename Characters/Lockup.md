## Origin

Lockup was a mutant that was infected by the Brood when they attempted to invade Earth. A paramedic named Harry Palmer was implanted with a [[Brood Queen|Queen]] egg and started infecting mutants to create a Brood force with abilities to rival the X-Men. Numerous mutants were infected including Lockup, Brickbat, Tension, Temptress, Blindside, [[Dive-Bomber]], Spitball and Whiphand.

Lockup would paralyze [[Wolverine]]with his touch but is forced to retreat when Brickbat and Dive-Bomber are killed in battle. Lockup would later battle the X-Men outside an amphitheater but is killed when a collapsing wall crushes him and Spitball.

## Creation

Lockup was created by Chris Claremont and Marc Silvestri in 1988 and first appeared in *Uncanny X-Men *#232.

## Powers & Abilities

Lockup can temporarily paralyze a person with his touch. Having been infected by the Brood, Lockup was granted fangs, claws, and a degree of enhanced strength like all Brood.
