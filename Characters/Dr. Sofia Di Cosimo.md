> Dr. Sofia Di Cosimo is the director of Il Museo Della Mitologia Antica (The Museum Of Ancient Mythology) in Rome. She is a world's expert in mythology and archeology among other subjects.

## Brief History

Sofia attempted to help Hulk and Amadeus Cho protect the Pandora's Box from ill use and found herself endangered by Hulk's foe Tyrannus and subsequently saved by the Hulk. Not long after, Sofia was tasked with assisting in helping Hulks friends better understand the nature of a magical wishing well that resulted in Hulk, Betty Ross and several of Hulks foes being sent to the Dark Dimension. She then accompanied a rescue party consisting of  Hulk's friend Amadeus Cho, Dr. Strange, and Monica Rappaccini into the Dark Dimension where they found themselves in the middle of the Wishing War.
