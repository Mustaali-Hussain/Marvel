> Tyler Guthrie was the father of Cannonball, Husk and their siblings. He was a coal miner in Kentucky and died of a lung disease contracted through his work.

## Origin

Heir to the Guthrie family farm in Kentucky. Worked at a coal mine to support his large family.

## Creation

Written by Chris Claremont and drawn by Bob McLeod.

## Major Story Arcs

Dies of black lung disease from working the coal mines and is buried on the family farm.
