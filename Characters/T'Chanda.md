> The Black Panther and King of Wakanda during World War Two, who befriended a young Steve Rogers. He is the father of T'Chaka and S'Yan making him the grandfather of T'Challa, Shuri, and T'Shan.

T'Chanda, also known as **Azzari the Wise**, was a monarch of Wakanda and the **Black Panther** during World War II, he is the son of King M'Teli and his wife, Imandla.

T'Chanda was married to Queen Nanali, with whom he had two children: T'Chaka and S'Yan.

## Creation

* T'Chanda in Fantastic Four Unlimite (1993) *

The character was first mentioned in Black Panther #1 (1976), written and illustrated by Jack Kirby, his name was **Azzari the Wise,** however, he only made one appearance in Fantastic Four Unlimited (1993), written by Roy Thomas. In that story, his name is said to be **Chanda**. He was later used by Reginald Hudlin in Black Panther (2005-2008) and Black Panther/Captain America: Flags of Our Fathers (2010), this time his name is spelled **Azzuri**. Another spelling used is Azzaria, which also came with a city named after him: Birnin Azzaria. Wakanda #5 (2023) establishes that his name is **T'Chanda** or Chanda and that Azzuri the Wise is his regnal name.
