> [!abstract] In brief
> Dentist from Alberquerque, New Mexico and the nephew of Bolivar Trask.

Donald Trask III was Bolivar Trask's nephew . He was able to control the Wild Sentinels. He sent them to capture Cyclops , Ugly John and Wolverine. Cassandra Nova spent ten hours "copying" his DNA before killing him.

Being the nephew of Bolivar Trask, Donald Trask III would also be related to Simon Trask, though it is not stated whether Simon is Donald's father or uncle.
