> The founder of the zombie-raising firm, Animators, Inc.

Bert Vaughn is the founder and manger of the St. Louis-based company Animators, Inc. He is over 6 feet tall and looks like a former athlete. He has blond-white hair that is cut very close to his head.

Bert and his number one animator, [[Anita Blake]], often argue about the clients he gives her. He is money hungry and often doesn't think about the safety of his employees if a client is willing to pay a large sum of money. Anita said once that Bert would take money for a hit on the Pope if he knew he could get away from it.
