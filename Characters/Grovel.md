> ###### Grovel
> **First appearance** The Story of the Year! #346

> [!abstract] In brief
> Grovel is a Klyruvian bounty hunter and partner of Spat.

Grovel has enhanced speed, durability, senses & reptilian strength. A former partner of Erik The Red he also has extremely sharp claws & teeth.

Grovel worked with [[Gambit]]. At once felt mild friendship and respect for Gambit, but when his partner [[Spat]] agreed to bring in him for the Morlock Massacre, his original perceptions changed.
