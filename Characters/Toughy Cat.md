## Origin

Toughy Cat was a comic character published by Timely Comics, Marvel Comic's predecessor, during the 'funny animal' craze in the 1950's. He most often fought with [[Ziggy Pig]] and [[Silly Seal]]. After this craze had ended, Toughy Cat was hardly ever seen again.
