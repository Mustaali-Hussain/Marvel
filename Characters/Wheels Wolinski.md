## ** Background**

### Wolfpack

 

### Dungston, Iowa

Most recently, [[Hawkeye]] called on Wheels to repair his S.H.I.E.L.D. van. Hawkeye asked [[Manifold]] to retrieve Wheels and bring him to Dungston, Iowa where the group was staying, which Manifold did. Wheels put up some resistance, complaining about the fact that the van was fitted with alien technology and other equipment he'd never seen before, but he was soon concentrating on the schematics of the van. Even when the town of Dungston came under attack, Wheels simply handed Hawkeye his bow and went back to work.
