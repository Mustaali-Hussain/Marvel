## Origin

Zarina Zahari is the new Spider-UK of Earth-834, taking up the mantle formerly held by [[Spider-UK|Billy Braddock]]. As the new Spider-UK, Zarina became a member of the British agency, W.H.O., and helps them investigate weird and supernatural phenomena.

## Powers and Abilities 

- **Enhance Physiology ****:** Zarina As a spider totem Zarina enhance physical stats which includes: enhance agility, enhance senses, enhance strength enhance speed. 
- **Electrostatic Energy Generation:** Zarina is able to generate a form of bio-electricity. Although she has little control over it, requiring the use of special earring to keep that ability in check.

**Ballooning:** By removing her earrings, she's capable of generating enough electricity to "surf" on Earth's electricity, thus controlling her falls.
- **Wall-Crawling:** Zarina can cling to any surface using her hands and feet.

- **Organic Webbing Generation** She has the ability to organically produce her own silk webbing.
