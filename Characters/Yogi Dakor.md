> [!abstract] In brief
> A Fantastic Four foe, hired by Doctor Doom.

Yogi was featured in the circus as the fire-proof man. He was arrested for trying to steal the show's payroll.

Yogi Dakor has the ability to resist flames. Naturally this interested [[Doctor Doom]], who hired him to oppose the [[Human Torch]]. Dakor's flame resistance was enhanced artificially using *XZ-12 Device*. Storm was tempted a near irresistible gift, a sports car. Much like Dakor, this car was also fireproof. A dose of nerve gas and Storm was captured. Upon completion of his mission, Dakor was placed in an alternate dimension until Doom had use of him again.
