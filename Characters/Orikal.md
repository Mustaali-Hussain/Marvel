## Origin

Orikal is a powerful and mysterious being from another reality. His origins are vague, but what is known is that he arrived in the realm of Asgard, lost and confused. The king of the trolls, Geirrodor exploited Orikal's bewilderment, and imprisoned him within a wall of flame. Geirrodor coaxed Orikal to help him amass knowledge, power and weapons. When the troll Ulik attempts to kill Orikal, the Asgardian god Thor steps in and saves him.    

 

## Creation

Orikal is a Marvel comics character, created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby. The enigmatic character first appears in Thor #138 released in 1967. 

 

## Powers and Abilities 

Orikal can use an artifact know as the Infinite Eye to see all.
