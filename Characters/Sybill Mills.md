## Origin

First met in the swamp around Citrusville, the young ballerina Sybill Mills was running away from a commando of masked types that kidnapped her. There she met [[Richard Rory]] who helped her to get rid of the tailing team along his friend [[Man-Thing]].

Later she went again to the swamps when Brian Lazarus was typing his opera in an abandoned mental hospital, with the company of his crazy fantasies made real.

When an older Lazarus Brian returns from California to the swamp to finish his novel, he stops by a restaurant to have a dinner, and by pure case (or fate touch?) Sybil meets him again as she's the waitress serving at the tables.
