## Origin

Ward is a synthetic person whose objective is to research humanity to determine what a soul is so that it can be determined if synthetic people have or can get a soul. Ward makes friends with a trucker, John Cray, but this makes both of them a target for the Sapien League. When the league tracks and attacks them at a diner, the Soul Stone chooses Ward as its host and gives him the ability to defend himself.

## Creation

Ward debuted in Avengers Annual #1 by writer Jed McKay and artist Travel Foreman. He created for the spinoff of the Infinity Wars event, which ended with [[Adam Warlock]] imbuing the Infinity Gems with the ability to choose their own destinies. He is one of the few hosts to not be introduced ahead of the Infinite Destinies event that would chronicle the Gems' new chosen ones.

## Major Story Arcs

### Infinite Destinies

[[Iron Man]] and [[Captain America]] were able to track the energy signature of "incarnations," when an Infinity Gem chooses a host, after each of them had separate run-ins with said hosts.

They responded to the attack on Ward at the diner but believed him to be the aggressor. Ward defended himself but ultimately negotiated for the safe passage of his friend, John Cray, which the Avengers obliged.

### The Soul of Arthur Douglas

Multitude continued to travel the American Southwest search for a sense of humanity. He was eventually sought out by Mantis and Gamora of the Guardians of the Galaxy. They were hoping to access the Soul Stone and take back Drax's soul that was still trapped inside. They believed this would help their friend feel complete.

Multitude mistook them as a threat and called a number of soul constructs to fight them off. The soul of Arthur Douglas took this opportunity to connect with Multitude. "Jazz Man," as Multitude called him, assured Multitude that he is more than the sum of his parts and will continue to be so if he loses Arthur as one of the souls.

After this talk, Multitude handed Arthur's soul over willingly, however, Mantis still gave him a large chunk of mysterium as payment.

### Infinity Watch

Multitude was uniting people with the souls of their lost loved ones as a carnival act on the Atlantic City Boardwalk. When he accidentally causes a riot among his customers, he is saved by [[Kamala Khan|Ms. Marvel]], who gives him a pep talk on being a superhero. Looking to put her advice to good use, Multitude tried to strike out on his own but ran into [[Apex]], a fellow stone bearer. Together, they felt their strength increase and sought out the other stone keepers together.

The two are drawn to Coney Island, where they cross paths with [[Spider-Boy (Briggs)|Spider-Boy]] fighting the Circus of Crime. Ward senses that Spider-Boy's soul is good so they join the fight. This proves to [[Colleen Wing]] (keeper of the Mind Stone) that they are heroes, so she reveals herself to them. The trio strategize their next move when they are approached by the remaining stone bearers.

They were confronted by Thor and [[Carol Danvers|Captain Marvel]], who had been led to the meeting by [[Phil Coulson]], recently resurrected by the Death Stone, the newly created seventh stone. In such close proximity to each other, their power got the attention of [[Thanos]]. He took out the stone keepers one by one, retrieving their power sources. Multitude was left vulnerable after being overwhelmed by Thanos soul and had his robot body crushed to pieces. Luckily, [[Overtime]], keeper of the Time Stone, managed to trap Thanos in a time bubble, creating a paradox that would destroy the universe. In order to preserve it, Thanos was forced to give back the stones, while Overtime turned back time, resurrecting the stone bearers.

Together, they became the new Infinity Watch and teleported away.

### Sticking Together

The current stone-bearers decided to stick together as a real team. Their first crisis brought them face to face with beings from another universe, called The Apeiron. They were stone-bearers from an alternate universe who have been travelling the multiverse stealing alternate infinity stones. The Watch learned this from Avery Zavala, a former stone-bearer from a universe already victimized by the Apeiron. After fighting off the Apeiron, Avery started to fight the Infinity Watch to steal their stones. Star used her abilities to create a new world to hide the stones from him.

Zavala networked together supervillains who went through Alcatraz to break him out and steal the stones. This eventually got the attention of both the Apeiron and the Infinity Watch, who were all drawn to Star, the only person who remembered the world as it was. Star was forced to revert the world so the Infinity Watch could agree to voluntarily share their power with Zavala. He wiped the Apeiron out but decided to keep the stones so he could remake his world. The Infinity Watch managed to outsmart him eventually but realized they were too dangerous to stick together. Using the power of the gems, they teleported themselves to where they could do the most good.
