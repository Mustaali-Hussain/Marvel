## Fall of the Mutants

Nazé is a Shaman and Medicine Chief on the Cheyenne. He is an incredibly powerful mystic. He has good heart, but his passions occasionally get the better of him.

He is a mentor to [[Forge]], he knows him his entire life. Sometimes forgets Forge is no longer a child or his pupil.

Nazé is an ally of [[Roma]] against the [[Adversary]].

## Powers

Nazé is a powerful mystic with the power to access incredible amounts of magic.
