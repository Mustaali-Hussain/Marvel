## Origin

Purple Hayes was the daughter of successful psychologist and hippie, Dr. Tim Hayes. He and his wife took Purple to a 10 pm showing of *Easy Rider*. As they approached their van, which they parked in an alley in protest of parking meters, they are approached by a conservatively dressed mugger. Purple's parents were killed in the encounter, so she dedicated herself to criminology so that she could hunt down her parents' killer. To do so, she banned the homeless people her father used to shelter in their mansion. She decided to create a merchandising phenomenon when one of the angry homeless people threw a Darth Vader helmet through her window.

## Creation

Nosferata first appeared in Sensational She-Hulk (1989) #19 by Steve Gerber and Bryan Hitch. She was a not-so-veiled parody of Batman

## Major Story Arcs

### Dorkham Asylum

Backed by a loyal Butler-ette and a Publicist Freda, she was aided by She-Hulk in battle against her Parents murderer, the psychotic Jack Serious.

Later she again fought with She Hulk against the She-Hulk impersonator Adrenazon.

### Time Trial

She was last seen along with almost every other She-Hulk related character at Jen's Time Infraction Trial heckling Razor Back for his misguided attempt to defend She-Hulk.

### Civil War

The Avengers Initiative considered her a "Potential Recruit," meaning she had yet to come forward on her own and might need more persuading to register.

## Powers and Abilities

Though somewhat kooky and obsessive-compulsive, Nosferata is a capable chemist, criminologist, disguise artist, and hand-to-hand fighter. She wears a flight-pack under her winged cloak, allowing her to fly for short distances. Nosferata carries throwing Nosferatarangs, sometimes used in conjunction with entangling cables. She drives the Nosferauto, which has an impressive-looking roof-mounted gun. The top floor of her mansion houses her base, the Nosferattic, which contains advanced computers. She is also aided by her personal assistant Freda.
