## Origin

Ben Liu was a normal man who worked at finances. One day while driving back home, he ended abducted by 3K, who experimented on him awakening a mutant gene on him. After the experiments were over, his memories were erased by Cassandra Nova and was released.

Despite the memory erasure, the trauma ended overcoming him, leaving him disoriented and delusional thinking that he has been abducted by aliens. He ended living on the streets of San Francisco as a homeless man.

## Major Story Arcs

His mutant powers ended manifesting and because of his delusions, he ended creating an illusion of an alien invasion that started to attack the city. Cyclops' X-men heard the news and decided to defend the city from the aliens. Once they realized the true nature of the invasion, the X-men were able to rescue Ben, faking his death and leaving him in a comatose state till they could estabilize his mind.

Psylock and Kid Omega soon restored his traumatized mind and discovered 3K's intervention in Ben's mutant powers.

Once he awoke, Ben decided to remain with the X-men to learn more about his powers.

## Powers and Abilities

Ben Liu has the mutant power of warping reality to create life life illusions. So far he has been able to create a full scale alien invasion with ovnis.
