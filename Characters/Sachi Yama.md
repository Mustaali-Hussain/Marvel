## Origin

Sachi Yama was a lawyer of the supervillain known as the [[Juggernaut]] in the alternate reality of the MC2 Universe. She not only helped Juggernaut legally, but also changed his view of the world. With her help, Juggernaut finally paid his dept to society and the two fell in love. They eventually got married and had a son, [[J2|Zane Yama]].
