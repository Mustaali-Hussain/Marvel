Falcona the Huntress is an Inhuman and a supporter of [[Maximus|Maximus the Mad]].

## Creation

Falcona was created by Gary Friedrich and Marie Severin in 1968 and first appeared in The Incredible Hulk Annual # 1.

## Powers & Abilities

Falcona can mentally control falcons and other birds of prey.
