> ###### Ego-Prime
> **First appearance** Odin Dies #198
> **Appearances** 12

> [!abstract] In brief
> Created by Ego to turn Earth into a living planet like itself, with Ego-Prime as its mind.

Ego created Ego-Prime with the power to evolve the Earth into a "bioverse" that would be driven by the mind of Ego-Prime. All life on the planet would merge with the planet itself and Ego-Prime.

Odin used Ego-Prime's power to transform three humans into the Young Gods: Harvest, Bright Sword, and Genii. The transformation cost Ego-Prime his existence.
