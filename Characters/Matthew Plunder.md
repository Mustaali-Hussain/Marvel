## Origin

Matthew Plunder is the son of [[Ka-Zar]] and [[Shanna]]. He was born in the Savage Land and is therefore a native to the land.

## Creation

Matthew Plunder was created by Chris Claremont and Art Adams and first appeared in a cameo in (Uncanny) X-Men Annual 12 (1988). He first fully appeared in Ka-Zar Volume 3 issue 1 (1997) by Mark Waid and Andy Kubert.

## Major Story Arcs

### Born

Matthew Plunder was born and raised in the Savage Land, but already found out the dangers of his native country from the moment he was still an infant.
