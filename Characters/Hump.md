> ###### Hump
> **First appearance** The Beginning of the End Part Two #99
> **Appearances** 25

> [!abstract] In brief
> Member of the Morlocks.

Hump is the brother of [[Brute]]. They fought alongside [[Masque]] to capture [[Sabretooth]], for his involvement in the Morlock Massacre. When Hump, Brute and Masque tracked [[Feral]] to the X-Mansion they found [[Cable]] had her and when Brute threatened Cable he was murdered.

Years later, Hump resurfaced again as part of the Morlocks, when the team battled the Reavers on Madripoor. His brother Brute, was resurrected, and also rejoined the Morlocks.

Member of the Morlocks.

## Creation

Hump was created by Fabian Nicieza and Rob Liefeld in 1991 and first appeared in New Mutants # 99.
