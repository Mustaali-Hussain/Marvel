## Origin

Atum is son of the female Elder God known as [[Gaea]] or Mother Earth and the Demiurge, which is the sentient life-force of the Earth’s biosphere. Mother Earth dispersed her own immortal energies into the planet that bears her name. When Mother Earth / Gaea gave birth to Atum he began a campaign to destroy all the Elder Gods who had fallen from their original divine nature and turned into monstrous demons. These murderous demonic entities were waging cannibalistic wars against each other to try and gain the most power. Atum's war against the demons ended their chaotic age but in the process of defeating the demonic Elder Gods by, ironically, consuming them, Atum became the Demogorge, a fairly monstrous entity all on its own.

Atum left Earth to radiate all the divine energies he had absorbed from demonkind. Atum's monstrous form that comes about when he has devoured massive quantities of evil energy is a kind of Jekyll-Hyde effect- his darkside, as it were. He departs to purge himself before returning to his home with Earth's sun. When Gaea deemed the dinosaurs and evolutionary dead end and sought to replace them with mammals Set opposed her. He had come to feed off their life energies and wished to continue so. She called upon Atum to defend her. During the conflict Atum cut off Set's head several times only for two more to grow in its place. After a fierce battle that lasted ages Atum was eventually able to drive him off, but the battle had destroyed the dinosaurs.

## Creation

Atum is a Marvel comics character first appearing in Strange Tales #70. the character is created by Alan Zelenetz and Robert Hall.

## Character Evolution

During the Atlantis Attacks event, Atum was summoned by Thor, his half-brother, to help battle Set.

## Powers and Abilities

Atum resides in the sun and is completely immune to the heat, pressure and cosmic forces of the sun. He can also absorb/consume foes thereby gaining all of their abilities. Atum is by a considerable margin one of the most powerful entities of the whole Marvel Universe. Atum is an extremely mighty being with unbelievable power sufficient to singlehandedly defeat the elder gods, or simultaneously overcome multiple pantheons of deities all by his lonesome. Atum possesses the power of the Sun itself, and can project intense forms of both heat and light and can also fly at the speed of light. Atum also possesses the power of flight. When fighting gods, Atum quickly reverts to God Eater form, and while possessing incredible strength, stamina, and flight in this form, it is not totally invulnerable as it has been hurt by divine weapons of exceptional quality. The God Eater's most disturbing trait is its ability to convert gods into energy and absorb them through orifices in the palms of its hands. During this act the rapidly dissolving god's form can be seen coursing through the God Eater's veins. A god consumed by the God Eater retains some shape and sentience and manifests as a face on the entity's body. Probably like all gods he has resistance to all Earthly diseases and toxins and some resistance to magic.
