> [!abstract] In brief
> Melinda May is a level 7 S.H.I.E.L.D. agent, nicknamed "The Cavalry". She's a veteran pilot and soldier with many years of experience. She withdrew from the duty as a field agent after an incident in Manama, Bahrain which left her slightly damaged. However, Melinda returned to the field when Agent Phil Coulson recruited her onto his team.

## Creation

The character originated in the* Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* television series, and like the other characters, was not an existing comic character. After becoming a fan favorite, Melinda May was brought into the mainstream Earth-616 comic canon by writer Mark Waid.

The character was initially conceived as a white woman named Althea Rice, but was heavily rewritten on the strength of Ming-Na Wen's audition, and renamed not wanting an Asian woman to have the last name "Rice."

## Major Story Arcs

### The Cavalry

In *The Cavalry* One-Shot from 2015, Melinda's nickname origin is shown to be the same as it is in the MCU TV-show. Being an agent sent in to save agents from an unknown Inhuman, only for it be revealed to be a little girl, who she's forced to kill in order to save everyone from her rampage.

### Joining Coulson's Team

Melinda May was recruited for [[Phil Coulson|Agent Coulson]]'s team, due to be a well-known agent with strong fighting skills. She helped his team to rescue, Asgardian god, [[Heimdall]]. The team would later be assigned to protect the son of [[Scarlet Witch]], [[Wiccan]].

### Ghost Rider

Melinda May and Phil Coulson assisted the newest Ghost Rider, [[Ghost Rider (Reyes)|Robbie Reyes]] with a demonic ghost known as [[Eli Morrow]]. While S.H.I.E.L.D. didn't fully trust the hero, they would be impressed by his work.

### F.B.I Agent

After S.H.I.E.L.D. disbanded, May not wanting to give up the good fight, joined the F.B.I., becoming a high level agent right off the bat. Even after S.H.I.E.L.D. was rebuilt, May would continue her new line of work.

### Siancong War

Melinda is on site in Siancong's capital when [[Lotus Newmark|Lady Lotus]] takes command of a dark energy to encase the entire country within a dome.

### Stark Unlimited

*Working with Iron Man*

When [[Iron Man|Tony Stark]] was ousted by Stark Unlimited by [[Feilong]], May was appointed to the board of directors. She attempted to mitigate the damage Feilong committed when he used the company for Sentinel production. After regaining control of the company Tony was once more victim of a hostile takeover by A.I.M. and Roxxon, and he sought Melinda's aid to turn the board against them. Although Stark successfully stopped the merger, every member of the board had to step down which led to Melinda losing her job. May then assisted Stark and [[Ironheart]] in the field to take down an ex-S.H.I.E.L.D. colleague, which leads them to a defunct Stark Industries factory in Chicago run by [[Lucia Von Bardas]]. May helps fight Von Bardas, The Heat and free the prisoners. In the aftermath Tony invites May back onto the Stark Unlimited board, but Melinda declines and offers an alternative: re-hire all the ex-S.H.I.E.L.D. agents who have become Heat mercenaries under Stark's company with May acting as Head of Corporate Security.

## Other Media

### Live Action

### Marvel's Agents of Shield

*Ming-Na Wen as Melinda May*

In the Marvel Cinematic Universe, Melinda May is introduced as a legendary S.H.I.E.L.D. agent recruited by [[Phil Coulson]] as part of his team. She has a dark and mysterious past, and was left traumatized after an experience where she was forced to kill a number of cultists during a mission. As a result, she is hesitant to get into field work again, and initially agrees to Coulson's offer on the condition that she only act as the team's pilot.

Melinda quickly moves back into active field work, and enters into a sexual relationship with [[Grant Ward]]. She also develops a mother/daughter relationship with [[Quake|Skye]], whom she grows very protective of. In the season finale, she defeats Ward after he is revealed to be a double agent working for HYDRA.

In the series, she is portrayed by Ming-Na Wen.
