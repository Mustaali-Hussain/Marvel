## Major Story Arcs

Joshua had left his wife Dorothy Walker and Patsy while she was still a teenager. Mostly because of his failing relationship with his wife.

Years later, while with the Defenders, Patsy started looking for him and found him. While Joshua was embarrassed for leaving his family, the two had a happy reunion and spoke more often from that point on.
