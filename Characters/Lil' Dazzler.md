## Brief History

Lil' Dazzler was created by Mojo alongside many other 'mini' versions of the X-Men to become the newest television hype for Mojo's show. She and the rest of the Lil' X-Men however fled the scene thanks to Rita Wayword. She and her other friends formed a team and started calling themselves the X-babies. She had a love/hate relationship with fellow teammember Shugah, cause both of them where in love with Lil' Longshot.
