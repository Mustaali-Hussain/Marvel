> A former love interest for Tony Stark, Rumiko was the daughter of Kenjiro Fujikawa, the man who took over Stark's company when he was in the Heroes Reborn universe.

## Brief History

Rumiko's romance with [[Iron Man|Tony Stark]] enjoys many highs and lows. She enjoys his jet-set lifestyle but finds he is often too obsessed with his work and does not pay enough attention to her. After breaking up with Tony, she sleeps with Stark's business rival and enemy, [[Tiberius Stone]].

She was murdered by an Iron Man impostor named Clarence Ward.
