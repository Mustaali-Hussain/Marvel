## Origin

Margaret Braddock is the child of Brian Braddock and Megan, and still a young baby. However, within days after her birth, the child showed intelligence that was unheard of. She could speak and learn things the way a young teenager would. This caused much distress with her parents. Escpecially Megan thought she failed as a mother. Luckily, Megan's old Excalibur teammates Rachel, Kitty Pryde and Nightcrawler where able to let her lose this feelings.

## Creation

Margaret Braddock was created by Marc Guggenheim and Alitha E. Martinez and first appeared in X-Men Gold Annual issue 1 (2018).
