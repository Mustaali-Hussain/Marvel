> Maria Trovaya Pym was the first wife of Hank Pym and the mother of Nadia Pym.

## Origin

Maria Trovaya was a young woman living in Hungary. There, due to her critisizing the authorities, she became a political prisoner. She however managed to escape to the United States. There she met, dated and eventually married [[Hank Pym]].

## Creation

Maria Trovaya Pym was created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby and first appeared in Tales to Astonisch issue 44 (1963).

## Major Story Arcs

### Honeymoon and Death

After Maria married Hank, they decided to spend their honeymoon back in Hungary, convinced Maria's new American citizenship would protect her from persecution. Unfortunately, it did not. She was arrested and seemingly executed not much later. This caused considerable trauma to Hank Pym, whom swore revenge.

However, Hank was unaware that Maria was also pregnant with his child at the time. Maria gave birth to a girl, who was later named Nadia and whom (many years later) became the All New Wasp. It is presumed that Maria was killed right after giving birth, since Nadia has no memory of her. 

Later stories have Pym commenting on [[Wasp|Wasp (Janet Van Dyne)]] resembling Maria in appearance. Maria was much later impersonated by [[M.O.D.A.M|Olinka Barankova (MODAM)]].
