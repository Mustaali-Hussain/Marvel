*"Aquarius is my name... and water is my game!"* -- Aquarius (LMD).

A Life Model Decoy that participated in all of the android Zodiac's operations.

They included: joining an insane [[Quicksilver]]to kill the Avengers, battling the Avengers after their massacre of the human Zodiac where he was apparently destroyed, and when resurrected by the Zodiac Key was transported to the Key's home dimension, but was deactivated when it cut off from the zodiac energies that gave the androids their powers.
