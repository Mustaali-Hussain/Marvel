## Creation

Sea Witch is a Marvel comic book character who was created by the team of Mark Gruenwald, Ralph Macchio, Keith Pollard and Gene Day. She first appears in"Thor" vol. 1 #300 (October, 1980). Many of the details of the character are first introduced in Spectacular Spider-Man #8 released in 1998. The character would also receive substantial development in the Marvel Comics Presents series.Sea Witch is one of the Young Gods, human beings evolved by the Celestials.

 

## History

Bridgit O'Hare was born in Ireland in the 12th century, she was a young seventeen year old who grew up in a small community of farmers and sheep herders. At tis time several Goddesses of the Earth were recruiting what they deemed as fine specimens of humanity to present to the Celestials at the time of their next judgement. If the specimen were deemed worthy, humanity would be allowed to survive and further achieve their potential. However if the Celestials did not fine them worthy they would be utterly eliminated.

The Celestials did arrive in the 1980's and the specimens from several past centuries were deemed worthy. Bridgit herself was evolved by the machinations of the Celestials into a Young God, she would forever go by the name Sea Witch. Able to transform her body into vast amounts of water, control water in the local vicinity, and swim unheeded in the depths of the Oceans.

 

 

## Major Story Arcs

### Return to Earth

Sea Witch was training aboard the mothership with the other Young Gods when they were spied upon by the High Evolutionary. Sea Witch is also in love with her fellow Young God, Highnote and they were romantically involved. Shortly afterwards Sea Witch has a prophetic dream in which they had been invaded by an evil spirit. Daydreamer and Varua determined that it was related to the encounter they had had in Israel before, and the Celestials permitted them to return to Earth. They were granted three days on Earth in which to investigate the matter.

Daydreamer became determined to halt his plans to reshape humanity's evolution. Highnote teleported the rest of Juniper's students to Earth so that they could oppose the High Evolutionary, and they fought his agents Quint and the Gatherers alongside Spider-Man. However, Katos came in pursuit of them with his half of the Young Gods and they fought each other. After Daydreamer accidentally harmed Calculus with her powers, the Young Gods all stood down.

Sea Witch, Caduceus, Highnote and Moonstalker investigated northern Ireland, and fought a band of terrorists. They located a mystical stone guarded by a creature from Sea Witch's dream, and Sea Witch destroyed it with a torrent of water. Sea Witch and the others were contacted by Daydreamer, who had found another one of the stones. Unable to contact the other four in Sri Lanka, they decided to rendezvous in Sri Lanka. Daydreamer seemingly granted Nauda's wishes, but she actually gave him illusionary stones. The Young Gods were reunited, and joined in a Uni-Mind to destroy Nauda.

### War with the Eternals

After years of waiting on the silent and unmoving Celestials for a sign, the Young Gods go mad and descend into deviant behavior for the hopes of even the slightest acknowledgement. They then decide that the Celestials must wish for them to use their powers and act on their own. Upon deciding this, the group left the Celestials' spacecraft and returned to Earth, where they intended to use the consciousness of all humans on the planet to create a new Celestial.

*Into the deep blue...*

They decide to start with the island nation of Madripoor and extend outwards. Upon learning of this, the Eternals, who were unaffected by the Young Gods' power to drain consciousness, encountered and attempted to stop them. Both Water Witch and her lover Highnote attempt to subdue the Eternal's Makkari and Sersi. Water Witch turns into a pool sized body of water and contains Sersi in an attempt to drown her. However Sersi defeats Water Witch by using her transmutation powers and turning her into silicon. Eventually, the Eternals convince Varua that the Celestials may have been testing them with their silence and coming to Earth may be the wrong choice. While Varua hesitates to ponder the possibility of being wrong, she is killed and Sea Witch and the other Young Gods who had not been killed during battle with the Eternals immediately fall to the ground, seemingly dead.

## Powers and Abilities

Sea Witch has the ability to exist underwater and has several genetically adapted physical traits to assist with attribute. She is able to survive in the deep waters of the oceans, withstanding its pressures the deeper she swims. Bridgit is capable of breathing and traveling through water as a naturally as through air. The swimming speed which she is capable of attaining is unknown but is most definitely superhuman to some degree. Her eyesight has also genetically adapted to the murky depths of the oceans allowing her to retain visual scenes even with practically no light at those depths. Sea Witch has developed a type of membrane which she can cover her eyes with, this dark blue and transparent film allows her more of a measure of vision protection.

*Marco...Polo...*

Sea Witch is also hydrokinetic, she is able to control water in all its forms (solid, liquid or gas). Sea Witch can control a large quantity of animated water to do her bidding. She can also control water vapor, and her control is so fine she's managed to utilize it in powerful sprays. As time went by she learned to emit high-pressure water blasts from her hand. She has also been seen to control large bodies of water to create tsunami like tidal waves.

Through the genetic engineered gifts granted to her, Sea Witch has mutated human physical condition. He is imbued with a high degree of strength, speed, endurance, physical resistance, agility, and reflexes possessed are considered superhuman. She can exert himself at maximum effort for far longer than most humans can endure.
