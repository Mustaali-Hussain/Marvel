## Creation

Ramshot was created by David Michelinie and Mark Bagley in 1993 and first appeared in Venom: Lethal Protector # 2.   

## Abilities & Weapons

Ramshot wears an armored suit based on the design of the Guardsman armor that provides him with enhanced strength, flight and various weapons. Ramshot is equipped with a device from his wrist gauntlets that fires a battering pulse blast and small hovering discs that attach to their feet to provide flight.
