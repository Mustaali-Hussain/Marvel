> Ricky Calusky is Piledriver's son. He decided to follow in his father's footsteps and became a criminal joining forces with the likes of Baron Zemo.

## Origin

When Ricky found out his dad was Piledriver he decided to join the Wrecking Crew. Piledriver agreed because he was going to retire soon and he needed a replacement.

## Major Story Arcs

### First Job, First Defeat

His first bank robbery was a failure because the Runaways attacked him and stopped him. Since he had no powers, he couldn't do anything about it and promptly gave up.

### Avengers Undercover

Some months later, Ricky was seen again in Baron Zemo's underground society for villains. There, he befriended a former superhero named Death Locket. She was there first as a spy, but eventually turned to villany. There was a mutual affection between Ricky and Death Locket, and he tried to make avances towards her.

### Arrest

While out on the town with his father, leaving a cinema, Ricky was arrested by the forces of S.H.I.E.L.D. and placed in a holding-cell. He has not been heard from since.
