## Origin

Tigerstryke was employed as an agent of Department K, the Canadian intelligence organization that investigated superhumans. To aid him, he was given a suit based on the same technology as the suit of Guardian.

## Creation

Tigerstryke first appeared in X-Force (1991) #11 by Fabian Nicieza and drawn by Mark Pacella.

## Major Story Arcs

### Weapon PRIME

Tigerstryke joined Weapon PRIME, a task force of Department K, working in conjunction with SHIELD. They were assigned to take down Cable when they received intel that he was running the Mutant Liberation Front. They lay siege on X-Force’s Adirondack headquarters to capture him, but he was defended by X-Force. Tigerstryke had the circuitry of his suit scrambled by Siryn’s sonic scream.

### Hunt for Northstar

After the failure to take down Cable, Tigerstryke took over leadership from Garrison Kane. Department K sent them to hunt down Northstar, who had been framed by Arcade for murder. They eventually tracked him down to a circus in Russia which was being manipulated by Arcade. Northstar was able to convince Tigerstryke that there was a bigger issue at hand. He ordered Weapon PRIME to aid Northstar for now, which ultimately gave Northstar the opportunity to run.
