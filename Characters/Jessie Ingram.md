## Brief History

*Jessie Ingram*

Jessie is the girl whom [[Juston Seyfert|Juston]] had a huge crush on while he was going to high school. When Juston first came to that high school, he was bullied around. From that day on, Juston remained in class most times during recess. Upon one of those days, Jessie came walking in the door and the two almost immediately started a friendship, with Jessie teasing the somewhat dorky Juston from time to time.

The two became real close friends and eventually she was even trusted with Juston's secret: the fact that Juston had rebuilt and reprogrammed a huge [[Sentinel Mark IV|Mark IV Sentinel]] in his backyard. Jessie and Juston remain good friends, and sometimes it seems that Jesse sees more in him then just another friend. Since Juston has moved to the Avengers Academy, nothing has been heard from Jessie.

 

## Powers

Jessie has no known superpowers and appears to be a regular teenage high-school student.
