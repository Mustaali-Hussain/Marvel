## Origin

A fighter in Cable's Clan Chosen, Silo lives in the future in which he fights against the New Canaanites and the machinations of Stryfe.

## Creation

Written by Fabian Nicieza and drawn by Art Thibert.

## History

In a battle, Cable and Silo try to effectively attack what they think is a New Canaanites assault. Their hope is to lead the attack away from their base camp so as to protect those housed therein. Their plan fails and a "therm" is "pumped" right on the base. Cable is distraught because his wife and son were in the base camp. Silo tries to keep Cable from entering the hot zone to no avail. Boak announces to the assembled members of the Clan Chosen that the attack was really started by Stryfe.
