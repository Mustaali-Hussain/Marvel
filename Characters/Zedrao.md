## Major Story Arcs

### The Death of Captain Marvel

When [[Captain Marvel]] was dying of cancer, countless friends and allies came to bid him farewell at his deathbed on Titan. Surprisingly, Zedrao attended as well, as a representative of the Imperial Skrull Empire. Even though Captain Marvel had long been an enemy of the Skrulls, they still respected his valor in battle, considering him their greatest enemy and one of the greatest warriors of all time. And so, by the will of the Skrull Imperial Council, Zedrao presented Captain Marvel with the Royal Skrull Medal of Valor. With that, Zedrao saluted the Captain, wishing for his passing to be swift and his afterlife great, after which he departed from Titan.
