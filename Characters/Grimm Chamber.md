## Brief History

Grimm Chamber is a character from Earth-295 who encountered the X-Force team when they went to the Age of Apocalypse to try and find a "Life Seed" to cure [[Angel|Archangel]], from his counter-part personality, Death. Grimm Chamber seems to be an amalgamation of Thing of the Fantastic Four and Chamber of Generation X. He is a member of the Black Legion who attempt to stop X-Force from breaking a mutant known as Gateway out of Prison.
