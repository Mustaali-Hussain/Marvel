## Origin

Detlef is a German-Born naturalized U.S. citizen, bio-engineer and a former Jr. Research scientist for the Weapon X Project. He specializes in Bio-Enhancements. Several years ago he was given Dr. Zander Rice's private journal and a vial of Wolverine's blood.

Recently Detlef has been taken under the watchful eye of Malcolm Colcord and the US Military.
