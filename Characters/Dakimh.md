> Dakimh is a powerful sorceror of earth's dimension.

Dakimh is a 20,000 year old sorceror and magician, whose former apprentice was Jennifer Kale. He allied himself with several other supernatural characters like the Man-Thing, Korrek, and Howard the Duck during their many adventures. He was later seemingly killed by an evil wizard, but survived by allowing his spirit to exist among a higher plane of existance. He was also friends with Steve Gerber, who wrote the Man-Thing book in the Marvel 616 Universe.
