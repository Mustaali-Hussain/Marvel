> ###### Supreme Hydra
> **First appearance** The Man For The Job!; Eternity Beckons! #135

> **Also known as** Imperial Hydra

> [!abstract] In brief
> Supreme Hydra is not a person but a title, held by the various supreme leaders of the HYDRA Organization. The original holder was an unnamed Japanese man of the World War II era.

Prominent holders of this title include Baron Strucker, Arnold Brown, Richard Fisk, the Deltite and Viper/Madame Hydra. Several of the users of the title remain unnamed.
