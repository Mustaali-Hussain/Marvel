> ###### Czar
> **First appearance** Another Fine Mess, Part Two #2
> **Appearances** 10

> [!abstract] In brief
> Marvel villain who fights Spider-Man and Wolverine through time along side his associate Big Murder.

His weapon is his Bat, which is studded with Time diamonds. Anything he hits with it is sent to a different era in time of his choice. It also enables him to travel to other eras, both past and future. This also means that he encounters several different versions of himself.
