> [!abstract] In brief
> Tracy Authier apparently has no powers of her own, despite the fact that her dad is a telepathic cop. She has had multiple run-ins with Generation X.

Tracy Authier has stolen things from the Massachuttes Academy, read Husk's diary, and forced the kids of Generation X into letting her join their crew.

Luckily, she thought Husk's diary was a collection of sci-fi stories she'd concocted because she was so bored being a perfectly ordinary student at the Massachuttes Acadamy for Gifted Youngsters.
