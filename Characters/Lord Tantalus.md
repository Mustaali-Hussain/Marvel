> ###### Lord Tantalus

> **Also known as** Tantalus, Lord T

> [!abstract] In brief
> An extra-terrestrial Deviant created by the Dreaming Celestial.

Very powerful extra-territorial Deviant who conquered worlds for his love Nirvana. He was trapped on Earth for centuries by the Eternals until he was able to use a Stargate near Sol to return to his throne world of Armechadon.

Fathered multiple children including Pelops, Id, and [[Blackwulf|Lucian]]. Tantalus murdered Pelops for betrayal, and was ultimately killed by Lucian as Blackwulf. As an immortal though, as his father-in-law [[Khult]]said, he is likely to return through his own ego.
