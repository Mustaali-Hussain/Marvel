> ###### Tachyon
> **First appearance** Split Decision #3
> **Appearances** 10

> [!abstract] In brief
> Tachyon is capable of faster-than-light speeds instantaneously

Tachyon is a robot member of Force. It has the ability to fly and can also reflect any form of energy back to its original source. Tachyon can travel faster-than-light instantaneously, but is only reactive in nature. Having no offensive capabilities, Tachyon can only use its enemies attacks against them. 

## Creation

Tachyon was created by Jim Valentino in 1990 and first appeared in Guardians Of The Galaxy # 3.
