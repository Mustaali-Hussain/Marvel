## Creation

Reptilla was created by Len Wein, George Perez and Joe Sinnott in 1977 and first appeared in Fantastic Four # 186.

## Powers & Abilities

Reptilla could magically assume a mutated form in which the lower portion of her body and her arms resembled the body of a snake, and in which snake heads took the place of her hands. Even in her human form, she had limited abilities to wield magic.

Her powers were enhanced by the Wizard, giving her arms and torso stretching capabilities similar to that of Mr. Fantastic.
