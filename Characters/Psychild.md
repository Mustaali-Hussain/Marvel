## Origin

Psychild was created by Mojo as part of the X-Babies, which would become a show in Mojo's reality. The X-Babies however fled to the real X-Men, but eventually decided to return to their own home-reality with Mojo.

During one of their playpen sessions, Psychilde was involved in an accident that caused her cellular structure to decay rapidly. The X-Babies needed to use [[Mojo]]’s machines to save Psychilde. The Mighty ‘Vengers battled the X-Babies, stopping them getting to the machines. However, the ‘Vengers quickly realised that Mojo was a bad guy and so helped the X-Babies, resulting in Psychilde getting a new body, an Asian body.
