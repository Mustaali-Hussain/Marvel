> [!abstract] In brief
> Epsilon Red was a Super Soldier created by the same program as Omega Red. He was designed during the Space Race, as a way for Russia to reach the moon first. He is also the father of Elena Ivanova.

## Story 

Wolverine was having nightmares about "Terry Adams", and had asked Professor Xavier to help him in finding out who Terry Adams was.  Xavier tapped into Wolverine's mind, trying to overcome the barriers.  It was somewhat unsuccessful, however they eventually made some leeway when Wraith discovered that Terry Adams was actually a Russian Space Center called Tyuratam.  Wolverine encountered Epsilon Red, a Super Soldier very much like Omega Red.  Epsilon Red's daughter removed Wolverine's memory implants so he can remember everything.
