> [!abstract] In brief
> The sole surviving member of the Zen Whoberi, Gamora, was saved as a child by Thanos. Through Thanos' augmentation and training she became a highly skilled and feared galactic assassin. Using her new formidable skills and abilities, she gained the reputation and title of The Deadliest Woman in the Universe. She eventually turned good and became a member of the Guardians of the Galaxy.

## Origin

The Zen Whoberi was a population who were wiped out by Badoon. (However in the original storyline they were killed by the Universal Church of Truth.) Gamora was the only one of the Zen Whoberi that survived. Thanos discovered Gamora alive and decided to save and train her as a weapon to use against and kill the Magus. Thanos made sure Gamora was raised with discipline, though he did on occasion show her affection as a father would show his child: such as celebrating the pagan Yule with elements of Christmas. Through rigorous training she would become a highly skilled master of various martial arts from throughout the universe.

Even though Gamora was learning and becoming a master martial artist, she still had more to learn. While still a young girl, she went on a trip with Thanos. Though explicitly against Thanos' orders and warning, out of curiosity she decided to venture planet-side, where she fell into an ambush set up by a group of thugs. The attackers used their numerical advantage and were able to defeat her in spite of her great skill. She was severely injured in the fight and gang raped.

Near death, her proverbial knight in shining armor arrived in the form of Thanos, who quickly dispatching the assailants. Thanos recovered the apologetic and near death Gamora. After returning to Sanctuary Thanos restored and augmented Gamora. This was achieved via bionic implants and replacement parts of Thanos' own design. These physical and power upgrades made her an equal to Adam Warlock's own physical abilities. She also received specialized training and abilities including resistance to reality distortion as well as an accelerated healing factor, that rivals even Wolverine's. Her purpose was to kill Magus, Adam's evil godlike counterpart.

## Creation

Gamora was created by Jim Starlin and first appeared in Strange Tales #180 (1975).

## Major Story Arcs

### Meeting Warlock

*Gamora's Original Appearance.*

When Gamora grew to adulthood it was time for her to begin the mission she had trained for all her life, to go after Magus and the Universal Church of the Truth. Her abilities and skill made her a dreaded assassin. She was able to get close to Magus but failed in her assassination attempt. This failure forced Thanos to take a more direct role in the affair. Thus he teleported to her location and joined forces with Warlock and Pip.

The Magus however had other plans and ordered 25,000 of his Black Knights to attack Warlock and any companions. This forced the allies to fight through the attack and ultimately escape the churchs home-world via Thanos teleporting them all aboard sanctuary. Even though this assassination attempt failed in due course the Magus was defeated. Soon after Thanos sent Gamora on a new mission, to befriend and keep tabs on Adam, in an attempt to prevent her from learning of his true nature and ambitions. However before she could truly start her mission the space craft she was piloting came under attack from Drax the Destroyer, whose sole purpose in life was to destroy Thanos. Drax had targeted Gamora's ship due to her relationship to Thanos and destroyed the ship.

As Gamora lay dying, her ally Adam Warlock was able to take her soul into his Soul Gem. It was after this that Gamora eventually discovered that Thanos was obsessed with Death and planned to commit stellar genocide and quit serving her master and adopted father.

Soulworld existed inside the Soul Gem. This was where Gamora, Pip and (eventually) Adam Warlock existed in peace. Years later, when Thanos returns from the dead, Soul World is visited by the Silver Surfer and Drax the Destroyer, who are there seeking aide from Adam Warlock, who with the aid of Master Order and Lord Chaos was able to defeat Thanos before. Finally Adam Warlock determined that Thanos must not be permitted to continue to grow in power. Warlock guided his allies Gamora and Pip the Troll out of their peaceful Soulworld to the real world. When they entered the real world they needed bodies to exist in so their souls took over the bodies of three people that had died in a car accident, leading into the events of The Infinity Gauntlet.

### Infinity Gauntlets

*Gamora with the Infinity Watch*

At the end of the Infinity Gauntlet ordeal, Adam Warlock was in possession of all six infinity gems but by order of the Living Tribunal was unable to use them together, so Warlock gave all but the soul gem away to guardians he trusted and called this group the Infinity Watch. Gamora was brought on board as guardian of the Time Gem; a power that Gamora didn't really want and had no idea what to do with; though almost as soon as the gem was on her brow she experienced a premonition of Warlock being killed by an unfamiliar figure who would later be revealed to be future Infinity Watch member Maxam.

Gamora's strength and skill would start to be truly recognized during her time with the Watch. Most notably after her decimating a UN assault battalion in 8 minutes. The UN in turn upgraded her strength threat level in their database from Spider-Man to Iron Man level. During her time with the Infinity Watch, Gamora also began to have romantic feelings for Adam, though she still could not seem to find the courage or the time to reveal them to Adam, largely stemming from the daily adventures and shenanigans affiliated with being a member of the Watch.

The Infinity War saw the return of Magus, this time in possession of even greater power in the form of five cosmic containment units, communally called Cosmic Cubes. Gamora with Warlock and the rest of the Watch found themselves once again in an alliance, formed from necessity, with the villainous Thanos. The allies soon were investigating an energy beacon. However unbeknownst to them, the Magus had placed it to distract and force a confrontation between the various forces that sought to thwart his goals. Forces such as the Avengers, Hulk, Quasar, the X-Men and Alpha Flight united.

Soon a full blown confrontation broke out among the two factions. Gamora faced off against multiple Earth heroes, even incapacitating several, utilizing her superior fighting skills and training. Heroes such as the Invisible Woman, She Hulk, Rogue, Cyclops, Vindicator, Strong Guy and Wolverine. She was eventually overcome by stealth and numbers that resulted in her being knocked out. Ultimately the battle was halted by the arrival and intervention of Galactus. He would later take control of Gamora in order to assist him in reviving a catatonic Eternity. That would be her last significant act in the Arc. After the Infinity War, Warlock was left in a catatonic state and Gamora's true feelings emerged. She stayed by his side the entire time, often taking charge of the team that could evaporate at any moment without a leader. At first Adam did not feel the same way but after some time Adam began to feel the same.

Odin called for every able bodied warrior to stop the trespassers, thus Gamora found herself in battle against the Asgardians on Bifrost. Once again her formidable skills were put to the test. ## Powers and Abilities

*The Deadliest Woman In The Universe.*

Gamora’s martial artist skills are considered to be among the highest in the Marvel Universe as she has mastered 83.4% of most known armed and unarmed techniques from various warfaring cultures. Her fighting skills are so advanced she can defeat opponents who are physically far superior to her. Her skill gives her the ability to outfight others who would be considered more powerful and/or stronger than her.

She has engaged and fought to a stand-still the likes of the Thing, She-Hulk, Rage and even Thanos - all of whom are vastly stronger and more durable than she is. She also defeated a whole platoon of highly trained military personal in a short time. She has the ability to paralyze or kill opponents by striking vital nerve cluster points on their bodies. Her preferred weapons are swords and knives. Her favorite being Godslayer.

Thanos augmented her to be the physical equal of Adam Warlock. Gamora has highly enhanced speed, reflexes, agility, senses, stamina/endurance, durability and healing factor. She possesses super strength comparable to Ironman (35 to 50 tons of lifting capacity), although some incarnations of her character placed her strength level equivalent to that of Spiderman (10-15 tons of lifting capacity). As well as a precognitive tracking instinct , She has demonstrated the ability to survive in the vacuum of space un aided. Though if she can do this indefinitely is undetermined.

Through specialized training also provided by Thanos she has resistance to an unknown extent against reality distortion and telepathic assaults.

Most of Gamora's skeleton has been replaced with an unnamed, nearly unbreakable substance by Thanos.

Zoe Saldana portrays Gamora in the live-action *Guardians of the Galaxy *movie. In the film, Gamora is the adopted daughter of Thanos who betrays him after having a crisis of conscience. She is arrested after attempting to steal a rare orb (which unbeknownst to the heroes, contains the Power Stone, one of the legendary Infinity Stones) from Peter Quill, but escapes along with Quill and several other inmates.

The fugitives band together to keep the stone from Ronan the Accuser, and Gamora begins to fall in love with Peter. After defeating Ronan, the heroes choose to stay together as a band of galactic heroes.

### Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 (2017)

Zoe Saldana reprises her role in the sequel, *Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2*. The sequel has a major focus on Gamora's relationship with her sister, Nebula, and the abuse the two suffered while being raised by Thanos. It is revealed that Thanos used to force the two girls to fight one another, and that every time Nebula lost, he would replace another part of her body with a machine. This caused her to develop a bitter hatred of Gamora, leading to their present day rivalry. When Nebula arrives to kill Gamora, a fierce battle ensues, which eventually leads to the two sisters reconciling after Gamora apologizes for the pain she caused at Thanos' behest. Though Gamora tries to convince Nebula to join the Guardians, Nebula declines, saying that she needs to find closure by killing Thanos once and for all.

### Avengers: Infinity War (2018)

*Thanos and Gamora*

Zoe Saldana reprises her role again in* Avengers: Infinity War*, and plays a major role in the storyline. Additionally, Ariana Greenblatt portrays a child version of Gamora in flashbacks. Through flashbacks, more of Gamora's backstory is revealed, and it is shown that as a young girl, she was taken in by Thanos after the Mad Titan slaughtered half of her planet's population, including her birth mother. Thanos regards Gamora as his favorite child and holds her in high esteem, even after her betrayal. When the Guardians find the injured Thor floating through space and learn that Thanos has begun actively collecting the Infinity Stones, Gamora tells the heroes of his ultimate goal, which is to balance the universe by wiping out half of all of the sentient life in existence. The Guardians travel to Knowhere to protect the Collector and the Reality Stone, but arrive too late, as Thanos has already taken the Stone. Thanos kidnaps Gamora and takes her back to his ship, where he attempts to coax her back to his side, saying that he hoped she would one day be his successor. When Gamora refuses, Thanos reveals that he took her because she learned the location of the Soul Stone, the only Infinity Stone whose location is still unknown.

*Young Gamora*

Gamora refuses to give up the information, but relents after Thanos reveals that he has captured Nebula and begins torturing her. The two travel to the planet where the Soul Stone is hidden, where they learn from the stone's mysterious guardian that to gain the object, the person seeking it must sacrifice the one thing they love most in the universe. Thanos breaks down in tears and admits that Gamora is the only thing he loves, and that he must kill her to complete his mission. Gamora attempts to thwart him by committing suicide, but is ultimately thrown to her death at the bottom of a cliff. At the conclusion of the film, after completing the Infinity Gauntlet and killing half of the universe, Thanos is visited by a child apparition of Gamora. She asks Thanos what it cost to finally reach his goal of balancing the universe, and a distraught Thanos simply says "Everything."

### Avengers: Endgame (2019)

*The alternate Gamora*

Zoe Saldana reprises her role again in *Avengers: Endgame*. Despite Gamora having been killed in the previous film, the use of time travel allows a past version of Gamora from the year 2014 to appear. This version of the character still serves Thanos, and travels with him to present day Earth in order to steal the Infinity Stones from the Avengers. Despite this, the present day version of Nebula is able to plead with Gamora and convince her to turn against their father, and the two sisters end up fighting alongside the Avengers against Thanos' army. At one point, the sisters team up with other female heroes such as Rescue, Shuri, Valkyrie, Okoye and the Wasp to run interference for Captain Marvel. At the end of the film, Gamora vanishes after Thanos is destroyed, and the Guardians of the Galaxy are last seen set off to find her.

### Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 (2023)

Zoe Saldana reprises her role for *Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3*, the final MCU film for director James Gunn. According to Saldana, this is her final appearance in the MCU, as well. This version of Gamora is the one lost in time after the events of Endgame. She has no relationship to the Guardians of the Galaxy, especially Peter Quill, who had previously softened her personality. This version is very abrasive, although finally has a genuine connection and trust to Nebula, her foster sister. Rather than join the Guardians, she has joined the pirate clan, The Ravagers, and only helps the Guardians after a special request from Nebula.
