## Origin  

Vapor was a surgeon that extracted the eggs from a former Weapon X project called the [[Native]] at a private facility in Montana called the Workshop. Vapor would poison the Native with a radioactive isotope to nullify her healing factor for a few hours so she could extract the eggs. During the examination, Vapor discovered that the Native was pregnant and [[Wolverine|Logan]] was the father. Logan would kill Vapor and escape with the Native from the Workshop.  

## Creation

Vapor was created by Greg Rucka and Darick Robertson in 2004 and first appeared in Wolverine # 17.  

## Powers and Abilities

Vapor was a talented surgeon.
