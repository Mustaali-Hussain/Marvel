> Questa is a member of the New Orleans Assassins Guild. He made his first appearance in Rogue #1 - An Affair to Remember (January 1995).

Questa was created by Howard Mackie, Mike Wieringo and Terry Austin in 1995 and first appeared in Rogue # 1.

## Powers & Abilities

Questa possesses the power to scramble synaptic signals, so that when they reach the optical nerves, they cause his victims to see things other than what they really are.
