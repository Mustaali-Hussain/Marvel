> Armageddon man is a mutant that has kept on ice since the 1950's. He possesses immensely destructive powers that if left unchecked, could bring forth the aspects of an apocalypse, or an event that has a major impact on society as a whole.

## History

Little is known of the Armageddon Man's early life, not even his real name has been revealed to us. But sometime after entering adulthood his ability to cause disastrous happenstance went out of control, leading to the government placing him in suspended animation for further study out of fear that his powers could lead to a biblical cataclysm. It seem as he had no family that was interested in his well being or perhaps they were paid off/killed by men in black whom had taken him. It also remains unknown as to why mutant organizations like the Brotherhood, X-Men or Weapon X had simply not freed him to add his power to their ranks, either he was unknown to them at the time as the government erased and denied all known record of his existence. Or, more likely, even they were too afraid of his powers to attempt freeing him. Its unknown how [[King Bedlam]] came to find out about the localization and imprisonment of this powerful mutant, all that we truly know is that he banded together several mutants to free him. Believing that upon being unleashed the Armageddon Man would force the government to give in to their demands. When awakened he was completely mindless. As a result of this a device was crafted for use by [[King Bedlam|King bedlam]] to give him control of the creatures body and mind. All was going according to plan until X-force interfered and the device was ruptured, thus making him and his powers go out of control again. In the end X-Force was able to subdue him and return him to hibernation.

## X-Men Green

The Armageddon Man is now situated in a special prison complex somewhere in California. Hordeculture, having teamed up with X-Men Green, opt to utilize the mysterious mutant's disastrous prospects with intent of culling the human population to manageable levels. The problem being, none amongst the botanist team had a rightful means of controlling their desired secret weapon. At least until they found [[Nature Girl|Lin Li]] and the staff made out of Krakoan biotech, the two sides work together thinking they can utilize the formers ability to connect with nature to control the Armageddon Man. Both sides manipulating the other to get at their targeted quarry for each's own particular ends.

## Powers and Abilities

**Apocalyptic Force Generation: **Sometime after entering adulthood, Armageddon Man's ability to cause natural disasters and affect weather phenomena went out of control. The government placed him in suspended animation, in fear that his powers could cause a cataclysm.

**Ecological Connection: **It is speculated that the Armageddon Man's powers give him a psychoempathic unity to the Earth. Explaining his ability to cause meteorological effects within his immediate vicinity.

**Nigh-Invulnerability:** Armageddon Man possess a high degree of physical durability, to an unknown degree.

**Radiation Channeling: **The Armageddon Man seems to feed off of, as well as emit, copious amounts of radioactive energy. The longer his powers are initiated. The more and more nuclear force he seems to recycle as he emits.

**Superhuman Strength: **Armageddon Man possess vast amounts of strength, the limits of which has yet to be determined.

**Size Enhancement: **The Armageddon Man's mutation seemingly affects his physical height. The longer he remains active, the larger and stronger he will become in tune with his ecological destabilizing power.

**Superhuman Reflexes:** In spite of his considerable size & mass, Armageddon Man could easily swat Cannonball out of the air.

## Weakness

Armageddon Man can't control his powers (It is unknown if he could be treated or trained in the use of his abilities).
