Yrik is a Kymellian who is the father of [[Kofi|Kofi Whitemane]] and the ex-husband of [[Onomi Whitemane]]. Before the Kymellians were conquered by the Inhumans, he was their king. Yrik has since served as ambassador to Snarkworld.
