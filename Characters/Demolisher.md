> The Demolisher is Luther Manning from a alternate future where he has gone on to drop the Deathlok part of his name. He was transported to the past to battle alongside other Deathloks.

## Brief History

In an alternate future [[Deathlok|Luther Manning]] has succumbed to his [[Deathlok]]identity after his world is ravaged by war. The character was transported into the past and fought alongside and against the various Deathloks and [[Siege]]. 

He later took to living in the Morlock tunnels and fought alongside [[Daredevil]] against [[Bushwacker]].
