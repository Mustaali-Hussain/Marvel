## Brief History

Carnivore is the avatar of Farallah.

Zorba was originally part of an organization called the Council of Antiquarians, otherwise known as the Collectors. They were consisted of people who would do anything to gain an artifact. They had confrontations with the Black Panther.

## Powers and Abilities

Carnivore has super strength, super speed, super senses (which allow him to see through illusions), enhanced reflexes and stamina, and increased durability. He also has extremely sharp claws that can cut almost through anything.
