## Brief History

Kathleen "Kathy" Dare was Tony Stark's paramour, but mental instability led her to shoot him, rupturing his spine. Sometime later, Stark appeared to die and his death was attributed in part to Dare's gunshot. She attended his wake, arousing disgust by James Rhodes' (War Machine), who confronted her verbally. She committed suicide soon after and Rhodes wondered if he had contributed to her final choice.
