> [!abstract] In brief
> An underling to Victor Von Doom.  Though he has no superpowers, he still succeeds in capturing the Invisible Girl!

Behind his movie star features is the cunning, cool brain of a merciless con man.

Harry Phillips became the first male FF foe, without any superpowers, to succeed in defeating Susan Storm. However, Doctor Doom did increase Harry's hearing ability so much using *XZ-12 Device* that he could hear the blonde girl when she turned invisible.

NOTA: Hence, Peggy McCarthur's defeat of Susan Storm in FF #287 was even more impressive than that of Harry Phillips, as Peggy had no special abilities whatsoever.
