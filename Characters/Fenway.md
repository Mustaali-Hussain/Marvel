> Fenway was one of the mercenaries hanging out with Deadpool in the Hell House. He is strong, tough, and his favorite weapon is a baseball bat. His speech pattern often includes baseball references.

Fenway was typically a background character at the Hell House, much like [[CF]]. However, both he and CF were both rather disappointed when [[Deadpool]] left the Hell House to work for Landau, Luckman, and Lake, indicating that they both considered him a friend of sorts.
