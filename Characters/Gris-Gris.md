> ###### Gris-Gris
> **First appearance** TPB #1
> **Appearances** 43
> **Also known as** Gris Gris, GrisGris

> [!abstract] In brief
> Gris-Gris is a member of the New Orleans Assassins Guild.

Gris-Gris has the power to manipulate the emotions of others by the use of his voodoo drugs.

As Bella Donna's henchman, he followed around and spied on Gambit and Rogue, and attacked Kaine.

## Creation

Gris-Gris was created by Howard Mackie, Mike Wieringo and Terry Austin in 1995 and first appeared in Rogue # 1.
