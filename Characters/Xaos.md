> [!abstract] In brief
> Xaos (also known as Chaos) was a synthetic copy of an autistic mutant named Daniel Dash, created by Cerebro.  He was a member of Cerebro's X-Men.

Xaos could fire plasma waves from his right eye.

 

As a synthetic creation of Cerebro, Xaos was a combination of mutants [[Cyclops]] and [[Havok]].
