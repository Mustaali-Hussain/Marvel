> [!abstract] In brief
> The only villain the Marvelman Family ever fought.

In the program that combined people with alien technology Young Nastyman was the lone villain during the simulations that he and the Marvelman family were subjected to, he was given more control over what he did and how acted in the simulations. Eventually he escaped and the Marvelman family was sent out to try and bring him in in a discreet manner. Marvelwoman eventually found him and saw that he had become completely insane.
