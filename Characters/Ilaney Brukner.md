> ###### Ilaney Brukner
> **First appearance** Payback Part One: Karmic Relief #18
> **Appearances** 15

Ilaney Brukner was [[Dr. Killebrew]]'s neighbor. When [[Ajax]] was hunting down [[Deadpool]], Dr. Killbrew and Deadpool hid at her home. Because of this, she was forced into the fray and her home was destroyed. 

She was almost killed when she was knocked unconscious in freezing waters, but was resuscitated by Deadpool.
