> [!abstract] In brief
> Tomas Lara-Perez is a student at Empire State University and the crime-fighting partner of rookie hero Koi Boi. He has the power to talk to chipmunks.

## Origin

Very little is known about Tomas Lara-Perez. Apparently born with his superhuman abilities, he became a costumed crime-fighter with his friend Koi Boi when both began attending Empire State University.

## Creation

Chipmunk Hunk was created by Ryan North and Erica Henderson. He made his first appearance in *The Unbeatable Squirrel Girl* #1.

## Major Story Arcs

### Squirrel Girl

Tomas meets and becomes friends with fellow student Doreen Green, later becoming superhero friends with her after encountering her in her superhero alter ego while both are attempting to defeat Hippo. During Ratatoskr's attempt to take over the world he and the other heroes join forces to try to take her down. In the attempt he is corrupted by Ratatoskr, and briefly joins her army of brainwashed humans before she is ultimately defeated.

## Powers and Abilities

Chipmunk Hunk is able to communicate with chipmunks. He is superhumanly strong and durable. He is fairly agile, and a good hand-to-hand combatant.

## Other Media

### *Squirrel Girl: The Unbeatable Radio Show!*

Chipmunk Hunk is a main character on this radio play/podcast. He is performed by Davied Morales.
