> Leader of the sword, this female deviant was responsible for retrieving Arcadia Daville. She was an enemy of X-force.

### History 

 

 A female deviant who was trained by Ulyssess Dragonblood while he mentored Sword and was a member of the Damocles Foundation. When he left she became leader of the sword unit and was to bring him back dead or alive along with arcadia.  

 

She fought x-force and was defeated twice and later de-evolved by the celestial Gatherer.
