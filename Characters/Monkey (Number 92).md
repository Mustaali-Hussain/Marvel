> ###### Monkey (Number 92)

> **First appearance** Is He Man or Monster or... Is He Both? #1
> **Appearances** 18
> **Also known as** Number 92, Monkey

> [!abstract] In brief
> Monkey was originally brought to Bruce Banner by General Ross. He was to be used as a test subject for Banner's gamma bomb. He was saved by Rick Jones, thus causing Banner to become the Hulk.

He became Rick Jones' best pal and participated in Rick's adventures with the Hulk. He first appeared in Marvel Adventures: Hulk #1.
