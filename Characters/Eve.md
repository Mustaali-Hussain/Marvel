## Origin

Eve is an informant and operative for [[Gideon]] planted in the DaCosta business.

## Creation

Written by Fabian Nicieza and drawn by Rob Liefeld.

## Major Story Arcs

When Gideon deems the time right to take [[Sunspot]] under his wing, he orders his operative in the DaCosta family business to kill [[Emmanuel DaCosta]]. Eve is that operative and her methods are quick and effective: she poisons Mr. DaCosta's coffee and watches him die on the floor.

A few years later, when [[Cable]] comes to Gideon's lair, we discover that Eve and Adam, another of Gideon's personal assistants, have been killed, but have been "cyber-upgraded"; their consciousnesses have been programmed into the A.I. of robots.
