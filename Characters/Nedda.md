Nedda is [[Moon Knight|Marc Spector's]] housekeeper, or more specifically, Steven Grant's (Marc's wealthy alias). With [[Samuels]], the butler, they keep Marc's mansion in order while he is away doing his Moon Knight duties. Both Nedda and Samuels stood by Marc during his breakdown, and were more than happy when he came back around.
