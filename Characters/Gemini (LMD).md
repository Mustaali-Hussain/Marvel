*"How do We know He deserves it? I am Gemini... a paradox in personality... and I am not of one mind on this matter"* -- Gemini (LMD).

The Gemini LMD (Life Motion Decoy) was created by [[Jake Fury|Scorpio]] to be a member of his Zodiac organization.

Self-styled schizophrenia is his speciality. He resembles he could replicate in two androids. 

Finally he decided it did not want to follow Scorpio's path and abandoned him.

He briefly aided the [[She-Hulk|She Hulk]] but was eventually apparently captured and rejoined the Zodiac to battle the Avengers. He was later reprogrammed and rebuilt in a female form.
