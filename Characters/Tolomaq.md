> Beast of the Fire

Tolomaq the Fire Beast is one of the Great Beasts. He has battled Alpha Flight on several occasions. The first time he faced them was when they travelled to the Realm of the Beasts in search of Walter Langkowski's soul. 

## Creation

Tolomaq was created by John Byrne in 1985 and first appeared in Alpha Flight # 24.
