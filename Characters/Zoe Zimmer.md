## Origin

Zoe Zimmer first appears in Ms. Marvel issue #1 and seems passively insulting towards those who aren't of an upper-class white background, though it is not certain if she does this entirely with the intent to insult or purely out of ignorance. In Issue #2, Zoe is the first person ever saved by Kamala when she first tranforms into Ms. Marvel, disguising herself as Carol Danvers in her black and gold uniform.
