## Brief History

Josie owns a bar called 'Josie's Grill' where [[Daredevil]] and [[Daredevil|Kingpin]]'s men hang around from time to time. Daredevil would often visit her bar to meet Turk.

## In Other Media

### Daredevil (Netflix Series)

*Susan Varon as Josie*

Josie appears in the live-action Daredevil series, played by Susan Varon. Her bar is frequented by Matt Murdock and his friend [[Foggy Nelson]].
