Tiger-Claw was created in 1975 and first appeared in Giant-Size Master of Kung Fu # 4.

## Story Arcs

Tiger-Claw reappeared in Morocco and came into conflict with [[Shang-Chi]] when he was after the Elixir Vitae, Fu Manchu's serum of immortality. Shang-Chi was able to defeat Tiger-Claw when he removed his gloves and false claws. Shang-Chi knocked out Tiger-Claw with a kick to the jaw, followed by a fierce uppercut. It turned out that the statue they believed had the serum was actually empty.

## Skills & Abilities

Tiger-Claw is an excellent hand-to-hand combatant and highly skilled in various forms of martial arts. He also wears tiger stripe gloves equipped with razor sharp claws.
