Wife of Howard Stark and mother of Tony Stark. She was abducted during World War II by Red Skull but survived. She was eventually killed in an accident. Her son, Tony Stark, started a charitable business named after his mother known as the Maria Stark Foundation.

## Creation

Maria Stark was created by George Tuska and Bill Mantlo, and first appeared in Iron Man #104 - Triad.

## Other Media

### Films

### Captain America: Civil War

Maria Stark appears briefly as a hologram by Tony Stark in Captain America: Civil War. Later in the movie, it reveals how she died. She was killed by Bucky Barnes as the Winter Soldier, while he was under HYDRA's control. She was portrayed by Hope Davis.
