> Randall Spector is the psychotic younger brother of Marc Spector. Jealous of Marc's life, Randall rivaled Moon Knight, first as a dark reflection as Shadowknight and then as the avatar of a different god as Jackal Knight.

## Origin

Randall Spector grew up in Chicago the son of a rabbi, [[Elias Spector]], and the brother of future [[Moon Knight]], Marc Spector. He was an overlooked Jewish child that never seemed to be appreciated no matter what he did. His older brother affected him a lot by constantly playing war which Randall didn't like because he always got shot. Rand preferred baseball instead. Slowly, he became better than Marc at this game. Their father predicted that all this violent games would result in no good. He would be proven true when the two brothers became mercenaries together after leaving the military.

## Creation

Randall appeared for the first time in flashbacks in a Moon Knight backup story in The Hulk! #17 by Doug Moench and Bill Sienkiewicz

## Major Story Arcs

### Mercenary

At one point, Randall and Marc had teamed up as mercenaries. While running guns in Italy to anti-communist factions for the CIA, Rand betrayed his brother by running guns to the other side. When Marc found out, Rand spent the next three days running from his brother. The two got in a fire fight that ended when Marc threw a grenade that took Rand out. Luckily, he survived, but the doctors were afraid the injuries had driven him mad. Rand managed to escape the hospital by killing a nurse. This would be the last time that Marc saw Rand until after Marc was chosen by Khonshuto be the Moon Knight.

It was not the last time Rand would see Marc. Rand had fallen in with an Egyptian rebel contingency. They were attacked by Marc and his new partner, [[Bushman]]. Rand was injured but not killed. Although Marc hadn’t noticed his brother among the masked rebels, Rand believed he had and took his assault personally. Ironically, just like his brother, Rand would seek refuge in a nearby temple. He found an ancient scroll and a long passageway. The passageway brought him to the temple to Khonshu, where Marc had been left for dead shortly after by Bushman. He witnessed Marc be chosen as the Fist of Khonshu and became insanely jealous.

### Blood Brothers

While on the run, Rand was taken in by rogue CIA agent, Sandahl Swarn, with whom he was previously intimate. As Princess Nepthys, she was the high priestess of the Cult of Khonshu. She planned on using Rand to have Marc Spector replaced as the Fist of Khonshu. Part of her plan was finding a patsy she could brainwash and surgically alter to pass as Rand. She would send him out into New York as the serial killer, The Hatchet Man, to draw Marc out. Moon Knight’s confrontation with the killer ended in the patsy’s death. While Marc thought he had killed his own brother, Rand got close to his residence, stole the head of his Khonshu statue and blew up the house.

Moon Knight tracked the cult down, but Nepthys and Rand only had enough time to gloat before [[Punisher]] attacked. He had been tracking them over the AIM weapons they stole. Rand and Nepthys are able to make their getaway when Marc tries to keep Punisher from killing anyone. They retreat to another lab under the Central Park reservoir, where she had combined Rand’s Egyptian scroll with CIA technology to create a “lunar absorber,” that would transform Rand into the Shadowknight, giving him the power to kill and replace Marc.

Marc and Rand dueled to the death while Punisher kept the rest of the cult busy. Although Rand’s skin was now nearly invulnerable, Marc was able to cut him using his adamantium weaponry. Marc hesitated to kill him and was distracted by Nepthys. While he and Punisher put her and the rest of the cult down for good, Rand made his getaway.

### Revenant

Rand was one of a number of superhumans summoned as enslaved revenants to be used as security for The Blasphemy Cartel, black market occult arms dealers. He was specifically summoned to attack [[Clea]], who at the time had taken [[Doctor Strange]]’s place as a defender against the occult since Strange’s death. Although the previous revenants had been heroes, Rand was a trained killer. As a revenant, he was much more feral than the rest. Clea performed an exorcism on Rand, disconnecting him from his dead body once again.

### Jackal Knight

After he was freed from the land of the living once more, he found himself in The Duat, the Egyptian afterlife. He pledged his life toAnubis, becoming The Jackal Knight, as Moon Knight is to Khonshu. From The Duat, he was able to influence a street gang into becoming the Sons of the Jackal. They had been using the hearts of orphans to beat the test of the scales to earn entrance into The Duat. He sent them after a young boy, Khalil, who happened to be the avatar of Osiris. Randall planned on taking Osiris’ power for himself.

When Moon Knight failed to stop the gang in the land of the living, he entered the land of the dead, with the help of [[Hunter's Moon|Hunter’s Moon]], who slowed his heart. To combat Moon Knight (who had teamed up with a dead former mercenary partner, [[Scarlet Scarab (El-Faouly)|Scarlet Scarab]]), Randall summoned a team of Moon Knight’s dead rivals. They were at least able to keep Moon Knight busy long enough for Randal to steal Osiris’ power from the boy. He was now too powerful for both Moon Knight and Scarlet Scarab.

When Khalil awoke during the fight, he committed suicide, freeing Osiris' physical form to take the power back from Randall himself. Marc then shared his own heart with Randall, proving that the life Randall was so jealous of was one of sadness, guilt, and pain. In exchange for their help, Osiris sent both Marc and Khalil back to the land of the living to live out the rest of their days, while Randall was left behind.

## Powers & Abilities

**Military Veteran:** Rand joined the U.S. military along with his brother where he learned many skills including marksmanship and hand to hand combat.

**Lunar Absorber:** A combination of Egyptian scripture and modern technology strengthened Rand and made his skin nearly invulnerable. It was still vulnerable to adamantium.

**Optic Blast:** While overseas, Rand had developed a dormant power, possibly from exposure to uranium ammunition, that was revitalized during Shadowland. He could shoot radioactive energy from his eyes

**Magic:** As the avatar for Anubis, Rand was capable of sorcery in the City of the Dead. He could summon souls that had passed into the afterlife

- **Divine Power:** He briefly steals the god-like power of Osiris.
