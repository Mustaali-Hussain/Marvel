The newly rich inheritor of Enduque Family, he manages to kill his 7 older brothers in order to reach his fortune and might. His grand-grand father built up their fortune on the back of thousands of african slaves. Now he is part of the latest incarnation of Hellfire Club leaded by [[Kade Kilgore]].

At Museum of Mutant History oppening, Manuel and Hellfire Club inmates attacked the roster of X-Men gathering there, managing to defeat them and incinarate the place. Manuel beat [[Colossus]] in frontal confrontation.
