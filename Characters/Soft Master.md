> ###### Soft Master
> **First appearance** Subject: Snake-Eyes #26

> [!abstract] In brief
> The Soft Master is part of the Arashikage clan in Japan. He was part of the head of the clan with his brother the Hard Master and his nephew the Young Master (Storm Shadow).

The Soft Master was part of the ninja clan Arashikage. He and his brother and their nephew were leaders of the clan until it was destroyed by Zartan who framed the nephew in the killing of the Hard Master. The Soft Master started hunting the truth and found that the nephew was innocent and helped the nephew and his friend of the clan Snake Eyes learn the real killer and sent them a message before he was also killed by Zartan's Dreadnoks.
