## Brief History

Bodyguard and friend of [[Jeremy Locke]]. Philip helped his boss to capture Elektra and try to destroy her without killing. He and other mercenaries kidnapped Elektra and had her brought to his mansion where they showed her what a monster she had become. Heavily drugged, they showed her video images of all the harm she had done to people in her life, including to her own kidnappers. Eventually Elektra was released in the dessert where she would be hunted by other mercenaries. Elektra, however heavily beaten, managed to defeat the mercenaries including Philip and returned to the mansion. She confronted Jeremy Locke, but to both their surprise, did not kill him. She left him alone and told him to never come after her again. Philip however remained a part of Elektra's life the following months and was killed a few months later.
