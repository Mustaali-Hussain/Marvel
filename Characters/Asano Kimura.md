> [!abstract] In brief
> Asano Kimura was a member of the Department of Immigration in Japan (really working in Japanese Secret Service). He was amongst older and best friends. He used to work with Wolverine, before he entered the X-Men.

One of Logan's oldest friends, Asano Kimura was originally a contact in Japan for Logan ([[Wolverine]]). He worked in Japanese Secret Service as a special agent. Logan and Asano fought against [[Lord Shingen|Shingen Yashida]] and his organization until Asano's assassination at the hands of [[Yukio]]. Asano first warns Logan about the dangers in Japan, as well as telling him that Shingen Yashida (the father of Logan's Fiancee) has kidnapped [[Mariko Yashida]] (Logan's wife) and holds her "prisoner" in Japan. This leads Logan to Japan to face Shingen, and save his Fiancee, as well as finding out who is Asano's killer.
