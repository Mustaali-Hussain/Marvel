## Major Story Arcs

After hearing that his mother was dead, Jeff holds out hope that he'll see her again one day. That one day happens to be the night [[Deadpool]] sneaks into his room. Jeff learns that his mom's mind was placed in Deadpool's head. He is ecstatic that his mom is still alive but promises not to tell anyone. He then witnesses his father shoot Deadpool in the butt.
