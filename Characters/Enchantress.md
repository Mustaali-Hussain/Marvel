> [!abstract] In brief
> Amora the Enchantress, an Asgardian Goddess and immensely powerful sorcerer. Being backed by years of experience, she is one of Thor’s greatest and most ruthless foes because of her immense power, and because he is her love interest.

## Origin

With unknown parentage, Amora was born in Asgard, and at a young age, she began to learn the ways of magic as an apprentice of [[Karnilla]]. However, she would soon be banished for being too undisciplined. Amora quickly decided to train herself in the ways of magic by seducing other sorcerers, forcing them to reveal their ancient secrets to her. In time, Amora became one of the most powerful sorceresses in all of Asgard. She was also very adept in the use of manipulation and charm to get her way.

## **Creation**

Amora first appeared in Journey into Mystery #103 and was created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby. She is heavily based on the Norse mythological story of the Golden Apple, yet she is not based in any specific norse deity,.

## Character Evolution

### Silver Age

*The Enchantress*

Amora first appeared as a foe of Thor, albeit with the intention of seducing him and then soon after as an enemy of the Avengers.

**Modern Age**

Amora's adolescence was explored in the Thor: Son of Asgard mini-series. It was later revealed in the Thor mini series Thor: Ages of Thunder and Thor: Reign of Blood that although not strictly a god like other Norse figures in the Marvel Universe, Amora had been known by many 'Godly' names throughout history after reaching adulthood.

## Major Story Arcs

### Adolescence

*Amora Surprising Loki*

From a young age, Amora had always desired [[Thor]], although she admitted that this was only because he preferred [[Sif]] over her. This caused a bitter rivalry with Sif to ensue after Amora attempted to seduce Thor in hopes of keeping him for herself. To do this, Amora used [[Loki]] and developed a plot in which they manipulated Sif into stealing the Mirror of Mycha from Karnilla's palace. Once in possession of the mirror, Amora took it so that she could use its power to have Thor for herself. After their success, Amora and Loki decide that the mirror must be returned to its rightful place, lest their plot be discovered. However, they could not do this without the cloth that the mirror was originally draped in. Before Amora and Loki even start arguing about who was to blame for the loss of the cloth, Sif and [[Valkyrie|Brunnhilda]] appear, demanding that Amora revert her spell on Thor as payment for the return of the mirrors cloth. Amora immediately refused, and the four young gods quickly entered into a brief battle until it is interrupted by Thor who threatened to inform Odin himself about the whole ordeal. Brunnhilda then shattered the mirror, breaking the spell placed on him. With his mind clear, Thor admitted he expected no less from Amora, but was disappointed in Sif because she attempted to use trickery to gain his love, although the two made up before the story's end.

### Ages of Thunder

*Amora with the Golden Apples of Idunn*

The gods of Asgard have repeated their lives endlessly through the ages; they have lived, through Ragnarok countless times with their roles and personalities shifting slightly during each retelling of their story. Amora has been known as Freyja, Iduna, Mardol, and Gefn, though even in these iterations she always retains her core attributes: control of powerful magic and unparalleled beauty.

In 'Ages of Thunder', which tells the story of a long-past cycle, Amora is known as Iduna, the goddess tasked with an immensely important task of collecting the Golden Apples which grew from Yggdrasil, the life tree. The Golden Apples were consumed by the Asgardians in order to maintain their youth and power. The apples would only grow if Iduna were present, and only she, the essence of feminine perfection, could remove them, unspoiled, from the tree. During this time, The Enchantress was the object of desire for many. Her beauty caused her to be sought after by a stonemason who was assigned to repair the great wall which surrounded Asgard after an attack by the Frost Giants.

In a second story of the same cycle, a lost and wandering Loki bargains the Enchantress to the Frost-King so that he may find his way to the Bifrost bridge and back to Asgard. After this deal is struck and the Asgardians are reminded that only the Enchantress can retrieve the Gold Apples that sustain their power and vitality, the Frost giant is slain by Thor, thus allowing the Goddess to return to her garden.

Amora also obtained the mythical story that centered around the Nordic Goddess Freyja, where she gave herself to three Dwarves in order to obtain an exquisite jeweled necklace that they had created. It was also noted during these stories, that Amora had many lovers and would openly reject [[Odin]], by refusing to bed with him. This caused him to violently take her newly acquired necklace while also informing her that she had defiled herself when she gave her body to the Dwarves. Out of humiliation and anger, Amora cast a spell which caused the dead on Earth to rise and torture humanity, forcing Thor to intervene and defeat the newly risen army of the dead.

## **Powers**

*Amora attacking the World Tree*

The Enchantress possesses the conventional attributes of an Asgardian Goddess, as well as a mastery over certain sorcerous disciplines. Her abilities include: superhuman strength (she can lift roughly 25 tons), enhanced speed, durability, agility, reflexes, and stamina. Amora's physiology also grants her an extremely long life span, as well as a regenerative healing factor. While she cannot regrow organs or limbs, she is immune to all known Earthly diseases and infections.

**Sorcery:** The Enchantress' magical powers are among the most powerful of all Asgardians. Her skill is high enough to rank her among the top twenty magical practitioners in all of Asgard. She can utilize enough power, even in the Earthly realm, to cloud the sight of Agamotto through Doctor Strange's Orb, and has bested [[Nightmare]] in his own dimension.

The Enchantress' powers derive from two primary sources: her innate capacity to manipulate ambient Asgardian magical energy, and her acquired knowledge of Asgardian spells and enchantments. She can use her sorcery to generate powerful blasts of concussive force, heat, or light. She can also alter her appearance, taking the shape of other humanoid beings, or teleport herself and others across great distances.

*One kiss is all it takes*

The Enchantress has used her mystical prowess to enhance her natural beauty and allure so that both men and women, mortal or otherwise, are overwhelmed with desire for her. She has enchanted her lips so that a single kiss is sufficient to make virtually any man a slave to her will for about a week. Thanks to her powers, Amora was able to seduce the majority of Asgard's most experienced mystical practitioners to learn their secrets. Subsequent kisses have enabled her to dominate someone's will indefinitely.

Her knowledge of arcane lore enables her to cast spells with a variety of specific effects. The many spells she has employed include: telepathy, illusion-casting, paralysis, matter/energy deflection, inter-dimensional travel, time control, sleep induction, and elemental conversion. Once, at the maximum peak of her magic, Amora’s transmutation enchantment upon the Black Knight changed him into a statue, and not even Doctor Strange could break the enchantment for some time.

Although Spider-Man initially refused to fight at her behest, Amora kissed him and Spider-Man quickly went to battle with and defeated Heimdall. Once inside Asgard, Amora sat on Odin's Throne, but is immediately attacked by other Asgardians. Knowing that she was out matched, Amora gave Spider-Man the Nor Stones, giving him powers similar to Thor so that he can easily defeat the Asgardians coming at them. Soon after, Thor arrives and defeated the empowered Spider-Man, forcing the stones from him and bringing him back to his senses. Amora attempted to seduce Thor, saying she only wanted to impress him. However, Thor is unconvinced, knowing her true desire, and asked Balder to place her in the Royal Dungeon.

Some time later, Amora managed to free herself and returns to Midgard in order to capture [[Live-Wire]] . She managed to convince him to attack a guard who had accused Amora of stealing a number of items. After the guard was dealt with, Amora admitted to stealing the items, much to Live-Wire's surprise. However, she kissed him before he could protest further. Skurge then appeared and knocked Live-Wire unconscious as Amora teleported them to the Masters of Evil Headquarters. Live-Wire awoke, only to discover that he was being held captive and interrogated by Amora while the other Masters of Evil battle with Dr. Doom and his Circus Freaks. Amora easily gathered the information she desired from her captive before Skurge appeared, claiming the rest of the Masters of Evil had been overwhelmed by the Circus Freaks. Doom then appeared, claiming that the entire thing was a test and that he wanted the Masters of Evil to join with him.

Amora and the rest of her team were later seen on an alien planet, with the mission of capturing an alien named Quinn. Amora managed to restrain herself, though [[Blastaar]] came to the aliens rescue and despite being out-numbered, he managed to gain the upper-hand against the Masters of Evil. Doom then used illusion gas to trick the hero, allowing them to capture the Alien Quinn with little effort.

### Heroes Reborn

*Amora in Heroes Reborn*

The Heroes Reborn reality was not an alternate reality, but rather a parallel Earth, located in the Earth-616's reality. Here, Amora the Enchantress assisted Loki in attempting to take over Asgard and defeat the Avengers. Loki created a team called the Lethal Legion, which was made up of himself, Amora, Skurge, Wonder Man, Ultron and the Scarlet Witch (who had mistaken Amora for her mother). When the entire team, minus Loki, attacked the Avengers' mansion, Amora was able to seduce Iron Man and effectively take him out of the battle. When the Lethal Legion was on the verge of defeat, Amora and Wanda were forced to abandon their team in order to escape. Later, Amora used her powers of mystic seduction after kissing Thor and effectively causing him to join with Loki, who had also turned the other Avengers against each other. After a second attempt to defeat the Avengers, Amora easily defeated Hank Pym, although any victory was short lived, as Loki had managed to absorb an abundance of magical energy from a reactor and quickly turned on his teammates. By using Amora, [[Agatha Harkness]] ,and Wanda's vast magical energies, the Heroes were able to deceive Loki by making him believe that Thor was actually Odin. Despite being previously defeated, the Thunder God was able to hold his own, giving the Avengers the time they required to undo Loki's schemes, which caused him to lose his excess power, at which point he was easily defeated.

Later, during the Onslaught Reborn saga, Enchantress (and the Lethal Legion) ambushed the [[Invisible Woman]], [[Eric O'Grady|Ant Man]], the Vision, [[Franklin Richards]] and [[Bucky Barnes|Bucky]] (a female version of Captain America's sidekick). The Enchantress and Wanda were quickly dispatched by the Invisible Woman, but were quickly awakened by Loki. Together the two witches took down Ant Man. However, later Amora and the others joined forces with the heroes so that they could defeat [[Onslaught]] once and for all.

*Ultimate 1610*

Amora recently appeared in the Ultimate Universe aiding Loki and a team of Asgardians who attempted to take down the New Ultimates. This version of Amora appears to have similar powers to her 616 counter-part, though she is capable of manipulating women just as easily as she does men. However, she does not appear to be in love or obsessed with Thor in this time-line.

### The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes

*The Enchantress and The Executioner*

Amora The Enchantress makes several appearances throughout this series as one of the main antagonists of The Avengers. She is voiced by Kari Wahlgren. Throughout the majority of her screen time in the series Amora is accompanied by her mute servant known as Skurge The Executioner.

*Amora using her magic to allow Loki to speak in the Isle of Silence.*

She first appears in the episode "Thor the Mighty," assuming human form in order to manipulate Thor into leaving Asgard and attack the Wrecking Crew. While Thor is distracted she visits Loki in the Isle of Silence, where Odin had imprisoned him for his attempted attack on Asgard. Loki, unable to break his prison's no speaking spell, sits and waits while running his plan in his mind. A flash of green light appears, and Amora and Skurge enter Loki's prison. She looks at him for a moment, and then uses her magic to allow Loki to speak. Loki clears his throat as Amora ridicules him saying *"Did you miss the sound of your own voice?."* Loki and Amora then talk about their plan, after which she frees him offscreen.

The Enchantress next travels to Earth and manipulates the Hulk to fight against the Avengers. The Hulk eventually breaks free of her mind control, and teams up with the Avengers to fight against The Enchantress and her Executioner. The Avengers barely defeat the two, and before the Avengers can capture them, they escape. The Hulk then leaves as he no longer feels like he is welcome with the Avengers.

*Amora recruiting Zemo*

In "Living Legend" the Avengers find Captain America, which provokes Baron Zemo to attempt to kill his old foe, with the help of one of Arnim Zola's creations. The attempt is foiled by the team, and Captain America officially joins the Avengers. Zemo retreats to Arnim Zola's hideout, but Zola is nowhere to be found. Zemo turns around to see a flash of bright green light. He sees Zola knocked out at the feet of The Enchantress and The Executioner. The Enchantress tells Zemo that she has an offer for him. Zemo quickly analyzed the situation, and realized that he had no choice but to accept her offer, as the alternative was suffering the same or worse fate than Zola had.

Amora then uses Zemo to create an alliance of many powerful foes of the Avengers. In the next few episodes they worked behind the scenes to recruit members such as Wonder Man (recruited in "Everything Is Wonderful" after Amora revives him and tells him that in order for him to stay alive he must obey her), Crimson Dynamo, Grey Gargoyle, Chemistro, Living Laser, and the Abomination (recruited in "Gamma World Pt 2"). The group called themselves the Masters of Evil.

*Amora telling Thor that she is using Zemo*

The Enchantress next appears in "Masters of Evil" where the group attacks the Avengers mansion. While Amora restrains Thor on the Avengers table, she shares some vague details about her plan with him. When Thor asks her why she, an Asgardian and clearly a much more powerful being, was working with a weak villain like Zemo, Enchantress replies that Zemo is just a toy which will be discarded when she tires of him. Due to a miscalculation on Zemo's part, Enchantress being distracted with Thor, and a conflict between Abomination and Zemo the group is defeated and Enchantress transports them back to their base. When they reach the base another fight breaks out between Abomination and Zemo, The Enchantress stops them and tells them that they are all pawns in a much larger game. She then walks into a private room to speak to Loki, who she teasingly calls "My Lord" implying that Loki has fulfilled his part of the plan by conquering Asgard.

Amora briefly appears in "The Casket of Ancient Winters" to collect the Casket from Malekith and in "Ultron Imperative" to revive Thor after his death at the hands of Ultron in an attempt to make him fall in love with her and save him from the impending attack. She fails as Thor insists to leave to go fight with the Avengers.

*Enchantress restrains Thor before summoning her army to Earth*

She is next seen in "This Hostage Earth" where she finally reveals the true plan. Amora was merely distracting Thor and the Avengers while Loki and his army conquered the other 8 realms. She kills Karnilla and takes her Norn Stones so that she can bring the army to help conquer Earth as well. The Avengers attempt to foil the plan and are dismantled by the Masters of Evil. Zemo knew that after he played his part Amora would destroy him, so when the Enchantress starts to call the army to Midgard, Zemo betrays her. Amora's sudden shock gives the Avengers the opportunity to destroy the stones, and foil The Masters of Evil's plan. The destruction of the Norn Stones causes the Avengers to each be transported to one of the 8 other realms. Thor wakes up in Asgard where he finds Loki, who has been amped by the Odin Force, on the throne of Asgard. Loki is inevitably defeated by the Avengers and imprisoned once again by Odin, this time in a much worse, and much more permanent prison.

*Amora interrogating Chemistro to find Zemo*

Amora is later seen in "Acts of Vengeance" hunting down Zemo. She wants revenge since Zemo betrayed her. She starts off systematically locating, interrogating and eliminating the Masters Of Evil to find Zemo. After losing both Chemistro and Arnim Zola, Zemo panics and goes to the Avengers for help. Both teams work together in an attempt to defeat The Enchantress, and briefly capture her by using a machine that suppresses Asgardian magic. However, Zemo orders his team to destroy the Avengers. Due to this, Crimson Dynamo breaks the machine, giving Amora her powers back. Zemo, panicked, attempts to use the Muspelheim Norn Stone as a last resort, but Wonder Man breaks it, transporting both he and The Enchantress to the fire demon Surtur's realm. There, Surtur takes control of Amora's mind turning her into The Demon Queen.

*The Enchantress possessed by Surtur*

Amora as The Demon Queen under Surtur's mind control sends her to Korbin in "The Ballad Of Beta Ray Bill." Thor senses the attack and joins forces with [[Beta Ray Bill]](after Bill proves himself worthy enough to lift Thor's hammer) to battle The Demon Queen. Thor and Beta Ray Bill are outmatched by the Demon Queen and her fire demons. Neither of them can fight her alone. Thor and Beta Ray Bill are forced to use both their powers together to momentarily wound her. The attack helps Amora break her mind control momentarily, but Surtur quickly retakes possession of her mind, and retreats.

Amora The Enchantress' most recent appearance was in "Powerless" when she visits Loki in his new prison, being tortured by the Midgard Serpent. By this point Amora had broken Surtur's mind control, and was now working with him willingly due to the major power increase.

*Amora teasing Loki by showing him The Avengers battling the Wrecking Crew*

Amora spends some time toying with Loki, clearly taking pleasure in watching him in his helpless state. To add insult to injury she teases him by using her magic to show him Thor and The Avengers battling the Wrecking Crew.

Loki angrily asks Amora if she had risked the wrath of his father Odin by entering his prison just to torment him. Amora, taking advantage of Loki being tied, caresses his face, pushes it to the side and mocks him. She tells him that she did not come here to torment him, in fact she thought Loki would be grateful as her mere presence was a vast improvement to the current scenery. Amora then threatens Loki by telling him that she could do whatever she pleased to him in his prison without the slightest fear of Odin's so called "wrath", and demonstrates this by pinching Loki's eye causing him to grunt in pain. Loki now began to realize that he was at Amora's mercy, and he had no choice but to obey his new master.

*Amora Threatening Loki*

*Loki: Did you risk the wrath of Odin by coming here just to torment me Enchantress?*

*Amora: Mmm torment Loki? I thought you would appreciate the view. Besides getting into your father's prison without his notice is but the least of what I can do.*

Amora then demonstrated further. With a single wave of her hand, The Demon Queen shooed away the Midgard Serpent leaving Loki in complete shock. Now that they were alone, Amora tells Loki that she had inherited an immense amount of power recently (alluding to the power she had received from Surtur) and had come to offer Loki a chance to get revenge on Thor and the Avengers, but this favor would not be for free. Loki immediately accepted. Amora then demonstrates her magical abilities to render the Avengers completely powerless.

*Amora warning Loki about her spell*

Loki asked Amora to release him at once so he could destroy the Avengers. Before sending Loki, Amora tells him that there is something that he must know. She explains that the spell would only hinder the powers of the Avengers until they learned internal strength. Loki disregards The Enchantress' advice, as he does not believe he can be beaten.

Amora summons the [[Destroyer|Destroyer Armor]], and effortlessly removes the trickster god's life force from his body and throws it into the Destroyer. Before giving Loki permission to embark on his mission, Amora reminds him the terms of their arrangement saying *"Remember the terms of our deal, trickster. Destroy Thor, and I will free you from this prison. Bid farewell to your brother for me."*

*Amora reminding Loki of the terms of their deal*

While Loki in the Destroyer armor is clearly superior to the three de-powered Avengers, he underestimates Thor and allows his brother enough time to gain the internal strength required to regain his powers. The Avengers also regain their power along with Thor. Taken by surprise, Loki is unable to fight back, and is easily defeated. Loki fails in his mission and his essence is returned to his original body.

*Amora tightening Loki's binds*

Loki awakens in his original body. Amora smiles and tightens his binds as Loki winces in pain. She allows the Midgard Serpent to return to torture Loki, saying *"He's all yours"* as the Midgard Serpent roars at Loki. As Amora leaves Loki pleads with her, as he doesn't want to remain in his eternal prison saying *"Enchantress! Amora! Do not leave me here!"*

*Amora amused by Loki's begging*

Amora responds coldly by saying *"I hope you savored your freedom. You may never taste it again"* and walks away as Loki screams.

The Demon Queen then opens a portal to inform Surtur of Loki's failure and Surtur tells her that Ragnarok approaches and soon both Asgardian and mortal alike will succumb to the flame.
