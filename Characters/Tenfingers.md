> Tenfingers is a cult leader and crime boss that has taken over Chinatown in New York City.

### Origin

The man known as Tenfingers was a former member of the Hand originally from Asia but illegally moved to New York City where he founded a cult known as the Church of the Sheltering Hand. His Church began rapidly growing by preaching to other disenfranchised illegal immigrants and offering them power and salvation. After incorporating other Tongs and Triad gangs in to his organisation Tenfingers is now the number one crime boss in Chinatown.

### Powers\Abilities

Tenfingers is a charismatic and manipulative individual who is able to bend the down trodden to his will. He is able to naturally harness the mystic energy originally used by The Hand to empower his disciples giving them enhanced reflexes, strength and fighting techniques.
