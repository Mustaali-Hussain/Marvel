## Brief History

Martha is a known person to Peter Parker, as being the wife of hiss old editor, Robbie Robertson, at the Daily Bugle. She is seen rarely, but she supports her husband's paper career. 

Together with Robbie she had a son named Randy, who is a former room mate of Peter Parker.    

Martha was once attacked by [[Carnage]], but she was lucky enough to tell the tale.
