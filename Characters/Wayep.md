## Origin

The Mayan god of mischief, Wayeb or a a part of Wayeb was summoned by a Dr Benjamin Rabin, a mathematician. With sinister plans and plenty of power, it takes Spider-man to prevent his plans from coming in to fruition.

## Major Story Arcs

### Fear Itself

When [[Mephisto]] goes to The Infinite Embassy looking for information about [[The Serpent]] he asks Wayep what he knows of what's coming. Wayep only speaks about the Mayan doomsday ahead and hints of a plan but gives no details. He ends with a bit of how Itzamna thinks of the Serpent.

## Powers and Abilities

Superhuman strength, and durability, Wayeb also possesses the power of flight.
