## Origin

Along with [[N'astirh]], S'ym is one of the better known demons of Limbo. He used to serve under [[Belasco]], an ancient evil sorcerer serving the Elder Gods.

 

During Marvel's company-wide crossover called* Inferno*, S'ym and N'astirh took in [[Madelyne Pryor]]. She was a clone of [[Jean Grey]], and the demons were going to use in a demonic invasion of Earth starting with Manhattan and at one point, S'ym posed as the green two-headed Duke Bleys.

 

S'ym was last seen in the storyline* Quest for Magik *found in* New X-Men*. [[Magik|Illyana Nikolievna Rasputina]], formerly known as Magik, returns to rule Limbo in her Darkchylde persona. S'ym, who used to be infected with the Transmode virus, is shown to be one of her servants now free of the techno-organic virus.

 

## POWERS

S'ym possesses great superhuman strength. He was at one point stronger than Colossus.
