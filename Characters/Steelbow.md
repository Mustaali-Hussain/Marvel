## The Armored Archer

Steelbow was originally designed after the death of [[Hawkeye|Clint Barton]] (Hawkeye) after the battle against [[Absorbing Man]] in Washington D.C. He was programmed to think and act like Barton by [[Iron Man|Tony Stark]]. It is unknown if he possesses an ability similar to the original Hawkeye, but does possess powers like many of the Iron Man armors of the past. Sometime after his construction he along with the rest of the Iron Avengers was sent as guardians for President Osborn against the Skull and his inhuman army. After Osborn was murdered by the child terror, the Skull attempted to take control of the Iron Avengers. When he learned he could not, he had them all destroyed.

## Powers and Abilities

Steelbow along with the other Iron Avengers were reconstructed by order of [[King Britain]] and was carried out by the [[Vision]] and the Union Jacks, but no longer had the original programming, and no longer able to speak. Last time they were seen their was an army of duplicates made all programed to act as a global police force. Their current whereabouts and missions are unknown, some were seen being sent into space along side [[Iron Maiden]] to rescue the Eternals.
