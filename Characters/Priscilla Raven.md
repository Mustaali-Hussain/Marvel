## Brief History 

*Priscilla and Anna Marie *

 Priscilla married Owen Raven shortly after they entered into a relationship. Together, they lived in a hippie commune in Caldecott County, Mississippi. This was where their daughter, Anna Marie (Rogue) was born. The goal of the commune was to use Native American mysticism to reach the Far Banks, a dream realm. Owen planned to use Priscilla as a human sacrifice to obtain entry into the Far Banks. Instead, Priscilla took over the spirits of the realm and closed the doorway to Owen.   

Rogue wouldn't learn until years later what had happened to her mother. She found out by forcibly absorbing the memories from Priscilla's sister, Carrie. Rogue was able to free her mother's trapped spirit and allow her to move on to the afterlife.
