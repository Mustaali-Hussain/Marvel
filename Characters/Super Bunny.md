## Origin

A rabbit who can fly, speak like humans, has super strength and is invulnerable. Super Bunny was a comic character published by Timely Comics, Marvel Comic's predecessor, during the 'funny animal' craze in the 1950's. He most often teamed-up with his friends [[Ziggy Pig]] and [[Silly Seal]]. After this craze had ended, Super Bunny was hardly ever seen again. Strangely, although he is named Super **Bunny**, his comic was named Super Rabbit.
