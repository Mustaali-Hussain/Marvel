## Origin

Victoria is the illegitimate daughter of the ruler of the Spartoi, J'son of Spartax. As she was not of full royal blood and of darker skin tone, she was treated as such. She had to fight like hell for everything she has. His father gave her nothing.

Despite her father turning his back on her, Victoria remains loyal to Spartax. After Emperor J'son was desposed by his son Star-Lord, Victoria takes command of the Royal Guard to protect the millions of refugees after the Spartoi Empire began to crumble. There are millions of refugees who need help. Royal Guard are the only ones protecting them. 

She planned to capture her half-brother to collect the bounty on his head and fund her troops, but he was able to convince her to double-cross the bounty collectors and steal their assets.

### Marvel's Guardians of the Galaxy

Victoria appears in the animated series, voiced by Cree Summer. Like in the comics, she is Star-Lord's younger sister and the captain of the Royal Guard.
