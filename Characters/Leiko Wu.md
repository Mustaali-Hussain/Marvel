> Leiko is a young Chinese-British woman and also a lover and ally to Shang Chi.

## Origin

As a young Chinese-British woman, Leiko became a feeled agent for the British MI-6. She had worked a number of missions before she became romantically involved with another MI-6 agent named [[Clive Reston]]. Though the two loved one another very much, the romance did not last. They did however remain good friends and teammates.

## Creation

Leiko Wu was created by Doug Moench and Paul Gulacy and first appeared in Master of Kung Fu issue 33 (1975). Leiko Wu was created as a replacement for Sandra Chen (created by Len Wein and Paul Gulacy), who died in the first appearance, Shang-Chi mentions the similarity between Leiko and Sandra.

## Major Story Arcs

### A New Love

In her first appearance, Leiko Wu is relaxing in Reston's bath tub when he and the visiting Shang-Chi enter. She insinuates a romantic past with Reston, but that the relationship is over. She becomes embroiled in a conflict against the robot-obsessed villain[[Mordillo]]. Leiko Wu subsequently becomes a key ally of Shang-Chi.

### Death

After being pursued through the streets of London's Chinatown by the Shadow Ghosts, Leiko Wu is dismembered by [[Razor-Fist]]. She is later resurrected by[[Midnight Sun]]'s ritual, channeling the spiritual powers of her ancestors.

## Alternate Realities

*Earth-1610*. Leiko also exists in the Ultimate universe as a love interest for Shang-Chi, though she is not a secret agent.
