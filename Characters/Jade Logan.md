> Daughter of Wolverine &amp; Maureen Logan.

Jade was the daughter of Logan and Maureen. She lived with the rest of her family in California. The Logan family was threatened to be killed if he didn't find the rent money owed to the Hulk Gang .   Logan was able to return to the farm with the money over two weeks early, only to find that Jade, Maureen, and Scotty had all been killed because the Hulk Gang "got bored".
