> ###### Tandy Snow
> **First appearance** Two Worlds in Frenzy! #14
> **Appearances** 13
> **Also known as** Queen Tandylla

Tandy Snow was a reporter of the London Times and a lover of Kirk Marston. Her lover left to search for the Savage Land and never returned to her. (Marston became the newest incarnation of [[Garokk]]). She arrived in the Savage Land to search for him and was briefly adopted by the Quarlians as their "Queen Tandylla".
