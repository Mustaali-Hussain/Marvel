## History

Olinka Barankova was transformed into MODAM at AIM headquarters. She was dispatched to steal the Quantum bands from [[Quasar]] for [[Dr. Minerva|Doctor Minerva]], a client of AIM. MODAM failed, and was later sent on a mission to capture [[Diamondback]], [[Asp]], [[Anaconda]], and [[Black Mamba]] for yet another AIM client, [[Superia]]. Her plans were ruined by [[Captain America]]. When Captain America invaded AIM Island with Diamondback and The Falcon disguised as super-villains, MODAM was unable to prevent Superia from destroying a Life Model Decoy of the ruler of AIM. This was MODAM's last known mission.
