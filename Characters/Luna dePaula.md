## Brief History

Luna DePaula was a young lesbian entrepreneur whom had established her own coffee shop, which became very popular with the youth of Westchester County, Salem Center. Among her costumers where a group of mutants, all new arrivals of the Xavier Institute. She was very fond of them, especially towards X-men and New Mutant veteran Karma, whom she was romantically drawn to. Although both Karma and Luna flirted publicly, nothing known came out of it and Luna has not been seen since.
