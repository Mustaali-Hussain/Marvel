## Brief History

**Jamal Peoples** was recruited by the 50 States Initiative to become a member of the superhero group known as The Order. He agreed and soon became what he always wanted. He used an energy mallet, which provided flight and an energy covering his entire body as well as the ability to reduce any problem down to its component parts. Jamal and a few of his teammates where however fired from the team when they where caught on camera at a strip-club celebrating their victory over a defeated villain. New rectuits came and Maul was not seen again.
