## History

Jack Frost was an original member of the Liberty Legion, a group of superheroes in the Marvel Universe who fought on the homefront during World War II. His powers included the ability to form and manipulate ice from his hands--similar to [[Iceman]].

Jack Frost was also an alias of Gregor Shapanka, before he changed his name to the much more poetic Blizzard.

## TRIVIA

Jack Frost was the first character created by the teenage Stan Lee for Timely /Atlas / Marvel.
