> [!abstract] In brief
> Evolved bulldog.

Lord Churchill is believed to be Winston Churchill's former pet that was evolved by the High Evolutionary and took his name from his beloved master in which the High Evolutionary had great respect for.

One of the Knights of Wundagor, often seen with Lady Vermin on his shoulder.

Churchill showed signs that he feels emotions stronger than human beings and can smell fear.

He died in the Savage Land after being attacked by Anon who he had been tracking.
