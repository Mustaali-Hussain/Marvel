## Brief History

Beta Red is a character from Earth-295 who encountered the X-Force team when they went to the Age of Apocalypse to try and find a "Life Seed" to cure Archangel, from his counter-part personality, Death. Beta Red is an assassin with powers of Omega Red. She is a member of the Black Legion who attempt to stop X-Force from breaking a mutant known as Gateway out of Prison.

She later appears in the destruction of the last human city on Earth 295. She killed a man crossing his chest with her cybernetic arms.
