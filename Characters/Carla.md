> ###### Carla
> **First appearance** Poptopia - part 1: Useless Beauty #395
> **Appearances** 28

> [!abstract] In brief
> Carla was one of the mutants living in the sewers below London, England. She is a cantoranthrope (Were-Beaver) or human/beaver hybrid. She possesses the heightened senses and physiology of a beaver.

She was one of the mutants who survived Mr. Clean's massacre of the mutants living in the London sewers. She has many animalistic attributes and the nautical prowess of a beaver.

## Creation

Carla was created by Joe Casey and Ian Churchill in 2001 and first appeared in Uncanny X-Men 395.

## History

Carla was one of the London based Morlocks, a community of mutants who lived in the sewers. The community was being hunted down by Mr. Clean, an agent of the Church of Humanity, who was genetically engineered to murder mutants.

## Powers

Carla has taken on the appearance and attributes of a giant anthropomorphic beaver.
