## Major Story Arcs

President Reneau was in charge of Genosha during the X-Tinction Agenda.  It was there that Cameron Hodge used her and her associates to bring about his revenge on the X-Teams.  Madame Reneau was not completely for Hodge's plans but she did see some upsides for enhancing Genosha's world presence.  Because of her willingness to go along with Hodge, Madame Reneau's seat was overthrown by the Genoshan Magistrates.  It was reported that the president had suffered a mental break and was in psychiatric care, though it cannot be confirmed.
