> ###### The Antiquary
> **First appearance** The Man of Steal #1

> [!abstract] In brief
> The Antiquary is an exiled member of the Thieves Guild. He is also a sorcerer.

The Antiquary would steal and sell of children. He caused the Thieves Guild to steal Remy because of his unique, glowing red eyes. He had plans of keeping him in the Velvet Ministry, which still exsists within the Guild, under the Ministers Tome and the Hoard of the Thieves Guild.

On occasion, the Guild calls the Antiquary is regard to the ancient prophecies.
