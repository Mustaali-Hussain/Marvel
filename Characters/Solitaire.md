## Brief History

Solitaire was a Prime Being, like Scion: a descendant of the Makers who was supposed to have seeded the universe with the particles of her own body, creating new life.  She originally refused to do this, but ended up helping the Micronauts and took part in their recreation of life in the Microverse.
