## Origin

Linda Littletrees was a typical young woman who attended a college in the American east. However her roommate Jennifer Glatzer was a satanist. Jennifer managed to get Linda involved, reassuring her that it was innocent harmless and fun. Things got rather serious when Jennifer attempted to then successfully summoned Mephisto. She offered Linda to Mephisto. When Linda arrived home and realized what was happening, she tried to escape but was trapped by burning flame and fire. Linda was bound and stabbed with a special sacrificial blade, thus making her a bride of Satan or in this case, Mephisto. At first she was forced to become loyal and slavish to her new master, but with time she had gained more control and consciousness and it was with that time that she eventually became Witch Woman. 

## Creation

Witch Woman is a Marvel comics character and she was created by Gary Friedrich and Tom Sutton.

 

## Powers and Abilities

Witch Woman could generate, control, and manipulate hell fire. She could project them, redirect, and even construct flames into shapes. When possesses by Mephisto her powers would increase exponentially. Witch Woman can cause small flames to grow considerably.
