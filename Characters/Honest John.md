> Honest John, The Living Propaganda, is a member of The Red Skull's S-Men.

## Origin

Honest John has the taken the monger "The Living Propaganda". He is an expert orator and negotiator who has fully committed himself to the ideals of the Red Skull and his anti-mutant agenda. It is unclear how he received his powers and his ability to appear as anyone's ideal leader, but it can be assumed Red Skull had something to do with it, as Honest John is his second in command of the S-Men.

## Creation

Honest John was created by writer Rick Remender and artist John Cassaday. He first appeared in the story "New Union" in Uncanny Avengers #1 (Oct. 2012).
