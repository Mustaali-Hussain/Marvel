Gabriel or "Gabe" is Robbie's younger brother who is confined to a wheelchair. He loves comic books and idolizes his older brother. He was attacked by a group of thugs who tried to steal Gabe's wheelchair, but soon Robbie intervened. A gun was put to Robbie's head by one of the thugs and so they stole Gabe's wheelchair and left Robbie and Gabe in an alley.
