## Origin

Several centuries prior to the birth of humankind, the Eternal known as [[Mentor]] created ISAAC - Integral Synaptic Anti-Anionic Computer to aid him in rebuilding and running Titan.

ISAAC initially was a gargantuan computer, both teacher and servant, which lays below Titan. 

## Creation

ISAAC was created by Jim Starlin and first appeared in Iron Man #55 (the same issue which features the first appearances of [[Thanos]] and [[Drax the Destroyer]]).

## Major Story Arcs

### Absolute Vision

*Merged with Vision*

When [[Vision]] was badly injured during an adventure in the Negative Zone, [[Starfox]] sought the aid of ISAAC in helping to repair and fix the Avenger. In the process, ISAAC and Vision's minds became linked and ISAAC effectively replaced Vision for a period of time. While influencing Vision, ISAAC took over all of Earth's computers and proposed the formation of a second group of Avengers in an attempt to make Earth a paradise similar to Titan.

Vision was eventually freed from ISAAC's influence and he and the [[Scarlet Witch]] quit the Avengers shortly afterwards.

### Maximum Security

**For more information see:** Maximum Security

During the Maximum Security crossover, ISAAC attempted to remove the moongem from the former supervillain [[Moonstone]] deeming her unworthy of it's power. The Thunderbolts and [[Genis-Vell|Captain Marvel]] fought ISAAC in defense of their team-mate. Eventually ISAAC rescinded and allowed Moonstone to continue to use the gem while she sought redemption with the Thunderbolts.
