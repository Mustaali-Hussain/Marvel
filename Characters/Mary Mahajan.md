> Mary Mahajan is an engineering student at ESU, and a frequent supporter of Squirrel Girl and her friends.

## Creation

Mary Mahajan was created by Ryan North and Erica Henderson. She made her first appearance in *The Unbeatable Squirrel Girl* #2.

Her last name was provided for the first time in issue 39 so that, as the narrator at the bottom of the page says, "she is not only a more well-developed character, but now also **not** completely impossible to look up online!"

## Major Story Arcs

### Squirrel Girl

Mary has helped Squirrel Girl in several adventures, from Dr. Doom to, most recently, a Skrull impersonating Tony Stark.

Squirrel Girl considers Mary to be a very capable computer engineer (Doreen knows Mary as a fellow CS student at Empire State University) with a Chaotic Neutral personality. Mary is known to be interested in a variety of outré weapons.

Mary has been dating Chipmunk Hunk.

## Powers and Abilities

Mary has no powers but is a skilled computer engineer.
