> Warp Savant was a mutant who single-handedly attacked the military base of Cape Citadel, Florida.  He has the power to transport people and objects inside his own mind.

## Brief History

Warp Savant was a teenage drug user and drinker. The day before his eighteenth birthday he told his friends it will be a day to remember. On his eighteenth birthday, Warp Savant attacked the military base of Cape Citadel, Florida. Warp started using his powers to teleport military equipment and people to a limbo-like landscape within his mind. With the military unable to stop him, the X-Men (consisting of Jean Grey, Wolverine, Cyclops and Angel) tried to take him down. Unfortunately, Warp shunted Jean and Logan into his subconscious. Angel and Scott discovered the young mutant's power had a reach limit and Angel was equipped with an EM pulse device to target Warp and neutralize his powers from afar. Warp returned Jean and Logan to physical reality when Cyclops confronted him. After being hit with the EM pulse a second time, instead of being captured, Warp Savant chose to turn his power on himself, possibly killing himself in the process. His ultimate fate remains unknown.
