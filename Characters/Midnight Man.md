## Origin

A costumed thief who steals antiques. He has also worked with Bushman on several occasions. His son is former sidekick of Moon Knight called [[Midnight|Midnight.]]

## Creation

Midnight Man was created by Doug Moench and Bill Sienkiewicz in 1981 and first appeared in Moon Knight #3.

## Other Media

### Moon Knight

Anton Mogart appears in the 2022 *Moon Knight* TV-series. He is played by actor Gaspard Ulliel.
