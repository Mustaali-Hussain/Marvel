> [!abstract] In brief
> Vision's synthezoid son and Vivi twin.

Vin, along with his "twin sister" [[Viv]], was created by the [[Vision]] in his attempt at achieving humanity though the existence of an American nuclear family. Possessing the same powers as the other members of his family, Vin's brain wave pattens are based upon those of the Vision himself, as well as his "mother," [[Virginia]]. He is sent to high school to help along his cognitive development.
