> ###### Crimson Mage
> **Real name** Azaziah Hornsby
> **First appearance** Night of the Crimson Mage #43
> **Appearances** 11
> **Also known as** Azaziah Hornsby, Azazih

> [!abstract] In brief
> An enemy of the Ghost Rider

Azaziah discovered a mystic orb long ago. The orb gave him great power and allowed him to live for centuries. However, the orb grew weak over the years and threaten the mage's existence. Azaziah attempted to regain power from the [[Ghost Rider (Blaze)|Ghost Rider]]. His plan involved splitting [[Zarathos]] from his mortal body (Johnny Blaze). Both halves began to become weaker after becoming separated. Blaze noticed this change and confronted the Crimson Mage. During the fight, Blaze managed to destroy the mage's mystic orb. This act caused Azaziah to quickly age and died. Blaze after studying Azaziah's spell reunited with Zarathos.
