## Origin

Holly Gillis was a student at Empire State University who majored in art and dated Hector Ayala for a while. Holly and Hector were once held hostage inside the Coffee Bean shop by the Enforcers until they were saved by Spider Man. The Lightmaster would kidnap Hector because he believed the ESU student was Spider Man. When Hector revealed himself on national television as the White Tiger, Holly no longer wanted to be with him and hated him for deceiving her.
