## Origin

Onome is the daughter of one of Wakanda's greatest engineers. As with her father, Onome has great intelligence as well.

She met [[Valeria Richards]] and the Future Foundation on their trip to Wakanda, where she and Val both agreed she should come with them to New York and be a part of the team.
