> ###### Ringtoss
> **First appearance** #5
> **Appearances** 68

> [!abstract] In brief
> Ringtoss is part of the extraterrestrial cadre named the Technet. She is able to toss out energy rings from her faceplate which then binds beings it surrounds.

When Technet returned to Excalibur, Ringtoss joined the hero group N-Men along with the rest of her teammates. In Excalibur #45, she is seen with teammate [[Waxworks]], possibly romantically involved.

### Powers

Ringtoss can create rings of solidified energy that can entrap opponents. Ringtoss must concentrate in order to keep these rings in operation, however. If Ringtoss is rendered unconscious, the rings will fade away.
