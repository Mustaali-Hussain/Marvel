> [!abstract] In brief
> A humble horse from Vanaheim who is rescued by Jane Foster. Mr. Horse shows his appreciation by accompanying Jane, and later Runa, as a companion of the Valkyrior.

## Origin

Mr. Horse was co-created by Al Ewing and Jason Aaron, with artist CAFU designing his look. His speech patterns are modelled after a Yorkshire accent.
