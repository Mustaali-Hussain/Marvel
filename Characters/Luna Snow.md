## Origin

*Luna becomes a superstar*

Seol Hee was an aspiring young singer hoping to become a K-Idol and earn enough money to take care of her grandmother. Seol got her big break being chosen as part of a new girl group 4L1T. Taking on the stage name Luna, alongside groupmates Minni, Hae Won and Jessica, 4L1T hosted their debut performance at Stark Arena in Seoul. The group's manager had unknowingly bought tech from A.I.M. scientist the Joro Spider to "boost the performance's SFX," which would breach Stark's security and grant Joro access to the facility's cold fusion energy. When A.I.M. ambushed the event, Seol tried to defend the innocent people attending 4L1T's show.

However she was easily overpowered and the A.I.M soldiers locked her in the cryo-reactor. While attempting to escape the reactor Seol was exposed to a blast of cold-fusion energy, and the combination of radiation and intense cold gave Seol the power to create light and dark ice crystals. Seol used these strange powers to protect her friends and defeat the A.I.M. guards, before fighting Joro Spider and forcing her to retreat. The crowd cheers her stage name - "Luna! Luna Snow!" - and Seol becomes an overnight Idol/superhero sensation.

## Creation

Luna Snow was created Danny Koo, Bill Rosemann and Jeehyung Lee for the mobile game Marvel Future Fight. Her comic book debut was in War of the Realms: New Agents of Atlas issue 1 (2019), by Greg Pak and Gang Hyuk Lim.

## Major Story Arcs

### Joining the New Agents of Atlas

*Luna joins the Agents of Atlas*

As part of [[Malekith|Malekith's]] invasion of Midgard, the fire giantess [[Sindr]] commanded her demons of Muspelheim against the armed forces of South Korea and the people of Seoul. Luna Snow intervenes alongside fellow South Korean heroes [[White Fox]] and [[Crescent|Crescent and Io]], helping civilians escape before the Agents of Atlas can provide assistance. In the ensuing battle Seol's powers are turned against her and she is captured by Sindr within the Gyeongbokgung Palace, but is quickly rescued by [[Amadeus Cho]] and Luna Snow formally joins the New Agents of Atlas.

To counter Sindr's melting of the polar ice caps, Luna Snow is teamed up Shanghainese hero [[Aero]] and Filipina newcomer [[Wave]] to form a trio dubbed the Elementals. While Cho distracts Sindr and her army, the trio leap through the Queen of Muspelheim's portal and combine their powers of water, wind and ice to create a vast tornado that rapidly cools the Arctic climate. Once the damage is reversed, the Elementals rejoin their fellow Agents of Atlas at the Great Wall of China north of Beijing to defeat Sindr for good.

### Portal City of Pan & Atlantis Attacks

*Luna, Wave & Aero team up as the Elementals*

Luna Snow reunites with the Elementals to lure and send home a stray fire dragon from Sindr's invasion. There she, along with the Agents of Atlas, encounter a new hero called [[Isaac Ikeda|Protector]] who brings them to the new city of Pan. Pan is a city made up of numerous Asian cities around the globe, brought together through portal technology masterminded by billionaire CEO Mike Nguyen. Pan is almost immediately bombarded by numerous wyvern attacks which Seol Hee and her teammates fight back, defending the city's tourists all while Seol balances flirtationus advances by both Protector and Amadeus. The Agents are officially branded the new "Heroes of Pan," but with her business savvy Seol Hee sees through Nguyen's PR gimmicks, and confides her suspicions of Pan's utopian facade with Amadeus. The two begin to bond which reveals a potential romantic attraction, but the two are interrupted when Pan soldiers begin attacking Madripoorian immigrants who have made a long perilous journey by sea to get to Pan. Both heroes ardently defend the immigrants and Protector reels in the guards, apologising and promising to keep the people safe. Now alone, Seol Hee and Amadeus kiss, a private moment that is captured by surveillance cameras and weaponized by Nguyen as part of his Pan Pass marketing campaign. Luna is outraged by Nguyen's illegal use of their kiss - as K-Pop stars have non-dating clauses in their contracts and the footage could could destroy her career - so she sues for copyright and trademark infringement in response. Nguyen settles with Luna, however the publicity of their intimate moment and potential risk to Seol's idol career sours any romantic potential the couple could have had.

*Seol and Amadeus share a moment*

The source of Pan's magical portal technology is soon revealed to be powered by a kidnapped Atlantian dragon, and [[Namor|King Namor]] launches a singular invasion of the city. Luna is among the Agents of Atlas who want no part in the war, insisting that it is their job to protect civilians and aid in evacuation efforts. When Namor returns to Pan in a blind rage to kill Mike Nguyen and sink the city, Luna Snow and Aero manage to hold him back for an extended time until the team formulates a plan.

### Silk Team-Up

*Luna Snow's first US tour*

While Korean-America superhero [[Silk]] was a huge fan of Luna Snow the K-idol, the two became genuine friends working together as part of the Agents of Atlas. Both were the moral center of the team, consistently defending the rights of the people of Pan, and Seol Hee appreciated Cindy Moon's friendship with her as an actual person. After the battle between Atlantis and Pan, Luna Snow begins her first U.S. solo tour to promote her new album and sends Silk tickets to attend her sold out NYC concert at Madison Square Garden. Silk is currently investigating the mystery surrounding a 17th Century Manyeo (Korean witch), and her warriors attempt to kidnap Luna in the middle of her performance. Cindy suits up as Silk and intervenes the brawl breaking out between Luna and the warriors on stage, which the two ladies win to the crowd's fervant applause. After the concert ends Luna invites Cindy back to her dressing room, where she offers her assistance in defeating the Manyeo, but Cindy declines and reminds Seol she still has a tour to finish.

### Joining Tiger Division

*Fighting a possessed Taegukgi*

Off-panel, White Fox officially invites Luna Snow to join the National Intelligence Service's superhuman response team Tiger Division. Shortly after she joins, the team's heavy hitter [[Taegukgi]] is mind controlled by the gangsters Choi Faction, who threatens to unleash him upon Seoul unless the government cede control of state to them. White Fox blackmails the American thief [[Black Cat]] to assist with the team's rescue operation, and the team set off from their base on Burasam Mountain to investigate the Choi's hideout at an abandoned DMZ ski resort. While Fox & Cat investigate the resort, Luna joins the rest of her team in "distracting" South Korea's most powerful superhuman. Tiger Division are woefully outmatched and Luna is injured in the battle, being guarded by her teammate [[Lady Bright]]'s sorcery before a falling building can crush them. The team hold on long enough for Fox & Cat to locate the man psychically possessing Taegukgi's body, Mongdal, and free Taegukgi from his control.

### Concert of Champions

Luna Snow later joins a line-up of huge musical superstars - including [[Dazzler]] and [[Lila Cheney]] - as headliner of the California-based festival Concert of Champions. She clashes with Dazzler over her commitment to the fans and the concert's schedule, not knowing that the mutant songstress' mental health is spiralling after a series of scandals and attacks. Their conflict is shelved when a suspicious metal band called Deep Void gatecrashes the festival and, while Lila and Luna decide to confront the newcomers, Dazzler shuts herself off within her trailer. [[Gwen Stacy]], a guest at the festival, helps confront Deep Void in her Ghost-Spider costume and Lila teleports the crowd to safety while Luna attacks [[Angar the Screamer]], [[Forearm]], and [[Ruby Thursday]]. Dazzler overcomes her fears and joins Luna, Ghost-Spider and [[Domino]], and together the women defeat Deep Void. The ladies regroup and perform an uplifting anthem together, this time with Gwen Stacy on drums, as Marvel's first supergroup.

### One World Under Doom

After Victor Von Doom announces a state of Pax Victoris, Tiger Division is drafted in defeating a Bulgae while filming a music video for Luna, that includes pro-Doom messaging and lyrics welcoming Doom's "evergreen peace." Luna expresses concern for its potential immorality, however her producer Mr. Baek insists that she must comply to continue being profitable for the company. At her halmoni's apartment, Seol laments being strong-armed into making propaganda. Her grandmother states that Korea is a place of constant change, prospering now, but to remember that Japan's occupation came under the guise of prosperity and modernization. Luna must keep an open, but wary, mind on Doom's impact on Korea.

*Forced to push Doom's propaganda*

After nine days of Doom's new emergency response drones, Tiger Division begin to feel redundant and debate the validity of Doom's rule. Director Han orders them back to headquarters and announces Doom's Pan-Asian expansion protocol, revealing Aero, Wave and [[Karma]] as Tiger Division's new team members. With the regional alliance comes rebranding, and Tiger Division are renamed Doom's Division. While the rest of the team are sent to aprehend the renegade [[Sunfire]], who defies Doom's world order, Luna is ordered to stay behind and produce her new pro-Doom pop album. The record is fast-tracked in days and Luna is sent on a publicity tour, being coerced to feign happiness at Doom's rule. At a Seoul stadium rehearsal Luna feels sick singing propaganda, but meeting fans whose medical treatment was expedited by Doom's new legislation convinces Luna she can't let the people who depend on her down.

*Luna's ice armor*

Standing on stage, Seol receives a magical messages from Lady Bright informing her that Sunfire is dead, and White Fox betrayed them. Luna leaves the concert and heads to Buramsan Mountain, where she finds Tiger Division fighting off White Fox in her kumiho form. Luna joins the team and pursues White Fox when she flees, and traps her within an ice shard cage. When White Fox reveals she and Sunfire had been working against Doom from the start, Luna feels guilt for not fighting against her record label from the start. Together, the heroes go into hiding.

### World Tour

*Backlash for her role in Doom's takeover of South Korea*

After the fall of Emperor Doom, Luna faces backlash within South Korea by opposing groups for both aligning with a foreign fascist power and for betraying Doom by siding with the hero Sunfire. She is also dropped by her management agency for being a controversial celebrity, and immediately replaced with the more palettable hero/idol Vibe. Luna continues to perform in Tokyo and defends its civilians when a Vibe robot hypnotises them into donating all their money. Teaming up with Sunfire and fellow Agents of Atlas member Wave, Seol decides to investigate the bewitching effect of Vibe's megahit.

They follow Vibe's tour to New York City, where Vibe's manager reveals himself to be the Eternal and ex-Avenger [[Starfox|Eros]]. Dissatisfied with the divisive political rhetoric in the world, his plan of uniting everyone under the generic concept of love led him to hijack Vibe's powers to amplify his own seductive abilities to pacify people. Eros attempts to convert Luna to his way of thinking, promising her a world where she is the world's most beloved performer who unites everyone through feel-good music. Acknowledging that being palletable to everyone erases her individuality or opportunities to make mistakes, Luna rejects Starfox. Vibe apologises to Luna for being so easily controlled by Starfox's powers, but Luna forgives her and accepts her own current situation of being a controversial public figure, but authentic to herself.

Luna produces a special type of cryokinesis, allowing for 'dark ice' that can be used offensively and 'light ice' for healing.

## Other Media

### Video Games

### Marvel Future Fight

*Luna Snow's launch poster in January 2018*

Luna Snow was created as the first original character made by Danny Koo for the NetEase mobile game Marvel Future Fight. She was added to the game in January 2018 as a Speed type character, and has become the poster character for the mobile game since her launch.

When A.I.M. ambushed a Stark Industries party where aspiring singer Seol Hee was performing, her attempts to defend the partygoers caused A.I.M. agents to lock her in a storage freezer. During her escape, she knocked over the contents of an energy experiment, exposing her to elements that granted her ice powers which she then used to defeat A.I.M. Her heroic actions made her an overnight sensation, and she continues to use her talent and powers under the name "Luna Snow" -- part time pop star, part-time Super Hero!

- Luna Snow's official bio

As a NetEase creation for the game, Luna Snow has received consistent updates with new costumes and upgrades to her power-set. She received her Tier 3 update in December 2018, alongside her Andromeda costume which is now the default uniform Luna wears in the Marvel 616 comics. Her Tier 4 update was released in August 2023 along with a new K-Idol inspired outfit and a new single. Luna Snow is consistently towards the top of the Future Fight meta, as one of the best damage-dealing characters with self heals and sytlistic power effects.

As promotion for her updates throughout the game's history, Future Fight has released several K-pop songs "by" Luna Snow, including:

- "I Really Wanna" sung by Krysta Youngs

### Marvel Super War

*Luna Snow in Super War*

Luna Snow was added to the NetEase MOBA game Marvel Super War in December 2020 as part of the game's 1st Anniversary event. To celebrate Luna Snow's arrival to a new game, NetEase released another K-pop single "by" Luna. This song, "Flow" was sung by Luna from K-pop group f(x).

As a Super Hero in *MARVEL Super War*, Luna Snow will step into Wakanda Battlefield as a powerful Energy hero with the ability to create dark ice for destruction and light ice for healing. By switching and manipulating dark ice and light ice, she can cause AoE damage and inflict a freeze effect on enemies while healing and saving herself from danger.

### Marvel Rivals

*Luna Snow in Marvel Rivals*

Luna Snow returns in NetEase's PvP hero shooter Marvel Rivals, which launched in December 2024. Luna is a Strategist class hero, whose kit comprises healing ice shards and an ultimate where Seol performs a song that boosts damage or heals her allies. She has team-up abilities with her former Agents of Atlas teammate [[Lin Lie]], and her ice can boost the heroes Namor and [[Jeff the Land Shark]].

In the lore Luna has helped quell the tensions between Atlantis and the surface world, even with Namor setting his sights on Jeju Island.

Luna is voiced by Judy Alice Lee.
