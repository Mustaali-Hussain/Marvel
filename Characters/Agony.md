> ###### Agony
> **First appearance** The End Has Just Begun #44
> **Appearances** 11

> [!abstract] In brief
> The ex-lover of Seth of the Hellbent.

Agony is a member of the Hellbent and private assassin to Seth the Immortal.   

 

 

She had similar abilities to Medusa of the Inhumans. She and her partner Basilisk were used as assassins by Seth the Immortal. Seth had to drain lifeforces in order to survive. Agony willingly sacrificed her own lifeforce to allow a weakened Seth to survive.
