> Alysande is the twin sister of Alistaire Stewart and a Brigadier in the Weird Happenings Organization, otherwise known as W.H.O.

## Origin     

 

Alysande was first seen with Commander Dai Thomas investigating a suspicious "Nazi" train which was later found to come from across the Multiverse. She was a Brigadier in the Weird Happenings Organization (W.H.O) with her brother as the their chief Scientist. Soon Alysande, along with her brother; met Excalibur, and the "Weird Happenings" really began!
