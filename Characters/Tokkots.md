> ###### Tokkots
> **First appearance** . . . And the Home of the Brave! #8

> [!abstract] In brief
> Tokkots is the Dark God of corruption and decay.

Tokkots was a large winged Dark God, who always spoke in rhyme. He went to Earth and battled [[Thor]] and [[Spider-Man]], and brought back the information that Thor was alive. He subdued [[Jake Olsen]] and brought him before [[Perrikus]]. He was killed by [[Replicus]] moments before he could slay [[Odin]].

## Powers & Abilities

Tokkots was very bloodthirsty, always wanting to kill anyone he could. He exhibited exceptional strength and durability, could rot things with his touch, and make copies of himself which apparently all shared one mind. He could also fly with the use of his wings.
