## Known History

She is the youngest of the Wagner clan and daughter of Nightcrawler of the X-men and Kymri, an alternate dimension princess. Born after the Skrulls and Shi'ar attacked she was named after her father's dead former love Cerise. Out of all her family she is the most sheltered due to the danger that once permeated their lives. It is unknown if she has/will attend the Xavier institute.

### Distinguishing features

Like her father and brother before her she has iridescent yellow eyes, blue velvet fur, three fingers on each hand, two toes on each foot, fangs, and pointed teeth.

**Skills and abilities**

She has been trained in swordplay by her parents and circus acrobatics by her father. In addition she has unarmed combat training from both parents and her older sister Talia.
