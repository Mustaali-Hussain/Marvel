## Origin

A Dire Wraith Queen who ambushed an Xandarian space cruiser to earth and sought the Turbo armor that was made from her people. Upon tracking the armor down, she started to have a body count of humans and one Xardarian under her belt. After killing a member of the New Warriors, the team fought hard and eventually [[Night Thrasher]] supposedly kills her.

## Character Creation

Volx was created by Even Scholnick and Phil Gosier.

## Character Evolution

Volx teamed-up with Dire Wraith Warlock Doctor Dredd to bring their planet Wraithworld out of Limbo, which was banished by Rom the Spaceknight. You see both Galador's sun and the black sun are now merging together and the only way to save them is to merge Galador and Wraithworld together. The team known as the Annihilators must confront them and solve the current dilemma.
