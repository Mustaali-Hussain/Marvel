## Origin

Siri was a Skrull warrior who was also a member of the Dard'van sect. She was tasked with the mission of infiltrating and spying on Earth under the guise of the assassin [[Elektra]] as a means to test if anyone on Earth could detect that she was a Skrull. Due to the success of the mission, she was deployed again to defeat and capture the real Elektra and take her place on Earth.

## Creation

Siri was created by Brian Michael Bendis and Jimmy Cheung and first appeared in *The New Avengers* #40.

## Character Evolution

Much confusion exists over Siri, many mistakenly identifying her as the Skrull Elektra who featured heavily in *The New Avengers*. *The Mighty Avengers* #16 reveals that that Elektra was a Skrull called [[Pagon]] who took Siri's mission after she died. Despite this, many people erroneously acredit Siri as the Skrull Elektra. Not particularly helped since *Skrulls!* one-shot gave Siri a profile before it was established she wasn't the Elektra imposter who led the Hand. The two can be told apart by outfit; Siri wears black whereas Pagon wears red.

## Major Story Arcs

### Secret Invasion

Siri tracked down Elektra and engaged her in a fight. Holding her own for a while, Siri was eventually killed by Elektra when she was pierced through the chest with a sai. Subsequent Skrulls were sent to avenge Siri and capture Elektra, and when one known as Pagon succeeded, he became Elektra himself to fulfill Siri's mission.

## Powers and Abilities

Siri is able to shapeshift and underwent a process to make her undetectable by magic or science. She is a skiller combatant and also has various other powers, mostly mimicked from human superheroes (such as [[Cyclops]]' optic blasts, [[Thing|Thing's]] muscles or [[Wolverine|Wolverine's]] claws).
