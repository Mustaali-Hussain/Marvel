> [!abstract] In brief
> Self-proclaimed "Monarch of the Murder Chair," Cadavus is a member of the villainous Exiles whose main weapon is a hovering chair outfitted with deadly energy blasters.

Cadavus is paralyzed below the waist and uses a hovering chair for transportation. The chair is equipped with various weapons - primarily guns capable of firing energy blasts. Franz calls it his "Murder Chair."

## Creation

Cadavus was created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby in 1968 and first appeared in *Captain America *#102.
