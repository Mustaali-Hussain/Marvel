## Brief History

A minor villain whom fought Spider-Man and Captain America once when he was imployed by the crimeboss known as Silvermane. He was one of many villains to die by the hand of the Scourge.

## Creation

Rapier was created by Ralph Macchio and Jim Mooney in 1980 and first appeared in The Spectacular Spider-Man Annual # 2.

## Abilities & Weapons

While he had no powers, he was agile and strong. He also had a fencing foil equipped with a stun function.
