## Origin

Amka Aliyak is an Inuk teenager living in Pangnirtung, a village on Baffin Island in Nunavut, Canada.

## Joining the Champions

After the [[Master of the World]] established a facility in Pangnirtung with the objective to repair the damage caused to the glaciers by global warming, Amka broke into the base to investigate its origins. Once she infiltrated the facility, Amka encountered the Inua spirit, Sila, in containment. When she tried to free Sila, Amka was attacked by the base's defense drones. She managed to shove the drones into the containment field surrounding Sila, which caused an explosion. While Amka was fatally injured, the explosion freed Sila, and the spirit healed her injuries in a flash of light. Having been imparted with Sila's energy, Amka was trapped inside the factory until her rescue by [[Miles Morales|Spider-Man]] during the Alpha Flight's fight with the Champions. The battle was interrupted when Spider-Man brought to them the newfound information that the Master was using Amka as a battery, leeching off the supposedly limitless energy of the Soul of the North. The collected forces of the Champions and Alpha Fight took down the Master of the World, then later Amka chose to become a superhero, and train with the Champions.

## Powers

Infused with spiritual magic from the Inua Spirit, Sila, Snowguard can channel animal traits to enhance her physical abilities
