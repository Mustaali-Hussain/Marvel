> [!abstract] In brief
> Timmy Lange is the son of Leap-Frog. Forced into a difficult situation, he stopped his villainous father and was subsequently adopted by Ben Urich's family, with the help of Daredevil.

When Timmy was four years old, he climbed up onto the kitchen cabinet. He climbed his little body all the way to the top shelf here and grabbed ahold of this big bottle of Flintstone's Vitamins. He loved those damned vitamins. Because they're like candy. He ate the entire bottle. His mom sent him to emergency room.

He saw something that shocked him into a catatonic state. The kid had totally lost his grip on reality. Something related to [[Daredevil]].

Years later, Timmy would have words with his father about what Daredevil did for their family as well as his ethics.
