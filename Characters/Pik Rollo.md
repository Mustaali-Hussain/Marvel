## History

Pik Rollo is one of a few remaining Ruu'lto the other being her daughter Hilla. Hilla was kidnapped by a rogue Gramosian named Mercurio in order to have Pik work for him or else he'd kill her daughter. Using Pik as his assassin she built up the reputation of being a murderer and criminal.

Pik's next target was Flash Thompson who had been causing a lot of trouble for Mercurio's operations to gather energy. However when both were captured on the unnamed planet that she confronted Flash on the two were forced to fight in an arena against a number of creatures. It was during these fights that Pik disclosed her situation to Flash. Flash decided to strike a deal that instead of taking Mercurio his head that the two would take down Mercurio together.
