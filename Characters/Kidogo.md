## History

 

Lazaro Kotikash is Maasai. In his normal state he is almost a head taller than anyone else on the team. Kidogo was a member of training squad Alpha Squadron and his advisors were Northstar and Karma after the events of House of M/Decimation he was de-powered but his current whereabouts are unknown. He possibly was in the bus that Stryker exploded and presumably killed. His codename means "something small" in the Swahili language.

 

## Likes and dislikes

 

In the New X-men Academy X Yearbook special he says that he likes to disappear and to have the best sight. He dislikes people who treads on him when he is small. He was voted "Most ironic power" possibly because he's one head taller than the rest of his squad.

 

## Powers

Lazaro is a micromorph able to shrink down to only four inches tall by displacing his mass extra-dimensionally.
