> Mzee is a tortoise-like member of the Red-Skull's S-Men.

## Origin

Mzee was born in an Ethiopian village. When he was a child, Magneto's henchmen attacked his village and slaughtered everyone. Mzee escaped by hiding in an oil drum.

## Creation

Mzee was created by writer Rick Remender and artist John Cassaday. He first appeared in the story "New Union" in Uncanny Avengers #1 (Oct. 2012).
