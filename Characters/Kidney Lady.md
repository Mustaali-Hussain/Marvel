## Origins

Selma Blotte is obsessed with kidneys. Tricked when she was a young farm girl by a door to door salesman Elmer, she was used by Elmer, and appears to have sought comfort from the book he sold her, The Human Kidney, Seat of the Soul. She is convinced that Howard the Duck has nefarious plans with her kidney's.

## Creation

Kidney Lady is a Marvel comics character created by Steve Gerber and Frank Brunner. She first appeared in Howard the Duck #2, and has remained predominantly a Howard the Duck foe.

## Powers and Abilities

Kidney lady seems to have very limited range of mystical abilities. She can animate inanimate objects and teleport. She will often use her cane for offensive attacks and strikes.
