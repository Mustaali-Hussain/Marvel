Member of the Knights of Pendragon.

Recreational android for the amusement theme-park Quest-World in Northumberland, England, he was a product of the Stark Industries, like all the other tech-actractions of the park.

## Origin

Gone rogue during the attack of the park by the Mys-Tech, somehow he's possessed by the Pendragon spirit, and joins the hero team.
