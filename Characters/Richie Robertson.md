## Origin

Richie Roberston is the grandson of former Daily Bugle editor Robbie Robertson. He was a frequent aquantance of Jack Jameson. Eventualy the two would become good friends.

## Creation

Richie Robertson was created by Tom DeFalco and Ron Frenz and first appeared in The Buzz issue 1 (2000). Richie Robertson has so far only appeared in the alternate reality of the MC2 Universe.

## Major Story Arcs

### The Buzz

Richie was an unrelunctant participant in the hostage of his employer, J Jonah Jameson. Together with Jack and Buzz, Richie managed to defeat the hostage-takers, but with Buzz's death as a result unfortunately.
