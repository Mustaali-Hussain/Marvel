## Origin

Agent of GRAMPA, Global Reaction Agency for Mysterious Paranormal Activity, One Eyed Jacquie often partners with [[Ace]] as duo extraordinaire Blackjack. One Eyed Jacquie has collaborated with the Mighty Avengers, even offering the team roles with GRAMPA, giving them clearance in countries not governed under [[Norman Osborn|Norman Osborn's]] authority. She has offered more traditional assistance to the Avengers team by helping them defeat the gigantic sea creature known as Titan. At the scene first, [[Hank Pym|Hank Pym's]] team arrived and offered help. With Pym and [[Cassie Lang|Stature]] growing to their near limits to fight the incredibly huge sea creature, One Eyed Jacquie, Ace and [[U.S.Agent|USAgent]] were equipped with special advanced guns, that would shoot a special fluid that in theory would absorb Titan's moisture at a rapid rate depowering it. After defeating the giant creature One Eyed Jacquie and Ace acting on behalf of GRAMPA offer Pym and his team GRAMPA's sanction and resources.   

  

## Creation

One Eyed Jacquie is a Marvel comics character created by Greg Pak and Takeshi Miyazawa. One Eyed Jacquie first appears in Amazing Fantasy #15 released in 2006.
