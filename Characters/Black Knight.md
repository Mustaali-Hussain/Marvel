> [!abstract] In brief
> Young female Black Knight and member of the Young Masters.

The new female incarnation of the Black Knight first appeared in the *Vengeance* limited series, written by Joe Casey and illustrated by Nick Dragotta. She has since joined the Young Masters, an enclave of young villains who have patterned themselves after members of various incarnations of the Masters of Evil to oppose the Young Avengers and students of the Avengers Academy.

Her relationship to the late [[Black Knight (Garrett)|Nathan Garrett]], the original villainous Black Knight, or to his nephew, the former Avenger [[Black Knight|Dane Whitman]]--if any--has yet to be revealed.
