## Origin

Jahf is one of the guardians of the M'Kraan Crystal and he comes into conflict with the X-Men and the Starjammers when they try to stop D'Ken from using the powerful jewel to tear the fabric of time and space apart.

## Creation

Jahf was created by Chris Claremont and John Byrne in 1977 and first appeared in X-Men # 108.

## Powers

Jahf is invulnerable to physical injury and has virtually unlimited strength.

## Weaknesses

Supersonic attacks can scramble his circuits.

## Alternate Realities

Jahf doesn't have counterparts in alternate realities. He is possibly omniversal.
