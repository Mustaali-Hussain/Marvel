> Tracy Speck was a DP7 villain called Receptor.

Ms. Speck could track people with her mind and used this talent to consistently locate the runaway DP7. She was later revealed to be involved romantically with Mr. Charne. She was killed when Charne was staging a coupe to gain control of the Clinic for Paranormal Research and accidentally shot her.
