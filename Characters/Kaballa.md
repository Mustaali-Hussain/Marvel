## Brief History

Kaballa dwelled deep beneath the sands of the middle east in a place where the sun has never shone. A practitioner of black magic and particularly the summoning and control of elementals including some powerful enough to go up against the Hulk, Kaballa is presumed dead after the Exo-Mind explosion.
