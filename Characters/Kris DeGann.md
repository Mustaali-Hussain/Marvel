## History

Kris DeGann (sometimes misspelt as *DeGunn*) was a largely unremarkable and generic love interest introduced for Iron Boy ("Teen Tony") following The Crossing. Her most notable contribution was being victim to an explosion accidentally caused by the inexperienced superhero she was unknowingly dating.

## Creation

Kris DeGann was created by Terry Kavanagh and Jimmy Cheung and first appeared in Iron Man #326.
