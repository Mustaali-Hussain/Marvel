## Brief History

Cr'reee is a small creature usually seen sitting on the shoulder of Ch'od, it is intelligent, but the extent of Cr'reee's intellect remains widely unknown, though Ch'od seems to understand it's speech.   

## Creation

Cr'reee was created by Chris Claremont and Dave Cockrum in 1977 and first appeared in X-Men # 104.
