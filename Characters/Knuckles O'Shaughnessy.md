## Creation

Knuckles O'Shaughnessy debuted in Ghost Rider (2006) #33 by Jason Aaron and Tony Moore.

## Major Story Arcs

### History's Mightiest Avengers

In 1924, Knuckles found himself in Hong Kong. He was being hunted by goons possessed by [[Mephisto]]. He had possessed people across time trying to cutoff the legacy mantles, including the Spirit of Vengeance. He was teamed up with Grandmaster Wong, the Iron Fist of the time. Thankfully, the present-day Avengers and the stone-aged Avengers teamed up with other heroes from the timeline at the God Quarry to end Mephisto's assault on history.
