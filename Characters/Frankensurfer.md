> ###### Frankensurfer
> **First appearance** The Heir of Frankenstein! #7
> **Appearances** 14
> **Also known as** Silver Surfer, Frankenstein's monster

> [!abstract] In brief
> A murderous slave made by a heir of Doctor Frankenstein modelled on the Silver Surfer.

Ludwig Frankenstein made a monster using the Silver Surfer as a model and duplicated all of the Surfer's powers onto an empty body. The result was a murderous slave with the Power Cosmic and his own silver surfboard.

After devastating Earth and terrorising Frankenstein's enemies, Frankensurfer was defeated by the real Silver Surfer.

## Creation

Frankensurfer was created by Stan Lee and John Buscema and his only appearance was in The Silver Surfer (1968) #7

## Powers

Frankensurfer, despite being a clone of the Silver Surfer, shares all his powers including the Power Cosmic. In terms of strength and power, the two were equal - the only difference was the real Surfer's humanity.
