## Brief History

The Two-Headed Thing tunneled up from the earth`s core and had to power to mimick every appearance he saw. First he became an alligator and then a tree when humanity started looking for strange happenings where came up. It was at this time that a prisoner tried to escape from jail and got caught by the creature, who then mimicked himself into his appearance. When the police came, the creature showed it's true nature and was bound to attack. Luckily for the police and bystanders, a rainstorm had gathered and lightning came out of the sky and hit the Two-Headed Thing. He died there on the spot.
