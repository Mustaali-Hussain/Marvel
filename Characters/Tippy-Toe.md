## In Other Media

### Animation

*Marvel Rising*

- Tippy-Toe appears as Squirrel Girl's partner in the *Ultimate Spider-Man* animated series.
- Tippy-Toe appears as Squirrel Girl's partner in *Marvel Rising: Secret Warriors* and the various other *Marvel Rising* animated projects, voiced by Dee Bradley Baker.

### Video Games

*Contest of Champions*

- Tippy-Toe appears as Squirrel Girl's partner in the *Marvel Avengers Alliance* Facebook game.
- Upon encountering the Mandarin in *Marvel Heroes*, Squirrel Girl mentions that Tippy-Toe stole his ten rings.
- Tippy-Toe appears as an assist character for Squirrel Girl in the *Marvel Contest of Champions* mobile fighting game.
- Tippy-Toe appears in the *Marvel Future Fight* mobile game.
- Tippy-Toe appears in the *Avengers Academy* mobile game. She can be equipped with a suit of Iron Man armor to become **Iron Tippy-Toe**.

### Merchandise

*Marvel Legends*

- Funko's Squirrel Girl Pop! bobblehead came with a miniature Tippy-Toe as an accessory. 
- Tippy-Toe was featured in the HeroClix figure game.
- Hasbro's Squirrel Girl doll came with a miniature Tippy-Toe as an accessory. 
- Hasbro produced a deluxe Marvel Legends Squirrel Girl figure for the Ultimate Riders line that came with a miniature Tippy-Toe figure as an accessory.
