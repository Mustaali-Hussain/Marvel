## Origin

A famous and controversial attorney from Boston, Rosalind Sharpe enters the life of [[Daredevil]]when she makes an offer to Matt Murdock and Foggy Nelson to become her associates. Daredevil is surprised to find that share and Foggy know one another until it is later revealed that she is Foggy's birth mother. She divorced her husband and left the family to better pursue her legal career. She was ultimately what inspired Foggy to become a lawyer.

## Creation

Rosalind Sharpe was created by Karl Kesel and Cary Nord and first appeared in Daredevil issue 353 (1996).

## Major Story Arcs

### No Son of Mine

After he and Matt join Sharpe's firm Foggy becomes a victim of [[Mysterio]], who manipulates him into having an affair and then framing him for murdering that woman, thus ruining Foggy's relationship with [[Liz Allan]]. This also results in Sharpe terminating him from her firm, as she does not want her son's affair and murder accusation to bring negative publicity. When Matt starts preparing Foggy's defense, Sharpe orders him to cease, not wanting their firm to be involved at all. This results in Matt quitting the firm in disgust. Eventually Foggy was cleared of the accusations, but forming bond with his biological mother was forever halted.
