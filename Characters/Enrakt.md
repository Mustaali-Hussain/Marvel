> ###### Enrakt
> **First appearance** And Soon Shall Come the Enchanters! #14
> **Appearances** 14

Enrakt is the younger member of the Enchanters and did not took part in their early battles because of being in training. He eventually emerged as the most powerful of them, able to lift c. 100 tons and challenging in an equal standing. His spells allowed him control of Mjolnir but a miscalculated spell disembodied him. He was still able to manipulate events against Thor and his spirit was still bound to Mjolnir and effecting it. At least until Thor sought help from the Scarlet Witch who severed the ties between Enrakt and Mjolnir. Thor was then easily able to lay Enrakt to final rest.
