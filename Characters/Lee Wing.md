## Brief History

A professor of Oriental Studies at Colombia University and the father of [[Colleen Wing]]. He lives in a Manhattan townhouse.

He ordered his daughter to find the superhero [[Iron Fist]] in hopes of helping him in a mission he set for his daughter. Iron Fist and Colleen both helped out. He had been robbed of his mind for some time, but soon managed to make a full recovery. He has not been seen in recent times.
