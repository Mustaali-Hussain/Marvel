## Origins

Bodolf the Black was once a viking leader and warrior who would pray to Thor before every battle, which would always aid his victory. At times, Thor would fight beside him. Once, he did not pray to Thor and lost, in his defeat he prayed to Loki and was told how to obtain power to get revenge on Thor

## Powers & Abilities

Bodolf was a man before his powers with years of combat experience, swordsmanship training. After his powers, he gained a larger and more powerful body and super human strength. He was able to defeat an entire army of men and Thor with relative ease.
