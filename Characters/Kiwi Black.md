> Kiwi Black is of Maori descent, and also the son of Azazel of the Neyaphem and half-brother of Nightcrawler and Abyss.

## Origin

Kiwi Black was one of the many mutants fathered by Azazel with the aim of freeing him and the rest of the Neyaphem. After surviving a ritual that was meant to free the Neyaphem, Kiwi Black fought against his father alongside the X-Men. He was depowered by M-Day. 

 

## Creation 

Created by Chuck Austen and artist Philip Tan, Kiwi Black first appeared in Uncanny X-Men #429. Kiwi Black is one of a few Maori characters in mainstream comic books.
