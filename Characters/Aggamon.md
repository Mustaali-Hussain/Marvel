## Information

 

For at least a millennium Aggamon had ruled the Purple Dimension, from which he tricked natives of other dimensions into entering his realm and forced them into slavery in his mystic gem mines.    Aggamon is the Sorcerer Supreme of the Purple Dimension.

 **

** 

## Powers

 

Aggamon can conjure mental visions, open dimensional portals, and perform other magical abilities.  He has access to a wide variety of mystic gems, and is served by an army of underlings.
