> Maria Hill was the Director of S.H.I.E.L.D. after Nick Fury went awol. During the Heroic Age she acted as supervising liason for The Avengers, and was a key player in the Secret Empire event.

## Origin

Maria was born in Chicago, Illinois. Her mother died during childbirth, something her father always resented her for. She attended the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor. When she grew older she joined the armed forces then went on to join S.H.I.E.L.D. Maria Hill was elected into the Directorate position by the President after [[Nick Fury]] went AWOL at the end of Secret War. Though there were others more suited for the position, Maria was chosen for not being a “Fury Loyalist” (Which was seen as highly unfavorable).She has very little respect in the Super-human community for many reasons, such as trying to prohibit the Avengers reforming, and blackmailing [[Wonder Man]] to work for SHIELD.

## Creation

Maria Hill is created by writer Brian Michael Bendis and artists David Finch and Danny Miki. She first appeared in New Avengers Vol.1 issue 4 (2005).

## Major Story Arcs

### Civil War

*During the Civil War*

Maria Hill became a mayor player soon after Nick Fury left S.H.I.E.L.D. She was adamantly in support of the Super-human Registration Act, nearly forcing [[Captain America]] into the Resistance after attacking him (Since he would not sign the act). Maria's allegiances lie more towards the United States, than to the international community as SHIELD is supposed to.

As the 50 State Initiative was set in motion, Maria passed the mantle of leadership to [[Iron Man|Tony Stark]], realizing she was not the right person for the job. She became Stark's deputy director. During her time as his deputy, she grew to trust Stark more and her methods became much more in-conventional then they had been before.

### Secret Invasion and Dark Reign

*In the Siege*

During Secret Invasion, the Helicarrier is attacked and taken over by Skrulls whilst she was on it. The Skrull tells her the Skrulls had caused her to be made Director and her job was to publicly surrender on behalf of humanity. Maria doesn't back down and she's gunned down. However Hill had used a LMD and the real Maria escaped and blew up the Helicarrier, killing the Skrulls on board. During Dark Reign, S.H.I.E.L.D was turned into H.A.M.M.E.R, Hill was fired by [[Norman Osborn]] and Maria began to work more closely with Tony and helps in various missions. Hill and Stark grow closer in their time spent together, even acting more intimately with each other on occasion.

**Siege**

After Osborn invades Asgard during Siege, Maria arrives before the Avengers and on her own with a huge gun. Maria saves [[Thor]] from being murdered by Osborn's forces and is instrumental in the fight against him. [[Captain America|Steve Rodgers]] took notice of her actions during Siege and appointed her to lead a new team of Avengers. During a meeting of the three Avengers liaisons, [[Victoria Hand]], [[Sharon Carter]] and Maria are discussing various Avengers affairs when the [[Absorbing Man]] attacks them. Maria comes up with a plan to defeat him. Together they beat him and arrest him again. Maria still works with Tony even during her time spent with the Avengers.

### Back as Director

Maria Hill has since resumed her role as Director of S.H.I.E.L.D. As such, she has been working more closely with superheroes and even with the mutant team known as the X-Men in hoping to capture the fugitive Cyclops for his role in the murder of Charles Xavier. She still has a no-nonsense attitude, but is more open to working with the superhero community then before.

## In Other Media

### Film

### The Avengers (2012)

*Aboard the Helicarrier*

*How I Met Your Mother* star Cobie Smulders plays Maria Hill in the live-action film *The Avengers*. She is a central member of S.H.I.E.L.D. and works closely with Nick Fury, though she is skeptical of his Avengers Initiative. She is injured during[[Loki|Loki's]] attack on the Helicarrier, but is shown to trust the Avengers by the end of the film. She is also depicted as a skilled marksman and combatant, as she manages to avoid being killed by a brainwashed [[Hawkeye]] at the beginning of the film.

### Captain America: The Winter Soldier (2014)

*Maria Hill in The Winter Soldier*

Smulders reprises her role in *Captain America: The Winter Soldier*. Hill plays a more active role this time around, assisting Captain America, [[Sam Wilson|Falcon]], and [[Black Widow]] in their battle against rogue factions within the S.H.I.E.L.D. organization.

### Age of Ultron (2015)

*Maria with Hawkeye*

Cobie Smulders returns as Maria Hill in *Avengers: Age of Ultron*. In the film, she is now working for Tony Stark after the dissolution of S.H.I.E.L.D., and helps coordinate the Avengers. It is later revealed that she has secretly been reporting to Fury the entire time, and is present when Fury brings out the Helicarrier to help evacuate Sokovia. In the finale, she is shown helping run the New Avengers Compound in upstate New York.

### Infinity War (2018) and Endgame (2019)

*Maria is disintegrated by Thanos*

Cobie Smulders briefly reprises her role as Maria Hill in the post-credits scene of *Avengers: Infinity War*. After [[Thanos]]completes and activates the Infinity Gauntlet in Wakanda, Maria and Fury are seen as two of the numerous people who are vaporized in order to balance the universe's population.

In the immediate follow-up, *Avengers: Endgame*, both Maria and Fury are resurrected after the [[Hulk]] uses the Infinity Stones to bring back those who had been killed by Thanos.

### Spider-Man: Far From Home (2019)

*Soren and Talos masquerading as Hill and Fury*

Cobie Smulders once again reprises her role in *Spider-Man: Far From Home*. In the film, she and Fury work with a new hero called [[Mysterio]] to help battle a group of enemies called the Elementals . At the end of the film, it's revealed that Fury and Maria were actually the disguised Skrulls [[Talos]] and Soren, who had impersonated the S.H.I.E.L.D. agents at the behest of the real Nick Fury.

### **Television**

### Marvel's Agents of SH.I.E.L.D.

*Maria in the TV series.*

Maria Hill appears in the pilot episode of *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.*, with Cobie Smulders reprising her role. Her role is limited to that of a cameo, where she assists [[Phil Coulson|Coulson]] vet candidates for his new team. She also displays some knowledge of the darker mystery surrounding his resurrection. She is alluded to at several points later in the series, but does not actually make a physical appearance due to Smulders' commitments to other projects.

Hill eventually returns in the episode "Nothing Personal," where she returns from giving a testimony before politicians in Washington. It is also mentioned that with S.H.I.E.L.D. having been disbanded, she has now taken a job at Stark Industries. Hill makes one final appearance in the Season 2 episode "The Dirty Half Dozen," where Coulson contacts her with intel about Loki's scepter, which leads into the events of the *Age of Ultron* movie.

### Secret Invasion (2023)

*Maria Hill is killed*

Cobie Smulders reprised her role as Maria Hill, aiding Nick Fury and Talos in an attempt to stop a terrorist attack in Russia. During the chaos following the explosion, the insurgent Skrull Gravik taunts Fury by shooting Hill. She dies in Fury's arms.

### The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes

*Avengers: Earth Mightiest Heroes*

Maria Hill is featured in *The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes* voiced by Kari Wührer. She appears as a central member of S.H.I.E.L.D. and a protege of Nick Fury serving as his second-in-command and being explicitly taken for tasks. She is often being corrected over mistakes or quizzed on her knowledge of S.H.I.E.L.D. affairs. She maintains her comic self's hard-edged and aggressive nature as seen in the episode "Iron Man is Born" where she immediately tried to arrest Iron Man at gunpoint when he took a threatening stance towards Fury. After she and Nick Fury foil a break-out plot by Baron Strucker and Grim Reaper at the Vault, Maria asks Nick Fury if the three supervillain prisons (Vault, Cube, and Big House) and are like this. Nick Fury tells her that "all four are different" causing Maria to wonder what the fourth prison is. In "The Breakout" Pt. 1, Maria Hill's question about the fourth prison is answered when it turns out to be the The Raft. In "Hail, Hydra" she is the new director of S.H.I.E.L.D after Nick Fury's sudden disappearance, and tried to arrest the Avengers. She did offer them a chance to register and work for S.H.I.E.L.D but the Avengers refused. In "The Ultron Imperative" it is revealed that at least in this reality her middle name is Christina.
