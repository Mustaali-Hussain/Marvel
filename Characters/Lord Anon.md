> ###### Lord Anon
> **First appearance** The Beast In Me #1
> **Appearances** 14

> [!abstract] In brief
> Evolved red wolf and Knight of Wungadore

Lord Anon was one of the second leaders of the Knights of Wundagore and was a rival of Man-beast during their early days of the New Men. He was almost killed by Man-wolf during the Assault on Wundagore Mountain but was saved by Iron Fist and Quicksilver. He current remains as second in command of the Knights.
