> Vaipala is a young Indian woman who was to be wed to Paras Gavaskar's older brother, but when he became incapacitated - she was expected to wed Paras.

Vaipala was to marry [[Indra|Indra's]]brother. Indra was summoned back to to India after his brother fell into a bizarre coma. [[Rav Gavaskar]]tell the X-Men and his son that he is to marry Vaipala to fulfill the arranged marriage.  

 

After [[Luz]]comes back with  the team, the Children of the vault attack the Gavaskar home and only leave after Luz agrees to go with them. What had happened is Luz used her powers to impersonate each other and Vaipala was taken by the children of the Vault and Luz almost married Indra.
