## Origin

*Helping Bruce banner*

Rick Jones is an orphan who eventually became an honorary Avenger. At the character's pinnacle usage, the Marvel Universe is often seen through his eyes as the reader. He is often portrayed as the "ultimate sidekick" character, however the character's raw roots are of a more angst ridden nature. Rick Jones is a musician, a fighter, and a rebel...with the underlying potential to be a hero.

As a teenager, Rick Jones knew no fear. When he was dared to sneak onto a military testing facility, he didn't give it a second thought. What he and his friends did not know was the facility was a test center for an experimental Gamma bomb. When scientist Bruce Banner noticed Rick sneaking on, he raced out to save him seconds before the bomb was to detonate. Time only allowed for Banner to push Rick into the safety of a trench as the explosion irradiated his shocked body. Thus the incredible [[Hulk]] was born. Rick Jones swore never to leave Dr. Banner again.

The sacrifice that Banner had made caused Rick to grow up fast. He knew it was his fault and took the responsibility to assist Banner and the Hulk, knowing how dangerous it would be.

## Character Creation

Rick Jones was created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby and first appeared in The Incredible Hulk #1 (1962).

The more Genis fought, the more they both destabilized until Rick used the nega-bands to merge with Genis once again. Together, they are able to defeat Shatterax and free Marlo from Ren-Varr. Unfortunately, Marlo agreed to take merge with Death, whom became disillusioned by all the resurrections. Thanks to their mergers, Rick and Marlo had to separate after finally reuniting.

### Undone

Rick was on vacation in the Microverse when he felt his connection to Genis sever. After finally returning home, he found his apartment destroyed. According to Carol, it was ruined in a fight between Genis and a The Omen, a dark agent for a being known as The Undone. The Undone was trapped in the Negative Zone and needed the Nega Bands to escape. On her quest, she had enthralled Genis, and Rick was recruited by Carol to try and reach him emotionally.

Unfortunately, it did not work. The Undone literally clawed her way through Genis' chest, seemingly killing him. After altering the power of the Nega-Bands multiple times, Carol and her allies were finally able to defeat her. This left the Nega-Bands vulnerable. In a final act, she brought Genis back to life, which caused the bands to disintegrate. Despite appreciating being healed, Genis was crushed losing the last thing he had of Mar-Vell's. He left, telling Rick not to reach out to him.

### Venom Suspect

*Sleeper Agent*

As the new superhero columnist for the Daily Bugle, Rick Jones was in attendance for Madame Masque's trial for her role in the gang war. Unfortunately, the courtroom was attacked by Andrew Forson and his AIM troops, looking for revenge against Masque. While Rick ran from the courtroom to find help, Venom arrived bonded to a new, unknown host. This made Rick a suspected host by [[Dylan Brock]], who was separated from Venom during the Venom War.

Rick started to investigate with his boss, Robbie Robertson, and the mayor, Luke Cage, all of whom were unaccounted for during Venom appearances, until Rick is revealed as the new Sleeper Agent (unknowingly bonded to [[Sleeper]]). When Flash and Venom stage a fight to keep Flash's cover as a SCAR agent, other agents came to Flash's defense. Sleeper took this chance to pilot Rick to SCAR's now understaffed holding facility to save him and Toxin. Unfortunately, the holding facility had a secret staff member: Doctor Octopus.

Doc Ock was able to remove the piece of Sleeper from Rick and prepared to dissect him. Thankfully, Venom came to his rescue thanks to a psychic distress call from Sleeper. Rick was re-bonded to the piece of Sleeper and helped Venom release the captive symbiotes. Rick agreed to cooperate with Sleeper if he stopped wiping his memories and remote controlling him. With Flash Thompson's help, they all managed to escape.

Because Toxin desperately needed to bond to a human, Sleeper disconnected from Rick and convinced him to help Toxin.

## Powers

**Skilled Human: **Rick Jones is an athletic man that previously had no superhuman powers.

- **Superhero Training: **He has received training in combat gymnastics by Captain America and developed a strong courageous spirit.
- **Musician: **He is also a skilled self-taught folk and rock n' roll singer and guitarist.
- **Latent Psychic: **During the Kree-Skrull war, Rick demonstrated vast psionic powers which the Supreme Intelligence claimed were latent in all humans.

*Rick with Nega Bands*

**Temporary Superhuman: **As a consistent friend of the superhero community, Rick would often find himself in possession of temporary superpowers

- **Mar-Vell Bond: **Rick would receive further training by the hero Mar-Vell as they would be bonded symbiotically. He temporarily had one of the Nega-bands, which provided him with the same powers as Mar-Vell, such as flight, energy blasts, and simple matter manipulation. He was also, for some time, able to shift spacial/temporal positions with Mar-Vell and later Genis-Vell.
- **Destiny Force: **Rick would learn how to further his inherent heroism due to his experiences with Captain Marvel. However, this display of power was retconned as the Destiny Force acting through Rick, and not his own innate power.
- **Beyonder Gift: **He was temporarily given superhuman powers by the Beyonder, including strength, flight, durability, and energy blasts.
- **Incredible Rick: **Also when Joe Fixit was around, Rick Jones fell into the mineral bath that was intended to merge the Hulk and Bruce Banner causing Rick Jones to gain Hulk-like powers. During the day Rick Jones was an average human, but at night The Incredible Rick manifested. His appearance was like the green Hulk's: gamma-powered behemoth of great strength and limited intellect but with mullet.
- **Symbiote Host: **He was bonded to a fragment of the Sleeper symbiote, which increased his strength, speed, agility, and endurance, as well as abilities unique to Sleeper, specifically secreting a variety of chemicals and pheromones to induce euphoria, sleep, and other states of mind.

**Gamma Mutate: **It was believed that Rick Jones was mutated with gamma radiation by MODOK leading to a number of gamma mutations over the years. After Genis-Vell's resurrection, Genis hypothesizes that Rick didn't clear the gamma bomb that created the Hulk, and that exposure has led to his constant mutations.

*Rick as A-Bomb*

- **A-Bomb: **Rick Jones was turned into a creature resembling the Abomination, calling himself "A-Bomb." Through his transformation, he gains protective plates of armor, vast superhuman strength and durability ( as Rick would put it, he is in the strongest there is club), and the ability to track both Hulk and Red Hulk (by zeroing in on their gamma signature). He also has a camouflage ability, being able to turn nearly invisible to match his environment.
- **Whisperer: **After he was cured of his A-Bomb mutation, a lingering side effect of his gamma mutation was increased intelligence. His mind was a sponge, an he used this new ability to study computers and communications, becoming a skilled hacker.
- **Abomination Hybrid: **When combined with the corpse of Abomination, he had Hulk-like strength, speed, and endurance. He also secreted an acid capable of dismembering the Hulk.
- **Immortal: **As a gamma mutate, Rick Jones is immortal. When he dies, he goes to the Below Place and is capable of returning through the Green Door. As he recovered from his separation from Abomination's corpse, Rick's body developed a new ability to emanate gamma radiation, allowing him to fly and project energy blasts.
