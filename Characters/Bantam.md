## Brief History

Bantam acted as a doorman for Fitzroy. Basically, Bantam found the teleportation nodes for Fitzroy and Fitzroy would drain life force to open them up. During the Younghunt, Bantam was captured by X-Force and used as a way to backtrack Fitzroy's path. He was also used as a decoy during teleportation in case X-Force was heading into a trap.
