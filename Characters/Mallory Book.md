## Character History

Mallory is a smart, determined, and beautiful lawyer who has worked as part of Goodman, Lieber, Kurtzberg, and Holliway. She is a former Ms. Utah and graduate of Brigham Young University's Law School with magna cum laude honors. During law school she also distinguished herself as the senior editor of the BYU Law Review.

During her professional career she had one of the longest unbroken records of courtroom victories at GLK&H and in the practice of law. Many have been so impressed by her success they have taken to calling her "The Face who's never lost a case."

Mallory would later form her own firm, where her biggest mistake and best choice she'd ever make would be hiring [[She-Hulk]], this would give her firm plenty of attention from the superhero community but with that came less normal & easy cases and more bushiness expenses when her office is turned into a fight arena for heroes & villains.

She hired [[Awesome Andy]], a humanoid android who was a kind and gentle soul in comparison to Mallory's temper. Despite their differences in personality and species, the two hit it off, forming a romantic work place relationship.

After a time when Jen wasn't in the right state of mind, Mallory had to continue her business without the help of She-Hulk. However when Jen was back looking for work as a lawyer, Mallory without hesitation hired her again, under the condition no superhero clients. That didn't work out, with Jen's friends in constant need of a lawyer but after a sit down with Andy and She-Hulk, Mallory finally agreed to allow hero clients, showing that despite her cold personality, she has a heart.
