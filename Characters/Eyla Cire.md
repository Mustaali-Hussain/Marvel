> Eyla Cire is a woman from the future who meets Cable.

Eyla Cire lives in the future, in Niers, a city in Harmony, where Cable found himself teleported to. 

She and all of Niers' residents carry sabretooth tigers with them. 

She and her family offers to host Cable, a wanderer in her city. 

 

Her father is Manuel Cire, and her brother Ezra Cire.
