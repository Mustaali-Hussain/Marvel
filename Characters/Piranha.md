> Piranha is a fish that was mutated into humanoid form.

## History 

Years ago, the superheroes [[Spider-Man]] and the [[Namor|Sub-Mariner]]battled the Aquanoids.  This race of "men-fish" was the result of genetic tinkering done to fish by the evil [[Dr. Dorcas|Doctor Lemuel Dorcas]] at his undersea base.  When, in the course of the battle, the base was destroyed, the surrounding waters were contaminated with radiation from its equipment.  Caught in this mutagenic cloud was a piranha.  Becoming humanoid with a great intellect, he was also "filled with hate" for the Sub-Mariner. 

Using his telepathic control over other fish, Piranha ordered them to enter the cloud and thus created his own army of "men-fish."  However, he was not quite up to slaying the Sub-Mariner.  During their battle he was wounded, his blood seeping into the sea and attracting his own non-mutated, carnivorous piranhas, who devoured him and forced him to flee.   

## Creation

Piranha was created by Marv Wolfman and George Tuska in 1974 and first appeared in Sub-Mariner # 71.

## Powers & Abilities

Piranha was mutated into humanoid form and possesses superhuman strength, durability and endurance. Piranha has razor sharp teeth and claws that emit a sleeping venom. Piranha is a submariner with enhanced speed while swimming.
