> Christina Xu is a young woman who lives with her parents at F.E.A.S.T., she took in Spider-Boy after he had saved her from Mr. Negative's Inner Demons.

## Origin

Christina and her family live at a F.E.A.S.T. homeless shelter. This is after [[Mister Negative]] was exposed as the villain using the non-profit as a safe house and as such it was once again a place for truly helping people.

One day, Christina found a safety deposit key in a crawl space and planned on using it to use it open the box and get the richies, but The Inner Demons learned about it and tracked Christina down.

It look bad for Chiristina but [[Spider-Boy (Briggs)|Spider-Boy]] came along and saved her by beating up the Inner Demons, destroying the key, giving them no reason to pursue Christina anymore. Christina thanked Spider-Boy and in return Spider-Boy revealed to her his secret identity of Bailey Briggs.

Learning Bailey had no where to go, Christina brought him back to FEAST where he was excepted by the Xus and the others in the shelter.

Christina has been one of Briggs one true friends as he tried to get his life back together. She is one of the few non-super power people to know Spider-Boy alternate identity and that infact that he has a mother, but doesn't know why he doesn't see her.

## Creation

Christina was created by Dan Slott and Humberto Ramos, and first appeared in Edge of Spider-Verse #3 - Nobody Knows Who You Are. She then became a reoccurring character in Spider-Boy.

## Major Story Arcs

### Edge of the Spider-Verse

Christina meets and befriends Spider-Boy after he saves her life.

### Web-less Wonder

Over the first few months of being friends with Bailey Briggs he has saved Christina from [[Hellifino]], Balloon Man and Boy-Spider, the last one put a strain in Spider-Boy and Christina's relationship.

The night Boy-Spider was running a rampage on the city, Briggs over hears Christina tell Spider-Man that Briggs has been acting weird. This hurts Briggs feeling an he runs away from his Spider family and his only friend, Christina.

### Fun and Games

Christina feels bad for how she acted towards Briggs, but niether her nor Spider-Man can find him. It isn't until Spider-Boy returns to save Christina from [[Hellifino]] that the pair become friends again.

But their reunion is cut short when Hellifino captures the pair and brings them back to Stillwell Farm where [[Madame Monstrosity]] splices Christina DNA with a pigeon.

Pigeon Christina is then sent to work in the fields with the other Humanimals. There she learns she can communicate with pigeons and is able to get a note to Spider-Man.

After Spider-man, Thor, Squirrel Girl, Captain America and The Toy Solider take down Madame Monstrosity's operation, Christina volunteers to be the first one to be changed back to human.

### Lost Boy

Christina is seen a vision that Spider-Man has of the night he scared Spider-Boy away due to him and Bailey's friends mistrust.

### Massive Course Correction

Christina is there when [[Tabitha Briggs]], Bailey mother, returns to FEAST, now having memories that Bailey is her son. Christina stands back as Tabitha slaps Spider-Man for endangering her son, and she even comments that Spider-Man could avoided the slap, but realizes he allowed her to hit him as punishment for endangering Bailey. 

Later she is seen helping Tabitha mover her stuff into the office above FEAST, and although Christina tries to convince Tabitha to allow Bailey to be Spider-Boy, she is unsuccessful and it isn't until Bailey saves a school bus full of students that Tabitha gives her blessing.
