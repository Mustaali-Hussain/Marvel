> ###### Waxworks
> **First appearance** Sid's Story #4
> **Appearances** 67

> [!abstract] In brief
> A member of the other-dimensional bounty hunters called Technet.

Waxworks was a minor member of the group Technet, led by [[Gatecrasher]]. In the later Excalibur issues, Technet is defeated and reforms into a hero group called the N-Men, taught by Night Crawler. Waxworks never talks and it is unknown how he/she/it communicates.

**Romance**In Excalibur #45, Waxworks is seen with [[Ringtoss]], possibly romantically.

### Powers

Waxworks has the ability to remove all the rigidity in someone's body by touch, making them floppy like rubber.
