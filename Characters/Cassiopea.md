> ###### Cassiopea
> **First appearance** Bi Me That; Ulysses, Part 6: Heir Apparent #412

> **Also known as** Cassiopea, Cassie

> [!abstract] In brief
> Cassiopea is a member of the Pantheon and has the ability to absorb certain types of energy and re-channel it in forms of concussive blasts.

Cassiopea grew up as a Pantheon member with her family. Her father Perseus was a longtime Pantheon member until he left and became an innkeeper near Loch Ness.

As a teen she was very much a pacifist and did not like all the fighting and violence. Her views would change once she got older and more mature.

She joined the [[Hulk]]and other members of the Pantheon during a mission to find [[Atalanta]] who was abducted by [[Trauma]] and [[Lord Armageddon]].

She has been a valuable member ever since and adored for her sense of humor and beauty. She is particularly close to Pantheon member named [[Hector]]. She tends to flirt with him despite his interest in men.

## Powers & Abilities

Cassiopea can absorb certain types of energy and re-channel it in forms of concussive blasts. She is skilled in hand-to-hand combat and has a type of healing factor that allows her to recover from injuries much faster than a regular human being.
