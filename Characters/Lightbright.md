> Lightbright is a Somalian mutant who has been a heroine, a mercenary, and a terrorist in her few appearances.

## Origin

Born and raised in Somalia, the mutant who would be known as Lightbright became involved with the Bio-Genes, a faction of Somali mutant rebels led by the emotion-manipulating Leecher and dedicated to purging all United States influence from their nation and seizing control from the government

## Creation

Lightbright was created by Gregory Wright and Steven Butler in 1993 and first appeared in Silver Sable & The Wild Pack # 16.

## Major Story Arcs

### Wild Pack

The Bio-Genes attacked a U.S. Marine compound 10 miles outside of Mogadishu where they were confronted by the Wild Pack, a professional soldier unit led by Silver Sable (under the auspices of Silver Sable International). The Wild Pack was hired by the U.S. Government to ensure that food and medicine made it to their destinations safely. The battle was interrupted by Baron Wolfgang von Strucker and his HYDRA forces, who sought to conquer the nation in the name of Somalia's southern war lords. The Bio-Genes and the Wild Pack joined forces to oppose Strucker and, during the course of the battle, Lightbright's life was saved by Battlestar, a member of the Wild Pack.

Upon the Wild Pack's return to the U.S., Battlestar invited Lightbright to join him at the Symkarian embassy in New York City. In New York, she began a romantic relationship with Battlestar and joined the Intruders, an elite shoot off of the Wild Pack. With the Intruders, she travelled to Bosnia to oppose Genesis Coalition members who were posing as HYDRA agents. Lightbright served with the Intruders until Silver Sable decided to return her base of operations to Symkaria.

### Civil War

After the passage of the Superhuman Registration Act (SHRA), Lightbright was one of the many superhumans to oppose the registration. Lightbright was defeated by Iron Man and Spider-Man alongside several other superhumans when they were cornered in Manhattan, and she was arrested and imprisoned in the Negative Zone Alpha Prison until escaping in a mass breakout orchestrated by Captain America's Secret Avengers movement. Lightbright battled in the final battle of the Superhuman Civil War but eventually surrendered on the orders of Captain America.

### Venom War

Lightbright eventually rejoins Silver Sable's employ as a member of Wild Pack. They are hired by Alchemax CEO, Liz Allan, to retrieve a special package from the The Life Foundation during an outbreak of zombiotes, mindless symbiotes that multiply and spread like an infection. This was seen as the perfect time to test the new Lethal Protectors Initiative upgrades, provided by Alchemax. Each of the Wild Pack members would be bonded to a symbiote and outfitted with a sonic emitter to keep it in check. Lightbright was bonded to Scream.

While they were attacked by zombiotes, the large container they were moving broke open. Inside, was a clone of Shriek, whose sonic scream was considered useful enough to clone. With their new ace in the hole, the team commandeered Dominic Fortune's steamboat ferry so that they could rig Shriek to an Alchemax sonic emitter out on the water, containing the zombiote horde to Manhattan Island. Despite a betrayal from Strickland, which cost him his life, the Wild Pack were successful and made their exit, seemingly still bonded to their symbiotes.

## Powers

Lightbright possesses a photokinetic ability, she can release luminous light and intense heat from her body. She can utilize this light and heat energy in a variety of different ways such as projection of a powerful energy blast and flight. Lightbright can also emit a calming glow that fills all of those who it touches with euphoric bliss. It is unknown whether she can manipulate light and heat energies that she has not created.
