> ###### Lord Molyneux
> **First appearance** The Origins of the Species #512

> [!abstract] In brief
> Lord Molyneux was a mutant-hating man who reigned the Hellfire Club in the early 1900s. He built the first 'Sentinel' to kill mutants.

### The First Sentinel

Molyneux's original design was a personally piloted Sentinel which was more than thirty feet tall and had a mounted machine gun, claws and dense body structure.
