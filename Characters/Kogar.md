Kogar was created by Doug Moench and Jim Craig in 1978 and first appeared in Master of Kung Fu # 63. 

## Story Arcs

- 

### Civil War

Kogar recently encountered the Heroes for Hire during the Civil War saga when he tries to get a new identity, passport, drivers license and plane ticket to leave the country from Vienna. Kogar along with other criminals that oppose the Registration Act included: Grindhouse, Shockwave, Goldbug, Equinox, Bloodshed, Ghost Maker and Shadow Slasher. They would clash with the Heroes for Hire and eventually be defeated.
