## Origin

Professor Power is a super-villain who blames Charles Xavier, Spider-Man, and the original X-Men for the death of his son Aaron, he has sought revenge on Spider-Man and the X-Men (Beast and Xavier particularly) as well as fighting Iron Man. He wears a high-tech armored suit.

## Creation

Professor Power was created by J.M. DeMatteis and Herb Trimpe and first appeared in Marvel Team-Up Vol.1 issue 117 (1982).

## Major Story Arcs

### Versus Noman

Professor Power has recently been seen, seemingly alive and well, working with the Secret Empire to conduct an experiment in crowd mentality in students at a New York High school. His scheme was discovered by Rikki Barnes aka [[Rikki Barnes|Bucky/Nomad]] but she was captured by Professor Power's minion[[Mad-Dog|Mad Dog]] and brainwashed into joining the plot. However one of the students incited a riot and Professor Power was prepared to stop the experiment, however his Secret Empire bosses determined it to be a success. Professor Power destroyed the lab and ran before Nomad could capture him.

Professor Power was captured by the Secret Empire just outside New York. Placed in a cell he is trying to find a way back into 'Number One's' good graces.
