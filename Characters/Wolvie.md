## Origin

Wolvie was created by Mojo to become a part of the X-Babies, a planned tv show in the Mojoverse. Wolvie and his friends however did not feel like doing this and with the help of Rita Wayword the escaped the Mojoverse. Eventually the team of X-Babies returned to their own reality, but they have returned on a number of ocassions. Wolvie could be a real hothead sometimes, sometimes resulting in fights with his teammembers. His teammate Shadowkitty was very fond of him.
