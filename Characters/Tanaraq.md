> Tanaraq is one of the Great Beasts of the North. By making a deal with Tanaraq you gain the ability to transform into Sasquatch.

## Exiles

Tanaraq was briefly the self-appointed leader over of the Exiles when he had became the dominating personality in the Sasquatch body. This was short-lived as the Exiles figured out a way to get rid of him in the next issue.

The Exiles are group of time-traveling characters that are doomed to jump across multiple parallel realities. In each one they must resolve a certain issue before they can move on to the next reality.
