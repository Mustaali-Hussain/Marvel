### History 

Bryce was the Frost family Chauffeur for years, so trusted was he that Winston had him transport Emma from school to home every day in his rolls Royce. He 

 was very fond of Emma and was often concerned about how her day was and her many instances of headaches. Despite this however when he was ordered by Winston to fake a break down so that Emma and Ian could be caught together he did so out of fear of losing his job. 

 

After this he continued to transport Emma to and from school until she graduated and later left the frost family. He remained with the family even after her absence. 

 

It is unknown if he is still alive.
