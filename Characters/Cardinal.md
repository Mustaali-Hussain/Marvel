Cardinal is a genetically-engineered mutant pacifist in the distant future, cloned in a laboratory from [[Nightcrawler]] and [[Rachel Summers|Prestige's]] DNA.

## Powers and Abilities

Due to being a genetic clone of Nightcrawler and Prestige, Cardinal seemingly possesses a spliced version of their power sets. 

- **Teleportation: **Similarly to Nightcrawler, He possesses the ability to teleport himself, the clothes he is wearing, and within limits a certain amount of additional mass which is in contact with him.
- **Telekinesis: **He can move, lift, and levitate objects and matter with the energy of his thoughts. 
- **Telepathy: **He can also read minds, and communicate telepathically with other sentient beings.
