> Patrick Carney is an award-winning baseball player of the 1950s. He then wanted to take his athletic ability and use it for good. He wears a belt that gives him many powers and abilities.

## History

In the 1950's Patrick was an all-star baseball player. After he had finished his ball playing he decided to earn an engineering degree. After he acquired the degree he was to work at a top secret government building called Deep Freeze. That's where they were holding [[Captain America]] and [[Bucky Barnes|Bucky]] after they had became insane. When he found this out he was very shocked. He made an argument that they were needed now. Of course his opinion was not important. So when he was driving home from work he met a girl [[Cassandra Locke]]. She was a time traveler. She had collected information that the Skrulls were trying to hurt the Earth in her era. The girl was dying so she gave him all the information about her era and the plan of the Skrulls. She also left him a belt which is how she teleported. He couldn't figure out how to use the belt, but he used it to boost his strength to superhuman levels. So the Yankee Clipper was created as his hero identity. Patrick became one of America's champions. Doing good for all who needed help. Later, he joined the team First Line. His brother just happened to also be on the team. His brother was [[Mister Justice]]. Later, Patrick met Locke again. When the two belts they had met it enabled Patrick's teleport ability. So he teleported 20 years into the future. That's when Patrick went to find his brother and the rest of First Line. One of the members, [[Pixie]], helped Patrick get used to the time with the help of her family the Eternals. A full year later Patrick teleported again. He appeared right around when the Fantastic Four was born. Later he learned Pixie had died defending against the Skrulls. So he decided it was time to hang up the costume and entered retirement.

## Powers & Abilities

Yankee Clipper has agility and was very athletic. He has a high-tech belt that grants him super strength, the ability to teleport through time travel, and it could also create force fields around himself.
