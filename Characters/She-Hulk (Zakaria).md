> The She-Hulk of Earth-6160. A Pacific Islander whose island was irradiated by the Ultimate Universe's gamma explosion that created The Hulk.

## Creation

Lejori Joena Zakaria was created by Deniz Camp and Juan Frigeri, and first appeared in The Ultimates (2024) #3.

Camp took inspiration for what happened to Lejori's Pacific Island community from the testing of nuclear weapons by the United States on Bikini Atoll, one of the Marshall Islands.

## Origin

*Lejori is exposed to gamma radiation*

As a young girl Lejori, along with her family and every living organism on her home island, were exposed to dangerous levels of gamma radiation following the detonation of the 22.8 megaton Banner-Ulam Gamma Bomb. While humans by the hundred mutated and died from radiation posioning, Lejori's body was able to absorb the gamma in a way that retained her sanity, intelligence and health. She acts as a guardian for those few that survived the blast and its horrific fallout.
