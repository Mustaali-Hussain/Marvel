> ###### Sledge
> **First appearance** I Know You Are but What am I? #60
> **Appearances** 45

> [!abstract] In brief
> A member of he deviants race, sledge is appears as an out of proportion human on crutches. He was allied with X force and Ulysses Dragonblood against the Damocles Foundation.

He is a genius level inventor and has invented means to travel to alternate realities as well as a harness based forcefield generator.

 The forcefield is his main source of in combat power as it can be reshaped into a humanoid shaped construct around himself and he can than use it as an extension of himself to fight in hand to hand combat. With this technology he defeated a gatherer one on one and they are beings created directly by the celestials (implying a large amount of power) possessing flight, superhuman strength, energy blasts.
