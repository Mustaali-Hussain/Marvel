> ###### Segismund Joshua
> **First appearance** Everything Hits the Fan #13
> **Appearances** 31
> **Also known as** Dr. Joshua

Segismund Joshua is a brilliant geneticist who has at times been employed by Gideon, Operation Zero Tolerance and the Damocles Foundation. He is responsible among other things for the Ekatarina Gryaznova being converted to a Prime Sentinel, the abilities of Sunspot pushed to their limits and granting powers to Reignfire.
