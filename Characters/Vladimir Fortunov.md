> [!abstract] In brief
> Vladimir was the ruler of Latveria from World War II until killed and deposed by Dr. Doom

He was responsible for persecuting Cynthia and [[Werner von Doom]] as well as the Zefiro Gypsy minority in general. His sons Rudolfo and Zorba have claimed the throne of Latveria, as did his grandson Dimitri Fortunov.
