## Origin

Tucker Ford is a socially awkward young man with mother issues who wants nothing more than to be a hero.  His unique abilities enabled him to created the "Biogeneral" persona and join Colonel Fury.

## Powers

Tucker appears to be a mechanical/engineering genius who invented the suit of armor he wears as Biogeneral.  The armor is composed of nanites that respond to his thoughts and have a variety of abilities including flight, strength augmentation, and protection from the environment.
