## White King

Ned Buckman is a member of Hellfire Club. He is their White King who is romantically affiliated with Paris Seville. He was  hypnotized by Emma Frost to kill all members of the first Hellfire Club  through gunshots including Paris. Ned was killed by Sebastian Shaw by a neck snap. He only appeared in flashbacks.
