> ###### Arsenal Alpha
> **First appearance** Turf War, Part 1: When Worlds Collide #84
> **Appearances** 14

> [!abstract] In brief
> A robot created by Howard Stark during World War II to prevent the Axis powers from winning the war. Arsenal Alpha was stored away following the end of the war and reawakened years later.

Arsenal Alpha was accidentally activated when Iron Man received a transmission from Abe Zimmer. The Avengers battled against Arsenal Alpha and eventually had the robot dismantled.
