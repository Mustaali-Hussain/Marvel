## Original

The original Titania was a female wrestler who worked as leader of the Grapplers, a quartet of mercenaries. Titania utilized an energy rod, which she used to project long-range blasts. During her match with [[Thundra]], she stabbed the woman with a paralysis solution, winning the match by default. Alongside [[Poundcakes]], Titania battled Giant-Man while participating in a mission for Roxxon Oil. Later, Titania was killed by the [[Scourge]].

## Major Story Arcs

### Dark Reign

Currently the Hood has resurrected some of the victims killed by the Scourge of the Underworld including Titania. The Hood has augmented the abilities of some of these resurrected criminals and Titania was dubbed Lascivious when given her new powers. The Hood told his new army that the Scourge was secretly the Punisher and he has ordered them to eliminate [[Punisher|Frank Castle]]. The spell that brought them back to life only lasts thirty days and only the Hood can extend their lives.

Together with Letha,Lascivious hatched a plan to get Punisher's attention. She went to a war veterans meeting and used her powers to make one of then fall in love with her, then she ordered the veterans to kill each other, she commented how she wanted to keep the survivor as a pet, but Letha killed him, since their power over him would fade eventualy. Later, the duo killed the oweners of a flower boutique and and Letha posed as the owner. Letha then tried to use her powers on the Punisher to make him commit suicide, but when they proved to be ineffective. Lascivious appeared and attacked, knocking down the Punisher and used her powers to make Castle fall in love with then, but when their powers proved to be ineffective, they tried to kill him with their physical strength, but Lascivious was quickly beaten when Punisher put a grenade on her mouth.

As Letha was about to be killed, [[Spider-Man]] appeared and saved her. Letha used her powers to make Spider-Man try to kill the Punisher as the duo escaped by car. Letha and Lascivious, certain that Spider-Man will kill Punisher, doesn't realize the vigilante had made his way into their escape car. Punisher points his gun at the women, but Spider-Man once again saves them. In a final attempt, Punisher shoots a giant hammer from a sign on a skyscraper. It flies down and smashes Letha's car, knocking her unconscious and allowing Spider-Man to slip back to his senses. Lascivious,as she tried to wake Letha, is attacked by the Punisher. Spider-Man stops him from killing the women, and while they argue, Lascivious uses her powers to make Spider-Man fall in love with Punisher. She grabs Letha and finds [[Bird-Man]], who flies them to safety.

Later, Letha and Lascivious once again attacked Punisher, this time using Mirage's illusions to disguise them as the Avengers. With the Deadly Dozen, the women were defeated by Punisher. However, [[Death Adder]] and Basilisk were able to defeat Punisher, the surviving members of the Deadly Dozen were able to stay alive.

### A Council Of Masters

[[Max Fury]], the leader of the Shadow Council has assembled a new Masters of Evil in the sovereign nation of Bagalia where Lascivious is presently a member. Lascivious is present when Max Fury and the Masters of Evil capture [[John Steele]] after he attempts to escape Bagalia with the Serpent Crown and the Crown of Thorns.
