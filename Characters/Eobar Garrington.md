## Origin

Eobar Garrington held the mantle of Black Knight during King Richard's Crusades. Following the machinations of the [[Enchantress]] centuries later, Garrington's ancestor [[Black Knight|Dane Whitman]] was turned to stone and in trying to help him, [[Doctor Strange]] sent his conscious into the past where it possessed the body of Eobar Garrington.

## Creation

Eobar Garrington was created by Steve Englehart and Sal Buscema and first appeared in *The Defenders* #11.

## Major Story Arcs

### Fall of Avalon

With the help of [[Doctor Druid]], the Avengers travelled back in time themselves and met the Dane Whitman-possessed Garrington. Garrington died when closing a portal to a dark realm and Whitman's consciousness returned to the future where he was himself once more.
