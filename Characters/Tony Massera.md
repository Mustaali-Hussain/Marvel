> [!abstract] In brief
> Son of Micky Massera, one mobster killed by the Punisher.

Son of the gangster Mickey Massera. Medecine student, he is manipulated by his uncle Charlie Siciliano who wants him to avenge the death of his father, killed by the [[Punisher]]. He follows the [[Punisher]] but gets detected and finally has to confess that he despises bosses and mob murderers. After the [[Punisher]] gets Alaric adress, he uses Tony as his driver to get there and knocks him out when they arrive.

In the end, the [[Punisher]] gives him the choice of embracing revenge and become like his father or go on with his life. Tony cannot pull the trigger.
