> Originally created for a mature readers imprint called Epic before being moved to Marvel proper, Terror Inc. is an undead detective with the power to assimilate body parts, along with core memories, skills, and superpowers associated with that body part.

## Origin

*Shreck vs the Beast*

Centuries ago on Earth-88194 (Also known as *Shadowline*) the man who was to become Terror Inc. was a brave knight during the middle-ages. He had a wife he loved and was respected by his people but also feared. However, during one of his crusades, this man killed a gruesome demon. By slaying this demon, he was cursed. He soon found out his flesh began to rot, and spikes grew out of his face similar to ones on the demon. His wife and loved ones became terrified of him. For this, he took the name Schreck (German for "fright"). He left his family to wander the earth. It was at this time that he found out that he could assimilate body-parts into to his own body if the need arose. He would therefore tear off body-parts of enemies or deceased persons.

## Creation

Schreck was created by D. G. Chichester, Margaret Clark, and Klaus Janson and first appeared in St. George issue #2 (1988) from Marvel's EPIC line, part of the Shadowline Saga.

## Character Evolution

As a part of the Shadowline Saga, Schreck belonged to a world without superheroes, despite his resemblance to the supernatural detectives that often rub elbows with the capes and costumes bunch. According to Dan Chester, creator of the Shadowline Saga, Marvel requested use of Schreck to be used as a vehicle to re-introduce horror characters from the 1970s.

Although that plan never panned out, Terror still made his Marvel debut in Daredevil (1964) #305. This led into a spinoff of Terror touring the Marvel universe as a soldier of fortune and private investigator. Unfortunately, that series would end 2 issues shy of its original planned run. Terror would be put on the back burner until a variant version appeared in a Marvel Team-Up arc.

Two years after the MTU appearance, he would receive a Marvel MAX miniseries, which also received a sequel, that were not canonical to the 616 Universe. Details of his origin were changed and elaborated on, and by the time Terror showed up as a member of Deadpool's Mercs for Money, some of those details had been made canon in the Marvel Universe proper.

## Major Story Arcs

### Shadowline

*Schreck attacked by piranaha*

Before his appearance grew too poor, Schreck became the squire to a man calling himself [[Doctor Zero|Draghignazzo]]. Draghignazzo was no mere mortal. He was able to heal, but fatal wounds took centuries of rest. During one of these hibernations, Schreck struck out on his own, becoming a black knight and fighting alongside a brave woman, [[Talita]], who he fell in love with. When she was slain on the battlefield, Schreck collected her left hand and had it mystically encased in armor so that it would never rot while attached to him.

Fast forward to the 1980s, Schreck is still alive and working as an enforcer for the Ravenscore crime family. He has an on-going feud with the heroic knight of the Order of St. George, [[Michael Devlin]]. He tracked him to Nicaragua, where he tried to crucify Devlin. Devlin got free and tossed Schreck into a piranha infested lake.

His skeleton was found by fisherman, who hoped to grab his gold fillings, but the curse kept him alive, even as a skeleton. He was able to rebuild his body from the pieces of the fisherman.

### Fear is His Business

*Meeting with Daredevil*

Terror’s first gig on Earth-616 was to give an upstart Las Vegas crime family an edge in the division of [[Kingpin]]’s crime ring. Terror would sneak around and spy on the various criminals negotiating their piece. He gets some info on a possible Hand attack on the Circus-Circus Hotel and Casino. He passes it on to [[Daredevil]], in exchange for his partnership if Terror ever decides to work in New York among the superheroes.

With that, Terror was officially open for business in the Marvel universe. People in his employ include:

- [[Alexis Primo]] - his personal assistant
- Brunhilda - his personal tailor
- Rekrab - his consultant on all things supernatural
- Boneyard - his personal source of specialty black market body parts

*Terror, Wolverine, and Jubilee*

Terror took on a number of cases that caused him to team up and clash with Marvel superheroes:

- He teamed up with [[Wolverine]] and [[Jubilee]] to take on eco-terrorists
- He competed with[[Punisher|The Punisher]] over who gets to kill [[Piranha Jones]]
- He was hired to find a mystical item for Priapus, who was trying to increase his power, but Terror ultimately joined [[Silver Sable]] and [[Luke Cage]] in defeating him
- Terror also seeks out [[Ghost Rider (Ketch)|Ghost Rider]] to experience the Penance Stare and ensure his reunion with his dead soulmate in the afterlife.

### Mercs for Money

At the height of his popularity, [[Deadpool]] hired a number of mercenaries, including Terror, to dress in his uniform to help him with the new influx of missions. He called them the Mercs for Money. When he was first considering it, he was already on another case retrieving a prized possession from an occultist. That occultist shot Terror with a bazooka, knocking off his armored hand, which inadvertently cost Terror the memories of his wife, the best thing that ever happened to him.

Deadpool funneled most of the revenue they brought in to the Avengers Unity Squad, which he was funding for [[Captain America]]. Like his fellow teammates, this upset Terror that he wasn’t being paid properly and that many of their missions were non-paying hero gigs. Despite his public outrage, he did personally answer one letter from an abused child to beat up his dad. After his teammate, [[Madcap]], drove Deadpool fans crazy in an attempt to ruin Deadpool’s reputation, Deadpool dropped the requirement to wear his uniform. They started working more as a team rather than going out on individual missions.

### Quitting the Mercs for Money

*Mercs for Morale*

Team morale in the Merc for Money was at an all time low when the [[Celestial Destructor]] attacked. The Mercs were assigned to crowd control, but when the Avengers were successful in ending the threat, Deadpool did not let them tag along to the victory party at Stark Tower. They considered taking the team from Deadpool and going independent.

With no way of funding a separate endeavor, the team decides to rob a bank, specifically the one Deadpool uses for his finances so that they would only be taking what is owed to them. Deadpool was ahead of them thanks to the precognitive abilities of [[Profiler|The Profiler]], a nuhuman who caused a second Civil War among the Avengers.

In light of Deadpool thwarting their bank robbery, Terror said he was quitting but ended up staying on for one more mission: rescue[[Negasonic Teenage Warhead]]. When Deadpool takes the wrong kind of risks to save her, Terror and the rest of the team finally walk away for good, leaving Deadpool high and dry.

## Powers and Abilities

**Matter Absorption: **Terror Inc. has the ability to replace body parts with parts or organs taken from others, a process which he must continuously do since he is constantly decaying. Any body part he assimilates will immediately start to decay.

*Terror with a fresh arm*

- **Memory and Power Absorption: **He also gains memories and eventual skills or super-powers from any body part he assimilates. It is not necessary that the body parts he assimilates be human, he could add wings or a tail to his body if he wanted. Terror has even replaced his head with another persons to gain their knowledge and memories.
- **Spikes: **The spikes on his cheeks are part of the curse of the demon he slayed. If removed they will grow back.

**Immortality: **Since his body is dead he does not require to eat, sleep or breathe and doesn't feel pain. He can continue to function even if he is reduced to a skeletal state, or to just his head. He can even survive without a body at all. In his MAX series he was dismembered and dunked in acid but survived as a black ooze that was able to inhabit other bodies.

**Expert Talents: **Since being alive several centuries, Terror has acquired all sorts of skills and is an excellent marksman and detective. He is also proficient with most conventional weapons.

### Ultimate Terror

Terror, named Terry Schreck, is a New York cop who is on the same trail of a superhuman drug ring that the All-New Ultimates are on. While busting it up, he came face-to-face with [[Styx]], who left Terry deformed by his corrosive touch. He died at the hospital but then mysteriously awoke and wandered into the city as the undead. The Ultimates found him wandering around the sewers and took him to his old partner’s home.

There, he started training, getting used to his new status quo. When his cop buddies were suspended for aggression towards the superhuman gangs, he rallied them together as Terror, Inc, a very short-lived vigilante squad.

### Earth-2099

Terror's immortality allows him to make it to the year 2099, the version of 2099 that was rebooted in the early 2020s. In this 2099, he pretends to run a Kill Room to bring in undesirable tourists looking to kill for money and then kill them to stockpile on body parts. His stockpile also includes preserved items from present day heroes, including the heart of Beta Ray Bill which allows him to wield Stormbreaker. Spider-Man mistakes him for genuinely running a kill room but doesn't care for the truth much either.
