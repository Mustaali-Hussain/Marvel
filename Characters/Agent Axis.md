> [!abstract] In brief
> Enemy of the Invaders

Agent Axis was one of the Invaders’ main foes. In a freak accident; three top agents from Japan, Germany, and Italy were combined into a single person. Agent Axis had the strength of three men and the confused mind of three people from different cultures and different languages. He was, however, committed to the destruction of the Invaders.
