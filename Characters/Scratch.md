## Creation

Scratch was created by Warren Ellis and Carlos Pacheco; he first appeared in 1996's *Excalibur* #96.

## Powers & Abilities

Scratch is a mutant with small energized slits in his palms that build up bio-energy and can discharge it as explosive blasts of electricity, or anti-gravitons for a form of flight.
