## History

Framed for murder and sent to the electric chair. When he woke up, he was given a new identity, and placed under the training of Chiun, Master of Sinanju, greatest of all martial arts. He became an assassin for CURE, a super-secret government agency, and went on to earn the title of the legendary Destroyer of Sinanju.
