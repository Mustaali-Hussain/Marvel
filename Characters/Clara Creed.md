> Sister of Sabretooth. She worked as an animal handler for Hugo the Great's circus where she met Logan (Wolverine).

## Origin

The youngest of the Creed children, she was tortured by her brother [[Saul Creed|Saul]] at their brother [[Sabretooth|Victor]]'s orders. Later, wanting to know the extent of her regenerative powers, she asked Saul to drip acid on her face. She discovered her powers had a limit when the left side of her face became permanently disfigured.

## Creation

Clara Creed was created by Kieron Gillen and Adam Kubert and first appeared in Origin II issue 2 (2014).

## Major Story Arcs

### Meeting the Wolverine

As adults, Clara and Saul work for Hugo Haversham at his circus, where they meet [[Wolverine|Logan]]. Clara sees the man in the bewildered beast, as Logan is presented to the circus audience. She tries to civilize him, but Saul isn't happy with their relationship. He rats Logan's location out to [[Mr. Sinister|Nathaniel Essex]], who immediatly sends his Marauders to capture Logan. In the ensuing battle, Logan accidently stabs Clara. Thinking her dead, Saul offers Logan revenge on Essex. In Essex' lab, Essex tells Logan that Saul had sold him out. Confronting Saul with this fact, Clara turns out to be alive. Being Saul's sister, she has the same regenerative abilities that her brothers Saul and Victor have. Seeing no way out, Logan kills Saul by drowning him. Angered and grieved, Clara dismisses Logan, claiming she never wants to see him again.
