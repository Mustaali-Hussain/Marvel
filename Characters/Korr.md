## Brief History

Korr was born in the Forgotten City of the High Evolutionary. Since not devolved like the other residents, Korr and others born there were treated like animals. Being very young, Korr must be carried and another outcast named [[Turg|Tong]]assists in this. Although young, Korr is very intelligent. After being rescued by the [[Thing]], Korr and the others were taken in by the Fantastic Four.
