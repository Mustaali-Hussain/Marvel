## Origin

Chester Weems was a young teenager whom became, in fact, a Teen-Hulk.

## Creation

Teen-Hulk was a parody of the Hulk whom starred in a number of issues of Crazy Magazine. After years in comic-book limbo, he was more recently seen on the covers of Marvel's Secret Wars.
