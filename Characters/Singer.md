> [!abstract] In brief
> Singer is a member of the New Orleans Assassins Guild.

Singer was created by Howard Mackie, Mike Wieringo and Terry Austin in 1995 and first appeared in Rogue # 1.

## Powers & Abilities

Singer possesses the power to act as a channel for psionic energy, being able of transmitting it from one place to another. This allows her to view distant events and use her mind as a holographic communicator through some unknown effect of her powers it also gives her the ability of flight. She can also project messages by emitting light from her eyes that originates in her mind. She wears a blindfold to keep her powers suppressed.
