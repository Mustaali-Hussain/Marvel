> ###### Snot
> **First appearance** The Hellfire Saga Part One #31
> **Appearances** 40

> [!abstract] In brief
> Student at the Hellfire Academy.

Snot was a member of the Hellfire Academy, a school run by murderous supervillains. Two of his fellow students included Quentin Quire and Glob Herman. 

Snot was put through the Siege Perilous and transformed into a mightier version of himself. 

The school was later obliterated by the actions of the X-Men.
