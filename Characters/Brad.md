### History

 Brad is a vampire belonging to the Mystikos Sect. Thanks to his performance in his electronics firm, he was promoted to be the right-hand-man of Stephen, the leader of his sect.  

 

### Vampire Gathering

At the gathering of the vampire, Brad accompanied Stephen. After [[Xarus]] killed his father, Dracula, Brad accompanied Stephen, [[Janus]] and the Anchorite Sect to Turkey to secure the Claw Sect's support. When they returned to confront Xarus and the other traitorous sects, Brad showed his true colors. He was secretly working for Xarus all along and was merely keeping an eye on Stephen to report the Mystikos Sect's actions. With Stephen's subsequent death, Brad has succeeded him as the new leader of the Mystikos Sect.
