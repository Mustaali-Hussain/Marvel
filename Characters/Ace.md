## Origin

Agent of GRAMPA, Global Reaction Agency for Mysterious Paranormal Activity, Ace often partners with One Eyed Jacquie as Blackjack. Ace has collaborated with the Mighty Avengers, even offering the team roles with GRAMPA, giving them clearance in countries not governed under Norman Osborn's authority. He has offered more traditional assistance to the Avengers team by helping them defeat the gigantic sea creature known as Titan.  

  

## Creation

Ace is a Marvel comics character created by Greg Pak and Takeshi Miyazawa, first appears in Amazing Fantasy #15 released in 2006.
