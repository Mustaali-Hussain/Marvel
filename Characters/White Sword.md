> ###### White Sword
> **First appearance** Amenth #12
> **Appearances** 59

> [!abstract] In brief
> An immortal healer and bearer of the sword, Purity. He once led his 100 Champions into Amenth to fight to protect the mutants of Arakko, only to grow addicted to battle and turn on Arakko as well.

## Creation:

The White Sword first appeared in X-Men #12 - Amenth, the character was created by Jonathan Hickman and Pepe Larraz for the X of Swords event. White Sword's characteristics appear to have been based on the Tarot card of The Emperor.

## Powers:

White Sword is an omega-level mutant with the following powers:

- 

**Immortality**: Like all Externals mutants he is immortal.

**Stamina**: The White Sword can seemingly fight years on end.

- **Healing/Resurrection**: The White Sword can heal wound and even bring the dead back to life. **Mind Control**: When The White Sword resurrect someone they are under his control unless he releases them.
