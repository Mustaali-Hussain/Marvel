## Brief History

Manoli Wetherll works for NPR-TV and covered the X-Men's fall at the hands of the [[Adversary]]. She also did some coverage of the Genoshan crisis during the X-Tinction Agenda. One of her devices here was to televise a debate between the [[David Moreau|Genegineer]] and [[Moira MacTaggert]].
