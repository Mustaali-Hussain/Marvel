## Origin

Link was a telepathic mutant whom lived during a distopian future where the mutant Trevor Fitzroy ruled the lands. Link and her teammembers encountered Bishop and they became allies in fighting the evil Fitzroy. When Bishop returned to his own time, Link remained with her teammembers in her own time. She has not been seen since.
