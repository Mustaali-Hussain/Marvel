## Creation

Inan first appears in Ultimate Black Panther #9 (2024) by Bryan Edward Hill and Stefano Caselli.

## Major Story Arcs

### Wakanda

[[Black Panther]], ruler of Wakanda, required a sorcerer to take on the Moon Knight. He enlisted the help of his friends, [[Storm]] and [[Erik Killmonger|Killmonger]], to find one. This led them to a prison where Inan was locked up. Grateful for her rescue, she accompanies them to Wakanda. They arrived just in time to stop Panther from executing [[Lord Khonshu]]. Inan recognized that Panther had a vibranium problem. She confirmed his suspicions that vibranium has a consciousness and a will of its own that he was becoming a slave to. She attempted to reveal this consciousness to him, but she manifested an ancient being instead. After a brief fight, she teleports the being to space and recommends Wakanda receive an exorcism.

## Powers, Weapons, & Abilities

**Sorcerer: **As the Sorcerer Supreme, Inan is capable of multiple forms of spell casting. The following list is not a thorough list of her abilities.

- **Possession: **Inan was able to possess and control people like puppets
- **Animal Control: **She has shown an ability to summon creatures to her side
- **Time Manipulation: **She is able to create rifts in time and space, once to allow someone's natural healing process to occur over a shorter time span.
- **Energy Constructs: **She can manifest force fields and energy whips.
- **Precognition: **She is capable of seeing future events through meditation.

**Magical Artifacts: **As Sorcerer Supreme, Inan is capable of wielding magical artifacts

- **Cloak of Levitation: **Her cloak allows for regular levitation
