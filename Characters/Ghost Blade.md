> Sanjay is an Indian vampire. He is a member of the Forgiven.

## Origin

Sanjay was a Hindu pandit, who lost his faith after his mother was murdered. He was seduced by a sexy vampire riding a tiger and turned. Later, he joined a vampire clan called the Forgiven led by Raizo Kodo alongside Visigoth, Quickshot, Nighteyes and Inka. The Forgiven infiltrate the domain of Castle Dracula and attempt to put old feuds aside and try to join forces with Dracula to stop the Hulk who has been decimating the Vampire Nation as a hammer wielding member of the Worthy. Dracula refuses to join forces with Raizo and has Sanjay and the rest of the Forgiven placed in the dungeons.

## Creation

Sanjay was created by Victor Gischler and Ryan Stegman in 2011 and first appeared in Fear Itself: Hulk vs Dracula # 1.
