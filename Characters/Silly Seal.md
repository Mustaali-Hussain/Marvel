## Origin

Silly Seal was a comic character published by Timely Comics, Marvel Comic's predecessor, during the 'funny animal' craze in the 1950's. He most often teamed-up with his friend [[Ziggy Pig]]. After this craze had ended, Silly Seal was hardly ever seen again, except for a small few images or references of the character.
