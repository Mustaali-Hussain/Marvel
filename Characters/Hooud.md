## Brief History

Hooud is a Dire Wraith of the Universal Inhumans. She was one of six warriors chosen for the Light Brigade. A group that was meant to sacrifice themselves by going through Eldrac for "a battle they cannot win". Imprisoned by [[Annihilus]] in the Negative Zone she was forced to fight in gladiatorial matches to the death. She would then be resurrected and forced to repeat the cycle over and over for the Negative Zone inhabitants enjoyment. She along with the rest of the Light Brigade were led by the Johnny Storm to not only escape imprisonment but defeat Annihilus and gain control of the Annihilus Wave by taking the Cosmic Control Rod.
