## Origin

Smasher is a member of the Imperial Guard. Not much is known about the history of the wearer of the costume except that he became one of the most prominent fighters in the galaxy and was awarded a place among the Shi'ar's Imperial Guard, where he fought for the Emperor of the Shi'ar. It is known that he knew the original Captain Marvel.

## Creation

Smasher was created by Chris Claremont and Dave Cockrum in 1977 and first appeared in X-Men # 107.

## Mayor Story Arcs

### Imperial Guard

*Vril Rokk* was a veteran of the Imperial Guard whom briefly fought the X-men wen the Imperial Guard was ordered to take them out by the mad emperor D'ken. However, when D'ken was dethroned and Empress Lilandra took his place, Smasher fought alongside the heroes on more then one occasion. He also fought in the so called Operation Galactic Storm, witch was a war between the Kree and the Shi'ar empires.

### Death

Vril Rokk met his demise at the hands of Casandra Nova after he tried to find help from the X-men. Casandra Nova had taken over the body of Charles Xavier and was out to destroy the Shi'ar Empress Lilandra. Smasher was send to earth, and although he did make it, his ship crashed. He however managed to warn some earthlings, unfortunately, these earthlings where cows, whom could not pass the message. Smasher died after this, believing to have fulfilled his mission.

### War of Kings - A Replacement

*Gladiator sides with Lilandra*

After his death, Smasher was soon replaced with a newer recruit, *Salac Tuur*. Emperor Vulcan of the Shi'ar Empire sent Gladiator to capture Lilandra and bring her to him. When Gladiator captures her she begins to plea with him to let her live and not take her to Vulcan but the Imperial Guard, and especially the new Smasher, interjects her plea and demands that Gladiator does as the Emperor ordered and kill Lilandra and the others. Gladiator then apparently makes his choice and turns on the Guard and the Shi'ar, killing Smasher and thus saving Lilandra.

A new Smasher originating from earth has been recruited into the ranks of the Imperial Guard. This Smasher can be found here.

## **Alternate Versions**

**Age of Apocalypse**

As in the regular Marvel 616 universe, Smasher was a member of the Imperial Guard in this alternate reality. Along with Gladiator and the rest of the Guard, he confronted Gambit and the X-Force, mistaking them for enemies when they teleported to the M'Kraan Crystal. With the help of Rictor, Smasher and the Guard attempted to capture the mutants, only to be killed by the M'Kraan's expanding crystal wave while Gambit and his team escaped.

## **Powers**

It is not known what alien race Smasher belongs to. Thanks to variable DNA, Smasher can adapt different powers for limited amounts of time, including super strength due to increased density, flight, super-sight, and x-ray vision.
