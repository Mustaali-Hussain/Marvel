> ###### Black Lotus
> **First appearance** Back In The U.S.S.R.; Kaa's Hunting #11
> **Appearances** 14

> [!abstract] In brief
> An Asian assassin and enemy of Black Widow

Black Lotus was hired with several other assassins to capture the Black Widow. The Black Lotus helped to subdue the Widow with a kick to the head. However, she and the others were instead defeated by the Black Widow after she regained her composure.

## Creation

Black Lotus was created by Ralph Macchio and George Perez in 1983 and first appeared in Marvel Fanfare # 11.

## Story Arcs

Black Lotus was one of the super-villainesses recruited by Superia to come to Femizonia Island and join her Femizons. While en route to Femizonia, Captain America and the Paladin located them, but were discovered. Black Lotus participated in the assault that captured the two heroes.

## Abilities & Weapons

Black Lotus is a highly skilled martial artist that uses a variety of weapons including sais and a bladed fan.
