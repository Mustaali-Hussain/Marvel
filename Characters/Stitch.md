> [!abstract] In brief
> Member of an early version of Alpha Flight

Stitch was a member of an early version of Alpha Flight. Deeply traumatized by the emergence of her powers, she largely withdrew from the world, sleeping in the team's barn. Stitch's ability to exert fine control over small amounts of metal initially seemed of limited use to the team. However, she played a key role in defeating an attack by [[Egghead]] and a group of super powered criminals. Taking control of the [[Andreas von Strucker|Swordsman]]'s namesake weapon, she used it to knock the [[Rhino]] unconscious, while at the same time distracting the Swordsman long enough for Groundhog to knock him out.  

## Creation

Stitch was created by Scott Lobdell, Simon Furman and Pat Broderick in 1992 and first appeared in Alpha Flight Special # 1.
