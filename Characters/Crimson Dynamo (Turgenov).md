> [!abstract] In brief
> KGB Agent and the second person to wear the Crimson Dynamo Armor.

The Soviet government was not taking losing one of its best inventors well. They thought that if they could not have him, nobody could. They sent the best agents of the KGB, [[Black Widow]] and Boris Turgenov, after Vanko. Once in America, Turgenov stole the Crimson Dynamo Armor. He attacked Stark and defeated him. He was ready to celebrate, but Vanko stopped him. Vanko fired from the experimental ray gun, but it killed both Turgenov and he. Boris fulfilled the mission with which he was sent to America.
