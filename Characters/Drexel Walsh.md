## Origin

Drexel Walsh is a U.S. Senator who has a wife and children. At some point he discovered Project X and was killed because of this knowledge.

## Major Story Arcs

### The Hunted

**For more information see: The Hunted**

Drexel Walsh has gone into hiding for a month, using state of the art tech and highly armed guards, to hide from a group of "evil men." For all his protection Walsh is still killed by [[Wolverine]]. 

It is later learned that Wolverine killed Drexel under the control of Weapon X's new leader [[The Director]]
