## Brief History

Wasabi is a master chef. He is a weapon master and can also generate knives with his mind out of Qi Energy. These knives have the ability to render his foes unconscious.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

## Other Media

### Movie

Wasabi appears in the feature-length animated Walt Disney film based on Big Hero 6. In the movie, Wasabi is African-American and a neurotic student at San Fransokyo Institute of Technology. He designed gauntlets with retractable plasma blades that can slice through any material.
