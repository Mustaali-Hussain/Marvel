She's the daughter of the Nazi geneticist [[Arnim Zola]] in Dimension Z where she's the commander of his army. She is the sister of [[Nomad|Ian Zola]].

## Powers & Abilities

Jet is a skilled combatant in the ways of Tachyon Fu, and possesses various superhuman senses. Her "bare skin can read the future," allowing her to stay a step ahead of her opponents. She is also able to "smell, hear, and taste things one hundred miles away." When using any one of her senses at "Omni-level" however, - a virtually infinite degree - all her other senses turn off.
