## Origin

Before joining Team America (later renamed The Thunderiders), the mellow Luke "Cowboy" Merriweather was a renowned rodeo star. Once he reached the pinnacle of success in the rodeo, his love of competition spurred him to switch his focus to extreme motorcycle stunts and racing.

## Powers

Cowboy is extremely skilled in trick riding and motorcycle stunts.

He is also a mutant who shares a mental link with the other members of The Thunderiders (previously known as Team America). The Thunderiders can project their physical strength, abilities and knowledge into a host body. This host body takes on the form of a black-leather-clad individual known as The Black Marauder.
