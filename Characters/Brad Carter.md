## Origin

Brad Carter is an Oscar winning Hollywood actor. You may remember his from such films as "Kong", "The Return of Kong" and "Tomorrow I'll Cry". While filming on a remote island Carter became a real hero when he rescued [[Nick Fury]] from drowning in quicksand. The island turned out to be the hideout of the megalomaniacal mad scientist [[Centurius]]. Fury convinced Carter and the film crew to help him and [[Jimmy Woo]] defeat the madman.

## Creation

Brad Carter was created by the legendary Jim Steranko and first appeared in "So Shall Ye Reap... Death!" in 1968.
