## Origin

Ginger Beach became Snap after she gained her powers when she was caught in a flood of ionic energy that Wonder Man had inadvertently released when his powers flared out of control. She was only one of eight individuals that gained powers in this manner, including her brother, Esteban "Stevie" Beach. All eight of these individuals would join forces and become the team known as The Crazy 8.

## Powers

Snap has the ability to produce and release bursts of light in the form of concussive blasts. She is able to release them in any direction and location as long as they are within her line of sight.
