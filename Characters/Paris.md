## Storyline

Paris was recruited into the Pantheon by the original Ulysses and the man that would become the second [[Ulysses]], he is a descendant of Agamemnon and thus inherited an extended life. Paris is a master manipulator who causes chaos in the Pantheon in order to move up the ranks. He is not a fighter and is more involved in the planning of missions and division of labor. During one mission he travels to Las Vegas with [[Hulk]], [[Hector]], and Ulysses to look into the murder of Hulk's old boss Michael Berengetti, when they get there they team up with the [[Punisher]]to take down his killer. When Hulk is placed in charge of the Pantheon Paris is jealous and begins to cause chaos among the ranks. He nearly kills [[Achilles]]when he tells [[Ajax]]to go see [[Atalanta]]when she is with him knowing that he will go ballistic. After the leader of the Pantheon, [[Agamemnon]], is killed Paris is placed in charge and he fires the Hulk.

## Creation

Paris was created by Peter David and Dale Keown in 1990 and first appeared in The Incredible Hulk # 376.
