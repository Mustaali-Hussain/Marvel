## Return to the Mad Dog Ward.

Captain Zero is a former patient of the so called "Mad Dog" Ward but is now a superhero. While he's not the smartest or even strongest crimefighter, his courage and passion more than make up for it. He's one of the few people Spider-Man can trust and goes out of his way to help him even if it may mean risking his own life to do so, if that doesn't make him passable as a true hero what does?
