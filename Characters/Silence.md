## History

Following the death of the Scream, Doctor Steven harvested samples obtained from the skin and blood of its host,Andi Benton . When his efforts to revitalize the Scream symbiote proved unsuccessful, Dr. Steven combined its remains with a sample of Anti-Venom Serum created from Flash Thompson's symbiote, creating a new symbiote.

Mortally-wounded by Phage on the orders of Carnage, Andi bonded to the symbiote thinking it was Scream. Dubbing itself **Silence** -- characterizing itself as the absence of Scream -- the symbiote took over Andi's body and handily defeated Phage using a version of Anti-Venom's cleansing touch, disconnecting it from the Symbiote Hive-Mind

## Powers & Abilities

Silence shares much of the same abilities as traditional symbiotes and Anti-Venom.
