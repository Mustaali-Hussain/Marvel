## History 

 

Fleeing a trap set by Baron Mordo, the magic superhero Dr. Strange found himself in a strange dimension.  There, he was greeted by a young woman who told him that she was the rightful ruler of the land and that her throne had been usurped by her evil half sister, Shazana.  Strange discovered that the queen's power came from a "gleaming pulsating" mystic globe which was hidden beneath her throne.  After a quick battle, Strange smashed Shazana's power source and the shock of her defeat left her a babbling idiot.  Her sister resumed her own benevolent rule.
