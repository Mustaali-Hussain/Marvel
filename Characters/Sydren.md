> [!abstract] In brief
> An Agent of Sword. Sydren is a telepath with the ability to sense things remotely. He also possesses some animalistic abilities, such as a heightened sense of smell.

Fairly soft spoken and unassuming, Sydren is a member of S.W.O.R.D, a division of S.H.I.E.L.D which specializes in protecting the Earth against extraterrestrial threats. He is a fairly powerful telepath, able to sense things from great distances and with high accuracy. He has a habit of speaking obvious things that he learned with his sensory abilities, which usually earns him a snappy remark from Agent Brand, his superior officer.
