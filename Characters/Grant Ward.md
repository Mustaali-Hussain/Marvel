> [!abstract] In brief
> Grant Ward was a HYDRA infiltrator in S.H.I.E.L.D. disguised as a level 7 agent. Trained by John Garrett to become a cold-blooded killer, he was more loyal to him than to HYDRA. As an agent of S.H.I.E.L.D., he was recruited onto Agent Coulson's team, though he eventually broke cover and returned to Hydra.

## Creation

Grant Ward was originally created as an agent of S.H.I.E.L.D. and later HYDRA on ABC's Agents Of S.H.I.E.L.D apart of the MCU, although would later make an appearance in mainstream comics.

## Major Story Arcs

### Agent of S.H.I.E.L.D

It was said Grant Ward was once a top agent of S.H.I.E.L.D., known for his skill and loyalty. He has a hinted past with [[Phil Coulson|Agent Phil Coulson]], who he'd become close friends with. However it didn't last, with Ward being soon revealed as a secret double agent for HYDRA.

### Iron Man Armor

*Grant Ward as Iron Man*

During the time when [[Iron Man|Tony Stark]]'s Iron Man armor designs were leaked to the public, with how to create one. Grant Ward being a member of Hydra, was one of the few people with the resources to make one for himself. Ward would create an army of Hydra iron soldiers to face S.H.I.E.L.D., and would assassinate Coulson's lover, Lola Daniels. Starting a war between Ward and Coulson, which would end with Ward being brought into S.H.I.E.L.D custody.

### Battleworld

Grant Ward appeared as an agent of the HYDRA empire. He talked to [[Arnim Zola]] about the fate of an other Hydra agent.

### Agent of S.H.I.E.L.D...... Again

In Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. #9 when Elektra took over S.H.I.E.L.D, she enlisted Ward to become an agent again, although of course with insurance of having a bomb around in his neck to make sure he'd work with the team.

## Other Media

### Television

### Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.

*Brett Dalton as Grant Ward*

Grant Ward grew up in the Massachusetts area. His childhood was less than ideal, his older brother Christian was abusive and would frequently force Ward to beat up his younger brother. One incident in particular Maynard threw his younger brother into a well and wouldn't allow Ward to rescue him, though Ward eventually managed to do it without his brother knowing.

In 1999, Ward set fire to his parents' house not knowing that his older brother Maynard was inside at the time. Maynard survived the blaze and pushed for Grant to be tried as an adult. While at a juvenile detention center, Ward was approached by [[John Garrett]]of S.H.I.E.L.D., who offered him a choice - leave with him now and be taught the skills needed to survive or face prison. Ward agreed, and for five years Garrett trained him, teaching him self-sufficiency by spending months out in the wilderness with no one but a dog named Buddy for company. Eventually, Garrett revealed his allegiance to HYDRA and had Ward implanted in S.H.I.E.L.D. as a sleeper agent.

When [[Phil Coulson]] assembled a new team of specialists to investigate new, strange and unknown threats, Garrett suggested to Coulson that Ward be a part of his new team, in order to "smooth out" Ward's "rough edges", but in reality, Ward was to be Garrett's undercover asset to try to discover the truth of Coulson's resurrection.

*Interrogating Skye*

During the team's first mission together, they encountered a Rising Tide "hacktivist" known only as Skye, who was their only lead in finding [[Michael Peterson|Mike Peterson]], a man who exhibited superhuman abilities. Ward took an instant dislike to Skye, having had a negative experience with the Rising Tide after their release of classified data during ongoing operations, he also saw her as an unknown factor that neither Ward nor Garrett had calculated. After the mission, Phil Coulson recruited her to the team as a consultant, despite Ward's objections. However, after Skye's quick thinking saved his life while taking back the Bus from Peruvian hijackers, Ward saw a way to learn more about her, thus removing any unknown element she might bring into the mix. Ward agreed to be her "Supervising Officer" and oversee her training and education. Ward began Skye's training but felt that she wasn't fully committing herself to it or to S.H.I.E.L.D., so Coulson suggested a different, more personal approach. Ward responded by giving some details of his personal life and rough childhood to Skye, describing his so-called "defining moment" to her.

During the operation to rescue [[Graviton|Dr. Franklin Hall]]in Malta, Ward objected when Skye volunteered to infiltrate the compound of Ian Quinn, stating that she wasn't nearly qualified. Despite his doubts Skye was able to complete her mission, allowing Ward and Coulson to infiltrate the compound. Afterwards, Skye seemed to fully commit herself to her training and S.H.I.E.L.D. However, a few weeks later, Skye seemingly betrayed the team by Skye, by alerting her former boyfriend, hacker Miles Lydon, that S.H.I.E.L.D. was closing in on him while tracking down a leak concerning [[Scorch|Chan Ho Yin]], a pyrokinetic living in Hong Kong. Ward discovered the two and took them into custody aboard the Bus. When Ward produced evidence that Lydon had accepted payment of one million dollars for the information that endangered Chan's life, Skye was infuriated and felt betrayed by Lydon. Later, during the operation to extract Chan from Project Centipede Ward released Skye, needing her help to rescue Coulson and [[Melinda May]]. Skye managed to hack Centipede's mainframe and release the locked doors, allowing Coulson and May to escape. Coulson later agreed to let Skye remain on the team as long as she came completely clean with the team and agreed to wear a Tracking Bracelet at all times.

Ward further gained the trust of the team by jumping out of the Bus to rescue [[Jemma Simmons]], who had jumped from the Bus without a parachute in order to protect her team from a Chitauri Virus. Ward was never in any danger as he had a parachute and administered the cure to Simmons during free-fall. He also bonded with teammate [[Leo Fitz]], when [[Victoria Hand|Agent Victoria Hand]] assigned the two to a mission in Georgia to destroy an enemy weapon known as the "Overkill Device".

*Ward wielding part of the Berserker Staff*

After the events of *Thor: Dark World*, Ward along with the rest of the team proceeded to clean up the mess the battle had left in Greenwich. However, the clean up was interrupted as they were called to investigate an ancient Asgardian weapon known as the Berserker Staff, part of which had been discovered by pagan hate group, lead by Jakob Nystrom and Petra Larsen. With the assistance of a professor in Spain named Dr. Elliot Randolph the team is able to discover the location of the second piece. While retrieving the staff section, Ward touches the staff and relives a traumatic childhood memory involving his abusive brother and a well. He becomes irritable, aggressive, and possesses increased strength. The team discovers that Randolph is actually an Asgardian who has lived on Earth for centuries, and he gives them the location of the third staff piece. The team arrives, but are confronted pagans who had beaten them to the location. Seeing no other choice, Grant reassembles the Staff and, with the help of May, defeat the group. Later, Ward declines Skye's invitation to having a chat and follows Agent May into her room instead, seeing her as the biggest threat to his mission, he allowed things to get intimate.

*Raiding the compound*

Following a lead on the Clairvoyant, the team boards a train in Italy where they split into groups, with Ward undercover as a train conductor in order to track down package being shipped to Ian Quinn. However, the security team catches on causing Coulson and Ward to jump off the train, which mysteriously disappears afterward. Meeting back up with May at the "Bus", the three are able to locate the train and Simmons, but Fitz and Skye are gone. They track them to Quinn's compound and raid it, arresting Quinn. However, they discover Skye near death. Simmons trying to help, but can only sustain her temporarily by placing her into a hyperbaric chamber. Coulson and the team rush Skye to a S.H.I.E.L.D. medical facility to save her life. However, the doctors are only able to stabilize her, forcing Coulson to bring her to the same location where he was revived. Joined by agents John Garrett and Antoine Triplett, the team travels to the "Guest House" to recover GH-325, the miracle drug used to revive Coulson. Coulson, Ward, Garrett and Fitz enter the facility and eliminate the two guards there only to find the place rigged with explosives. They manage to recover the drug and get out before the facility implodes, returning to the "Bus" they administer the drug to Skye, successfully saving her.

The team is summoned to investigate an energy reading from the Bifrost, where they encounter [[Sif|Lady Sif]], She explains she is tracking [[Lorelei]], an Asgardian sorceress with power to control the minds of men. They team trace Lorelei to a biker bar where they fight her men, but Lorelei enchants Ward and escapes with him to Las Vegas. Together, they hijacked the Bus, eluding Coulson, Sif, and a S.H.I.E.L.D. team, and commandeer the "Bus". Lorelei encounters May and reveals that Ward’s heart is with someone else. Sif confronts Lorelei while May fights Ward. Sif fixes the collar to Lorelei’s neck, undoing her enchantment, and returns Lorelei to Asgard. May ends her relationship with Ward.

*Shooting the "Clairvoyant"*

Agents Garrett, Triplett, Hand, [[Jasper Sitwell|Sitwell]], and Blake join the team to hunt for the Clairvoyant. The agents pair up to pursue different Clairvoyant candidates. Ward was teamed with Triplett to investigate Elijah Fordham, but before they could find him, they were called to help May and Blake against Mike Peterson, now calling himself [[Deathlok]]. Concluding that Thomas Nash is the Clairvoyant because of Deathlok's presence, the team converges on his new location where they briefly encounter him again, but he flees into the sewers. Nevertheless, they find Nash, a vegetative man on life support who must speak through a computer. When Nash boasts about being the Clairvoyant and says that Centipede will kill Skye, Ward shoots him. Afterwards, Ward is taken into custody and locked in interrogation on board the "Bus".

However, after Skye deciphers a secret code on S.H.I.E.L.D.'s network revealing HYDRA's infiltration, Coulson releases Ward. When the Bus arrives at the Hub, the group splits into teams to capture Victoria Hand, whom they believe is the Clairvoyant. Skye and Grant Ward team up heading to the central computer to plant a bomb, while the rest of the team went to get Simmons and Triplett. During this time, Skye and Ward admitted their mutual attraction, but Skye chooses not to pursue any relationship. Afterwards, Ward and Garret along with several other HYDRA agents raid the Fridge, releasing its prisoners, including [[Blackout]]and Ian Quinn, and obtaining its confiscated weapons, including the Berserker Staff, the Peruvian 0-8-4, and the Gravitonium.
