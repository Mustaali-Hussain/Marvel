## Brief History

*Riding Rose to the Howlett Estate*

Mister Kenneth was a man whom lived in the late 19th century and was one of John Howlett's most trusted workers at his home in Alberta, Canada. Mister Kenneth worked as a handy-man on the estate and was also a good friend to both young Rose and the young James Howlett (whom became known as Wolverine in later years). Unfortunately, Kenneth was killed by a blow to the back of his head by Thomas Logan when he and his son tried to rob the Howlett estate.
