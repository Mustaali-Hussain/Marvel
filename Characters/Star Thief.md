## Brief History

Star Thief was originally a normal human living in India. That ended when an experimental test for a space program accidentally crashed onto his car while he and his family where still it. He was the only one to live. In order to ensure his survival he was put in to an energy suit. This enabled him with powers and he swore to destroy all space flights by humans, to prevent what happened to him from happening to any one else. He ran into the New Warriors at a space launch that the Stane Company had administrated. It turned out that this was just a cover up. The Stane space craft was really carrying a bomb to kill the Inhumans. Star thief and the Warriors stopped it, and Star Thief decided to live on the moon with the Inhumans.
