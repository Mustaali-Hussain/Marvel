## Pre-Cataclysm 

King Kamuu was the king of Atlantis, and husband of Zartra, about 21,000 years ago.

 

Kamuu banished the sorceress Zhered-Na from Atlantis for prophesying that the content would sink below the ocean.  

  

## Sunken Atlantis

 

 Kamuu was also the name of the first Homo Mermanus King of Atlantis. 

 

The spirit of Kamuu visited Namor, modern-day heir to the throne of Atlantis, in his dreams. Kamuu warned Namor that Morgan Le Fay sought to raise Atlantis, giving Namor the knowledge to stop her.
