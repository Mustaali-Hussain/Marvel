> ###### Sister Voodoo
> **Real name** Vanna Black
> **First appearance** "When Titans Plug!" #3
> **Appearances** 17
> **Also known as** Vanna Black

> [!abstract] In brief
> The long lost sister of Brother Voodoo.

Vanna Black, birth name unrevealed, was spirited away from the Drumm family during her infancy and raised in secret. She later married a musician in a Jimi Hendrix tribute band, and was subsequently widowed. She first appeared in public at a taping of 'The Fred HembeckShow' where her brother, [[Doctor Voodoo|Jericho]], was appearing with the other Marvel Super Heroes. She revealed that she has an infant son known as Voodoo Chile.

While living with her brother, she suggested he form a team with [[Toad]] and [[Blob]] called the Rejex, but he declined.

She was later seen with Fred Hembeck again, speaking out in support of the comic series What The--?!.

She was then seen at her brother's apartment taking care of her son and dealing with a neighbor.

An alternate reality version of Sister Voodoo encountered the Exiles while they sought to bring down her master Kulan-Gath.
