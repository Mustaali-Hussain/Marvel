> ###### Eustace Hayseed
> **First appearance** Joker Comics #1
> **Appearances** 28

Lil' Abner-like created by Marvel. A silly hillibilly in wacky adventures.

He began as a traveling simple man, having an African chief as sidekick. Later he returned to his hometown (Mudville), settled down, meet his cousin Choo-Choo again, and became even dumber that in his traveling days.

At the end, he was Lil' Abner, with different hair color.
