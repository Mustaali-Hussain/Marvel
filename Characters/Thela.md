> Brazen, violent and loyal member of the Ano-Athox, Thela is loyally devoted to her King, Uthana Thoth. She holds a high ranking among her race and she is deadly with a blade.

## Origin

Thela is fast and lethal, and as bloodthirsty as the color of her skin. A high ranking member of the Ano-Athox race, who bring war to the realms of Asgard, but are eventually defeated on Earth by the Asgardians. 

 

## Creation

Thela of the Ano-Athox was created and designed by Matt Fraction and Pasqual Ferry. Her first appearance was in Thor #615, and the character debuted with several other members of the Ano-Athox race.

 

## Powers and Abilities

Thela appears somewhat analogous to the many standard gods that fill the ranks of many Earth pantheons. Strong, fast and vicious in battle.
