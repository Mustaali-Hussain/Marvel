> [!abstract] In brief
> Plex is a Living Database for the 18th Kree Diplomatic Gestalt.

A Plex Supreme Intelligence system is a programmable piezoplasm that houses the entire knowledge base and wisdom of the Kree species. This knowledge is accessed through a multiple personality complex interface ranging from counsellors to strategists. Such systems are standard aboard Kree diplomatic ships and advised their crew on the best course of action.
