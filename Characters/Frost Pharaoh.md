> A young man with an obsession with Egyptian culture. He brandishes a magical staff that produces ice. Enemy of Miles Morales.

## Creation

Frost Pharaoh debuts in *Miles Morales: Spider-Man* #4 by Saladin Ahmed and Javier Garrón.

## Major Story Arcs

### Stone of the Gods

Frost Pharaoh was eyeing an artifact called the Stone of the Gods, on exhibition at the Brooklyn Museum in Pacific Heights. His attempts to steal were often foiled by Spider-Man (Miles Morales). In one attempt, he read the Tome of Hoary Horrors, learning new magic spells. With these new spells, he created a mummy kaiju he could pilot. It was still no match for Miles' venom strikes.

### Gang War

Frost Pharaoh had gang territory in Brooklyn when Hammerhead incited a gang war across New York. Frost Pharaoh led his new army of henchman, the Ankh-Colytes, into a fight with the other Brooklyn gang The Buzz Boys, led by The Bumbler. Their fight was broken up by Miles Morales, who took out his henchman, but Pharaoh had a new mech to fight back. This required the help of the Daughters of the Dragon and Agent Gao's Cape-Killers to take out.

## Powers & Abilities

**Ice Control: **Frost Pharaoh has an Egyptian looking staff that has a freeze ray.

- **Frost Mech:** Has an Egyptian themed mech that shoots devastating ice blasts.
- **Ankh-colytes: **Ice mummies created by the pharaoh with undying loyalty to him.
