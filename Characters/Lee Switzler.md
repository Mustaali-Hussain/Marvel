## Origin

Lee was the owner of a diner and lived in New York City. He was also the uncle of Beverly Switzler (named after him), whom is Howard the Duck's best friend.

## Creation

Lee Switzler was created by Stever Gerber and Gene Coland and first appeared in Howard the Duck issue 20 (1978).

## Major Story Arcs

### Meeting Howard

When Howard the Duck ended up in New York City and needed a job, Lee Switzler answered the call. Howard walked into Lee's diner and found employment as a dishwasher... for about an hour before Lee's diner was destroyed by his former employee and SOOFI agent, Sudd. In the wake of the ensuing destruction and chaos, Howard made a new friend in Lee and was shocked to discover that Lee's real name was none other than Beverly Switzler. It seems that his niece, Howard's girlfriend Beverly, was named after her uncle. Lee let Howard stay at his apartment for awhile until Howard was unexpectedly kidnapped by Sinister Soofi after Lee had left. After escaping from Soofi, Howard decided to leave Lee behind so he can rescue Beverly from her new "husband", Doctor Bong (who had actually hypnotized Beverly against her will to marry him).

### A New Job

Howard would later find employment in Lee once again as a driver for Lee's new "To Hack and Back" cab company. Howard is still working as a cab driver to this day, although it is currently unknown whether Lee is still his employer or not.
