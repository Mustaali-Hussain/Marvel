## Overview

Katherine Glover is in charge of, Flash or Venom's, missions. She originally fried the brain of the previous test subject before Flash. While keeping this a secret from him, Kat is under strict orders to protect her country at whatever means necessary - this meaning keeping secrets from Flash and even going to the extent of killing him!  

 

The character, Captain Katherine Glover, was created by Dan Slott to be one of the main protagonist of the new ongoing Venom series.
