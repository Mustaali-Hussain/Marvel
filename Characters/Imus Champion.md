> [!abstract] In brief
> A wealthy entrepreneur and enemy of the Avengers and Squadron Supreme.

Imus Champion is a wealthy entrepreneur who wears an exo-skeleton while in combat. Imus has mastered all physical skills known to man and collects various mystical artifacts.    

## Creation

Imus Champion was created by Steve Englehart and Don Heck in 1972 and first appeared in The Avengers # 109.

 

He has currently: 1. A Hypno-Fish 2. Mask of Jinku 3. A Deviant Brainmine 4. Zodiac's Star-Blaster 5. Staff of Set 6. Wand of Watoomb 7. Duplictes of the Wizard's Wondergloves 8. Wizard's anti-gravity disc 9. A bow with trick arrows 10. A chest mounted weapon based on Porcupine's gas dispenser 

 

Imus is currently manipulating the Black Widow after discovering that she had created files on how to defeat her fellow Avengers. Imus is able to project his consciousness into other people through yet  to be revealed means.
