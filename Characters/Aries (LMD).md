> ###### Aries (LMD)
> **Real name** Aries
> **First appearance** Who Remembers Scorpio? Part 2: Rampage #49
> **Appearances** 19
> **Also known as** Aries III, Aries

> [!abstract] In brief
> Created by Scorpio (Jake Fury) using the Zodiac Key to serve as members of his own Zodiac team.

This version of Aries was a Life Model Decoy created along with an entirely new Zodiac team created by [[Jake Fury|Scorpio]](aka Jake Fury) using the *Zodiac Key*. During the battle Moon Knight and Nighthawk were able to cause Aries to slam into a wall knocking it unconscious. The Aries LMD was taken into S.H.I.E.L.D. custody following the battle.

Later, Aries and the rest of the LMD Zodiac, led by [[Quicksilver]], battled the Avengers whom Quicksilver was attempting to frame for treason. During an underwater battle near Avengers Mansion with a team of the heroes, the Aries and [[Taurus (LMD)|Taurus LMD]]s were flung out into the river and apprehended by the authorities
