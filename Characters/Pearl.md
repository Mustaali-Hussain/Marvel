> ###### Pearl

> **Also known as** Pearl Dimly

> [!abstract] In brief
> Marvel "creative" replacement for Irma Peterson.

Started as a secretary at Wortle Advertising Company. She was also one of the showgirls at The Silver Slipper.

In My Girl Pearl #7 her story was rewritten and she become a student at Midville High.

After the license of "My Friend Irma" ended, Marvel created a quick substitute, Pearl Dimly, who shared (at the beginning) many of Irma's characteristics.
