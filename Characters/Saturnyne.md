> Omniversal Majestrix and a powerful witch, Saturnyne is the ruler of Otherworld and overseer of the Captain Britain Corps. Through power, trickery and manipulation, she monitors and acts against threats to the omniverse.

## Origin

Opal Luna Saturnyne is the Earth-9 variant of Earth-616's Courtney Ross. Ruthless and brilliant, Opal Luna Saturnyne keeps a firm watch over the Omniverse. She does not believe in conforming to morals in her efforts to preserve the Omniverse, a necessary evil to her job. Some other versions of her from other dimensions are Sa'tneen and Sat-Yr-9.

## Major Story Arcs

When Fantomex was taken to Otherworld to be put on trial for the murder of the child who would be Apocalypse Saturnyne was the judge. After a short show trial, She sentenced Fantomex to be executed via molecular deconstruction.

### Spider-Verse

Saturnyne and the Captain Briton Corps were the first ones to become aware of the Multiverse dying due to Universal Incursion. At the same time the Inheritors were beginning their *Great Hunt *against the Spider Totems. Spider-UK brought this of the court attention however Saturnyne dismissed them as insignificant.

### Time Runs Out

Saturnyne was recently killed alongside most of the Captain Britain Corps during a vicious and bloody battle with the Mapmakers and their Sidera Maris slaves.

### War in Vanaheim

*Realm of X*

Following the Fall of X, a group of mutants fall through the Krakoan gates into the Norse realm of Vanaheim. There Saturnyne has corrupted the land and is ruling under the moniker of the White Witch, and is controlling events to fulfil a prophecy for great power so that she can return to Otherworld and reclaim her throne. She lures in the young mutant Curse, and attempts to manipulate her into tapping into the heart of Vanaheim's magical power so that she may steal it for herself. Unfortunately for Saturnyne, Curse sees through her lies thanks to the X-Man Magik, and Curse gives the power to Illyana. While Saturnyne had previously blocked access to Vanaheim from the other realms, this shift allowed All-Father Thor and Freyja to come to Vanaheim's aid and liberate its people from Saturnyne's grasp. Magik uses the source of power to heal the land, and Saturnyne flees.

## Powers and Abilities

**Omniversal Awareness: **As Omniversal Majestrix Saturnyne as access to a vast sum of magical sources and advance scientific aids which allows her to observe events across the multiverse simultaneously.

**Magic**: Saturnyne can channel vast amount of magical energy especially in Otherworld where magic is more fluid.

- **Dimension Manipulation**: Saturnyne can move freely within Otherworld plus transport herself to other realities.
- **Longevity**: Saturnyne has been alive for centuries and is seemingly ageless.
- **Precognition**: Sometimes get vision of possible futures which she uses to manipulate events to suit her needs.
- **Reality Manipulation**: Saturnyne can impose her will on reality, even casting arbitrary ruling which makes sense only to her. She is at her strongest within the Starlight Citadel and Otherworld realms.
- **Telepathy**: Saturnyne has displayed host of telepathic power such as mind reading, influencing, memory manipulation.
- **Temporal Manipulation**: Localize time distortion. Saturnyne is also resistance to time paradoxes.

**Leadership**: Saturnyne has command over the Captain Britain Corps.

**Manipulation**: Saturnyne is a career politician who views everyone as chess pieces to influence. She often orchestrated events manipulating the happening of the multiverse.

**Strategist**: Saturnyne is capable of crafting complex long term plans to achieve her goals.
