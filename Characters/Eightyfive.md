> ###### Eightyfive
> **First appearance** Split Decision #3
> **Appearances** 10

> [!abstract] In brief
> Member of the Force. Has the power of magnetism.

Eightyfive is a mutant Pink- Kree with complete mastery of magnetism. Born in captivity due to the racism and oppression of the blue-skinned Kree, his name comes from the cell block in which he was held captive. 

 

He is a member of Force.

## Creation

Eightyfive was created by Jim Valentino in 1990 and first appeared in Guardians Of The Galaxy # 3.
