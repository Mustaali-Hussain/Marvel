> [!abstract] In brief
> Glitter is a Shi'ar and a member of the Shi'ar salvagers crew.

She and her crew encountered the X-Men a few times, first when they came to earth and wanted to kill Rogue. On a different occasion, Glitter and her team teamed up with Rogue, Rachel, Magneto, and Gambit in order to save the X-Men's lost teammates, Havok and Polaris.

She has somewhat of a grudge against Rogue, even more so than some of the other crew members.
