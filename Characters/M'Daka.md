> A mechanic from Wakanda who Captain America recruited for the Avengers support staff.

## Origin

M'Daka was first seen delivering The Captain's shield which was a gift for him from the Black Panther. He was later recruited to act as a mechanic for the Avengers and fought against Doctor Doom's robot army during Acts of Vengeance.

## Creation

M'Daka was created by Mark Gruenwald and Kieron Dwyer and first appeared in *Captain America* #342.
