> [!abstract] In brief
> Supporting character in Pearl's stories.

Pearl's boss at Wortle Advertising Company. He was always unhappy with her performance and very often threatened to fire her.

After Pearl's character was rewritten in My Girl Pearl #7 he became principal at Midville High and in My Girl Pearl #8 his name was changed to mr. Fluster.
