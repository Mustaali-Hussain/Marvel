> ###### Midnight's Fire
> **Real name** Aaron Chord
> **First appearance** Mirror Moves #2
> **Appearances** 53
> **Also known as** Aaron Chord

> [!abstract] In brief
> A vigilante and a former member of the New Warriors.

Midnight's Fire and his sister, [[Silhouette]], are [[Chord|Andrew Chord]]'s only children. He met [[Night Thrasher|Dwayne Taylor]] while he and Silhouette were patrolling the streets. They joined forces and began hunting down street gangs.

Some time later, Silhouette was shot in one of their attacks, and her legs were paralyzed. He blamed Dwayne, and began killing police and drug lords hoping it would draw out Dwayne. Silhouette later joined Night Thrasher, therefore distancing herself from her brother.

He got his powers from the Universal Wellspring.
