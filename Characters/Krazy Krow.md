## Origin

Krazy Krow was a comic character published by Timely Comics, Marvel Comic's predecessor, during the 'funny animal' craze in the 1950's. He most often teamed-up with his friends Ziggy Pig, Silly Seal and Super Bunny. After this craze had ended, Krazy Krow was hardly ever seen again.
