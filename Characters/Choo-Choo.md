> ###### Choo-Choo

> [!abstract] In brief
> Eustace Hayseed's cousin and his second girlfriend. A hillibilly like him.

Eustace Hayseed was a comic character in the Joker comics volume. His storylines were uneven, contradictory and of diverse quality. His first girlfriend, Honey, disappeared after three issues.

By issue 12, a new romantic interest appear, Kinkie, a brunette cousin that returned home. The next issue, she was renamed "Choo-Choo" and became redhead. She became the feature's co-star. Eustace and Choo-Choo quickly became poor copies of Lil'Abner and Daisy Mae.

She evolved to became Eustace's girlfriend (no reference to them being cousins too). For a short time, she was renamed as "Lily-Belle" and became the feature's main star. Her design was made brunette again. She returned to be Choo Choo in Krazy Komics, ended her life as cheap knock off.
