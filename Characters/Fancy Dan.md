> [!abstract] In brief
> Long time member of the Enforcers

Though small, his footwork is so fast and dazzling, that it is very hard to lay a hand on him. He is a master of Judo, and entitled to wear the black belt. 

## Alternate Versions  

 

### Ultimate Universe 

 

Fancy Dan Crenshaw is a member of the Enforcers, a gang for hire. Fancy Dan and the enforcers are normally seen confronting Spider-Man.  He uses a pair of guns and usually does not let anyone touch them. It is common for him to end up with his hands webbed while grabbing both guns. 

 

## Media

 

Fancy Dan appeared in The Spectacular Spider-Man (Animated Series). He was a member of the New Enforcers and took on the name of Ricochet with his suit granting him super speed.
