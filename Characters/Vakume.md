> Vakume was born in New Salem, Colorado and was a member of Salem's Seven.

## Creation

Vakume was created by Len Wein, George Perez and Joe Sinnott in 1977 and first appeared in Fantastic Four # 186. 

## Powers & Abilities

Vakume could magically assume a mutated form in which he could become intangible, fly, create vacuums and drain energy from living beings or mechanical devices. Even in human form he had limited abilities to wield magic.
