## History

Imani Greene is the room advisor for Osborn Hall at Empire State University. Smart and dedicated, she quickly becomes the object of affection for Starbrand [[Star Brand|Kevin Connor]]. She also gathers the attention of his friend, [[Nightmask|Adam Blackveil]], but for completely different reasons.

Kevin and Adam meet up with her during her physical therapy session with [[Sha Shan|Sha Shan Nguyen]]. However, [[Libra (Brandt)|Libra]] ambushed the duo, attacking Imani and Sha Shan in the process. After Starbrand and Nightmask are brought back, Kevin takes Imani to her room. After witnessing Va-Sohn's sudden attraction to Kevin and finding out more about Imani's past, he figures out that she was meant to be true compatible candidate for the Starbrand, explaining his weird sensation around her and Kevin and Imani's quick infatuation with each other.

Following Kevin's death, she declared that she loved him and grieved him. Adam and Sha Shan ask her to talk to [[Juliet Marcos|Elementary]] about what it truly means to be a hero when they are worried her new powers have gone to her head. Imani had a heartfelt with Juliet about Kevin and her own heroic experiences, but is interrupted by [[Squirrel Girl]] and [[Tippy-Toe]].
