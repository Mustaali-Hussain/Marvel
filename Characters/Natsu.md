## Creation

Natsu Tsukishima was created for the new ultimate universe (Earth-6160) as a recurring character in the Ultimate X-Men title. 

Natsu's character concept is inspired by [[Cyclops]], with her name meaning Summer in Japanese, and having a similar power set, although only one eye instead of two.
