> Fusa is a mutant from the future who once traveled back in time to get Wolverine to save her world.

Fusa is a mutant from the future in the year 2058. Her abilities are time traveling to different locations. She can use this ability with other people, but must make close contact with her at all times. She can also sense a person's nature by her dreams like when she explained to Logan that she knew him in one of her dreams and learned all about him. Though this is not known to be a controlled ability. She is located in an underground base along with other humans to hide from the living bacteria; the Mandate.
