> ###### Jakarra
> **Real name** Jakarra
> **First appearance** A Cup of Youth #6

> [!abstract] In brief
> Son of T'Chaka, half brother to T'Challa and Shuri.

Jakarra knew he wouldn't be-able to become the Black Panther because of his size & so he joined the Wakanda military where he earned the rank of General.

Jakarra had a deep hated for his half-brother T'Challa because of his success in being deemed worthy by Bast and becoming the Black Panther where he could not. Once while T'Challa was way, Jakarra & his soldiers staged a coup in Wakanda because Jakarra felt he should rule instead of T'Challa.

Jakarra told N'Gassi (who T'Challa put in charge at the time while he was way) that he was now the new ruler of Wakanda and that he exposed himself to raw Vibranium to make up for him lack of size & physical power. Then Jakarra's body began transform to a monstrous from by his hair falling out & his skin turning pink. He became monster made from raw Vibranium which frighten his soldiers and they then turned against him.

Jakarra then ran from N'Gassi office to the jungle of Wakanda. N'Gassi assemble the members of the Royal Family who were in Wakanda at the time to battle there cousin.The Royals (Joshua Itobo, Khanata, Zuni & Ishanta) were able to temporally defeat there monstrous cousin & took him to a holding chamber. While The Royals, N'Gassi & Jiru (N'Gassi's assistant) were debating on what should be done with Jakarra he awoke and escaped.

Jakarra realizing his dream or being the ruler of Wakanda was shattered decided to make his way to the Vibranium mound in an attempt to destroy Wakanda and the rest of the world. However T'Challa arrive just in the nick-of-time to save Wakanda, but he had destroy the monster his brother had become in the process.

## **Powers **

Before his transformation Jakarra was a very skilled military strategist and Wakandan general.

After his transformation Jakarra possesses a limited level of superhuman strength, stamina and invulnerability. Also he could generate waves of vibrational force and bursts of flame.
