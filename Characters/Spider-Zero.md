## Creation

Spider-Zero was created by Jed MacKay and Juan Frigeri. She first appeared in Spider-Verse (2019) #1.

## Biography

### Spider-Verse

Spider-Zero came from Earth-19166 as a young woman with spider-related powers. After her reality was destroyed during an Universal Incursion. Afterwards she proceeded to travel the Multiverse so much that she no longer considered her original reality, or any reality for that matter, as her home.

Later, Spider-Hero recruited [[Miles Morales|Spider-Man (Miles)]]'s help with finding someone named Annie after the Web of Life and Destiny became corrupted.

When Spider-Zero was helping the Spider-heroes, she became haunted by memories of her home world. Miles was able to save her and she stayed with the Great Web to fulfilled the role of Master Weaver.

### Gwenverse

When [[Gwen Stacy|Spider-Gwen]] became lost in the multiverse when hit by something mid-travel through it, Spider-Zero found her and saved her. They talked briefly about their mutual friendship with Miles before Spider-Zero took Gwen back to her home reality, Earth-65. However, even though they returned to Earth-65, it was not the timeline of Gwen's usual world. Spider-Zero told Gwen that somewhere amongst the future, someone had developed an invention to blast parts of themselves so they could live on forever. When the invention was used, it was flawed, and the blast hit Gwen. Spider-Zero said that parts of Gwen had manifested itselves as versions of her as other heroes.
