## Creation

Idol Alabaster first appears in Avengers (2023) #3 by Jed MacKay

## Powers & Abilities

**Avian: **Idol has two sets of wings that she uses for flight

- **Claws: **Idol's foot is clawed like a bird of prey, and her wings are razor sharp

**Psychic: **Idol can make a psychic connection to large populations causing scrupulosity (pathological religious guilt). Her power increases with the number of people who worship her.

- **Adoration: **Idol can concentrate her supplicants' adoration into a blast of energy.
- **Sacrificial: **When she has supplicants under her spell, she is able to transfer any pain or injury she receives in battle to them, leaving her unscathed.
