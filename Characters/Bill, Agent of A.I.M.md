> [!abstract] In brief
> A member of A.I.M. sent to track down a biological weapon (Headpool) from The Savage Land.

Bill is very similar in appearance and function to [[Bob, Agent of HYDRA|Bob, agent of HYDRA]], and was even mistaken as him by [[Deadpool|Deadpool.]]

He works with his superior, Betty, to protect humans of an alternate world from super-zombies. Later he and Betty are sent to their rightful dimension by the being called 'The Contemplator'.
