## Brief History

Donald Meland first encountered Kaine when he rushed into his hospital with a half-dead Aracely. He was later saved by Kaine when the Salamander threatened him, Aracely and the hospital. A hit was later placed on him by a bitter relative of a deceased patient which meant Kaine had to protect him from the Assassins Guild.

He is married to Officer Layton
