## Origin

Reva and her brother Sam were living in New York city and were friends with [[Luke Cage]] before he gained powers. She and Luke eventually started dating and engaged in a romantic relationship with him.

## Creation

Reva Conners was created by Archie Goodwin and George Tuska in 1972 and first appeared in Hero For Hire Vol.1 issue 1.

## Major Story Arcs

### Death

Reva died in a conflict between Cage, [[Willis Stryker|Stryker]] and the Maggia. Her death still weighs heavy on Luke's memory.

## Alternate Universes

### House of M

Reva is stilled dead because of Stryker's actions but this time she is gunned down while hitmen are going after Stryker, for stealing drugs and planting them on Luke.
