## Origin

Lihla was a young but bright girl and the daughter of the much feared Axeman Bone. She lived on the planet Sakaar, the same planet where the Hulk was brought by the superhero-group known as the Illuminati in the Planet Hulk storyline. Lihla was a dreamer but strongly denounced the ways of her father and tried to make him a better person.

## Creation

Lihla was created by Greg Pak and Jackson Butch Guice and first appeared in Skaar: Son of Hulk issue 7 (2009).

## Major Story Arcs

### Hiro Kala

*Happier times for Lihla*

After the events that lead to the destruction of her home planet by the son of Hulk, Skaar, who had the chance to stop the planet-devouring creature known as Galactus, but refused to do so), she and her father joined Hulks second son, Hiro Kala, in his journey to find a new home planet for his people and followers.

Soon, a romance blossomed between the two. But even though Lilha tried to make Hiro Kala a better person, he often rejected her ways of goodness and kindness. He thought them to be foolish and non useful. In the end, he even scarified Lihla in order to keep his power. Although dead, Hiro Kala and many others did mourn the young girl who was taken away too soon.
