## Bio

Eosimias was originally from China, however he moved to The Xavier Institute after feeling that he was not treated with respect, or treated good at all. He was one of the students that was actually present when Jean Grey, the Headmistress at the time, was holding a media conference at the school, that was then attacked by the Imperial Guard. It is still unknown whether Eosimias kept his powers or lost them after the effects of M-Day.

## Powers

Eosimias has a primate-feline form, giving him fur, three-toed feet, a prehensile tail and his nails are actually claws. And Eosimias also possesses the ability of flight, through unknown means neccesary.
