> ###### Amos Jardine
> **First appearance** The Measure Of A Man #68
> **Appearances** 20

> [!abstract] In brief
> Texan millionaire who sought to add unique acts to his circus. By any means.

Amos Jardine was a Texan millionaire with a fascination with the circus industry. While first seen adding the [[Man-Thing]] to his freak-show, most of his later appearances concern his attempts to own [[Nightcrawler]]. From buying out Nightcrawler's original circus in Germany to drugging the mutant to a nearly mindless state.

Nowadays, Jardine is nearly bankrupt, living in isolation in the abandoned location of his circus.
