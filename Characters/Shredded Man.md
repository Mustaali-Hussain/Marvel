> ###### Shredded Man

> **Also known as** Ivan Guerrero

> [!abstract] In brief
> A villainous Inhuman who wishes to evolve every human genetically so they are as powerful as him, but under his control.

The Shredded Man was originally a man named Ivan Guerrero. He found out that he had cancer in his lungs. He was exposed by the Terrigen Mist when he isolated himself and his granddaughter [[Synapse|Emily Guerrero]] within a cacoon, becoming a Inhuman. Afterwards he traveled around the world for months but everywhere he went he would see the worst of humanity, making him develop a hatred for humans. Becoming the Shredded Man, he now tries to take over the world but is prevented from doing so by Cable and Synapse.
