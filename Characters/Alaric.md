## Origin

Alaric is one of The Trust bosses in the organization and the [[Punisher]]'s contact. He will provide weapons and everything that Punisher needs in order to get his help. He wants to have the Punisher in his organization, secretly, he blows up the Punisher's van in order to force him to join in. Alaric is [[Angela]]'s lover.

He is a great manipulator. Alaric initiated a brainwashing program in order to form a group of assassins called Punishment Squad. This team is using the identity of the Punisher to kill mobsters. Alaric wants to Punisher at the head of his team, but Castle despises their methods.

Alaric kills to his main contact of the Trust in Texas. However, he manages to imprison the Punisher in one of his brainwashing cells. But finally, he is defeated and forced by the Punisher to call to [[Ben Urich]] in the Daily Bugle and expose all the truth about the Trust.
