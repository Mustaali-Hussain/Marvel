> Rana "the Frog" is a member of the Children of The Vault, very powerful antagonists to the X-Men.

## Origin

Rana is a member of the Children of the Vault, she is a part of a team sent by Corregidora to apprehend fellow Children of the Vault member Luz. Tracking Luz to Mumbai Rana and her fellow Children defeat the X-Men and capture Rogue and Magneto. Rana herself defeating Indra. 

## Creation 

Rana first appears in the X-Men Legacy (#239) book, written by Mike Carey and with art by Clay Mann. 

## Powers and Abilities 

Rana's powers are a combination of genetic ability and advanced technological amplification. She can specifically, physically enter, and invade other people either to possess or simply injure them from within. She also has a degree of super strength, the ability to see all of the visual spectrum, and is fluent in English and Spanish.
