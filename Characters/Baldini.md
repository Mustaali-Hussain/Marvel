> [!abstract] In brief
> Member of the villainous Exiles. His weapon of choice is a weighted scarf that he uses like a whip.

Baldini is an Italian who uses a scarf to both whip and bind his opponents. He has some skills in unarmed combat, though he is more a brawler than a martial artist.

## Creation

Baldini was created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby in 1968 and first appeared in *Captain America *#103.
