> [!abstract] In brief
> Bliss is a mutant and member of the Morlocks. She has a very strange power, a giant tongue that can alter peoples minds.

## Origin

Little is known of Bliss's history, except that she is a mutant and lived beneath the city of New York with other fellow mutants whom hid from public. She was one of the survivors of the massacre of the Morlocks and later joined the Masque's Morlocks.

## Creation

Bliss was created by Chris Claremont and Kieron Dwyer and first appeared in The Uncanny X-Men issue 262 (1990)

## Major Story Arcs

### Meeting the X-Men

Bliss was a beautifull woman with a very strange power, a very large tongue. She was originally flesh-sculpted into the appearance of the X-Man Jean Grey by Morlock leader Masque. It was done so that she could trick X-Men Banshee and Forge into captivity. She has remained wearing an X-Man uniform, although she never really assocaited with the group. After a duplicate of Storm was killed she was flesh sculpted into Storm by Masque. She has remained with the Morlocks after this and was ocasionally seen.

### M-Day

Bliss was one of the scarce mutants to retain their powers after M-Day and has rejoined the remaining Morlocks. She seems to be less bloodthirsty than the other Morlocks and isn't completely happy with the methods of Masque, in particular his treatment of her fellow Morlocks Caliban and Leech. She used her powers to take Leech down.

### Utopia

After Cyclops formed Utopia, an independent nation of mutants, Bliss came peacefully and joined. Ironically, her former enemy Leech also resided on Utopia. After Norman Osborn lost control of the US superhuman forces, Cyclops has been aiding US superhumans with the forces of Utopia. As a result, Bliss could likely be called upon to be a superhero--if she wants to.

### Nation X

She is seen along with Dragoness,Toad,and Litterbug arguing about the X-men rationing water, but it is shown that Iceman gave them Hypothermia

### First To Last

She was seen with Toad and Masque trying to force information out of Irma Cuckoo in Cerebra by using her tongue to bite and somewhat alter Irma's face. In the next issue she was seen being attacked by some of the New X-Men that are trying to save the Cuckoo. Next she is seen being phased through by Trance and her tongue had apparently bit Pixie.Then Toad forced the Cuckoo to tell them the Truth but she broadcasted them to Everyone on Utopia

### X-Men Disassembled

Bliss was seen after the events in which Nate Grey (X-Man) almost changed the world, causing an explosion of anti-mutant hatred. She took refugee in the Morlock tunnels among former students of the Jean Grey School, such as Blindfold, Velocidad and Loa.

 

## Powers

Bliss has a second mouth on her tongue which has fangs that produces venom. The tongue can also extend to an unknown length.
