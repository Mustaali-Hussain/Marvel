## Origin

Angela Lipscombe has genius-level knowledge in the area of neuropsychiatry. She once dated Bruce Banner in medical school and has been and loyal and courageous friend to him since. She and Bruce broke up when he became jealous of Angela achieving the coveted grant for graduate study and he did not. Angela became one of the world's best in the field of neuropsychiatry.

## Creation

Angela Lipscombe was created by Paul Jenkins and Ron Garney and first appeared in Incredible Hulk Vol.3 issue 12 (2000).

## Major Story Arcs

### Banner is Back

After many years, Bruce came back to Angela in distress, thinking he'd contracted an incurable disease. Angela got a chance to study the Banner/Hulk Multiple Personality Disorder, as she saw it. She is currently partners with Doc Samson.
