> The Defender is a Golden Age hero created by Jack Kirby and Joe Simon.

US Marine Don Stevens decided it to put on a costume to help protect America. He fought the nazis along with other heroes during World War 2.  He had a teenage side kick by the name of Rusty. Don died by the hands of Alexander Bont, while breaking-up a mob meeting.
