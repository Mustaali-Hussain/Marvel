> [!abstract] In brief
> Viv is a Synthezoid created by Vision to be his daughter, she is programmed to act like a 16-year-old.

## Creation

Viv was created by Tom King and Gabriel Hernandez Walta.

She first appeared in Vision #1 - Visions of the Future. She is a synthezoid created together with her twin brother Vin by Vision, who wanted to create a family for himself.
