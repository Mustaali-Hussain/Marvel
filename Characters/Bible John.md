## Brief History

Vampire Hunter and member of the Carik. He has worked with and friends to Blade, the Vampire Hunter. Bible John is the defender and caretaker of the Chiaroscuro, a big library that is headquarters for a little group of warriors known as the Cathari. Aware of the threat the vampire threats, John covers himself with protective sigils, and wards.
