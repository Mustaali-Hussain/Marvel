> ###### Bloodstorm
> **First appearance** Strange Tales #16

> **Also known as** Bloodstorm One

> [!abstract] In brief
> A clone of Dracula birthed by Hydra's Department of Occult Affairs. Bloodstorm One turned on his creators and leads the Bloodcoven as the right hand man of Varnae, the first vampire.

## Origin

Bloodstorm One was cloned from Dracula's genetic material by HYDRA's Department of Occult Affairs.

## Creation

Bloodstorm One first appeared in Nightstalkers #16, and was created by writer Frank Lovece and artist Bill Wylie. He was later redesigned by Pepe Larraz as part of the Blood Hunt event.

## Major Story Arcs

### Birth

*Bloodstorm One is born*

A Dreadnought attacks the Nightstalkers' headquarters to steal the exorcist, an anti-occult gun, for use by Hydra. With this information [[Frank Drake]] reaches out to [[Doctor Strange]], who follows the lead to a bunker run by the Department of Occult Affairs. Within he discovers numerous mystical items of great import, and a lab where they are growing vampire stormtrooper grown from Dracula's DNA. Bloodstorm One is birthed and slaughters all D.O.A agents within the lab.

### Blood Hunt

*Leader of Varnae's Bloodcoven*

Bloodstorm One acts as second-in-command of [[Varnae]], and the field leader of the Bloodcoven.

Following the Blood Hunt, the Bloodcovern goes into hiding in New York. Bloodstorm One - the self-appointed leader of this new sect - observes newly-sired vampires being cured by the Blood Hunters, and sends [[Smoke Eater]] after them alongside [[Cruel]] and [[Unusual]]. The trio ambush monster hunter [[Elsa Bloodstone]] and briefly torture her, before [[Miles Morales]] and the rest of the team free her and join the fight. During this [[Dagger]] is injured and taken by Miles to the hospital, but the Bloodcoven intercept and capture Tandy. Bloodstorm One addresses his sect of vampires, claiming that Dagger's potent mutate blood shall give them the strength to take back New York and, soon, the world.

## In Other Media

### Marvel Rivals

*Bloodstorm One's statue in Dracula's Castle*

Bloodstorm One makes a cameo appearance in Season 01 of NetEase's arena shooter Marvel Rivals, both in the form of a newspaper image during the event and as a statue in Dracula's Castle in the Central Park convey map.
