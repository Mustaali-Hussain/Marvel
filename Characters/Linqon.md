## History

Linqon was on the run from the cops for being a mutant when [[Layla Miller]] helped her evade capture. Later, Layla convinced [[Ruby Summers]] to help Linqon, and Linqon joined in Ruby's rebellion against the anti-mutant state.

## Powers and Abilities

### Powers
