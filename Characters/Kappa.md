## Origin

Kappa is a young mutant living on [[Krakoa]]with the power to control water.

Her first appearance is in the Age of X-Man event, where she was a prisoner in the Danger Room Prison Complex.

In Earth-616 Kappa was a mutant of Krakoa appears for the first time on a mission together with the New Mutants, [[Magik]]and [[Warpath]], to save a mutant, whose newly activated power created seismic waves, Kappa uses her powers in synergy with Magik to help and save as many lives as possible.
