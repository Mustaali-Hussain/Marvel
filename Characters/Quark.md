## Origin

*In his original Uniform*

Quark once belonged to the troops of Mojo, in witch he soon became one of the best agents Mojo had. However, Quark's guilt over having to work for a tyrant soon got the best of him and he left Mojo to join the rebellion. He became close friends of former X-men Longshot and Dazzler and fought by their side in hopes of defeating Mojo once and for all. What happened to Quark after the many changes that went on in the 'Mojoverse' (as his reality was called) remains to be seen.
