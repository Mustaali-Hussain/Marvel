> ###### Ling McPherson

> [!abstract] In brief
> Ling McPherson was a bodyguard and a partner with Bethany Cabe of the firm Cabe &amp; McPherson, Security Specialists. She became a supporting character in the first Iron Man series.

Ling McPherson was a bodyguard who specialized in security for prestigious clients. McPherson  and [[Bethany Cabe]], McPherson's best friend and another bodyguard, owned a firm called Cabe & McPherson, Security Specialists.
