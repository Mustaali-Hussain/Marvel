## Origin

Clara Menninger is a member of the Quentin Carnival. She has no eyes but "sees" through her psychic powers. She came a friend of Johny Blaze whom, at the time, was the former Ghost Rider.

## Creation

Clara Menninger was created by Howard Mackie and Ron Wagner and first appeared in Ghost Rider Vol.2 issue 27 (1992).
