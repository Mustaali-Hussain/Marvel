## Equipment

### Teleporter

Jay Jay has a belt-mounted teleporter that opens wormholes for short range travel. The team theorizes it is for sample gathering from dangerous alien environments. 

### Smoke machine

The smoke produced from teleporting actually comes from a separate device in order to make it resemble Nightcrawler's teleportation. 

### Headgear

Jay Jay, and the rest of his team, have pyschic blocking headgear they bought on the black market, made of discarded Magento helmet pieces.
