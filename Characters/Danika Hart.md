> [!abstract] In brief
> Danika Hart is an internet personality and Spider-Man fangirl.

Danika runs a vlog dedicated to [[Miles Morales]]' exploits as Spider-Man. When Miles' costume was torn during a battle with [[Blackheart]], revealing his skin underneath, Danika excitedly vlogged about the significance of a non-white Spider-Man. Miles for his part was not too thrilled by Danika's vlog, feeling that she was reducing him to his skin color; he wanted to be Spider-Man, not just "the black Spider-Man".

 

Later, Danika reveals that she receives clips and tidbits to always be aware of what Spider-Man is up to. This leads in to her receiving a clip of Spider-Man being attacked by [[Hammerhead]] and his goons. The broadcast of this on her vlog is then seen by [[Ganke Lee|Ganke]], Miles' best friend, and [[Egg|Fabio Medina]], Ganke and Miles' new roommate.
