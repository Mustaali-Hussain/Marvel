## Brief History

**Lord Visis** was a cousin of **[[Jarella]]** and a rival claimant to her throne. He has some minor magic skills, particularly able to tap into the magical powers of others and use them to his advantage. He could also teleport groups of people between dimensions. He used a magic crystal to observe distant events. He led his own army against **Jarella** but also considered marrying her. He was defeated  and killed by the **[[Hulk]]**. 

 

Or so it would seem. Visis actually survived and went into exile, trading in his sorcery to hone his warrior skills so he could eventually get his revenge on the Hulk and take control of [[K'ai]].
