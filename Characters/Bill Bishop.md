> ###### Bill Bishop
> **Real name** William Bishop
> **First appearance** The Coming of the Avengers #1
> **Appearances** 14
> **Also known as** William Bishop, Willie

Bishop attended a reunion of the Teen Brigade at a bar alongside [[Rick Jones]], K.C. Ritter and Mike Armstrong. Just as they had begun to get reacquainted, Rick was abducted by the Kree scientist [[Dr. Minerva]]. When Bishop tried to shoot at the ship that had grabbed Rick, Ritter stopped him, afraid that Bishop might hit Rick.

Mike, Bishop and Ritter all ran to the police station to tell them about Rick being kidnapped. The police were skeptical at first, but Bishop used his status as a detective to participate in the hunt for Rick's abductors alongside the national guard. When they caught up to the crashed ship, they found Rick unconscious in [[Captain Marvel|Captain Mar-Vell's]] arms, and Bishop mistook Mar-Vell for Rick's abductor.

Bill Bishop stood down when Rick explained that Mar-Vell hadn't kidnapped him. He apologized, and before leaving, told Rick how good it felt to have worked together again. Bill Bishop, K.C. Ritter and Mike Armstrong all attended Rick Jones' bachelor party, and spiked the punch, only to watch in surprise as [[Hercules]] declared their spiked punch to be "barely stronger than milk."
