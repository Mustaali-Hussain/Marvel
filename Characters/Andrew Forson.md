## Origin

Formerly of a splinter group affiliated with the Wizard, Dr. Andrew Forson has been chosen by the leaders of A.I.M. to act as ambassador to the island nation of Barbuda, which was purchased by A.I.M. in a plan to gain international sovereignty. His ideas have been unanimously agreed upon by A.I.M. leaders as the new direction for the organization.

## In Other Media

### Television

- The Scientist Supreme appears in the *Avengers Assemble* episode "Adapting to Change," voiced by Jim Meskimen in a Scottish accent. He merges with three Super-Adaptoids to become the Supreme Adaptoid before being defeated by the Avengers and absorbed by Ultron. His ultimate fate is unknown, but he likely died when Ultron's physical body was destroyed.

### Video Games

- The Scientist Supreme appears in the *Marvel Avengers Academy* mobile app game as the head of the A.I.M. Institute of Super-Technology.

### Merchandise

- The Scientist Supreme was featured in Hasbro's Marvel Legends line as part of the Xemnu Build-a-Figure wave.
