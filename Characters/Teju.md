> ###### Teju

> [!abstract] In brief
> Member of the Minions of Menace

Teju is a Reptoid alien and an enemy of the Guardians of the Galaxy ([[Charlie-27]], [[Nikki]], [[Starhawk]], [[Martinex|Martinex T'Naga]], [[Yondu|Yondu Udonta]], [[Justice|Vance Astro]]). Teju is a semi-humanoid reptilian covered with dense green scales (yellow on his underside) that enable him to withstand the blows of [[Namor]] the Sub-Mariner. He can stand erect or walk on all fours, has superhuman strength and sharp teeth, and can use his tail as a powerful weapon. He speaks with a hisssssss....*"Sssir! Sssomeone comesss!"*

## Creation

Teju was created by Steve Gerber and Jim Starlin in 1975 and first appeared in Giant-Size Defenders # 3.
