> ###### Vesper
> **Real name** Raani Jatwinder
> **First appearance** Nature of the Beast #1

> **Also known as** Raani Jatwinder

> [!abstract] In brief
> Female member, mediator, and most of the time leader of Genetix. She has telepathic and technopathic abilities. She can even enter Cyberspace with her mind, though this leaves her body vulnerable.

An Asian woman of about 20 years of age at the start of [[Codename: Genetix:vol:18337]], little seems to bother the cool, calm Vesper.

Once, her genetic material was mixed with that of Box and injected into Krista Marwan by [[Death Metal]] in an attempt to create a being who could destroy Death Metal.

## Creation

Vesper was created by Andy Lanning and Phil Gascoine in 1993 and first appeared in Codename: Genetix # 1.
