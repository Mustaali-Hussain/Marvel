> A boy who was trained by Deadpool, but turned on him because he believes Deadpool killed his father

## Story

 

Christopher blamed Deadpool for his father's murder. Deadpool gave him a spare costume and gave him a little training, letting him have the opportunity to get back at the real culprit.  However, Christopher still blamed Deadpool and blew up Deadpool's home.  Deadpool would later get his revenge by making the Juggernaut think Christopher pinched his butt, which led to a pummeling.

 

## Powers

 

Christopher possessed no powers and was pretty scrawny, but he does have good deal of knowledge in explosives and has a fairly decent shot with a rifle.
