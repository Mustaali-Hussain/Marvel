> [!abstract] In brief
> Another evil scientist of the Frankenstein family

Vincent Von Frankenstein is the great grandson of Ernst Frankenstein, the brother of Victor Frankenstein, the original creator of the [[Frankenstein]] monster. Vincent is the first of a new breed of Mad Scientist since Victor, and has a hunchback servant named Ivan. 

 

When Vincent met Frankenstein for the first time, he befriended him, but only for his sinister purposes. He attempted to control Frankenstein for his own evil doing. He was eventually killed by his own servant and his son Basiltaken by her.
