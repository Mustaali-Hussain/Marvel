> [!abstract] In brief
> Crux is a synthetic copy of the mutant known as Cristal Lemiuex, created by Cerebro.  She was a member of Cerebro's X-Men.

## Character History

Crux has the power to fire blasts of hot or cold elemental force, as well as being able to manipulate fire and ice.

As a synthetic creation of Cerebro, Crux was a combination of mutants Iceman and Sunfire.
