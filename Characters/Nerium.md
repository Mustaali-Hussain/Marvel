> [!abstract] In brief
> A Latverian mutant in service to Doctor Doom

## Origin

A carnival ringmaster would capture Nerium and force her into his carnival's freak show. Doom would come to her rescue, clarifying his decree to find mutants did not mean they were to be enslaved for entertainment.

## Creation

Nerium first appears at the end of X-Men (2021) #28 by Gerry Duggan and Joshua Cassara

## Major Story Arcs

### Fall of X

When Orchis attacked Krakoa, resulting in Krakoan mutants being dead or on the run, Doom's prediction came true. Surviving members of the X-Men attempted to enter Latveria, believing their mutants were being held against their will. Members of the Seven Daggers quickly fought back against the X-Men, eventually defeating them. As a show of good faith, they promised their assistance.

## Powers and Abilities

**Plant Control: **Part of Nerium's physiology is plant matter. As such, she is able to extend her arms, of which she has four, like vines at will.
