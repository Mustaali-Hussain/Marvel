## Brief History

Macon was a former mercenary serving the Hellfire Club's Inner Circle as a trooper.  While his teammates were being held captive, [[Wolverine]] fought Macon and two other troopers, [[Cole]] and [[Reese]], leaving them all severely wounded.  The Inner Circle had the three men converted to be cyborgs.  When [[Donald Pierce]] rebelled against the Inner Circle, Macon went with Pierce and became a Reaver.
