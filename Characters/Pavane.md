## Brief History

Pavane was an agent of [[Carlton Velcro]]and a panther trainer. She was an expert at the use of whips and could command her pet panthers Bidi and Mara to attack at will. She survived the death of Velcro and then became the lover of Mordillo. After his death, it is revealed that Carton survived his death and sent Razor Fist after her. Later she will became the lover of Cat/Shen Kui.
