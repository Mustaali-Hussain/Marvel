> Gazelle was born in New Salem, Colorado and was a member of Salem's Seven.

## Creation

Gazelle was created by Len Wein, George Perez and Joe Sinnott in 1977 and first appeared in Fantastic Four # 186.

## Powers & Abilities

Gazelle could magically assume a mutated form in which she possessed superhuman gymnastic ability and agility. Even in human form she had limited abilities to wield magic.

Her powers were enhanced by the [[Wizard]], amplifying her speed and agility to levels similar to [[Quicksilver]].
