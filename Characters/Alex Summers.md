## History

*Escaping from Sinister*

Born into the wealthy socialite family of Frost and the adventuring family of Summers young Alex was the final addition to the Frost-Summers family. He was named after his deceased uncle, the x-man Havok. When he was a baby him and his siblings were captured by Gambit at the behest of Sinister. Luckily for them, he switched sides and he was later reunited with his family. Once his powers manifested he was enrolled in the Xavier institute. It is believed he still remains there now.
