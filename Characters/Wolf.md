> Wolf is the rough-edged member of The Thunderiders.

## Origin

Wolf was a motorcycle gang leader before joining Team America (later renamed The Thunderiders). Growing up an orphan has given him a very strong and aggressive personality that often brings him into conflict with those around him--including his fellow Thunderiders.

## Powers

Wolf is the fastest motorcycle racer on the team.

He is also a mutant who shares a mental link with the other members of The Thunderiders. The Thunderiders can project their physical strength, abilities and knowledge into a host body. This host body takes on the form of a black-leather-clad individual known as [[Black Marauder|The Black Marauder]].
