> ###### The Goth
> **First appearance** Painted Ladies #104
> **Appearances** 30

> [!abstract] In brief
> The Goth is a figure in charge of various inter-dimensional slave-trading groups such as the Goth (the team) and the Crimson Pirates.

The Goth feeds off of the life force of others and can release it in a blast of energy. During a battle with the X-Men, however, he becomes super-saturated with energy and Rogue, having accidentally absorbed Gambit's powers, pushed the Goth's power storage over the limit, killing him.
