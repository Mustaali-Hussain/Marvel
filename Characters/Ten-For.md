Ten-For is an Autocron, member of a race consisting of sentient robots.  

## Creation

Ten-For was created by Jack Kirby in 1978 and first appeared in Machine Man # 3.  

## Powers & Abilities

He possesses superhuman strength, durability and endurance.His power source is a controlled fission reactor located in his chest. He could fire concussive force blasts as well as beams which paralyzed opponents for a short time. Raising his face plate allows him to fire a concussive blast strong enough to demolish an entire building. He uses jet rockets to fly. His boots are equipped with extendable claws that slash at his opponents. His body contains communication devices which keeps in contact with other Autocrons even across intergalactic distances. His Vertigo Inducer disrupts the equilibrium of other sentient beings, organic or robotic.
