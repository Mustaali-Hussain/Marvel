## Origin

Seer's history and background remain blanket in mystery. She was the youngest member of the ancient Blood that swore to protect the mystic talisman known as the Medallion of Power.

## Major Story Arcs

### Encountering Ghost Rider

Seer possessed the appearance of a young adult female in her early twenties. Seer had black hair and blue eyes and a very interesting black symbol around her left eye. She was virtually unknown until she was identified and attacked by the vampire hunter known as [[Blade]]. He was under the influence of [[Atum the God Eater|Demogorge]] child of [[Gaea]] and Demiurge. Demogorge wanted Blade to rid the Earth of all evil demons. Seer was rescued by the [[Ghost Rider (Ketch)|Ghost Rider (Daniel Ketch)]] and [[Frank Drake|Nightstalkers' Frank Drake]], before Blade could kill her.

### Road To Vengeance

Seer travels with [[Ghost Rider (Ketch)|Dan Ketch]] to rescue to [[Ghost Rider (Blaze)|Johnny Blaze]] from [[Centurious]] who is trying to get a part of the Medallion of Power. They come back to Pennsylvania where [[Wolff]], [[Kody]] and [[Clara Menninger]] are waiting for Blaze. Quinn, a member of Quentin Carnival, helps to Blaze and implants the Hellfire armor on him.
