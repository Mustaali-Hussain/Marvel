## Origin

Rayshaun was a young boy whom watched the arrest of black superhero Rage, and Sam Wilson's struggle with the U.S. government while being Captain America. At a certain point, Rayshaun decided to take matters into his own hands. He painted his face as the mask of the arrested superhero Rage and acted out in violence.

## Creation

Rayshaun Lucas was created by Nick Spencer and Daniel Acuna and first appeared (unnamed in a cameo) in Captain America: Sam Wilson #18 (2017). He first appeared as the new Patriot in the pages of Secret Empire: Brave New World issue 2.
