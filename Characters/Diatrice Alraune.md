## Origin

She is the only daughter of Marc Spector (Moon Knight) and Marlene Alraune. It was later revealed by Yehya Badr (Hunter's Moon) that her very existence is impossible, as Khonshu's fists are incapable of fathering children.

## Powers and Abilities

### Living Weapon

Her very existence poses a threat to the Moon God, Khonshu, which is why he renders all his followers—men and women alike—incapable of having children. This is because one of the three items required to perform a ritual to kill the Moon God is a biological child of one of his "Fists."

### Weaknesses

Although her very existence poses a threat to a god, she has the same weaknesses as an ordinary human and can be killed just as easily as anyone else.
