> ###### Duchess Belladonna
> **First appearance** Death-Duel at Daytona Beach! #3
> **Appearances** 14

> [!abstract] In brief
> Duchess Belladonna was a leading aristocrat of Homeworld who helped Baron Karza maintain the loyalty of the aristocracy. She lived to become the record-holder of the oldest non-enhanced person in Homeworld's history. Baron Karza claimed to have known her for about 900 years. He at last offered her a younger body. She accepted but the body belonged to Slug. She betrayed Karza to help the youth. Both women were later killed by Karza in retaliation.
