> A student of the Pan-Asian School for the Unusually Gifted suffering from muscular dystrophy who wears an advanced suit.

## Origin

Gang is a boy with genius intellect, who designed and built a mobility mech suit to helps him live with multiple sclerosis.

## Creation

Gang was created by writer Matt Kindt and artist Steven Sanders. He first appeared in Infinity: The Heist #1.

## Major Story Arcs

### Infinity: The Heist

*Gang commanding the combined student forces*

Gang is one of three students representing The Pan-Asian School for the Unusually Gifted in the Contest of Champions. When the forces of Thanos invade Earth, Headmasters [[Jimmy Woo]] and [[Death (Javeed)|Sanjar Javeed]] place the students in a Panic Room escape pod that blasts into the planet's upper atmosphere. They receive a distress call from Atlantis, calling for all aid in response to the Black Order's assault. [[Gang]] is able to override the parental controls and crash the Panic Room into Wakanda, where himself, [[Shri]] and [[Pom Pom]] encounter students from the Wakandan School of Alternative Studies. The Pan-Asian students convince their Wakandan counterparts that Atlantis requires help, and as they travel over the ocean to the distress beacon, the Panic Room is hit by a damaged Avengers Quinjet, carrying the combined students of Avengers Academy, the Jean Grey School, Braddock Academy and Future Foundation. A large scale battle erupts but they are too late to save Atlantis, so Latverian student [[Demona]] teleports the entire group to the Thanos' next target: Wakanda. There [[Shuri|Princess Shuri]] is fighting a losing war against an alien leviathon, but with the combined help of every student the leviathon is defeated and Wakanda's safety is secured.

### Marvel Voices

*Defending himself from bullies*

Gang's time as a student at the Pan-Asian School for the Unusually Gifted has been marred by the long-term bullying he has suffered at the hands of the Dul Twins. When new student [[Crescent]] arrives at the school she is instantly targeted by the twins in the hallway, who steal her magic mask and turn its host [[Io]] on his friend. Gang interrupts the beating, helping Dan Bi up

and trying to be a hero by standing up to the two bullies, however they shove him in a locker and crack his helmet in the process. Bi lies injured and betrayed, but Gang activates the Battle Mode on his mobility suit, retrieves the Half Moon Talbak from the Dul Twins by force, and returns Io to Crescent. Together, the trio of Gang, Crescent and Io beat the Dul Twins in front of a crowd of students. Enraged at being defeated, the Dul Twins merge into their Oni form and begin destroying the school before Professor Javeed intervenes. Dan Bi and the twins are suspended for fighting in school and, while Bi comes to say goodbye to Gang and apologize for involving him, Gang is elated that he finally got to stand up for himself with the help of his new friends.

## Powers and Abilities

*Battle Mode*

Gang is a tech genius who built a specialized mech suit and helmet that helps his mobility. The suit can hover and transforms into a tanky Battle Mode for beating up bullies. In Battle Mode, Gang possesses superhuman strength and durability.
