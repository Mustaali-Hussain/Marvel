> [!abstract] In brief
> Aerika Harkness a.k.a. "Crimson Curse", was an exceptionally gifted elemental sorceress for team "A-Next".  She was reserved, humble, and one of the teams strongest members.  She bravely sacrificed herself in order to stop her universe's version of Doctor Doom.

## Brief History

Crimson Curse is the daughter of [[Agatha Harkness]], the sorceress who trained the [[Scarlet Witch]]. She was trained to be a hero by [[Hawkeye|Clint Barton]] and was a member of the Dream Team before they joined A Next.

She was tragically killed by pushing her powers too far to stop [[Doctor Doom]] from bringing his team of Avengers to this dimension.

## Powers

Crimson Curse was a powerful sorceress who, through magic drawn from the Natural Order, was able to control the earth's key elements: fire, earth, water, and wind.
