> A mutant Skrull alien who is a member of Cadre K. She possesses powerful telepathic abilities.

## Creation

Z'Cann was created by Kurt Busiek and Jerry Ordway in 2000 and first appeared in Maximum Security Dangerous Planet # 1.

## Powers & Abilities

Z'Cann possesses telepathic abilities that allow her communicate with others and control their minds. It was implied she would one day be as powerful as Professor X.

She also possesses the shape-shifting powers inherent to all Skrulls.
