> "The Campus Captain America," a college student in Boston who took up the mantle of Captain America to protect her roommate.

## Origin

Arielle was a sophomore at Hargrove University. She made a plan to get incriminating evidence from a frat guy who assaulted her roommate. She and her friends would dress up like versions of Captain America and run a diversion, while she got her friend's phone back from the assailant's room.

## Creation

Arielle debuted in The United States of Captain America #4 by Christopher Cantwell and Ron Lim. Her backstory was told in a back-up feature by Alyssa Wong and Jodi Nishijima.

## Major Story Arcs

### The United States of Captain America

The imposter Captain Americas used [[Captain America|Cap]]'s shield to gain access to NORAD where the [[Hate-Monger]] spirit was being kept. Cap called in reinforcements through the Captains Network, reaching Arielle for the first time. She traveled from Boston, picking up [[Aaron Fischer]] on the way, helping Cap and his team, now including US Agent, respond to the attack on NORAD.
