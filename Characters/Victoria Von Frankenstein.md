> ###### Victoria Von Frankenstein

> [!abstract] In brief
> Daughter of Ludwig Frankenstein and friend to his experiments

Victoria Frankenstein is a daughter to [[Ludwig Frankenstein]] (a [[Silver Surfer]]enemy) and older sister to Veronica Frankenstein. She has inherited the title of "Baroness von Frankenstein" and the hereditary castle of her family. She acts as "Mother" to the Children of the Damned, various surviving creations of the Frankenstein family. She is a pacifist and a healer but has found herself under attack often, mostly by people trying to use her as a hostage against the [[Frankenstein]]monster. They reluctantly consider each other family.
