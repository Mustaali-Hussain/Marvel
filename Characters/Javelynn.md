> ###### Javelynn
> **First appearance** Shafted #1
> **Appearances** 12

> [!abstract] In brief
> Professional mercenary and thief.

Javelynn and [[Trickshot (Chisholm)|Trick Shot]] were hired guns for the Secret Empire and [[Viper]] as they created canine humanoids as biological combat units. One of the canine humanoids escaped the compound and was rescued by [[Hawkeye]] after it was shot multiple times. Hawkeye named the canine creature Rover and it revealed to him the experiments going on through pictures. Hawkeye and Rover located the Secret Empire hideout in a jungle and fought their way into the compound. Javelynn was left solo after Trick Shot betrayed Viper and she attempted to pierce her enemies with her trademark weapons. Rover evaded the attack and Hawkeye knocked her out with an arrow to the head.

## Creation

Javelynn was created by Chuck Dixon and Scott Kolins in 1994 and first appeared in Hawkeye # 1.

 

## Weapons & Abilities

Javelynn carries several miniaturized javelins around her wrists and lower legs. Javelynn uses them for range and striking attacks.
