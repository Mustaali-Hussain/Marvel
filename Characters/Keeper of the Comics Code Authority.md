> **Appearances** 10

> [!abstract] In brief
> The Keeper of the Comics Code Authority is the person that observes the Marvel Universe for scenes of controversy and violence in order to cancel them.

The Keeper of the Comics Code Authority first appeared in Not Brand Echh #5 (December, 1967). His stated mission is to protect observers of the Marvel Universe from scenes of controversy and violence. He is a moralist and "preserver of human decency," or in other words, a censor.
