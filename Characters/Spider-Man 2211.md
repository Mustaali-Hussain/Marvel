> [!abstract] In brief
> Dr. Jamoff "Max" Borne is the Spider-Man of the year 2211. He is part of a future organization called the Time-Spinners, which seeks to prevent disruptions in the time field, and prevent any imbalance in the mainstream universe. He was killed by the Chameleon of 2211.

## Origin

*Spider-Man vs. Hobgoblin 2211*

Dr. Jamoff Borne is from a future organization, which prevents disruptions in the time field. In his first appearance, he helps Spider-Man and Spider-Man 2099 defeat Hobgoblin 2211, who is really his daughter, Robin Borne, who has become bitter from his neglect of his own child and being too focused on his work. When Hobgoblin 2211 introduced Uncle Ben from another reality to bring imbalance to the mainstream universe, this Uncle Ben was killed by the Chameleon of 2211, who then took on his shape. The Hobgoblin of 2211 was accidentally killed by Spider-Man when he threw her retcon bomb back at her, thus erasing her from all reality. Jamoff was killed by "Uncle Ben" who was really the Chameleon of 2211 in disguise. Later, the Chameleon of 2211 was also killed when Spider-Man put the helmet of Spider-Man 2211 on his head and commanded it to punish him; this caused him to switch places with a death row inmate who was about to be executed via electric chair; this fried him to a crisp. But, that is another story.

## Powers

*Cybernetic Arms & Guided Webbing*

Dr. Jamoff Borne posses superhuman strength, endurance, and agility. He wears a special power suit with four mentally controlled, cybernetic arms. His armor is also bulletproof and includes guided webbing, electro-webbing sensors, rocket boots, and time travel technology. He also posses some possible future telling technology inside his helmet.
