> [!abstract] In brief
> A new hero from Icon; Superior changed the life of Multiple Sclerosis sufferer, Simon Pooni.

Simon used to be a very popular athlete until his condition worsened. Leaving him wheelchair bound and lonely. His only friend was Chris. One night, Simon was visited by an alien resembling a chimpanzee who told him that he had the oppurtunity to make a wish. Simon wished to be like Superior and was suddenly transformed into an exact replica of his hero.  

 

## Powers & Abilities

 

As Superior, Simon Pooni has a vast array of superhuman powers. These include vast superhuman strength, flight speed and resistance to physical injury.  

 

His strength was sufficient to drag a submarine ashore after pulling it for nearly 20 minutes. He also lifted a space station and flew it from downtown New York to Central Park and stopping a moving passenger train without budging. His flight speed is so fast that he flew from New York to the North Pole in mere seconds. He has also demonstrated super hearing and heat vision.
