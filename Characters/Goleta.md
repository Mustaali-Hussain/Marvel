## Origin

Not much is known about Goleta, except that she lives in the strange domain known as Weirdworld and hates sorcerers for some reason.

## Creation

Goleta was created by Sam Humphries and Michael Del Mundo and first appeared in Weirdworld Vol.2 issue 1 (2016).

## Major Story Arcs

### Meeting Becca

When a plain crashed in Weirdworld, the ensuing chaos drew Goleta towards the spot. She saved a young Earth girl known as [[Becca Rodriguez]] and took her in to train her in the survival arts on how to survive in this strange land.
