> Ms. Farrell is a reporter for the Daily Bugle and head of The Pulse

## Brief History

Graduated from Notre Dame with a degree in journalism. Contracted by Daily Bugle as reporter. She hates capes.

She has a fish called Bilbo Baggins.

### Deadline

During a investigation into a group of superhero homicides, Kat finds the murderer to be a judge named Michael Hart. During this time in her life Kat's pet fish Bilbo Baggins is killed in an attempt to keep her off Judge Hart's back.

## Creation

Kat Farrell was created by Bill Rosemann and Guy Davis in 2002 and first appeared in Deadline # 1.
