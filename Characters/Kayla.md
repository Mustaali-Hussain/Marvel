> [!abstract] In brief
> Kayla is a Krakoan mutant who enters into a relationship with Colossus.

Kayla befriended [[Colossus]] after Piotr grew weary of conflict and bloodshed and retired from active duty to become a farmer, growing Krakoa's medicinal crops on his patch of land.

## Powers

**Hydrokinesis: **Kayla has the mutant ability to manipulate water, which she can use to fire blasts of water at enemies, or to rapidly drain someone's body of water.
