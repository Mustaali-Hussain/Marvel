## Major Story Arcs

### Pleasant Hill

*Skater kids illusion*

During Blue Streak's first heist with his new crew, they were interrupted by the Avengers. After they were arrested, they were sent to Pleasant Hill.

Pleasant Hill was an experimental prison created in secret by Maria Hill with SHIELD resources. By using the cosmic cube, she created a *Pleasantville-*like small town. Villains were hypnotized and camouflaged to keep them pacified. In The Fast Five's instance, they were convinced they were a couple of harmless skater kids.

Once the illusion failed, the Fast Five went right back to robbing banks. In this case, the Pleasant Hill bank was a cache of most of the locked-up villains' weaponry. They were stopped by SHIELD agents, [[Quasar (Kincaid)|Avril Kincaid]] and [[Quasar|Wendall Vaughn]]. They would later boast about how it took two Quasars to take them down.

### War of the Realms

*Death of Gold Rush*

After the War of the Realms, The Fast Five hit a Damage Control vehicle that was moving a mix of Dark Elf and Asgardian weaponry that was being transported to a safe location. 

[[Jane Foster]], as the new Valkyrie, tried to stop them, but Gold Rush, as the team's coward, made his escape with Dragonfang, the sword of the original [[Valkyrie]]. Jane was motivated to get the weapon of her friend back, but she was forced to stay behind and capture the four other members of the Fast Five.

While making his escape, Gold Rush is attacked by [[Bullseye]] and killed with Dragonfang, who needed it for a job.
