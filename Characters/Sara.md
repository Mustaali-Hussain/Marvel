## Major Story Arcs

Not much is known about her parents but Sara would spend the most of her early life in a Nunery. She would then leave later on. At that point she would get a ride from a truck driver who tries to rape her but is then saved by Johnny Blaze. As the two go to see Caretaker, they find him dying after an attack by the former Ghost Rider: Danny Ketch. It is there that Sara is revealed to be the new Caretaker. From there, Sara and Johnny Blaze go on a hunt in the Himalayas by passing from a magical door in her granddad's basement in search of Danny Ketch and to stop Zadkeil.

However Zadkeil gains his goal of opening the gates of Heaven. Sara later sets up the plans to bury Caretaker and is later visited by two Ghost Riders of the future to not give up the fight that it is not to late.

Sara finds Blaze and the two find info from two of Zadkiel's angel's that the Anti-Christ is the only one that can stop him. She and Blaze go to NY where they need to find Anton Satan the Anti-Christ. She is shocked when Blaze introduces her to their "allies" the Son of Satan and Cutter. They save Anton from Jaine and head to an abandoned Luna Park while he molests her. Her power is revealed to be the same as Master Pandemonium's but oppositely her belly is a means to travel to Heaven.

She ends up battling Deacon and succeeds in avenging the Nuns she grew up with whom the madman had killed. Sara paralyzed him by cutting his neck with her blade.

### Video Games

### Marvel's Midnight Suns (2022)

*Caretaker in Midnight Suns*

Sara appears as a major supporting character in Firaxis Games' tactical role-playing game, wherein the eponymous Midnight Suns are gathered to combat the threat of Lilith, Mother of Demons. Unlike the comics, Sara is depicted as older and far more experienced, most likely melding her character with her grandfather, the original Caretaker. The game also depicts Sara as the sister of Lilith, the aunt of Hunter, the game's protagonist, and the former lover of Agatha Harkness. Sara commands the Midnight Suns from their headquarters, The Abbey, sending them on missions and acting as resident expert on Lilith's past and powers

Throughout the game's story, Sara clashes with multiple Marvel heroes, including Nico Minoru and Magik. The young sorceresses seek to free the Scarlet Witch from Lilith's control, however Sara harbors deep resentment towards Wanda for her role in the death of Agatha, which blinds her judgement in leading the team effectively. Sara also withholds a secret alliance with Johnny Blaze, who has been safeguarding a page of the Darkhold for Sara for decades. The player can choose to side with Caretaker over these issues, or rebel against her and make their mark as a new leader of the Midnight Suns.

Once the Suns are able to free Wanda Maximoff from Lilith's thrall, Wanda discovers that the ghost of Agatha Harkness has been visiting and guiding Hunter in their quest. However, nobody but Hunter can see or speak with Agatha. Using her own magicks, Wanda prepares a ritual alongside Hunter to bring forth Agatha into this plane of reality, so that she may return to The Abbey and Sara. Knowing that Sara would not approve of the ritual given its risk of damaging Agatha's eternal soul, the two do not tell her. This proves unwise as Sara discovers their plot during the ritual to tether Agatha to the Earthly plane, and her shock and rage disrupts the energy flow, giving the Hell Lord Mephisto the opportunity to take possession of Agatha's spirit.
