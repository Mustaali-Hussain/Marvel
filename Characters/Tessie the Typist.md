> A very temperamental secretary in 40's Marvel.

The very first of the romance heroines that Marvel/Timely created in the golden age.

A beautiful and blonde secretary, that had an interesting and unique attitude to her life and job. Acting clumsily, being irritable, dumb and a bit of a cloudcuckoolander. She made life difficult for her boss, the people that she attended and her own boyfriend, [[Skidsy Spencer]].
