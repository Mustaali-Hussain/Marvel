## Origin

Shellshock was a minor criminal that partnered with two other men named Ivan and Live-Wire. They were given special weapons by Psycho Man and forced to battle members of the Fantastic Four, the Inhumans and the Black Panther. They were ultimately defeated and sent to jail. Shellshock and Live-Wire escaped from prison and teamed up once again to steal some statues created by Alicia Masters. This led to another confrontation with the Thing and they were beaten once again when the Thing was assisted by some members of the Yancy Street Gang. Shellshock would later reappear in a new costume and upgraded shockblaster when he and numerous other villains tried to take out the Thing while he was in the hospital. Shellshock fled the scene when Ant Man sent thousands of ants to swarm him.

Shellshock would meet up with a group of other minor villains at the Bar with No Name where they were all slaughtered by the Scourge of the Underworld.

## **Powers and Abilities**

Shellshock is a skilled marksman that was given a powerful shockblaster weapon by Psycho Man. This gun had numerous attachments that could fire concussive blasts, miniature missles, gas grenades, microwave blasts and even canisters that would release some type of gummy, sticky residue.
