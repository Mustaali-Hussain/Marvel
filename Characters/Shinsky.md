## Origin

Respected Polish geneticist Doctor Wladyslav Shinski shares the creation of Adam Warlock and Kismet and the resurrection of Jean Grey, who had been placed in a cocoon by the Phoenix Force. Later he worked with Maris Morlak, whom had taken on the identity of Crucible, but later slew Morlak and replaced him as Crucible.  

 

## Creation

Wladyslav Shinski is a Marvel comics character created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby first appearing in Fantastic Four #66 released in 1967.  

 

## Powers and Abilities

Wladyslav Shinski is incredibly intelligent, possessing genius level intellect, possessing particular expertise in genetics. Shinski also often utilizes a suit of armor which grants additional abilities and powers.
