> ###### Random
> **Real name** Marshall Evan Stone III

> [!abstract] In brief
> Random first appeared as a bounty hunter with the power to adapt his body to counteract any mutant power and to fire projectiles from his gun-morphed forearms.

## Origin

Not much is known about Random's past except that he really is just a young boy who can shapeshift his body into almost anything he wants. He changed himself into looking like a taught mercenary and became just that, a mercenary for hire.

## Creation

Random was created by Peter David and Joe Quesada and first appeared in X-Factor issue 88 (1993).

## Mayor Story Arcs

### X-Factor

He was hired by X-Factor to help in a few missions as a reluctant ally. He developed a friendship/crush on Polaris. Later, it became apparent that Random was an agent of the Dark Beast, who used him to capture Havok. Random's true nature was also revealed, that of a young man with little control over his body's gelatinous properties who had received aid from McCoy, for which the Doctor was now demanding payment in the form of service.

### Neverland

Random was seen at the concentration camp of Neverland and was guessed to have died there along with the other prisoners. He later re-appeared as one of Exodus' Acolytes along with Frenzy and Tempo, but was beaten by Northstar and Aurora.

### Messiah Complex

Random was shown fighting alongside many others in the last battle of the Messiah Complex.

### X-Men: Legacy

Random was shown as a member of the Acolytes and Exodus still as their leader, when [[Professor X|Charles Xavier]] goes to New Avalon. Xavier was able to convince Exodus to disband the Acolytes in order to help mutants in other ways. Random decided to relocate to San Francisco with the X-Men.

### Second Coming

*Utopia*

Random was seen during the siege with mostly mutants that were regularly villains, like [[Toad]] and [[Dragoness]]. Random and the former villains helped defend Utopia during [[Bastion|Bastion's]] attack. Shortly after Second Coming, Random joins members of the X-Men on a volunteer mission to help rebuild San Francisco.

### Brotherhood and Krakoa

When Joseph (posing as Magneto) reformed the Brotherhood of Mutants, Random was one of the members. He later appeared as a resident on Krakoa island.

## Powers

*Violent Acts of Random!*

Random has a form of reactive shape shifting. He can adapt to counter any powers used against him. He can also fire protoplasmic projectiles from his arms and has a degree of super-strength and invulnerability.
