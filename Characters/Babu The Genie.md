> [!abstract] In brief
> Babu is the apprentice to Jeannie in the Hanna-Barbera series Jeannie.

Babu has been an apprentice genie for 1,500 years because he is cowardly, incompetent, and often makes mistakes. He is also friends with Shaggy and Scooby-Doo and for a time was a member of their sports team, the Scooby Doobies, during the Laff-A-Lympics.
