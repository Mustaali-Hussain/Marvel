> ###### Valtorr
> **First appearance** The Wrath of Gods #49
> **Appearances** 13

> [!abstract] In brief
> Valtorr is a mystical demon that is known for using vapors as servants.

One of the eight extra-dimensional deities that took part in the Wager of the Octessence. He created the Verdant Vial of Valtorr, which gave the portion of his powers to his Exemplar , [[Decay]]. Valtorr can grant a magician who invokes his power  a variety of spells, most of them are connected with vapors. Valtorr also has an ethereal body.
