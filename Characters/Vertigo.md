## Origin

Vertigo is a member of the Salem's Seven, daughter to Nicholas Scratch and granddaughter of Agatha Harkness.

## Creation

Vertigo was created by Len Wein, George Perez and Joe Sinnott in 1977 and first appeared in Fantastic Four # 186.

## Powers & Abilities

As her name suggests, Vertigo has the power to disorient her opponents. This ability was augmented and amplified by the Wizard.

## In Other Media

### Agatha All Along (2024)

*Okwui Okpokwasili as Vertigo*

Vertigo appears in the Disney+ show, portrayed by actress Okwui Okpokwasili. To answer for her crimes of abusing the Darkhold and killing fellow witches, Agatha Harkness is being hunted by the Salem Seven. Vertigo leads the Seven and attempts to break into Harkness' home in Westview, before their target flees on The Witches' Road.

When Rio Vidal joins the coven on the Road, the Salem Seven emerge through the opening and pursue the witches relentlessly. Vertigo leads her sisters as they breach the borders of the Fourth Trial, however Lilia Calderu has led them into a trap and sacrifices herself to kill the Salem Seven.
