> ###### Briquette
> **First appearance** Belles & Whistles #80
> **Appearances** 37

> [!abstract] In brief
> The strongwoman of the Hell's Belles.

Gifted with super-strength, durability and molten hot skin, Briquette is the malicious strongwoman of the Hell's Belles, a terrorist group employed by [[Cyber]].

She prefers to do things her own way and dislikes taking orders from others.

She can use her powers to melt through things by touch - she once used this to cause a building to explode by heating up the boiler and later grabbed [[Quicksilver]] and began to melt his flesh until a heat-insulated [[Strong Guy]] stopped her.

While all of the other Hell's Belles have been revealed as depowered after M-Day, nothing has been revealed about Briquette. Presumably, she retains her powers.
