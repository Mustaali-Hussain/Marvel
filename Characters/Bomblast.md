## Origin

Parmenter was a Guardsmen for The Vault. Bomblast's first target was Venom along with the other Jury members. After Venom, The Jury went after Spider-Man. More Recently The Jury has battled the new Thunderbolts.

## Creation

Bomblast was created by David Michelinie and Mark Bagley in 1993 and first appeared in *Venom: Lethal Protector* #2.

## Powers & Abilities

Bomblast wears an armored suit based on the design of the Guardsman armor that provides him enhanced strength, flight, and various weapons. Bomblast is equipped with a rifle that fires plasma bolts and small hovering discs that attach to his feet to provide flight.
