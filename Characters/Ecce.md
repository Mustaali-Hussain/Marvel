> ###### Ecce
> **First appearance** Galactus Found! #168
> **Appearances** 14
> **Also known as** Watcher

> [!abstract] In brief
> Ecce is one of the few named Watchers known to exist and observe events throughout the mulitiverse. He observed the birth of Galactus.

Ecce is a member of The Watchers an eons old, vastly powerful race of extraterrestrials who have pledged an oath not to interfere in the goings on of the universe. Ecce was the Watcher who was present at and "watched" the incubation and hatching of The World Devourer [[Galactus]]. Ecce had the abilities to stop the birth of Galactus before he reached full power, but upheld his oath to merely observe and catalog the event.
