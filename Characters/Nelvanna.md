> ###### Nelvanna
> **First appearance** The Importance Of Being Deadly #7
> **Appearances** 28

> [!abstract] In brief
> Inuit goddess of the northern lights.

Nelvanna is an Inuit Goddess and the daughter of [[Hodiak]]. The conflict of these group of Gods with The Great Beasts left both groups trapped in their respective dimensions. Since The Great Beasts continued to influence the Earth through various avatars of them, the Gods had to retaliate.  

 

Nelvanna seduced mortal Richard Easton and convinced him to enter their realm. Their union resulted in Easton growing mad and Nelvanna becoming pregnant. She gave birth to a daughter, [[Snowbird]]. The Inuit then influenced terrestrial affairst through said daughter. Recently, Nelvanna was slain by [[Mikaboshi|The Chaos King]] moments after managing to sending her daughter back to Earth.
