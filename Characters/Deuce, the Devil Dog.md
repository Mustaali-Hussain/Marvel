> ###### Deuce, the Devil Dog
> **First appearance** Peter Porker The Spectacular Spider-Ham - Nevermore #3
> **Appearances** 21

> [!abstract] In brief
> Deuce was a retired police dog. He was purchased by Foggy Nelson as Matt Murdock's seeing eye dog. Foggy later lost the dog in a poker game to Weasel. Weasel turned over the dog to his boss Deadpool. Who in turn gave it to Blind Al. Who enjoyed tormenting it.

[[Blind Al]] seems to have successfully trained Deuce. By tying a string to Deuce and having him pull in the direction of the target, Blind Al is able to be a decent markswoman.

She used this tactic when attacking Deadpool due to his being a lousy friend.
