> ###### Lexi
> **First appearance** The Singer & Her Song #30
> **Appearances** 16

> [!abstract] In brief
> Nothing is known about this Gladiator beyond the fact that he looks kinda like a purple gargolye, has wings, and three tails.

Lexi was a member of the Gladiators which were a group of mutants that participated in an illegal, underground fight club. Lexi was present in the training room after Magma and Sunspot of the New Mutants were forced to stay with the Gladiators to prevent runaway children from being slaughtered. Dazzler would return to the Gladiators and try to befriend Sunspot and Magma but they were wary of her actions. During the training session, Dazzler was caught in the arms of Sunspot and he flung her towards the air. Lexi caught her in mid-air and brought her back down safely.  

## Creation

Lexi was created by Chris Claremont and Bill Sienkiewicz in 1985 and first appeared in New Mutants # 30.  

## Powers & Abilities

Lexi is a mutant with a gargoyle appearance that has three tails and the capability of flight.
