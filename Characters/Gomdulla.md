## Brief History

Gomdulla posed as a mummy, but was actually an alien bent on conquering earth. He was living in Egypt but was eventually defeated by some smart humans.

 

### Trivia

In his first appearance, Gomdulla is called the living PharOAH, instead of PharAOH. Years later, Stan Lee wrote in the Official No-Prize Book that he made a mistake in spelling the name of an Iron Man villain called the Mad Pharoah, instead of Pharaoh. Clearly it was not a mistake on it's own, because Gomdulla got the same mistake some years before.
