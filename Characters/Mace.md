## Origins

*Shigeru Ezaki "Mace." Cyborg bio-engineer/Ninja*

*Shigeru Ezaki* also known as **Mace** is a very powerful bio engineer cyborg formally from the Sunrise Society, a group of tech-armored soldiers lead by his "Brother" from where he escaped. **Mace** in his escape showed very fancy acrobatic skills; he also showed to be a *great strategist* and marksman and uses his surrounding very well due to his ninja training in the Sunrise Society. Mace has a healing factor similar to [[Wolverine]] and uses a mace along with a plasma blaster than can only be fired by himself.
