> ###### Detective Flint
> **First appearance** The Nightmare of Morpheus #12

> **Also known as** Lieutenant Flint

> [!abstract] In brief
> A NYPD detective who handles the Moon Knight-related cases.

Detective Flint is an NYPD Detective who handles all the Moon Knight-related cases that pop up. He has a habit of saying, "Sure, sure," in almost every sentence. He doesn't exactly approve of Moon Knight, though. He was even caught saying, "Some people get maybe Spider-Man. I get the king of rejects," showing that he isn't exactly happy to be dealing with Moon Knight, or any of his murder cases. 

## Power and Abilities 

Flint is a skilled police detective and criminal investigator who possesses no powers greater than a regular man his own age. He carries a police-issue sidearm for protection.
