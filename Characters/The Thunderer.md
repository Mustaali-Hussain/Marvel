> [!abstract] In brief
> Golden age hero.

He used a microphone in his costume to amplify his voice to scare his enemies. 

Later, he changed costume identities to the Black Avenger. 

Alongside of the other heroes, the Thunderer participated in an air drop on a nazi stronghold. 

The Thunderer attended a reunion of second world war costumed heroes.

In Marvel Comics #1000, it was revealed that he used the Eternity Mask as part of a secret governement project. The mask give him strenght comparabel to any of his foes.
