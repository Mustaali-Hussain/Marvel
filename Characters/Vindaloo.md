## Vindaloo

Few details have been revealed about Vindaloo's past. He was one of the newer members of the mutant villains known as the Acolytes, Vindaloo was loyal to the Acolytes until his possible death on Genosha when the island was obliterated by sentinels , but in the recent Messiah cross-over he was revealed alive and not only kept his powers after M-Day but is still an active member fighting against the X-Men. He is tracked because of Exodus' whereabouts regarding the new incarnation of the Acolytes.

## Powers

Vindaloo was able to produce flammable gel that ignites with air and may become offensive by converting it into blasts from his fists.

## Television

### Wolverine And The X-Men

Vindaloo's appearance was in episode 6 of Wolverine and the X-Men, although his powers did not ignite on contact with air, and instead, his powers were just described and portrayed as generating sticky goo from his hands.

He was among those mutants that were on board on a ship named Avalon. Suddenly, Spiral and a group of Reavers have attacked the ship. Since no one believes that their powers can work against the villainous team, they became hopeless. Nightcrawler then formed a team of mutants with Squidboy, Network, Shatter, Pixie and Vindaloo.

Vindaloo sustained the sticky goo and let it flow with a hose that Squidboy holds underwater to fix the damage of the ship. After the encounter, he headed to Genosha.

## Creation

Vindaloo was created by Fabian Nicieza and Alan Davis in 1999 and first appeared in Uncanny X-Men #366
