> [!abstract] In brief
> A mercenary and human trafficker with the powers identical to the Spot. He is currently a villian of Daredevil.

## Origin

Coyote was an expert smuggler whose creativity, smarts and greed made him perfect for a mysterious organization's plans to destroy Daredevil. The organization put Coyote, against his will, through the same procedure that created the Spot and aimed him at Daredevil.

## Creation

Coyote was created by Mark Waid and Paolo Rivera.
