## Origin

The person who would eventually be known only as Director None was an agent of Bravo Charlie, a subdivision of W.A.N.D. nicknamed The Blasphemy Cartel. They had been studying a way to use blockchain technology to simulate a digital Tibetan prayer wheel. Unfortunately, their trial run rose the dead prisoners of W.A.N.D.'s prison, Bigbox, and almost got them found out. The cartel was able to learn from their mistakes, so, when S.H.I.E.L.D. folded after the fall of the Secret Empire, they used that opportunity to run the blockchain once more. This time they erased the memory of W.A.N.D. from all non-agent's minds so that they could rebrand as a criminal organization. The price for this spell was Director None's own identity.

## Creation

Director None debuted in Strange (2022) #3 by Jed MacKay and Marcelo Ferreira

## Major Story Arcs

### The Revenants

As part of their black market protection, Director None was raising dead superheroes as a enslaved revenants. These resurrections were considered disrespectful by Death, who chose the late [[Doctor Strange]] as her Harvestman to take them out, in exchange for his life back.

The cartel was forced to pull out their big guns when they raised Sentry, powered by ten million ghosts. His power was too much for Strange and [[Clea]]. The sorcerer supreme duo was forced to combine their might into a single and force the ghosts out of [[Sentry]]. This caused an explosion that took out the cartel's headquarters and seemingly all members of the cartel.
