## Brief History

Avona was one of the first recruits for the 50 State Initiative and was soon brought over to become a team member of the superhero group known as The Order. She was a gifted swordswoman, given a sword with integrated talking AI called "Bluetooth" which Allen had a psychic link with. After defeating the Infernal Man Avona went out to party with a few of her teammates with was caught on tape. Due to violation of her Code of Conduct agreement she had her powers removed and was let go from The Order. It is later shown that she had died on a train which is later revealed to of been killed by The Black Dalilas.
