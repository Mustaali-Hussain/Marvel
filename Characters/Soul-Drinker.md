> [!abstract] In brief
> Extra-Dimensional Demon

Soul Drinker was a demon used by D'Ken for executions. The Soul Drinker was summoned to absorb the soul of an individual, then returned to where he came from. He was first seen when summoned by D'Ken to absorb the soul of Lilandra. She was rescued by Nightcrawler and the Soul Drinker instead absorbed the soul of the nearest attendant of D'Ken. Then it returned to where it came from.
