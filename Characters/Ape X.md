## Origin

After finding a Lucha Libre mask, Roy Reyna discovered that when he uses the mask he'll transform into a giant gorilla with superpowers.

## Other Versions

### Ape-verse

A small ape turns into a giant gorilla after he dons the Lucha Libre mask. He teamed up with [[Speedball]], and was member of the Ape-Vengers.

## Powers

When he puts on a magical Mexican wrestling mask, Ape X transform into a giant gorilla with super strength and with the power to blow energy bolts from his hands.
