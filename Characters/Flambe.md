> A mutant terrorist and a member of  Hell's Belles.

## Origin

She comes from France, and after discovering her mutant powers, she became a criminal. After being defeated alongside Cyber at the hands of the X-Factor team led by Havok , Flambé was not heard from for years. She was revealed to have lost her powers due to the Scarlet Witch 's "Decimation".

## Powers

Flambé can manipulate oxygen molecules to increase a fire’s size and temperature. She can also focus flame into concentrated jet streams. She carries flaming pokers to initiate her flame blasts.
