> ###### Vulture 2099
> **First appearance** Downtown Is Deadly #6
> **Appearances** 34

In the year 2099, a new Vulture is a recurring foe of [[Spider-Man 2099]]; this version of the Vulture wears high-tech armoring, possessing metallic wings and claws, as well as a helmet. Vulture 2099 is apparently insane, a cannibal (he refers to those he eats as "corpses") he wishes to have complete control of the sky and is the leader of a gang known as "The Freakers".

When most of the civilized world is flooded, the Vulture creates a floating boat-colony off the shores of the Savage Land. His new crew is called the 'Wild Boys'. There, he recovers an old associate, [[Wulff]], whom his gang torments.
