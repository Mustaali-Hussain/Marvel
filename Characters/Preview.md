## Origin

Jessica Vale was a young mutant girl with the ability of short-term precognition, usually minutes in advance.

## Creation

Preview was created by Nunzio DeFilippis, Christina Weir and Michael Ryan and first appeared in New X-Men issue 5 (2004).

## Major Story Arcs

### Joining the Xavier Institute

*Yearbook Photo*

Preview was a member of the Paragon Squad, once taught by [[Wolfsbane|Rahne Sinclair]] but then by [[Magma]] after the relationship between Rahne and [[Elixir]] was exposed. She was known as the biggest drama queen because she never acted on anything if she knew the outcome would be bad. 

**M-Day and depowermend**

Preview was confirmed depowered on M-Day, and left the school. She was, unlike her sister [[Network]], not present on the bus that was blown up by [[William Stryker|Reverant Stryker's]] Purifiers when they launched an attack on the school. With her sister dead, Preview left the school and was not heard from anymore since then. 

 

 

## Powers

 

Flash Precognition: This allowed her to see into the near future. The extent of her powers were not revealed, but most likely not that strong, since she did not foresee M-Day.
