## Brief History

David Munroe worked as a photojournalist who fell in love and later married N'Dare, a princess of a tribe in Kenya. Together they traveled back to Manhattan which is where the daughter Storm was born. Shortly after her birth they decide to move to Cario. When Storm was five, David and his wife N'Dare were killed by a bomb but their daughter survived and was trapped under the rubble of their home, giving her a severe case of claustrophobia.
