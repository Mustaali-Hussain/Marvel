> ###### Kingo Sunen
> **First appearance** The Russians Are Coming #11
> **Appearances** 71

> [!abstract] In brief
> He has has all the powers of the Eternals.

He spent centuries in Japan learning the ways of the Samurai, and is one of the most skilled swordsmen on the planet. In the present day and age, he has parlayed his skills into becoming a major action film star in Japan.

## Powers

Kingo Sunen presumably has all the typical powers of Eternals: near-immortality, super-strength, flight, energy projection, and molecular manipulation. However, he eschews the use of these powers in battle, preferring to fight in the traditional manner of the Samurai.

## In Other Media

### Film

*Kumail Nanjiani as Kingo*

- Kingo appears as in the live-action* Eternals* movie, portrayed by actor and comedian Kumail Nanjiani. In a departure from the comics, Kingo is no longer a martial artist known for starring in Japanese samurai movies, and instead is known for starring in various Bollywood films in India. He is also depicted as a vain and narcissistic figure, agreeing to help stop the Celestials in order to make a documentary chronicling his adventures. He also possesses the power to fire explosive energy blasts from his fingers.
