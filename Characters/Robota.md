## Origin

Robota is a Model III servant robot and when the Model IVs came on the market she was discarded in a junk pile on the Planet Bznko. This is where [[Planet Terry]] found her when the people of Bznko told him he can take anything in the junk pile as reward for getting rid of the Yungmun. The two then went on adventure across the galaxy on a search for Terry's parents.

## Creation 

Robota was created by Lennie Herman, Warren Kremer and Vince Colletta for their series Planet Terry.
