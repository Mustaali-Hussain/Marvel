> ###### Dr. Malheur
> **First appearance** Killing Zone #31
> **Appearances** 17

Dr. Malheur was an employee of Dai-Kumo. He designed the chemical drugs known as "Zap" and "Thunderbolt". He was later confronted with Patch/Wolverine who was investigating the local drug cartel. Malheur tried to kill his assailant but instead fell into a chemical vat, dying instantly.
