> Circus bear evolved into humanoid by High Evolutionary.

Lady Ursula was one of the [[High Evolutionary]]'s early New Men creations, she was originally a Russian circus bear that was being abused by it's trainer before the Evolutionary saved her and evolved her. After training, Ursula became one of the Knights of Wundagore's top fighters. She later received even more training in hand to hand combat from the [[Black Knight]].
