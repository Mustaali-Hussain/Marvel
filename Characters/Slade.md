> Slade is a Daredevil villain who was responsible for a major tragedy in the life of Matt Murdock.

## Origin

*Slade confesses*

A stereotypical New York mobster, Slade's position in the underworld is as the henchman for [[The Fixer]]. It is revealed in "The Origin of Daredevil" that Slade shot and killed [[Battlin' Jack Murdock]]. Later in the issue, Slade is hunted down by [[Daredevil]] in a subway station and deceived into admitting his crime to the police.

## Creation

Slade first appears in Daredevil #1 (April 1964) and was created by Stan Lee and Bill Everett.

## Other Media

In the Daredevil film Slade's character is replaced by a young Wilson Fisk.
