## Brief History

Fisher Todd worked with the Force Works team. He was hired as a technician for the now defunct superhero team. Starting out, Fisher attempted to help feel the only kid in the Force Works complex, Rachel Carpenter, at home. He showed her the base as well as the team's training facility the Vroom Room. However, the room's programs were hacked and Rachel almost got hurt because of it. Only because of the timely intervention of Rachel's mother, the Spider-Woman, Rachel was kept from harm. 

 

In the month's that followed, Fisher showed attraction to Spider-Woman, but she never showed the same interest in him. Eventually, due to circumstances involving Spider-Woman being manipulated, the two drew closer to each-other. However, they never became a couple. What happened to Fisher after Force Works was disbanded remains unknown.
