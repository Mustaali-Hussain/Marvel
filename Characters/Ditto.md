> ###### Ditto
> **First appearance** Class Action Comics! #2
> **Appearances** 28

> [!abstract] In brief
> Shapeshifter with some previous convictions.

Ditto is a process server for the wealthy Goodman, Lieber, Kurtzberg and Holliway law firm. His shapeshifting skills are very helpful and flawless. He is a playful and sneaky person. He has been to jail before. He is the roommate of Awesome Andy. They were friends until Ditto told Titania that She-Hulk might be back at work if she wasn't at the apartment. This drove Titania to attack Timely Plaza where Awesome Andy almost got killed.
