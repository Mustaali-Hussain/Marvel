## Origin

Tanalth is a Kree warrior who replaced [[Korath the Pursuer|Korath]]as leader of the Pursuer Corps. She is feared by her allies and enemies as well and has been praised by the [[Supreme Intelligence]] for her skill and making the Pursuers as powerful as the Kree Accuser Corps.

## Creation

Tanalth was created by All-New Invaders writer James Robinson and artist Steve Pugh.
