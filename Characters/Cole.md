> Cole is a member of the Reavers, cyborg enemies of the X-Men.

## Brief History

Cole was a former mercenary serving the Hellfire Club's Inner Circle as a trooper.  While his teammates were being held captive, Wolverine fought Cole and two other troopers, Macon and Reese, leaving them all severely wounded.  The Inner Circle had the three men converted to be cyborgs.  When Donald Pierce rebelled against the Inner Circle, Cole went with Pierce and became a Reaver.
