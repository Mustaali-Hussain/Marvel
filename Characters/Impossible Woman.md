> ###### Impossible Woman

> [!abstract] In brief
> Impossible Woman was cloned by Impossible Man &amp; has all his abilities.

The Impossible Woman was created by the Impossible Man after witnessing the affection of Thing and Alicia Masters. She shares all the abilities of her counterpart.

 

Together, they created a family for themselves, which was in the thousands.

 

 

She, like all the other Poppupians, have the power to transform into anything alongside gain any abilities that form might have. The Poppupians also have the ability to magically manipulate and create molecules to create, shape, and destroy anything. Some examples of this include creating a golden egg out of nowhere, creating water ballon meteors, and creating a purple hammer. She can fly, and manipulate energy as well just like the rest of the Poppupians
