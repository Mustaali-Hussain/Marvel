## Brief History

Rook'shir was one of (if not the first) wielder of the [[Phoenix Force]]. He only possessed the Force for a few days, but he managed to destroy planets. He was hunted down and killed by the first Imperial Guard and his sword was confiscated by the Empire. However, an infinitesimal piece of the Phoenix Force somehow remained within the sword. The sword can only be wielded by Rook'shir's blood heirs and [[Korvus|Korvus. Rook'shir]] is the current wielder.
