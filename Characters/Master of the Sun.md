## Origin

The Master of the Sun was once a great Ariguan scientist. He left his race and fought them and the atrocities they ravaged across the universe.

As the Master of the Sun, he gave astronaut [[Star-Lord|Peter Jason Quill]] the power of Star-Lord. He originally planned to make many like him, but Peter would be the only one, which he considered his greatest creation. He also united Peter with the shapeshifter known as [[Ship]].

He eventually allowed himself to be captured by those of his race to atone for his betrayal. He was presumably executed for his treason.
