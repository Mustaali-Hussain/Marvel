## Creation

Lavin Skee was created by Greg Pak and Carlo Pagulayan and made his debut during the The Incredible Hulk #93, during Pak's Planet Hulk story arc.

## History

Lavin Skee was the Guard Captain for the house of Kaifi. He was taken by force when Ronahn Kaifi and his daughter Elloe Kaifi were abducted and taken to the Maw. Here he died in gladiatorial combat against overwhelming odds. It was over his body that the remaining gladiators took their Warbound oath.
