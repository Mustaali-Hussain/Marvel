> ###### Jonas Hale
> **Real name** Jonas Hale
> **First appearance** The Old Man and the Sea Prince! #120

> **Also known as** Jonas Hale, Captain Hale

> [!abstract] In brief
> Long-time member of Roxxon, Hale posed a threat to the likes of superheroes as Spider-Man and Iron Man as a minor villain.

Jonas Hale was the head of the research division of Roxxon Oil.  He was first seen after discovering a vibranium resource in an island of the Atlantic Ocean. He attempted to remove the island's only inhabitant, Hiram Dobbs, in order to extract it.  His attempt was opposed by [[Namor]] and [[Iron Man]]. Hale decided to destroy the island rather than have anyone else lay claim to it. He was a frequent business rival of Tony Stark. He developed Nuform, a form of artificial vibranium, and attempted to market it but the product proved unstable. He was last seen allied with the [[Sphinx]] in the development of a new new super-soldier project.
