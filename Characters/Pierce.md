## Brief History

Pamela Pierce was an excellent archer and even won a Gold medal in the Sydney Olympic Games. Pamela was later recruited by the 50 States Initiative to become a member of the superhero group known as The Order. She agreed and soon became part of the team. As part of the Order she was given enhanced agility and used a bow with titanium tipped arrows . Pamela and a few of her teammates where however fired from the team when they where caught on camera at a strip-club celebrating their victory over a defeated villain. New rectuits came and Pamela was not seen again.
