## Origin

Little is known about Hulkette before she and her trainer The Mighty Enormo showed up in Madripoor, with a Golden Fang of the Gaping Maw, with intent to enter The Dragon's Challenge, a tournament for control of the Gaping Maw.

While Enormo was attempting to get a quick meal, Hulkette and her mentor were attacked by Daredevil and Spider-Boy. The four fought, but when Spider-Boy took a cheap shot and was able to steal the Golden Fang, he and Daredevil escaped to the location of the tournament, and Hulkette was forced to wait to enact her revenge.

## Creation

Hulkette was created by Dan Slott and Humberto Ramos for their run on the series Spider-Boy.

## Major Story Arcs

### The Dragon Challenge

Hulkette and Enormo went to Madripoor to fight in the Dragon Challenge but Daredevil and Spider-Boy stole their Golden Fang, the ticket to enter the tournament. 

The pair chased after the thieves but were too late and Daredevil and Spider-Boy were able to enter the challenge, but this didn't stop Enormo who attacked the robbers but he was stopped by the Gapping Maw. They Gapping Maw took Enormo away, to never be seen again, but Mist knowing what happened made a deal with Hulkette, once Spider-Boy was done was done with the tournament, Hulkette would be allowed to enact her revenge.

Hulkette then spend the next few days watching the tournament, cheering against Spider-Boy until he was eliminated by being defeated by Spider-Girl and her partner Bullseye.

Hulkette wasted no time attacking Spider-Boy with the power punch she had been powering up, but before she could make contact, Spider-Girl choice Spider-Boy as her new partner, after being betrayed by Bullseye, and Hulkette was forced to wait again.

This time Spider-Boy won the challenge and became the leader of the Gapping Maw, causing Hulkette to take a step back and become friends with Spider-Boy.

### Full Circle

When Balloon Man attacks New York City he is stopped by Spider-Boy and his Amazing Friends, a group of heroes that now includes Hulkette.

## Powers and Abilities

Hulkette is not part of the Hulk family but she is green and has super strength.

Where other Hulks get stronger when they get angry, Hulkette can store her anger and strength and release it in earth shattering punches.
