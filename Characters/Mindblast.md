> Mindblast is a career criminal and one of the few mutants to retain their powers after M-Day.

## Origin

Danielle Forte was born in Las Vegas, Nevada. She is a telekinetic mutant.

## Creation

Mindblast was created by Erik Larsen and David Michelinie, and first appeared in The Amazing Spider-Man #340, October 1990.

## Major Story Arcs

Mindblast has been a member of the Femme Fatales, where she was selected for her impressive abilities, as well receiving (and accepting) an invitation to be one of Superia's Femizons.

After Decimation, she is one of the few mutants to retain their powers.

During the Civil War, she was seen as being allied with Hammerhead and his army. Her fate in the melée with Iron Man and S.H.I.E.L.D. is currently unknown.

## Powers and Abilities

She has strong telekinetic powers, enabling her to lift 500 tons with her mind. This power is limited to a single tractor beam. She can also use her telekinesis to levitate and fly. Without using her telekinetic ability, she has average human female strength.
