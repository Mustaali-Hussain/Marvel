> ###### Big Ben
> **Real name** Benjamin Charterhouse Fortescue
> **First appearance** Summer Special 1982 #4
> **Appearances** 29
> **Also known as** Benjamin Charterhouse Fortescue, British Bulldog, The Man With No Time For Crime

> [!abstract] In brief
> Big Ben is a flawed superhuman that was designed by Project Zarathustra after Dr. Gargunza's disappearance. His abilities could not match up to Miracleman's and his shoddy para-reality programming left his mind completely unbalanced.

Following the destruction of London by Kid Miracleman, when Miracleman and Miraclewoman rebuild the world into a utopia they attempt to "cure" Big Ben, while unable to undo the damage via the shoddy para-reality programming, they are able to alter it to have Big Ben become more accustomed to the society in which he now lives in. He now views the Miracleman Family as allies rather than enemies and adapts the moniker of British Bulldog, and appears to be a little more well balanced than he previously was
