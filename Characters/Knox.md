## Origins

Knox first appears in Ms. Marvel issue #5, seen as more of a minion to villain known as The Inventor, we later learn that he is in fact the creator of said villain.

## Abilities

Knox has a genius level intellect, showing the ability to clone, if imperfectly, Thomas Edison, he also somehow gained access to Thomas Edison's DNA.
