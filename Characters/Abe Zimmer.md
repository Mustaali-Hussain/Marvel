## Brief History

Abraham Paul Zimmer was once among the board of directors of Accutech but the company almost reached a state a bankruptcy due to the manipulations of Ghost. Accutech was purchased and absorbed by Stark Enterprises. Abe was placed as research director of his new parent company. He served well until killed by Calico.
