## Brief History 

 

Narda Ravanna and her sister Desiree Vaughan-Pope founded Vaughan-Pope Cosmetics. When they refused to sell their company to a rival owner of a fashion industry, [[Hobgoblin (Kingsley)|Roderick Kingsley]]tried to smear their company with bad publicity by bribing a former model from the company to claim she received a severe skin disorder from using Vaughn-Pope cosmetics. When Kingsley's tactic succeeded in ruining Vaughn-Pope Fashion, Narda Ravanna created the identity of Belladonna to help her seek revenge against him. However, [[Spider-Man]]stopped her and she was incarcerated. 

 

Meanwhile, her attacks had convinced Kingsley that he was vulnerable and needed a way to protect himself, which became one of his many reasons for creating the identity of the Hobgoblin.
