## Brief History

Ed is first introduced as a "greenie" or someone who is green (new) to war.  It shows the last stages of his enlistment and the first stages of his deployment to Vietnam circa the mid 1960s.  He is assigned to a rifle platoon and quickly learns a lot of hard lessons about the war through first hand experience. He is usually portrayed as a simple soldier, not in theater for any ideology but merely because he chose to go. He has numerous close calls but eventually finishes his tour and goes home.  

 

Throughout the series he learns to challenge his fear of flying. Later he is shown as an army instructor in the USA and later as a student at Columbia University.
