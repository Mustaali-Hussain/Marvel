> Quickshot is a Korean vampire. He is a member of the Forgiven.

## Origin

Quickshot was an immigrant vampire to New York. He was disillusioned by his sect's plan to take over Wall Street and became a member of a vampire clan called the Forgiven led by [[Raizo Kodo]] alongside [[Visigoth]], [[Inka]], [[Nighteyes]], and [[Ghost Blade|Sanjay]]. The Forgiven are a special group of vampires because they refuse to give into their kind's predatory ways and feed on human blood. Quickshot is the youngest member of the Forgiven and was taken in by Raizo when he also chose to reject the basic nature of vampirism. Quickshot is young but a valuable member of the Forgiven that possesses amazing marksmanship skills with his pistol and uncanny precision.

## Creation

Quickshot was created by Victor Gischler and Ryan Stegman in 2011 and first appeared in Fear Itself: Hulk vs Dracula # 1.

## Major Story Arcs

### Fear Itself: Hulk vs Dracula

The Forgiven infiltrate the domain of Castle Dracula and attempt to put old feuds aside and try to join forces with Dracula to stop the Hulk who has been decimating the Vampire Nation as a hammer wielding member of the Worthy. Dracula refuses to join forces with Raizo and has Quickshot and the rest of the Forgiven placed in the dungeons.

## Powers & Abilities

Quickshot is a vampire so he possesses the normal powers and attributes of a vampire. However, Quickshot is a highly skilled marksman with a pistol that has specialized ammo filled with a toxin from the Viper Orchid which causes paralysis for up to twelve hours. Quickshot and the rest of the Forgiven wear these amulets or light benders that allow them to survive from the sun during daylight hours.
