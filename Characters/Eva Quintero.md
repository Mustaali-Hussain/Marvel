## History

Eva Quintero (Eva Lopez) is the cousin of Humberto Lopez, better known as the superhero Reptil. Her cousin considered her to be an overachiever, and she started attending college at a young age. At some point in her life, Eva began studying magic by herself and managed to master basic abilities such as the power to open portals and create blasts of energy. She later joined Strange Academy to continue her studies and befriended fellow Mexican student Germān Aguilar.

## Powers & Abilities

Eva possesses a gifted intellect, as she attended college at a young age. She also utilized that intellect to quickly learn the basics of magic for herself including energy blasts and portal creation, though her portals can sometimes be limited in their size.
