> Ophis was a member of the Neyaphem. He was killed by Wolverine.

Ophis was a member of the Neyaphem, who served under [[Azazel]]. At Azazel's command, Ophis was torturing [[Wolverine]]. When Logan broke free, he killed Ophis.

## Character Creation

Ophis was created by Chuck Austen and Philip Tan and made his first appearance in *Uncanny X-Men # 429* in 2003.

## Powers

Ophis had superhuman strength and resistance to injury granted by his massive size. Additionally, he was able to create a pyrotechnic charge from his fists.
