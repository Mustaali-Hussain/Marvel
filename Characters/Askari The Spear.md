## Brief History

While the X-men Warren Warthington and Husk joined Askari in his home-country for a charity event, they where attacked by the Weaponeers and the Hydra agent known as Viper. Askari assisted the X-men to defeat the attackers. They where succesfull and luckily nobody was hurt. Askari has not been seen since his initial encounter with the X-men.
