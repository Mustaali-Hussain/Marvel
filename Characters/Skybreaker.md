> [!abstract] In brief
> Skybreaker is an Inhuman named Aireo the Floating Man.

Aireo was an Inhuman criminal, who along with several others, was found guilty of treason by Black Bolt. For a time he served Maximus as one of his servants.

Skybreaker went rogue from the Inhumans and joined the Force of Nature.

 Along with the other members of the Force of Nature, he joined the Initiative's Oregon team. 

 His hurricane-force winds have been shown to punch through a large tree trunk.
