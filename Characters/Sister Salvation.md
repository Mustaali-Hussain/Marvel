## Origin

Sister Salvation led a mysterious life and little is known about her early life and details about her are sparse. She may have been a mutant. At some stage in her life Sister Salvation joined a convent and became a missionary, to work in abound. On her travels, she started to be referred to and nicknamed as Sister Salvation as she had the ability to heal people. When the X-Man and Avenger [[Wolverine]] was in a series of battles with a creature known as [[Spore|Spore,]] it was Sister Salvation's healing powers that ultimately proved most effective against Spore killing it permanently. Her hands were left terribly burned and scarred as a result, but as Spore had been such an incredibly powerful and destructive force, Sister Salvation felt some purpose in her loss and actions leading to her loss.  

 

## Creation

Created by John Byrne Sister Salvation first appears in Wolverine #18.   

## Powers and Abilities

Sister Salvation possessed the power of healing. A touch from Salvation would could injury, disease, or wounds as well as just ease pain.
