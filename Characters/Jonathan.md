### Infinity Comics

### The Family Snikt

**For more information see: The Family Snikt**

[[Scout|Gabby]]and Jonathan planned the perfect family bonding road trip from San Franisco to Ontario the only problem is they need to gather a family, that is where Family Snikt comes in. Gabby convinces [[Wolverine|Logan]], [[X-23|Laura]]and [[Daken|Akihiro]]to join them in their adventure.

While on their first stop, the Commonwealth Historical Mine, Jonathan is kidnapped by the Honey Badger Heaven, a cult like group with plans to turn Jonathan and the family into wolverine displays in their weird museum.

Jonathan is able to escape, rescue the human heroes and free the honey badgers who are captured. This act bonds the family just like Gabby and Jonathan's original plan and the road trip is a great experience for the Family Snikt.

### Ziggy Pig and Silly Seal

[[Ziggy Pig]] and his team of Scammers have been annoying the anthropomorphic and animal heroes of the Marvel Universe by destroying their reputations, Jonathan included. That is why he is part of the group to break into Ziggy Pig and [[Silly Seal]]'s hideout and confront Ziggy.
