> ###### Tara Ogord
> **First appearance** Let My Planet Go! #29
> **Appearances** 11

> [!abstract] In brief
> Starhawk's daughter

Tara is the eldest of the three children of Stakar and Aleta. She possessed latent mental powers like her siblings. She was kidnapped by her grandfather Ogord and put under mind control. He awakened the siblings mental abilities and turned them into psychic vampires, whose purpose was to kill their father Starhawk.

When the Guardians of the Galaxy removed the mind control devices, their powers reversed and ages them at a super fast rate, turning all three children to dust and killing them in seconds.
