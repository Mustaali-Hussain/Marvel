> ###### Pn'Zo
> **First appearance** Part 2 #2
> **Appearances** 15

> [!abstract] In brief
> The Pn'Zo was selected by Vulcan to serve him in his new Imperial Guard

A robotic/android being. Next to nothing is known of its origins. It was apprehended by the Shi'ar by order of Lilandra when it attempted to rob a bank in their sector. He would remain a prisoner until he was freed by Emperor [[Vulcan]] in exchange for loyalty to him. He went on to join Praetorians.

**Powers and abilities**

It can produce eletricity from its body of varying voltage. Stretch prehensile cords from its body all have unknown durability. He can also absorb unknown amounts of energy.

**Physical characteristics**

Its robotic body is colored blue.

**Hair:** None - a blue energy furnace is in place of hair.

**Eyes:** No visible pupils, completely blue.

**Weight:** Variable
