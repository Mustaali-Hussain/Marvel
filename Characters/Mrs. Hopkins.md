## Brief History

Mrs. Hopkins was the loyal housekeeper in the Howlett's household in the late 19th century. She was very strict, but befriended Rose and the young James Howlett (Wolverine) somewhat. She was witness to the murder of fellow employee mister Kenneth. How she spent the rest of her life has not been revealed.
