## Character Evolution

*Angel: Underdog to Superhero*

Angel Salvadore later Salvadore-Bohusk has evolved throughout her time with the X-men and the world of mutants. Notably, Angel was an abrasive adolescent with defensive, sassy, and self-loathing personality. Her first moments with the mutants were filled with hate, jealously, and avoidance. But later, after several subsequent story arcs with Stepford Cuckoos, her fellow classmates known as the Special Class and [[Emma Frost]], Angel found friendship, honor and bravery. She even found love in her fellow classmate Barnell Bohusk ( [[Beak]]) who was often partnered with Ange and would eventually become father of her children and husband. From their she essentially found acceptance in herself and her mutation which lead to shine from under her title as the underdog hero into a full fledged hero and X-Woman and then into a depowered hero of the New Warriors.

*Angel as she appears in X-Men: First Class*

In 1962, Angel was a stripper who joined the X-Men after being recruited by Charles Xavier and Erik Lehnsherr. She bonded with the team as they shared codenames and powers, but when the Hellfire Club attacked, their leader Sebastian Shaw convinced her to join his cause. Despite Havok and Darwin’s attempt to stop her, Angel defected, and Darwin was killed in the process. Later, during the Cuban Missile Crisis, Angel fought alongside the Hellfire Club against her former X-Men teammates, engaging in battle with Banshee and Havok until Havok damaged one of her wings, effectively ending her role in the fight.

After Erik Lehnsherr killed Sebastian Shaw, he formed the Brotherhood of Mutants, recruiting the remaining Hellfire Club members and Mystique, and together they set out to free Emma Frost from CIA custody. However, just days after joining, on November 5, 1962, Angel was killed in a firefight by Project WideAwake operatives, her body left heavily mutilated.

Angel harbored a cynical view of humanity and aligned with Sebastian Shaw’s belief in mutant superiority, though she initially lacked the power or confidence to act on it. When offered the chance to join Shaw, she accepted quickly—unlike her hesitant peers—and became a loyal follower, albeit less sadistic than the rest of the Hellfire Club. She enjoyed combat but preferred swift, merciful kills and showed reluctance to harm those she saw as innocent, suggesting an internal conflict between her ideals and her actions. Her loyalty, however, was flexible; after Shaw's death, she switched sides to join Erik Lehnsherr without hesitation. Despite her initial fear of Shaw’s violence, she was swayed by his seductive promise of power and equality—particularly his subtle assurance that, as a woman and the youngest mutant, she would be treated as an equal. Her doubts, likely rooted in past experiences of being dismissed or underestimated, drove her to seek validation through loyalty and combat, though her inexperience and restraint ultimately led to her downfall.
