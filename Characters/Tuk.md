## Brief History

Tuk, along with other members of his tribe, are Cro-Magnons and thus lived about 50,000 BC. Son of Phadionand Rhaya, Tuk was born in Attilan (called "Island of the Gods"). He is the first offspring of the Inhumans. Their genetic code was passed down from generation to generation and increased the possibility of the presence of the mutation in humans.
