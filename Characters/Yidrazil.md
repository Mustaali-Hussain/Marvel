Yidrazil was a member of the Neyaphem, who served under [[Azazel]]. He was decapitated by [[Kiwi Black]], his paternal half-brother.

## Character Creation

 

Yidrazil was created by Chuck Austen and Philip Tan and made his first appearance in *Uncanny X-Men # 429* in 2003.

 

## Powers

 

Yidrazil had large wings which granted him self-propelled flight. 

He also possessed enhanced strength, agility, and reflexes.
