> [!abstract] In brief
> Hired agent of Norman Osborn, Alison Mongrain is the alleged murderer of Peter and Mary Jane Parker's unborn baby May.

At his most bitter and twisted, [[Norman Osborn]] decided to get really nasty and target the unborn baby of [[Spider-Man|Peter Parker]] and [[Mary Jane|Mary-Jane Watson]]. In order to do this, he hired Alison Mongrain to poison MJ's food to make sure the infant was stillborn - or was it? Mongrain was last seen leaving the delivery room holding something roughly baby-sized that Norman wanted her to protect in Europe. It was later revealed that whatever she had was intended for the Brotherhood of the Scriers. Right after Alison had handed the baby off to them, her yacht exploded. Alison Mongrain escape to Paris, but was tracked down by [[Robbie Robertson]], who managed to help her return to United States. Alison Mongrain had a message for Peter Parker, but she was hunted by villains [[Molten Man]] and Creep who tried to kill her. Eventually she and Robbie managed to reach Parker house and Mary Jane. There Alison revealed that May is alive.
