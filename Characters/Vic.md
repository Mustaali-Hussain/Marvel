## Overview

Vic is a series of clones created by the extraterrestrial Bob to preform many different functions such as black ops, lab assistant, secretary and more. Every individual clone was partnered with a clone from the Frankie series and each clone thought that it was the only one and that it was unique. When an individual would come across the body of its predecessor, it would cause the individual to become angry and disillusioned with Bob, whom was held in high regard.

Each member of the combat series was designed to capture the [[Mania|Symbiote Clone]] and become its host. Vic seemed to be more passionate than [[Frankie]] and was more willing to be the host for the Symbiote Clone. All of the combat members were bald due to the control collar and wore red wig with long bangs and green beads at the end.

## Numerical Breakdown

(These are numbered in the order introduced, not chronological)

### Vic 1

The first Vic seen was a combat variant that was tracking the Symbiote through northern Canada and caught up to it and [[The Suit|the Suit]] in Voici. The night before they encounter them, they meet and a have a brief conversation with [[Wolverine]] in the town bar. In the morning the Symbiote Clone starts attacking the citizens in a local diner and as the citizens flee, Vic shoots and kills them so they do not become hosts. When her partner Frankie is attacked, she tries to help but is stopped by the Suit. When she recovers she joins the fight between the Symbiote Clone and the Suit and claims that no one has suffered from the Symbiote clone more than she has and that she has been waiting her whole life to be its host. The Symbiote considers but chooses another civilian who staggers out of the diner and stops Vic from shooting the man. The Symbiote escapes and Vic gets attacked by the Suit but is saved by Frankie who arrives in their ship. When she gets in they get authorization from the Canadian government to drop an atomic bomb on Voici to try and kill the Symbiote Clone. Vic and Frankie leave the ship donning radiation-proof suits and start to search for the Symbiote Clone. When they find it, it kills Frankie and Vic flees as she has no backup. She pauses to regroup in the woods but is ambushed by the Suit and killed. Vic 1 is partnered with Frankie 1.

### Vic 2

Shortly after the first team is killed, a second team shows up and they also search for the Symbiote Clone. Frankie gets separated right away and Vic starts searching frantically for her. During her search she stumbles across the body of Vic 1 and becomes highly disillusioned with the organization she works for, claiming that she was told that she was perfect and they would not make any more clones. She puts a gun to her head and a gunshot is heard by [[Patricia Robertson]] a short distance away. In New York City it is revealed that she didn’t kill herself but shot her collar and tracked Patricia, and the Symbiote Clone the city. There she is shot and killed by Frankie 7 but tells her that they are nothing more than Clones that Bob finds completely expendable causing Frankie 7 to go rogue. Vic 2 is partnered with Frankie 2.

### Vic 3

Vic 3 was stationed in New York City and monitored electronic communication made by Patricia Robertson and reports directly to Bob. Vic 3 goes by Victoria. Vic 3 is partnered with Frankie 3.

### Vic 4

Two years before the Arctic incident with the Symbiote Clone, Vic led a raid on a civilian’s apartment in New York City to acquire Venom’s severed tongue to create the Symbiote Clone. Vic and Frankie then drop the tongue drop the tongue off at an airport where it is dealt with by Vic 5. Vic 4 is partnered with Frankie 4.

### Vic 5

After Vic 4 drops off the tongue to the airport, Vic 5 and Frankie 5 pick it up and load it onto a plane while wearing radiation suits so the two series of clones do not become aware of other members of the same series. They then fly the sample to New Mexico for it to be cloned. Vic 5 is partnered with Frankie 5.

### Vic 6

Vic 6 is Bob’s personal secretary who tells him that the field operatives (presumably anther team of Vic and Frankie) has found the best spot to store the clone in the Arctic Circle, the research base where the Symbiote Clone eventually escapes from. This is the only member of the Vic series to be shown operating without a Frankie series partner; Vic 6 goes by Vicky and works in the Empire State Building.

### Vic 7

Vic 7 was a research assistant in Mesa Verde, New Mexico that helped create the Symbiote Clone. She studied the Clone extensively and was surprised by its tendency to just kill test subjects as opposed to eating them. When Bob told her and Frankie to move to the next phase after only one test with two subjects, she initially objected but trusted Bob and convinced Frankie that it was alright because Bob was smarter that both of them. Vic loads the Symbiote Clone into a transport container before giving it to Bob and receiving orders to destroy all data pertaining to the Symbiote Clone. She was left at Mesa Verde when the Clone was sent to the Arctic research base. This Vic had shorter hair than the others and no green beads, she was also afraid of flying. Vic 7 is partnered with Frankie 6.

### Vic 8

Vic 8 was another Combat model that was stationed in New York City to try to make sure that the Symbiote Clone and Venom met and combined. She observed a conversation between Bob, disguised as the Suit, and Patricia Robertson before leaving with Frankie to go track Patricia. In an alley, she is talking with Frankie when she is told that they are expendable clones and is shot by her partner Frankie 7 who had gone rogue. Vic 8 is partnered with Frankie 7.

### Vic 9

Vic 9 helps Bob set a trap for [[Mr. Fantastic]] and the [[Human Torch]] as they believe that they are transporting the Symbiote Clone. This Vic appears to have shorter hair and no green beads.Vic 9is partnered with Frankie 8.

## Powers

The Vic series had no powers but the combat variants were well and extensively trained in ranged and hand to hand combat. The lab assistant variant was very smart.

## Equipment

The combat variants had several types of weapons at their disposal including an assault rifle and a pistol. They also had a radiation proof combat suit that Vic 1 and 2 wore after the bombing in Canada in an attempt to find the Symbiote Clone.

The most important piece of equipment was the control collar that all of the combat variants wore. It allowed them to be a host for the Symbiote Clone but not have their mind dominated so they could control it.
