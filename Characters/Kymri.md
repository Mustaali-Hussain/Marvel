## History

*Original Costume*

Kymri was introduced during the Excalibur original series run. She made friends with the team and shared a flirtatious relationship with [[Nightcrawler]]. He even helped her regain her throne. She was also affiliated with the Captain Britain Corps, after rightfully becoming her world's version of [[Captain Britain]] alongside an alternate version of [[Lockheed]].

## Skills & Abilities

She is very agile and a her swordsmanship is comparable to that of the X-Men's Nightcrawler . Like her people, she has blue skin and three fingers and toes on each hand and foot. She does not however have a tail like Nightcrawler, and the structure of her feet also differs.

## Alternate Realities

### X-Men: The End

In *X-Men: the End* she is revealed to have been captured and turned into a hound for the slavers. She was rescued by Nightcrawler and the two got married and had two children: TJ, a boy, and Cerise, a girl. They also accepted the other-dimensional Talia Josephine, a.k.a. [[Nocturne]], as an extension of their family.
