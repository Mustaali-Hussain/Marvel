## Brief History

The Scarlet Beetle was a mutated insect that gained consciousness, free-thinking and the ability to command other insects to do his bidding. He captured Ant-Man and used his serum to become huge. He then launched an attack on the world, but Ant-Man defeated him and turned him back into an insect again. He has, however, returned to fight other Marvel heroes over time, including the West Coast Avengers and Iron Man. He appeared in the final issue of Sensational She-Hulk.

## Powers & Abilities

The Scarlet Beetle has a genius-level intellect and can command insects, arachnids, and other arthropods. He even can control human minds though it can only be one mind at a time. He has proportionate superhuman strength according to his size.
