> Japanese-American mutant who worked for the government and nearly executed Mystique.  In his own extreme ideals, he was strongly against mutant terrorists and would take great pleasure in taking them out.

**Johnny Kitano** was a special magistrate for the government organization Homo Superior Crimes Against Humanity.  

A mutant himself, Kitano despised mutants like [[Magneto]] and [[Mystique]] who gave mutants like himself a bad name through their public acts of terrorism.  Kitano captured Mystique and prepared to illegally execute her for her crimes with his mutant powers before she was rescued by [[Professor X|Professor Xavier]].

## Powers and abilities

Johnny Kitano is a mutant with the ability to generate and discharge electricity.
