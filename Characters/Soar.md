> ###### Soar
> **First appearance** Fast Feud #1
> **Appearances** 14

> [!abstract] In brief
> Armored intelligent eagle fighting to protect the planet

When an injured bald eagle was brought to the Multicorp agency, the eagle was slated to become part of a rehabilitation project that would not only heal the eagle but give him human level intelligence. He was named Slipstream and given a suit of armor to enhance his natural abilities, and was made a part of the Brute Force environmental protection team. However he renamed himself Soar, as he felt that he was the leader and deserved to name himself. Soar is the most arrogant member of the team and is highly independent, he will often ignore orders if he doesn't agree with them and constantly gives orders to the other members of Brute Force. 

## POWERS

 **Natural Animal Abilities:** Being an eagle gives Soar the abilities of flight, binocular vision, and claws

**Armor: **Soar's armor boosts all his natural abilities and is equipped with missile launchers. The armor can convert into a transport mode for rapid travel. His transport mode is a fighter jet. Special contact lenses give Soar the ability to fire lasers from his eyes.
