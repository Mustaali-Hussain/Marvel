> ###### Butte
> **First appearance** Outta Time! #17
> **Appearances** 13

Butte is a superstrong female made out of stone and was created by Dominus along with Cactus, Gila and Sunstroke. They first battled the West Coast Avengers when they traveled to Arizona to seek out Firebird but they were easily outmatched. Dominus would meet the Wacos sometime later and he created an army of Cactus, Butte and Gila soldiers. The Wacos would easily defeat this army and take down Dominus.   

## Creation

Butte was created by Steve Englehart, Al Milgrom and Joe Sinnott in 1987 and first appeared in The West Coast Avengers # 17. 

## Powers & Abilities

Butte is made out of stone and possesses superhuman strength. She is fair in hand to hand combat and also has a degree of superhuman durability.
