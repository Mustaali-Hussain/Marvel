## Brief History

Chinese national Alex Su and the Abomination represented China in the Liberators. Based on similar technology to Tony Stark's Iron-Tech, he was unable to exit his armor, having been fused inside, but could supposedly use it to control up to 50 giant-sized drone versions (which were revealed to be piloted independently). However, he was apparently killed when Tony Stark vaporized him while piloting the massive Iron Man Six airborne weapons platform..
