## Brief History

Years ago, Dr. Anderssen hired Karl Lykos’ father as a guide to a trip in Tierra del Fuego, during that, his young daughter Tanya disappeared, and it was Karl (also a child) who found her inside a cave full of Pteranodons.

 

 Dr. Anderssen is a wealthy greed man, that would never let his daughter marries with Karl Lykos, a poor and disgraced man, in his opinion. Because of him and his opinion about the marriage that Karl becomes Sauron, in order to achieve great fortune and then deserve to marry his daughter.
