> [!abstract] In brief
> Ancient Demon Lord of the Hand. During Shadowland he possessed Daredevil.

During Shadowland he possessed Daredevil and nearly pushed him to the brink of permanent evil by trying to kill, Foggy Nelson, Matt Murdock's dearest friend. A chi strike from Iron Fist chi was able to purify Daredevil of the Beast's influence and Matt subsequently relinquished control of the Hand.
