> [!abstract] In brief
> Jane Hastings was a stunt-woman and animal trainer who helped the local people of the Congo out in time of need. Becoming the adventurer Jann of the Jungle, she is a friend and ally to the Agents of Atlas.

## Creation

Jann of the Jungle was created by Don Rico and Art Peddy and first appeared in *Jungle Tales* #1.

## Character Evolution

Jann of the Jungle hasn't appeared much since days of Atlas Comics. Her notable appearances since then have mostly been alongside the Agents of Atlas, of whom she is an infrequent ally.*
