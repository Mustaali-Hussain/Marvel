> Armena is a Cuban immigrant and a teacher who still feels a strong connection to her homeland. Following the events of the House of M reality-shift, Armena lost her mutant powers.

## Origin

Little is known about this former mutant. She is married to Ismael Ortega but theit marriage has fallen on hard times, and she asked him to leave until he sorts out his life. Before the Decimation, Armena Ortega reflexively produced a transparent bubble of biological material around herself while sleeping. But unfortunately, like other mutants during M-Day, she also lost her powers and was converted to a human. She is a citizen of District X. She is the mother of Chamayra and Esteban Ortega who are also depowered during M-Day.
