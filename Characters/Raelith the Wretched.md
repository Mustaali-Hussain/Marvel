## Creation

Raelith debuted in The Amazing Spider-Man (2024) #11 by Joe Kelly and Pepe Larraz

## Major Story Arcs

### Starblood

Raelith found herself an unwilling test subject of [[Xanto Starblood|Dr. Xanto Starblood]], an extreme xenobiologist. She was rescued from her stasis pod by [[Spider-Man]], who had initially partnered with Starblood before learning the truth about his test subjects. Although Raelith suggested they torture Starblood, Spider-Man ultimately convinced her to show mercy.
