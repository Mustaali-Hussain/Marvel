## Story

One night, in the 1920's, Elisa helped [[Sarah Rogers|Sarah]] and [[Captain America|Steve Rogers]] after Sarah was beaten by her husband Joseph. Elisa took an interest in Sarah, and especially in her young son Steve. She introduced the civic-minded Sarah to "a small community group" - HYDRA - and attempted to persuade her to leave her physically abusive husband. When Joseph continued to beat Sarah, Elisa plotted to get rid of him, and one night she accosted a drunken Joseph with two henchmen, who threw Joseph into the East River to drown.
