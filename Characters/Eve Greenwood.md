> Twin sister of Dawn Greenwood. She has an acute case of wanderlust.

Eve Greenwood is Dawn Greenwood's twin sister and travel agent for Greenwood Bed & Breakfast. She tours the planet Earth, recommending peoples of all nations, cultures, and ethnicities to visit Anchor Bay and stay at Greenwood Bed & Breakfast.

She grew up in Anchor Bay and wanted to see everywhere else but the picturesque coastal town. Ironically, it is her sister traveling the far reaches of the cosmos with the Silver Surfer.
