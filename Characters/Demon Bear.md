> ###### Demon Bear
> **First appearance** Nightmare #3
> **Appearances** 165

> [!abstract] In brief
> The Demon Bear was originally an entity who haunted the dreams of Danielle Moonstar, threatening that he would eventually come and kill her. He was seemingly responsible for the deaths of her parents.

## Other Media

Demon Bear will be the antagonist in the upcoming 2018 movie 'New Mutants'. The movie is directed by Josh Boone and stars Charlie Heaton as Cannonball/Sam Guthrie, Blu Hunt as Mirage/Dani Moonstar, Maisie Williams as Wolfsbane/Rahne Sinclair, Anya Taylor-Joy as Magik/Ilyana Rasputin, Henry Zaga as Sunspot/Roberto Da Costa and Alice Braga as Dr. Cecilia Reyes.
