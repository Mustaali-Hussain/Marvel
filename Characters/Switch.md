> [!abstract] In brief
> A sometimes member of the Hellions, Switch was a very dangerous young man. He had the mutant ability to switch bodies with another person.

## Major Story Arcs

When Switch first appeared after being invited to assist the Hellions, he was seen using his power to trap Domino in his body while he used her body to try to catch her teammates off guard. However, he was literally beaten out of Domino and defeated. He fled the scene and was underground for a little while.

He was next seen working with Feral to try and kidnap Senator Owen Danville, which he did by tying himself up and switching minds with the Senator. He returned to King Bedlam, but was ambushed by X-Force and the Senator was rescued. Switch has remained rather inactive ever since.

Switch is suspected of causing chaos and trauma on Krakoa; utilizing his powers on unwitting and unwilling people.
