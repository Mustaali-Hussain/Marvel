> ###### Lily Lucca
> **First appearance** The Devil Takes a Ride, Part 1 #89
> **Appearances** 16

> [!abstract] In brief
> Lily was hired by Vanessa Fisk to mess with Matt Murdock's mind.

Lily Lucca was given a perfume by [[Vanessa Fisk]] that would make Matt Murdock, [[Daredevil]], think of [[Karen Page]], his one true love that was killed by [[Bullseye]]. She stopped wearing the perfume but now finds herself still giving off a scent and affecting males. Men find themselves fighting over her. She has gone to Matt for help. Matt doesn't want anything to do with her but finds it hard to say no. Lily tells Matt that Vanessa said one of his enemies is responsible for creating the perfume. Matt puts private investigator, [[Dakota North]], on the case.
