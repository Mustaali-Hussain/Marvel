## Origin

Reese was a victim of a group of vampires that had been kidnapping and transforming people throughout New York City. As the protector of those that travel at night, [[Moon Knight]] intervened, slaying the vampire kidnappers but taking sympathy on their newest converts. He set them up to live off donated blood, including Reese, who Moon Knight hired as his secretary. They keep the same hours now, so it was an easy transition.

## Creation

Reese made her debut in Moon Knight (2021) #1 by Jed MacKay and Alessandro Cappuccio.

## Major Story Arcs

### The Zodiac Tests

After the Midnight Mission accomplished a number of good deeds in the neighborhood, the criminal known as [[Zodiac]] was determined to prove Moon Knight a fraud. He did so by a number of complicated and elaborate schemes to defeat Moon Knight.

Zodiac would ultimately attack [[House of Shadows|The Midnight Mission]] after tricking Moon Knight and his fighter allies into going to New Jersey. Reese and newest recruit, [[Soldier]], were left behind and forced to confront Zodiac and his new gang despite being outnumbered. Unfortunately, Moon Knight could not get back to his office in time to save Soldier. Soldier's death caused Reese to lash out and almost kill Zodiac, but she was stopped by Moon Knight, who had slipped into his Steve Grant identity, revealing his dissociative identity disorder to Reese for the first time.

### The Structure

When Zodiac shot through Reese and killed Soldier, enough DNA survived on the bullet to infect Soldier with Reese's vampiric curse. This got the attention of Tutor, the vampire who had sired Reese and was running the vampire cult, The Structure. He believed that Soldier was the first of many, so he treated the Midnight Mission as a rival organization.

After Moon Knight did some investigating, they tracked The Structure to a convention center, where they were having a fancy meeting. Moon Knight intended to confront them, but he needed Soldier to use his domestic terrorism training. Soldier, with Reese watching his back, sabotaged the fire suppression system, forcing the sprinklers to go off in the main hall, for Moon Knight to consecrate into holy water.

### The Last Days of Moon Knight

*The new Mission*

The Midnight Mission was targeted by a mysterious new Black Spectre, who was working with a number of Moon Knight's old foes. One such villain was[[Sidney Sarnak]], who had perfected a way of brainwashing people with sound. One test incident was when they caused a riot during a concert that Reese and Soldier were in attendance of. After interrogating one of the his goons, Moon Knight learned that Black Spectre planned on using this technology on the entire New York population. Moon Knight tracked the operation to a skyscraper in the city and formed a field team, leaving Reese behind with the [[House of Shadows]] to continue the good work of the Midnight Mission.

Unfortunately, Black Spectre was also working with Zodiac, who took the opportunity to target the House of Shadows in Moon Knight's absence. He broke in and was impressed by Reese's grasp of her new vampiric abilities, however, Reese couldn't get close to him thanks to the holy symbol around his neck, his own name. Instead, Reese left the House of Shadows to take him.

Zodiac started singing a different tune upon learning that Moon Knight wouldn't be resurrected this time if Black Spectre was successful. Reese didn't trust Zodiac, but the House read his mind and let him go. Meanwhile, across town, Moon Knight sacrificed himself to take out Black Spectre's weapons. Per his last wish, Reese agreed to continue running the Mission.

### Vengeance of Moon Knight

*Back to business*

While trying to protect their part of New York, the Midnight Mission is attacked by a masked mystery man claiming to be the new Moon Knight. He had already attacked 8-Ball and was looking to target the rest for being too ineffectual or monstrous to carry on the legacy. He easily fought off the whole team, however, when he entered the Mission, the sentient building rejected him completely. This forced the new Moon Knight to give up on taking back the storefront but warned them to stay out of his way.

Unfortunately, this was only the beginning of the problem. This new Moon Knight went after masked criminals so aggressively, that those masked criminals started targeting the Midnight Mission, putting the community they protect in danger. They were forced to approach Lady Yulan and call in the favor they earned taking out The Structure. She led them to his daylight hiding spot where it was revealed he was [[Shroud|The Shroud]].

Shroud was deadset on being the new Moon Knight and thought he was honoring Marc's legacy. He believed he had ruined The Shroud identity and needed to rebrand himself, but Tigra managed to convince him that he needed work on himself instead. Unfortunately, just as he was coming around to accepting the Mission's help, he was transformed into a portal for darkforce (as did all the darkforce users on Earth) blocking out the sun.

### Blood Hunt

The Structure had used Atlantean magic to block out the sun with darkforce energy. This was their first step to world domination. The second was wave after wave of global uprisings, including New York, but Reese and the rest of the Mission were ready to fight back.

Reese was out on the street when the skies were cleared. She and all the other vampires were no longer weak to sunlight. She and the Mission, now including Moon Knight, resurrected by Khonshu to fight the vampires, had to continue working to clear the streets of vampires.

### Midnight Mission Tomb

*Framed*

In Marc's absence, a new drug kingpin, [[Achilles Fairchild]], had moved in on the Midnight Mission territory thanks to his magic drug, Glitter. When Moon Knight started to threaten him, Achilles changed the street name to Moon Dust and framed the Midnight Mission for its manufacturing and distribution. Achilles even sent his enforcer, Carver, with her enchanted Vanaheim sword, to take out the House of Shadows, leaving the team vulnerable to an incoming SWAT raid. The team was forced underground, to a decommissioned SHIELD station once used by Slasher, a villain Moon Knight had stopped previously.

Marc attempted to take down the Glitter operation from the top down, but Achilles revealed he was from Asgard and much too powerful than Marc. Instead, Marc decided they had to start from the bottom up. First step was asking a favor from [[Hank Pym]] to make an antagonist agent to help users kick the drug. The Mission used at on Achilles inside people in the police. Marc then freed Carver from her sword's enchantment by pitting the soul of the dragon possessing it against Khonshu and taking the weapon for himself. They then planned a two pronged attack on Achilles' office. Marc, Badr, and Reese would come in from the sky while Tigra, Soldier, and 8-Ball sneak in through the street.

Reese and the others are able to handle the supervillain goons that Achilles hired to distract them while Marc chases down Achilles. Scared of Carver's sword, Achilles doesn't try to fight. He fled but was teleported away by Zodiac.

## Powers & Abilities

**Vampirism: **Reese was turned into a vampire. She is immortal, hard to kill, with claws and fangs. She has yet to master many of the powers of Marvel vampires, but Blade is coaching her as a favor to Moon Knight

- **Mist: **Turning into mist was one of the first abilities Reese mastered.
- **Solar Immunity: **Ever since the end of the Blood Hunt event, Reese was now immune to the sun's effects.
