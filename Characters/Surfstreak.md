## Origin

When an injured bottlenosed dolphin was brought to the Multicorp agency, the dolphin was slated to become part of a rehabilitation project that would not only heal the dolphin but give him human level intelligence. He was named Surfstreak and given a suit of armor to enhance his natural abilities and allow him to walk on dry land and he was made a part of the Brute Force Environmental Protection Team. Surfstreak is the most intelligent member of the team, and the most sensitive. When he accidentally killed a mutant animal with his water cannons, he was the only member of Brute Force to be heartbroken over the creature's death. However, he has a pathological hatred of sharks.

## Creation

Surfstreak and Brute Force was created by Simon Furman for his series Brute Force.

## Major Story Arcs

### Ziggy Pig and Silly Seal

Surfstreak and the rest of Brute Force have a booth at "The Alley Behind a Closed Laundromat Comic-Con". Later, Surfstreak an Brute Force show up on a monitor screen of all the animal heroes in the Marvel Universe that [[Ziggy Pig]] plans on taking down.

## Powers and Abilities

**Natural Animal Abilities:** Being a dolphin gives Surfstreak amazing swimming and underwater capabilities, as well as echolocation and ultrasonic hearing.

**Armor:** His armor boosts Surfstreak's natural abilities as well as enables him to move on land. His armor is equipped with high pressure water cannons and can convert into a transport mode for rapid travel. His transport mode is a race car.
