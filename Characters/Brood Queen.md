> [!abstract] In brief
> The mother and matriarch of the parasitic aliens known as Brood. More than one Brood Queen can reign at the same time, so long as they are all subservient to the Brood Empress.

## Brief History

The Brood are a race of alien parasites that reproduce by implanting their eggs in others. They are a recurring enemy of the X-Men, Shi'ar, [[Carol Danvers]] and others in the Marvel Universe.

*Storm in Space*

In a 1992 edition of *X-Men Classic*, [[Storm|Ororo]] is infected by the queen with a Brood embryo. The only way for Storm to rid her body of this monster was to release herself into outer-space. During this experience, her mutant abilities, sustained by a variety of living worlds and stars in her vicinity, save her. And, she becomes bonded to a star-beast, [[Prophet-Singer|Acanti]].
