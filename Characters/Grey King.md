> ###### Grey King
> **First appearance** Children of the Atom Part Two #80
> **Appearances** 15
> **Also known as** Addison Falk

> [!abstract] In brief
> Grey King was a synthetic copy of a mutant educator named Addison Falk, created by Cerebro.  He is a member of Cerebro's X-Men.

Grey King is a super-intelligent specialized telepath who can neutralize a mutant's powers by locking specific sections of their brains. He is also a telekinetic who can move, lift, and manipulate very large objects with his thoughts. When he employs his telekinesis he manifests a psionic firebird similar to the [[Phoenix Force]]. Grey King also wore a Phoenix-like costume and had red hair.

As a synthetic creation of Cerebro, Grey Kingwas a combination of mutants Black King ([[Sebastian Shaw]]) and Phoenix ([[Jean Grey]]).
