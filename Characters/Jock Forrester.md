## Brief History

*D'Spayre Tormenting Lee *

Jock Forrester was the father of [[Lee Forrester]]. After being diagnosed with cancer and told that he only has one year of life he went to the Everglades and started to think about killing himself. The [[Man-Thing]]was attracted by this feelings and started to approach him, but before he could reach the human,[[D'Spayre]] managed to induce Jock to kill himself with a gun, feeding on his soul.

After that, D'Spayre used his body some times. He first attacked Jock's daughter Lee and Cyclops and almost won, but the Man-Thing managed to defeat him. Much later, Jock's memory would later be used by D'Spayre to torment Lee, but this time her companion was the son of Cyclops, [[Cable]].
