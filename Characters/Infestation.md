## Schooling 

Infestation was part of a large number of students in the Hellfire Academy. This was a school run by supervillains. Fellow students included Quentin Quire, Glob Herman, Tin Man and Snot. Unlike many students, Infestation took well to the evil lessons.

### Powers & Abilities

Along with any training received at the Hellfire Academy, Infestation possesses the unique ability to hide insects within special folds in her skin. She also seems to use them with a certain level of control by targeting select opponents with them.
