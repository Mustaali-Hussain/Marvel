> Scribe was a member of the Inner Circle of the London Hellfire Club who was once possessed by the villain Mountjoy.

### Background

Scribe worked for awhile in the Hellfire Club as a simple secretary but slowly worked her way through the ranks to the Club''s Red Rook. She was eventually possessed by the villain Mountjoy but was seperated during a battle Excalibur.

### Powers

She has superfast reflexes and is a match even for Nightcrawler's teleporting during a fight.
