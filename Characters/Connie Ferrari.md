> [!abstract] In brief
> Connie Ferrari is a lawyer who dated Steve Rogers (Captain America). She became an attorney for the Avengers and was soon busy dealing with the problems of the Great Lakes Avengers.

Connie is a former girlfriend of [[Captain America|Steve Rogers]]; although their happiness would not last, as she found out he was in fact Captain America and felt he had hidden a large important part of himself. She then broke up with him through a letter; although during his vacation to deal with it, [[Sam Wilson]] urged him to confront her. He then arrived in Ohio where she had moved to set up her law practice. As the two talk and Steve realizes he needs a relationship for Captain America, the two depart on friendly terms. 

Connie Ferrari set up a partnership law firm, Ferrari & Hindel Associates, in New York, New York, eventually becoming attorney for the Avengers. Most recently, Connie has tasked with offering the Great Lakes Avengers official Avengers status and with being the offical lliason between the GLA and the Avengers. Whenever Connie must visit the GLA, she can usually be seen with her assistant, Amy.
