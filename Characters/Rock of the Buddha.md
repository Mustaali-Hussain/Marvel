## Origin

Other than being a mamber of the Black Dragon Death Squad, nothing is known about the Rock of the Buddha's origin.

## Creation

Rock of the Buddha is created by Jason Aaron and Stephen Segovia.

## Major Story Arcs

### Manifest Destiny

Despite his armored form he is not completely protected; as [[Wolverine]] is able to find a weak spot under his arms. With one precise strike to his rib cage Rock of the Buddha crumbles into a pile of rocks.

### Goodbye, Chinatown

Somehow, the Rock of the Buddha had reformed and is seen alongside former teammate [[Soulstriker]], [[Darkstrider]] and [[Razor-Fist|Razorfist]], guarding the dragons who were used for moving massive shipments of drugs. Led by the [[Jade Claw]], the drugs is harvested from underground fields as big as Australia and transported to many places, among them San Francisco's Chinatown. Their operation is discovered by Wolverine and [[Gorilla-Man]]. With the help of [[Master Po]] and the [[Fat Cobra]], Jade Claw's operation is destroyed.

## Powers and Abilities

Rock of the Buddha was the martial arts team's analog to the [[Thing]]. He had half his body comprised of a rocky substance as well as spikes jutting from his spinal column.
