## Origin

Tyler Stone is the owner of the Alchemax corporation. It is eventually revealed that he is [[Spider-Man 2099]]'s father.

## Creation

Tyler Stone was created by Peter David and Rick Leonardi and first appeared in Spider-Man 2099 issue 1 (1992).

## Major Story Arcs

### Classic 2099:

Tyler Stone is the father of Miguel O'Hara (Spider-Man 2099) and [[Kron Stone]] (Venom 2099).

 

## Other Versions

### Timestream

In the story arc Timestorm 2009-2099 Tyler Stone is yet to be revealed as Miguel's true father, but after a lab accident at Alchemax, the radioactive shockwave infused Miguel's DNA with a spider he was experimenting on in school, turning Miguel into Spider-Man 2099. And the scorpion, Kron Stone, Tyler's other son was experimenting on was fused into his DNA creating the monster Scorpion 2099.
