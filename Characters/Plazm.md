> ###### Plazm
> **First appearance** Exit Wounds #116

> [!abstract] In brief
> Plazm was a member of the X-Statix who had the abilities to turn his body into a liquid, the ability to fly, tranquilize others on contact, and heightened endurance.  Killed in the BoysRUs event.

## Origin

Plazm and the other members of X-Force were already well-established popular superheros when they make their first appearance on TV. In their first detailed mission they combat drug-happy mutinous tribesmen in North Africa.Plazm receives low marks from team-leader Zeitgeist and a mental note to move him to defensive positions. The problem was in Zeitgeist's mind was that Plazm's kill rate of four percent though he was seen tearing apart a soldier was not acceptable. Later at home, Plazm uses his mutant powers to help calm the anxiety of his teammate Gin Genie.

## Creation

Plazm was created by Peter Milligan and Mike Allred in 2001 and first appeared in X-Force # 116.

## Major Story Arcs

### Death

The team organizer Coach tells the team he has found a perfect mission, one that would generate lots of good publicity. That Money-minded terrorists have taken the boy-band 'Boys R Us' hostage in the music studios of 'Sonic TV'. Rescuing them for free should go good with the focus groups. While a huge crowd awaits outside,the team teleports by the courtesy of U-Go Girl. Plazm battles well until an armed helicopter, hovering above the crowd, opens fire.

The only survivors of the entire incident are the rookie Anarchist, U-Go Girl and Doop. Plazm, the other mutants, the terrorists and even the boy band survivors all die from the attack. Then The helicopter crew is attacked by U-Go Girl and dropped to the pavement far below to get even with most of her teammate's deaths.

## Powers & Abilities

Plazm had the ability to turn his body into a liquid, the ability to fly, tranquilize others on contact, and heightened endurance.
