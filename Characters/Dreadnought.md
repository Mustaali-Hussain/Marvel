> ###### Dreadnought

> [!abstract] In brief
> Dreadnought was created for combat by Hydra

The Dreadnought is a non-sentient robotic combat instrument created by Hydra. The Maggia, directed by Madame Masque, stole Dreadnought designs from Hydra and manufactured modified models of the Dreadnought. They have often fought Iron Man and Shield.
