## Origin

Boyd and his brother [[Bufford Buzzard]] have spent years in the Canadian wilderness abducting people, eating them and using there bones for decoration.

## Creation

Boyd Buzzard was created by Jason Aaron and Jefte Palo in 2011 and first appeared in Wolverine # 5.1.

## Major Story Arcs

### Wolverine .1

Wolverine meets Boyd and his brother Bufford for the first time. Wolverine rescues a truck driver the pair plan on eating, and after giving the pair a severe beating he turns them in to the local police. It is there that the brothers are approached by The Hand, who breaks them out for a mysterious mission

### Wolverine: Goodbye Chinatown

Boyd and Bufford are hired to kill Mr. Takenaka, the current head of the Yakuza, while he is in New York City, in a meeting with [[Kingpin]]. The Buzzard brother have now been mystical powers, that make them much harder to kill .They fail due the unexpected appearance of Wolverine, and [[Seraph]] and her team. They are last seen being interrogated by Lynx, where they give up the identity of their employer, ninjas.
