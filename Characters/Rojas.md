> [!abstract] In brief
> Rojas is a Coyote.

Rojas ran a cartel in Mexico where she sold drugs through human mules and caught the attention of Logan after he discovered a semi-truck camper filled with nineteen dead people that included men, women and children. Logan decided to get involved and infiltrated Rojas' compound where he found out that the leader of the drug cartel was a pregnant woman. Logan refused to kill Rojas because she was carrying a child even after Rojas provoked Logan by calling him an animal. Logan left Rojas alone after he killed most of her men. Logan returned later that night and discovered Rojas in labor. Rojas died in Logan's arms after she gave birth to a baby girl. Logan discovered that Rojas had a sister in the states and she would adopt her niece into her family.
