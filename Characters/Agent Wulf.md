> ###### Agent Wulf
> **Real name** Wülf Schmidt
> **First appearance** The Earth is Red #21
> **Appearances** 11
> **Also known as** Wülf Schmidt

> [!abstract] In brief
> Corrupt agent of the Paideia.

Wülf Schmidt, along with [[Agent West|Vic West]], were first seen blackmailing a recently liberated slave to undergo the Morituri experiment under threat of executing him there and then claiming instead of a slave he was a traitor.

Wülf later was assigned to being in charge of Strikeforce Morituri following the death of their [[Yuri Pogorelich|commander]], although he mostly just tried to find ways to frame them for acts of terror and have an excuse to kill them without being incriminated.
