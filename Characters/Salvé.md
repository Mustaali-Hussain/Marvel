*X-Factor III - 43*

Salvé is one of the very few mutants free in the future. At some point, he was obviously in a camp, given that he does have the M brand, but escaped. 

  

He is French, but speaks with a very good English accent. He is a member of Cyclops' rebellion, and a healer. He healed [[Madrox|Jamie]]'s hand (the one that [[Siryn]]broke) and tells Madrox that his French accent is also quite good.
