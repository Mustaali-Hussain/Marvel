> ###### Big Ben Donovan
> **Real name** Benjamin Donovan
> **First appearance** Retribution #14
> **Appearances** 32
> **Also known as** Benjamin Donovan

> [!abstract] In brief
> Lawyer and Professional Criminal.

Makes his first appearance in the **Luke Cage** series. **Big Ben Donovan**, though just a human being, managed to floor **Luke Cage**. **Luke Cage** still prevailed in the end, but not without recognizing **Donovan's** strength. **Donovan** would later become a short ally of **Cage**'s.

Later it is revealed that **Donovan** is responsible for the death of the original **Thunderbolt's(Bill Carver)** little brother. **Thunderbolt** seeks revenge and justice is served.

He makes another appearance going up against **Daredevil**

## Other Media

### Television

### Daredevil (2016)

He appears in Netflix' Daredevil as a lawyer of Wilson Fisk. He is somewhat reluctant to do illegal acts for Fisk, but he is complicit in everything Fisk is planning. He is a middle aged man and doesn't look to pose any physical threat.

### Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes

He appears in Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes as one of Crossfire's henchman. He and the rest of his team battle Luke Cage and Iron Fist for the life of Cassie Lang.
