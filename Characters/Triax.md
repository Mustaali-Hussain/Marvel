> ###### Triax
> **Real name** Triax the Terrible

> **Also known as** Triax the Terrible, Ralph Shaw, Apollo

> [!abstract] In brief
> Evolved warthog

Triax is an evolved warthog and follower of the evil [[Man-Beast|Man Beast]]. 

 

Triax served the Man Beast while he was under the alias Omega. Together they attempted to steal the Infinity Gems from [[Adam Warlock]] and the Infinity Watch but failed.  

 

Later while the Man Beast impersonated a US Senator named Kyle Munson in an attempt to get revenge on Warlock, Triax impersonated a man named Ralph Shaw and worked as the Senator's bodyguard. Again they were defeated by Adam Warlock and taken into custody by the Avengers.
