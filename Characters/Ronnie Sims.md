Veronica "Ronnie" Sims is a private investigator in St. Louis. She uses the nickname Ronnie to sort of trick clients into thinking she is male because most people aren't accustomed to female detectives.

Her best friend is [[Anita Blake]] and they usually work out together. They try to jog 4 miles every other day. She is good at giving Anita advice and helping her through her guy problems.
