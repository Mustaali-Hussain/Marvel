## Creation

Henri Dugarry was created by Jonathan Hickman and Bryan Hitch in Ultimate Invasion #3 as part of an initiative to relaunch the Ultimate Universe.

## Origin

Henri Dugarry is a French nobleman who claims to be a descendant of The Sun King - Louis XIV. Henri wields both the Sword of Might and Amulet of Right granting him mantle of Captain Britain in 6160 reality. He used his newfound powers to conquer Avalon, desecrate its culture and take the Lady of the Lake as his concubine.

Henri Dugarry is part of the Maker's Council - a secret body which divided the earth into seven sovereign territories. He rules over European Coalition.

## Powers and Abilities 

- **Flight**: Can fly at staggering speed and use flight to stay agile in combat. 
- **Invulnerability**: Dugarry is resistant to blunt forces such as being stomped on by Giant Man however he was rendered blind after a repulsor blast the face. 
- **Super-strength**: Dugarry is strong enough to one-shot Sif and damage Iron Lad armor.
