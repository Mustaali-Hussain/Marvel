> ###### Narcisse
> **First appearance** ...Where the Monsters Go #1
> **Appearances** 20

> [!abstract] In brief
> Demon member of the Nightbreed, wears dead humans faces as masks to cover up his hideous features (they were already dead when he found them)

An ex actor that never made it big Narcisse, is somewhat of a tragic comical figure who constantly entertains him self & others with random acts of clowning about.

Though Narcisse is relatively kind natured, he is considered as somewhat horrific due to his habit of scavenging for dead human bodies in order to remove the face and wear it as a mask. Narcisse has confessed this action is both because he truly desires to be human, and that he is very ashamed of his demon face.
