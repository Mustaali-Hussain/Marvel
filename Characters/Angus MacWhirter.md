> ###### Angus MacWhirter
> **First appearance** The Gentleman's Name is Magneto #104
> **Appearances** 35

> [!abstract] In brief
> Boatman who develops a loathing for the X-Men after a ship he rents to them is destroyed by Magneto. He ends up later killed by Proteus.

## Creation

Written by Chris Claremont and drawn by Dave Cockrum.

## Major Story Arcs

Because he knows Moira MacTaggart is concerned with strange happenings in Scotland, Angus alerts her to the presence of a visitor (Cable). He warns her that the visitor is not receiving the warmest of receptions.
