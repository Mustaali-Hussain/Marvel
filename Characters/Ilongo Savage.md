> ###### Ilongo Savage
> **First appearance** Raydeen! #1

> [!abstract] In brief
> Ilongo Savage was one of three co-protagonists of the "Shogun Warriors" series. He was a marine biologist from Madagascar and was chosen to pilot Dangard Ace.

## Creation

Ilongo Savage was created by Doug Moench and Herb Trimpe in 1979 and first appeared in Shogun Warriors # 1.
