> ###### Caledonia
> **Real name** Alysande Stuart
> **First appearance** A Day In The Life #9

> **Also known as** Alysande Stuart, Champion

> [!abstract] In brief
> Greatly skilled in swordsmanship, she fights with courage in the ways of the great warriors of old.

Caledonia was born as a captive on an alternate earth, and is descended from a long line of Scottish warrior champions. When freed from her captivity, she arrived on Earth-616 in New York City. She worked for the Fantastic Four as a nanny for Franklin Richards, thus coming under their protection.

Caledonia's counterpart on Earth-616 is Alysande Stuart.
