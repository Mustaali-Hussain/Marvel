> ###### Zebra Daddy
> **First appearance** Wannabe: Part Three #3
> **Appearances** 17

> [!abstract] In brief
> A Pimp in New York City. Dealt small-time in drugs. Kiden Nixon, X-23, and Catiana's enemy in the NYX limited series. Deceased.

At the start, X-23 was working as one of Zebra Daddy's prostitutes, when one of her clients committed suicide in her hotel room. Kiden Nixon got a telepathic message telling her to go there with her friend, Cameron. The two ladies found a dead man on the floor and X-23, claws out, slitting her wrists.

Zebra Daddy wanted to find X-23 desperately and wanted to kill her because now he was losing money and business. When they got in a massive battle, X-23 stabbed Daddy through the chest just before he could shoot Kiden, killing him instantly.
