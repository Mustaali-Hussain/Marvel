> The captain of Crystar's Royal Guard. Warbow lived in an alternate reality but more recently was seen in the otherdimensional world known as Weirdworld.

## Origin

Warbow was a Captain of the Royal Guard of Crystalium. He was a skilled crossbowman who served in the Chaos War. When his leaders, the twin princes [[Crystar]] and [[Moltar]], found themselves fighting each other, Warbow sided with Crystar. He followed the Chaos sorcerer [[Zardeth]], and managed to shoot out one of the evil man's eyes before the favor was returned.

## Creation

Warbow was created in comics by Mary Jo Duffy and Bret Blevins and first appeared in The Saga of Crystar, Crystal Warrior issue 1 from 1983.

## Major Story Arcs

### Crystar

Being struck in his eye, Warbow was brought to [[Ogeode]], the old wizard of Order, whom saved Warbow's life and transformed him into a crystal warrior to help defeat Moltar's Magma Men. In his new form, Warbow had enhanced strength and endurance. He no longer needed to eat, drink or breathe. Unfortunately, his loss of an eye left his accuracy diminished.

Warbow appeared to be fond of [[Ambara]], and was internally sad to see her affections belong to [[Crystar]]. Later, he became close to [[Ika]], Ogeode's daughter.

### Weirdworld

Years later, Warbow reappeared during the events of Secret Wars, in which the whole Marvel Universe was destroyed and reformed by Doctor Doom. He was seen again as a prisoner in a place named Weirdworld where he met and befriended the barbarian named Arkon. Arkon helped Warbow in his quest to rescue Crystar from the evil Morgan le Fay.
