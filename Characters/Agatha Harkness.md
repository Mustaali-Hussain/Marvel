> ###### Agatha Harkness
> **Real name** Agatha Harkness
> **First appearance** The Return of the Frightful Four #94
> **Appearances** 399
> **Also known as** Agatha Harkness, High Priestess

> [!abstract] In brief
> A renowned centuries-old witch, survivor of the Witch Trials and founder of New Salem. During the Age of Heroes she became the nanny of Franklin Richards and mentored the Scarlet Witch in the ways of witchcraft. She currently acts as an anti-hero and foil to her former pupil.

## **Origin**

Agatha Harkness is a centuries old witch and possesses a great mastery of magic. Her remarkable talents already soon led to her meeting the Fantastic Four. She lived in a home on Whisper Hill, until it was destroyed during a fight.

## Creation

Agatha was created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby and first appeared in Fantastic Four #94 (1970) .

## Major Story Arcs

### Witch Hunts and the New World

*Betrayed by Laveau*

In 1428, in Transia, near Wundagore Mountain, Agatha was with her coven attempting a protection spell to defend them from the oncoming angry mob. They had found out that Agatha and her friends were witches. Among her coven were the ancestors of [[Nico Minoru]] and [[Zoe Laveau]]. Laveau’s ancestor withheld information that Corina, their youngest member, would be sacrificed to the dimensional being, [[Valtorr]], for the spell to work. Agatha, who disapproved of Laveau’s measures, continued for many years to save Corina but was never strong enough to take on Valtorr.

Centuries later, Agatha moved to the “new world” of America. She settled in Salem, Massachusetts and hoped to start a new coven and practice magic in peace. Unfortunately, this resulted in the Salem Witch Trials, which Agatha originally approved of thinking it would test their skills and only make them stronger. Her mind was changed by a time-traveling [[Firestar]], who showed her that war was not the only way.

She uprooted her coven and moved to Colorado, starting the town of New Salem. There she would marry and give birth to a son, named [[Nicholas Scratch]].

### Nanny of the Fantastic Four

*Agatha protecting Franklin*

For decades, Agatha had built a good reputation as a governess, a teacher of children in private households. When [[Mr. Fantastic|Reed]] and [[Invisible Woman|Sue Richards]] had a son, named [[Franklin Richards]], Agatha was hired to be the nanny to the young boy. This was not only done because she seemed to be a sweet older woman but also because of her capability to keep Franklin from harm, even from the supernatural if need to be.

Agatha and Franklin had a good relationship, and Franklin was often left in her care when the Fantastic Four went on an adventure. She soon noticed something strange about the boy. Franklin was an extremely powerful mutant, it seemed. She was the first to warn his parents of his reality-warping power.

Over the years, she became an indispensable ally to the Fantastic Four. She gave them prophetic warnings of threats from [[Overmind]]and [[Air-Walker]]. She sheltered them when Atlanteans attacked New York City. She even once rescued Reed when he was stuck in the Negative Zone. Ultimately, her work with Franklin's powers led to the defeat of [[Ultron]].

### Mentoring Wanda Maximoff

During the fight with Ultron, the Fantastic Four teamed up with the Avengers. This gave Agatha the opportunity to cross paths with [[Scarlet Witch|The Scarlet Witch]]. Sensing her potential, she announced to the Fantastic Four that Franklin no longer needs her and convinced Wanda to accept her as a teacher. She would continue to teach her until her marriage to the [[Vision]].

### Agatha Disassembled

Back in New Salem, Nicholas Scratch, Wanda’s now-adult son, fathered seven children. These children had immense magical power and were assembled as Salem’s Seven. Scratch would use them to help him take over the town of New Salem. He convinced the townsfolk that Agatha had betrayed them by taking her job with the Fantastic Four and had her and Franklin Richards kidnapped to be put on trial. The Fantastic Four came to their rescue, defeating the Salem’s Seven and revealing Scratch’s true evil nature. With this revelation, the townsfolk had him banished.

*Inexplicably resurrected*

Salem’s Seven would eventually take over the town again. They would kidnap their grandmother, Agatha, and burn her at the stake. This got the attention of Wanda and Vision, who fought and defeated Agatha’s grandkids. The magic Wanda absorbed from them would set the stage for the twins she magically conjured for her and Vision to parent.

Because this power was borrowed from pieces of [[Mephisto]]’s soul, he eventually reabsorbed the twins, causing Wanda to have a nervous breakdown. Agatha, inexplicably resurrected, would show up and wipe Wanda’s memory, hoping to relieve her of the trauma. This would eventually backfire, when Wanda’s mind fractured entirely.

During the events of Avengers Disassembled, it was revealed that Agatha had died by the hands of her former pupil, the Scarlet Witch. The Scarlet Witch had had a nervous breakdown over the loss of her children. When Agatha tried to help the Witch by confronting her with her loss, she became feral and slew Agatha in the process. Agatha has not been heard from since her death, but it seems logical that the Avengers had a burial for her.

### Resurrection

*Walking the Witches' Road*

Agatha was resurrected when her spirit came to the aid of Scarlet Witch, who was investigating an illness in witchcraft. She teamed up with the spirit of Wanda’s biological mother, [[Natalya Maximoff|Natalya]], to combine their powers with that of Wanda’s to take on Chaos, an abstract identity that sought to kill the Goddess of Witchcraft. Natalya sacrificed herself to take Chaos out, ensuring Witchcraft's safety and returning Agatha to the land of the living.

### Strange Academy

*Youth restored*

Eventually, she took a job at the Strange Academy, a school founded by [[Doctor Strange]]to prepare the next generation of magic users. There, she would work with student Zoe Laveau and guest lecturer Nico Minoru, descendants of women she once was in a coven with. One day, she, along with most of the magic users in the world, had a shared prophetic vision of the end of the world centered around Zoe. As vapor goons arrived to attacked Zoe, Agatha whisked her away to safety.

In an old safe house of Agatha's, she described to Zoe the mistake that her coven made when summoning Valtorr and wants Zoe's help in fixing it. Unknown to Agatha, Corina, the coven member who was left to Valtorr, was now going by Korrosion. She had slain Valtorr and was now manipulating the prophecy to get vengeance. With help from a magic mirror, Zoe and Agatha connected to the Spirit Rider,[[Kushala]], who was trapped in Valtorr's realm with a newly assembled Midnight Suns investigating the vision. She connected them to Midnight Suns from other realities fighting Korrosion.

With the help of Zoe and a variant Nico Minoru, Agatha used the ancestors of her former coven members to create the apocalyptic vision that started everything. This created an anchor in the present for the Midnight Suns to jump to from Valtorr's realm and protect Zoe and Agatha from the vapor goons. This gave Agatha the opportunity to create a magical dagger and stab Korrosion, causing them both to disappear. With the danger seemingly gone, Kushala and her other teammates returned Zoe to the Strange Academy.

However, Agatha did not die. She was reborn with her youth restored.

### Contest of Chaos

*Agents of Chaos*

Since Wanda had bonded herself with the Darkhold in order to imprison Chthon, Agatha decided to forge a new Darkhold by stealing Chthon's heart as a conduit. She believes this is the only way to protect reality from pure chaos breaking free. Working from the Bermuda Triangle, Agatha would pit heroes against each other to generate and contain chaos magic, leaving the victor of the face offs under her control. Those heroes include:

- Wolverine
- Storm
- Human Torch
- Taegukgi
- White Fox
- Venom (Dylan Brock)
- Cyclops

Keeping the heroes captive raised her profile to the point that The Avengers got involved. They tracked Agatha to the Bermuda Triangle, where they witnessed long lost sunken ships rise from the ocean carrying the mind-controlled heroes. These heroes did their best to stop the Avengers from entering the whirlpool at the center of the Triangle, where Agatha was putting the finishing touches on this second edition of The Darkhold. Wanda and Carol Danvers eventually made it to her and tried to stop Agatha. When Carol grabbed the Darkhold, an energy pulse was released, giving Agatha the opportunity to escape. She later washed up on a beach where she was greeted by a young boy who claimed to be The Darkhold.

### Blood Hunt

For months, Agatha pretended to be Darkhold's mother as they traveled the world trying to keep a low profile. Darkhold ran away after growing tired of Agatha's protectiveness. He was too powerful and easily hid from her tracking spells. It wasn't until her old students from the Strange Academy tracked him during an attempt by the vampire cult, The Structure, to take over the world.

When she attempted to approached Darkhold, he tried teleporting away, but Agatha managed to keep up. She cast a spell that made doppelgangers so that she could surround Darkhold. The Strange Academy students did not like Agatha trying to attack Darkhold. They decided to defend him from the many Agatha doubles. With Agatha preoccupied, Darkhold managed to determine which Agatha was the real one and hit her with an energy blast that forced her to retreat.

### Amaranth

*Mentors and Students*

Agatha is next seen recuperating on Amanna - a planet powered by magitech - and gazing into a seer's crystal ball. There a vision of her former student [[Amaranth]] appears as an omen, causing Agatha to destroy the crystal out of anger. Amaranth is the living bodiment of a truce between the abstract entities, Witchcraft and Chaos. Her power untrained and untested could be dangerous. So, Agatha departs Amanna, insisting Amaranth may require a new teacher, and makes her way to Wanda Maximoff's Emporium to pass the torch. Back on Amanna, it is revealed that Agatha sent a magitek android named Eliminator after both Wanda and Amaranth to showcase the young witch's power.

### Sorcerer Supreme

After Doctor Doom usurped the title and vestments of the Sorcerer Supreme from Doctor Strange, the heroes stood against him in a big battle which caused the death of Valeria Richards. Heartbroken by the loss of his goddaughter, Doom sacrificed himself, title and all, to the Living Tribunal for Valeria's return. Wanda heard the cries of the Cloak of Levitation and Eye of Agamotto and sacrificed the Last Door to save them from cosmic incineration. She believed the vestments were choosing her as the new Sorcerer Supreme, which she accepted.

Unfortunately, the Vishanti did not approve. After they failed to reason with Wanda, they forced Agatha Harkness to be their champion and betray Wanda. Agatha would try to get an edge on Wanda, including an alliance with Madelyne Pryor. Wanda and Agatha ended up in a brutal battle that was eventually stopped by the Vishanti so that Oshtur could try to shame Wanda. Sacrificing a young planet with no life, Wanda was able to strike down Oshtur and talk sense into Agamotto and Hoggoth to leave her be.

As they left, they also raised the magic obligation from Agatha so she would stop fighting and could be healed by Wanda.

## Powers

**Witchcraft: **Agatha possesses a great mastery of the mystic arts. She can also create illusions, move objects, project force-fields and thoughts, and teleport.

- **High Priestess: **Agatha Harkness' mystic abilities are so vast, that she is 1/3 the Earth's High Priestess, a title that she shares equally with her former student, Scarlet Witch, and X-man, Storm.
- **Familiar: **Her pet cat, Ebony, can transform into a mystical panther and is very dangerous.
- **Longevity: **Agatha has been alive for quite some time and recently been restored to a level of youthfulness.

## Other versions

### Heroes Reborn

In the Heroes Reborn pocket reality created by Franklin Richards, Agatha Harkness is once again teacher and mentor to a young Wanda Maximoff. Together they leave their home of Salem to live on Avengers Island, located in the New York harbour. Wanda had been left in Agatha's care since she was a child by her mother, [[Enchantress|Amora the Enchantress]], an Asgardian who is acting against the Avengers in secret alongside [[Loki]]. Agatha senses the incoming danger of Amora and Loki, but before she can warn anyone Loki uses his seidr sorcery to trap Harkness inside an oak tree. This gives Enchantress an opening to masquerade herself as Agatha and lure Wanda into a trap. Agatha eventually frees herself by possessing Ebony's body and travels to Avengers Island, alerting the team to the truth: Wanda's body is being possessed by a Loki-enchanted [[Hellcat]]. After Agatha banishes Hellcat's soul to the void, Wanda is freed and Agatha reverts to her human form.

### Ultimate Agatha Harkness

In the *Ultimate* Universe, Agatha is a Colonel in S.H.I.E.L.D. with Alpha Clearance. She visits the Baxter Building to have words with [[Franklin Storm|Dr. Franklin Storm]]. She feels that the needs of the Fantastic Four are not being met. She says the history of the program they are running is one of squandered potential and wasted opportunity.

She proceeds to interview each member, asking questions about their personal lives and relationships. She seems to want to really connect with [[Human Torch|Johnny Storm]]. Agatha is a professor in the Ultimate Universe. However, she does not like Susan Storm and she brilliantly leads Susan into an ambush. Agatha joins with the rest of Salem's Seven in beating Susan to the edge of unconsciousness and tossing her into the river to meet a watery doom.

### House of Harkness

In this reality, Agatha is descended from a line of renowned magical users who founded the eponymous House of Harkness school for witchcraft and sorcery. Agatha's parents Evanora and Hiram act as the owners and Headmasters of the boarding school and their daughter is the top student in her respective field. When the runaway mutant-witch Wanda Maximoff is invited to study at HoH, Agatha is threatened by her natural talent and the two become somewhat rivals to one-another.

### WandaVision (2021)

*Kathryn Hahn as Agatha Harkness*

Agatha Harkness is a "nosy neighbor" sitcom-trope named Agnes to Wanda and Vision in Westview, before she was revealed to use magic which she used to fight Wanda. Agnes is played by Kathryn Hahn in *WandaVision* on the Disney+ streaming service.

Agatha was a member of a coven of witches in Salem. She was caught practicing dark magic and put on trial by her mother. But amidst her execution by magic, she reversed it and killed her would-be murderers, including her mother.

Sensing a wave of magic, she ended up in Westview which was stuck in a warped reality under Wanda Maximoff's control. Intent on uncovering how it happened, she feigned submission and with knowledge from the Darkhold, she took on the identity of Agnes.

### Agatha All Along (2024)

Kathryn Hahn reprises her role as Agatha Harkness in the *WandaVision* spin-off, called *Agatha All Along*. The spin-off debuted on Disney+on September 18th and takes place after both *WandaVision* and *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*. In the show, Agatha has been freed from the enchantment Wanda had left her in and must form a new coven of witches to walk the Witches' Road to regain her powers.

The coven - made up of Agatha, Lilia Calderu, [[Jennifer Kale]], [[Alice Gulliver|Alice Wu-Gulliver]], and Mrs. Hart - alongside "Teen," walk the Witches' Road and are faced with five trials along the way. Each trial corresponds to the witch in the coven and their respective speciality. As part of the First Trial, it is revealed that Agatha previously gained possession of the Darkhold by exchanging her son, [[Nicholas Scratch]], for the book. After Mrs. Hart dies during the First Trial the coven summon a replacement green witch, inadvertently bringing [[Death|Rio Vidal]] - a woman with which Agatha shares an intense history - to the Road. Over the course of the show Agatha displays kinship towards "Teen," which is dashed when Rio reveals he isn't her son Nicholas.

*Agatha and Billy Maximoff*

During the Third Trial Agatha's body is assaulted and possessed by the spirit of her mother, Evanora Harkness, who warns the coven that her daughter has been evil since birth and must be killed. As the witches decide what must be done Agatha suffers great physical pain at the hands of her mother, resulting in Alice Wu intervening and using protection magic on Agatha. By doing so, Agatha's magic-siphoning abilities activate and she revitalizes herself by draining Alice of her life force, killing her fellow witch. Agatha deduces that Teen is not her lost son but the son of Wanda Maximoff, breaking the sigil and revealing Teen to be Billy Maximoff. He takes control of Jennifer and Lilia to throw Agatha from the road, but Agatha claws her way back and the two continue alone to the Fourth Trial.

*Agatha and Rio Vidal*

After Lilia passes the Fourth Trial and sacrifices herself so the others may proceed, Billy and Jennifer pass their individual Fifth Trial and ascend from the Witches' Road. Agatha eventually passes and returns to Westview.

She is joined by Joe Locke, Patti Lupone, Sasheer Zamata, Ali Ahn, Debra Jo Rupp, and Hahn's *Parks & Rec *co-star, Aubrey Plaza. Following them down the Witches' Road is the evil Salem's Seven.

### Marvel's Midnight Suns (2022)

*Agatha Harkness in Midnight Suns*

Agatha is a key supporting character in Firaxis Games' tactical role-playing game Midnight Suns, and acts as a mentor to the games' protagonist The Hunter. Before the events of the game, Agatha lived with her fellow sorceress and lover [[Sara|Sara the Caretaker]] on the mystic grounds of The Abbey, a Transian headquarters that they transposed across the Atlantic Ocean to the woods of Salem. After the fall of [[Lilith]], Agatha & Sara took in her child, Hunter, and raised them as their own. Years later later Lilith returned to The Abbey to lay claim on Hunter and set in motion the series of events that were foretold in the prophecy of The Midnight Sun. Agatha & Sara defended their home, however the original Midnight Suns, Hunter and Lilith were all killed by The Darkhold, and the two witches retreated into their mourning for hundreds of years.

In the 21st Century, Agatha & Sara slowly reintegrated into the world during the Age of Heroes.
