> [!abstract] In brief
> Gunthar the Rigellian was a member of a space pirate crew, serving under Nebula. He could mentally control others, fire psionic blasts and increase his density to gain armor-like skin. He was cruel and mirthless, tormenting those lower than him in hierarchy.

Gunthar is a Rigellian and member of a cosmic mercenary group led by [[Nebula]].   

## Creation

Gunthar was created by Roger Stern and John Buscema in 1985 and first appeared in The Avengers # 255.
