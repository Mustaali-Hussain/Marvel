## Origin

The second Spymaster was an apprentice of the [[Taskmaster]] who won the right to the name in a series of tests which included (but was not limited to) the killing of all other contenders. This Spymaster discovered Iron Man's secret identity, then ambushed and nearly killed [[Iron Man|Tony Stark]] who was saved in time by the [[Black Widow]]. It was later revealed that Nathan Lemon was this Spymaster's true identity, and that he had been working for [[Mandarin|The Mandarin]].
