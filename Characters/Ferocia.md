## Origin

Fera was originally a wolf from K'un Lun. She lead the pack of wolves that killed Daniel Rand's mother. Fera was later transformed into human-wolf hybrid by the sorcerer Master Khan. He would use the Amulet of Shirrairto control her and order her to do tasks for him. One, the first setting her up to fight against Iron Fist, the son of the woman she once killed as a wolf. She did beat him, and stole the Gem of Quon from him, taking it back to Master Khan. When Iron Fist, Misty Knight and Colleen Wing sought reclaiming his stolen item, Fera in fact aided them, not wanting to fight him if he was weakened and by explaining how she was exploiting her commanders command to only steal the item from Iron Fist rather than fight him. Eventually Iron Fist and Luke Cage would fight Master Khan, and Fera would take the opportunity to rip the Amulet of Shirrairto from Khan, freeing herself from is rule and commands. Having choice and freedom, she left thanking Iron Fist for the distraction he provided but warning him if they were to ever met again, it would be in combat.

## Creation

Ferocia was created by Roy Thomas and Gil Kane in 1974 and first appeared in wolf form in Marvel Premiere #15.

## Major Story Arcs

### The Femizons

Fera was apart of Superia's Femizons for a brief while.

### Civil War

Recently, Ferocia was implanted with Skrull organs by The Corporation. This allows Ferocia to shapeshift to take on a fully human appearance. She along with King-Size, Flame and Blue Streak busted Ricadonna out of prison. Ferocia was later captured by the Heroes for Hire.

## Powers and Abilities

Ferocia possesses superhuman strength and agility. She can see in the dark and track by scent. She has razor sharp claws and teeth. With the current addition of Skrull organs she can shapeshift to hide her appearance. Her speed, stamina, reflexes are all enhanced as well. Her five physical senses are enhanced and more comparable to a wolfs sense than humans. She is an expert martial artist and well versed in K'un Lun martial arts style. Her combination of strength, speed, and fighting abilities made her quite dangerous, she managed to fight groups of heroes the calibre of Iron Fist and Powerman at once. She once used a quick nerve punch on Misty Knight to quickly incapacitate her.
