## Origin

Sofia was an anxious, stuttering Singaporean schoolgirl. When her mutation manifested, she learned she could will herself to do anything for one minute. When this allowed her to excel in school, bullies branded her a cheater that was trying to make them look bad. They jumped her, and Sofia defended herself by willing kickboxing skills. Unfortunately, she accidentally disabled one of the bullies, they could no longer walk. She presumably ran away from home due to that.

## Creation

Jitter first appeared in Uncanny X-Men (2024) #1 by Gail Simone and David Marquez

## Major Story Arcs

### Red Wave

Jitter and the other Outliers were being chased by [[Sarah Gaunt]], a monstrous bounty hunter that was capturing mutants for Graymalkin, the prison that used to be the X-Academy. Member [[Ransom]] flipped a coin between X-Men and the Brotherhood, and it landed on X-Men. They tracked a few former members to Haven House orphanage in Louisiana. [[Rogue]] was inspired to restart the X-Men to train and protect these kids, working out of Haven. She was joined by [[Gambit]], [[Nightcrawler]], [[Jubilee]], and a reluctant [[Wolverine]].

Sarah Gaunt eventually caught up with them, attacking Wolverine. When Rogue and Kurt found his body, she sent Logan back with Kurt while she faced Sarah on her own. Meanwhile, an army of Sarah's followers showed up to Haven House forcing the X-Men and The Outliers to team up before the Outliers were ready. They proved successful, although it meant Gambit using the Second Eye of Agamotto to give them an edge.

### Raid on Graymalkin

During a supply run into town, new teammate [[Calico]] was captured along with Jubilee and taken to the Graymalkin Prison, formerly the Xavier Institute. Despite disapproval from [[Cyclops]], leader of the Alaska X-Men, Rogue mounted her own raid on Graymalkin, only to enter a standoff with Cyclops' X-Men, who were mounting their own raid. Believing their raids were in danger of failure due to the other's raid, with a psychic push from [[Philip Scurvy|Scurvy]], an empath working for Graymalkin. Members were pitted against each other with Jitter summoning temporary championship wrestling abilities to take on [[Kwannon]].

As Graymalkin guards got involved with the fight, they were interrupted by [[Professor X|Charles Xavier]], former leader of the X-Men who had escaped his cell in the prison. Xavier battled Scurvy releasing the two X-Men teams from his grasp to make a concerted effort against the Graymalkin security team. Outgunned, warden Corina Ellis resorted to threatening the towns in which the X-Men were operating with her sonic weapons in orbit. With no other choice, the teams peacefully left with only their imprisoned members returned to them. Xavier volunteered to stay, admitting to his sins and accepting his punishment.

However, Xavier later escaped and left the planet after receiving a psychic distress call from his daughter, Xandra.

### The Dark Artery

Jitter and the others were awoken by Deathdream who was communicating with a nearby spirit. The voice leads them to The Artery, a graveyard under a swamp guarded by Man-Thing. Behind a door was Lady Henrietta, Guardian of the Penumbra, an underworld for relatives of mutants who betrayed or abused them, but the actual Penumbra was ruled by Shuvahrak, who had been calling to one of the Outliers to take her place. Hotoru volunteers, but his teammates refuse to let him go alone.

Dressed in special living uniforms to protect them in the underworld, the Outliers were confronted by the spirits trapped in Penumbra, rageful and spiteful toward living mutants. Thankfully, they got back up from Man-Thing, who took a liking to the kids, and their X-Men mentors who came looking for them. Shuvahrak interrupted the fight to demand one of the Outliers be her successor, but the X-Men weren’t giving any of them up without a fight. Calico called forth Ember, who was revealed to have been brought back from the dead. Shuvahrak feared Ember and was, by extension, weak to Man-Thing, who caused her to burn.

Injured, Jubilee asked for mercy from Shuvahrak, that she not kidnap any of the kids under their guardianship as her daughter was kidnapped from her (her inspiration for creating Penumbra). She relented but demanded they never return.

## Powers and Abilities

**Skill Mimicry: **Jitter is able to will herself to take on skills for one minute.

- **Limitation: **Any longer than one minute, and she starts to feel a crash after she stops using her powers. The longer she uses them, the more significant the crash.
