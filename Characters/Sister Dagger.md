## Creation

Sister Dagger was created by Gene Luen Yang and artist Dike Ruan for the miniseries *Shang-Chi (2020)*.

## Origin

*Esme with her brothers Shang-Chi and Brother Sabre*

Esme is the daughter of Zheng Zu and an unknown woman. She rose to the rank of Sister Dagger, and is the acting Commander of the House of the Deadly Dagger. Since her half-brother Shang-Chi took over their father's organisation, Esme has accompanied her siblings on numerous missions.
