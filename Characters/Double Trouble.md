> Twin mutant sisters whom only manifest powers when in constant contact. The full extent of their powers is unknown, but they've shown the ability to fly as well as fire concussive plasma bolts from their hands. They were part of the X-Force rival team, Weapon P.R.I.M.E.

## History

Double Trouble is the only name that has been revealed for these twin sisters. Serving with Weapon P.R.I.M.E. they share a psychic link which, when activated through physical contact, allows them to fly and produce super-heated plasma-elemental blasts. They were teamed up along with several other Marvel mercenaries such as [[Garrison Kane]] (of Weapon X fame), [[Yeti]] (who was not a mutant, but in fact an Inhuman from Attilan gone rogue), and [[G.W. Bridge|George Washington Bridge]] (to name a few). The girls and their teammates turned out to be quite the proverbial thorn in the side of X-Men spin-off team, X-Force, and managed to give [[Northstar]] a super speedy run for his money as well.

As with the majority of their comrades, it is unknown if they retained their powers post M-Day.

Their current whereabouts are also unknown.

## Creation

Double Trouble was created by Fabian Nicieza and Greg Capullo in 1993 and first appeared in X-Force # 22.

## Powers & Abilities

Double Trouble are mutant, twin sisters that share a symbiotic link that gives them access to their mutation only when in close contact with one another. The twins can fire plasma bolts for range attacks or use the plasma charge between them to strike enemies. They also are able to use their psionic link to fly and communticate with one another. If they are separated, their powers are nullified
