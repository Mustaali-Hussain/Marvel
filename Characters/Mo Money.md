## Origin

A rap mogul who used super-goons to lean on his competition, until one of his hip-hop rivals took a forty story swan dive. Mo Money was convicted and sent to The Cage.

## Major Story Arcs

### The Hunted

**For more information see: The Hunted**

While in The Cage, Mo Money becomes one of the ruling class and runs the black faction know as The Brothers. The Brothers are consistently at war with the Skulls, a white power group that worships Red Skull. He uses a brick house, named Axe as his body guard. 

During Wolverine's brief stay in The Cage, Mo Money orders Axe to try to kill the X-man, but Axe fails and kills one of Skulls by mistake. This event starts a riot that ends with Wolverine being put into solitary.

### Fear Itself: Thunderbolts

**For more information see: Fear Itself & Fear Itself: Thunderbolts**

Mo Money is killed.
