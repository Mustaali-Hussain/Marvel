## Summary

Mr. Nobody is a small time crime boss on Earth-982 (MC2 Universe). He originally worked for the Kingpin as both an enforcer and an assassin.

Mr. Nobody first appears in Spider-Girl #1 , where he is stalking Peter Parker. But his plans fail when the new Spider-Girl intervenes. He uses his teleportation abilities but Spider-Girl proves too much for him. He escapes.

From time to time, Mr. Nobody visits the Kingpin in jail, making him aware of the crimes being committed in the city.

Mr Nobody once tries to break Crazy Eight out of prison, only to be stopped by Spider-Girl. They both try to fight her but lose after Spider-man himself helps his daughter.

Later, Mr. Nobody allies with Crazy Eight to fight for Kingpin to restore his glory in a crime-war with rival crime-lord, Canis. Things do not go according to plan when Spider-Girl, the 'new' Spider-man and Crazy Eight's brother, Funny Face (working for Canis) become involved in a battle, during which Mr Nobody shoots Crazy Eight. Funny Face's ensuing rampage leads to both crime-lords loosing millions, causing Kingpin to fire Mr Nobody, leaving him wide open to be captured by Dark Devil, the 'new' Spider-man and the original Spider-man.

Mr Nobody next shows up during 'Marked for Death,' apparently reformed and working with Special Agent Arthur Weadon's team of former criminal's seeking redemption. However, it is eventually revealed that Mr Nobody had in fact gone rogue, instead working for Canis. Using his powers to plant bombs to kill many of New York's crime bosses, including his previous employer, the Kingpin. He was finally defeated by Spider-Girl and was last seen in the custody of Agent Weadon.

## Equipment

- High-tech teleportation suit that also provides limited intangibility.
- Gas grenades.
