## Creation and Evolution

Odessa Drake was created by Nick Spencer and Humberto Ramos. She made her first appearance in The Amazing Spider-Man #8 - Heist Part One.

Later on, we learn that Odessa once had a romantic relationship with Black Cat. Although her sexuality has never been explicitly confirmed, her having had a romance with Felicia means that she is attracted to women and she is not straight. 

## Powers and Abilities

After making a deal with a demon, Odessa became immortal. She is also extremely wealthy and possesses leadership skills, seeing as she successfully leads the Thieves Guild's New York branch.
