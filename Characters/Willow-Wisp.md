## Origin

Ignacio Fuentes was from a poor family and kidnapped by Alchemax to be a test subject in their new super soldier experiment, the same one that gave powers to [[Aaron Fischer]]. Fuentes had a grudge against Alchemax because his sister died during the trials. He found spare goblin gear that [[Norman Osborn]] had abandoned, creating a new Willow-Wisp alias to terrorize Alchemax, including Norman, in OsCorp Tower.

## Creation

Willow-Wisp debuted in Marvel's Voices Infinity Comic #99 by Anthony Oliveira and Bailie Rosenlund

## Major Story Arcs

### Sinister Six

*Unhappy with Squid Kid's plan*

After a failed attack on the OsCorp Tower, Fuentes was recruited by [[Squid Kid]] for his new Sinister Six. Their first action was to break into OsCorp and kidnap [[Stanley Osborn]] for the goblin formula written into his genetics. Fuentes was one of the members who was not happy kidnapping a child.

Fellow member, [[Mysteriant]], convinced him to go along with it now so they could do something about it later. Stanley's brother, [[Normie Osborn|Normie]], was a student at the Avengers Academy and talked his fellow cadets into recuing his brother. Independently, Mysteriant worked with the cadets to set up an illusion to save Stanley and bankrupt Squid Kid. With a chance to leave, Fuentes leaves.

### One World Under Doom

When [[Doctor Doom]] usurped the title of Sorcerer Supreme, he used it to install himself as world emperor. A Hydra splinter group, led by [[Sin]] used the confusion to hide their new illegal activities as they rounded up seditious residents of America including Fuentes. He would be kept on a train prison transport along with friends of the Avengers Academy, Marcus Wetherell and Jara Rivensky. The cadets tracked them down and freed Fuentes along with their friends.

## Powers, Abilities, and Weapons

**Super Soldier: **Willow-Wisp survived a trial of an Alchemax super soldier program.

**Goblin Gear: **Wisp is armed with white goblin gear. He has armor suit, exploding pumpkins, a bat-flyer, and a flaming helmet.

## Variants

### Dystopian Future

Fuentes made it to a future where Hexus had destroyed the world. He did his best to save who he could from the [[Emplate]] vampires that roamed the new world. His most recent saves were Normie Osborn and [[Moon Girl]], sent to the future to fix an incursion by [[Iron Lad|Kid Immortus]]. He agreed to help them, which meant confronting the adult Normie who was running Hexus. Angry, Fuentes sacrificed himself trying to take the adult Normie down.
