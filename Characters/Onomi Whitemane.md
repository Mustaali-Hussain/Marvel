> ###### Onomi Whitemane
> **Real name** Onomi Whitemane
> **First appearance** Prime Elements, Part 3: Universal Inhumans #577
> **Appearances** 37
> **Also known as** Onomi Whitemane, First Chair

> [!abstract] In brief
> She is the Queen of the Kymellian race. She was married to former King/current Ambassador Yrik Whitemane and conceived Kofi Whitemane of Power Pack notoriety. When the Kymellians were conquered by the Inhumans, Onomi became one of Black Bolt's wives. Kymellians are alogoanthropes (Were-Horses, often mistaken for Centaurs whom are also Alogoanthropes, but have human torsos and horse extremities) or human/horse hybrids.

The Inhumans of Earth are not the only Inhumans in the universe. The Kree experimented on five different races. These were: humans, Alpha Centaurians, Badoon, Kymellians, and the Dire Wraiths.

Although their collective rules jointly, each house retains a separate structure unique unto itself. Onomi Whitemane is the First Chair of the Kymellian White Room, and ambassador to her world.
