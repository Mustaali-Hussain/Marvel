> ###### Vixen
> **First appearance** #9
> **Appearances** 36

> [!abstract] In brief
> Captain Britain foe, eventually turned into her namesake by Jamie Braddock

Vixen was a British crime boss, with access to wealth and high technology. She preferred that her closest henchmen were younger, more physically fit specimens. A frequent adversary of [[Captain Britain]] and a former ally of the original [[Slaymaster]], she was eventually transformed into her namesake through the powers of [[Jamie Braddock]]. Her criminal empire was then handed over to [[Nigel Frobisher]], whom Braddock had similarly transformed into a duplicate of Vixen herself. While Frobisher was eventually killed and Braddock's machinations were drawn elsewhere, the current whereabouts and fate of Vixen are unknown.
