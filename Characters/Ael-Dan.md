> ###### Ael-Dan
> **First appearance** The Fool on the Throne #53

> [!abstract] In brief
> Ael-Dan and Dar-Benn took over the Kree Empire together

Dar-Ben and Ael-Dan together assassinated the current emperor of Kree, [[Clumsy Foulup]]. He was only the emperor after a plot of the [[Contemplator]]. With what appeared to be the [[Silver Surfer|Silver Surfer's]] help they destroyed him and took command of the Kree Empire.

 

 It all turned out to be a plot by them the whole time. The Surfer was actually a robot they had created.
