## Origin

Oneg The Prober is a member of the Celestials. Nigh omnipotent and mysterious space gods, extremely powerful beings whose origins are vague, and actions and motivations as mysterious. They are responsible for Earths Eternals and Deviants races. Visiting the planet Earth every few thousand years in groups collectively known as Hosts to observe, analyze, judge or pass judgment on Earth and its species. Each Celestial typically stands at approximately 2000 foot tall, and each possessing levels of tremendous power that few in the universe can rival. Oneg The Prober's function as a Celestial, is to examine and study a planets subterranean resources and features, assessing and recording its data. Oneg is distinguished from his peers as he is purple hued. Oneg has appeared with the First and Fourth Hosts.  

## Creation

The Celestial Oneg The Prober is a Marvel comics book character created by writer and artist Jack Kirby first appearing in The Eternals #9 released in 1977. The Celestials as a concept bare some resemblance to the real life author Erich von Däniken projections of giant space gods who have influenced and directed the progress of humanity, with clues and signs of their involvement hidden in patterns and monuments from ancient civilizations. 

## Character Evolution 

Oneg the Prober is one of the original Jack Kirby created Celestials.

## Powers and Abilities

Oneg The Prober like many of the Celestials is an extremely powerful god like being, possessing incalculable levels of strength, durability, speed and energy projection. Enormous in stature, up to 2000 feet in height, they possess the ability to manipulate reality, control vast levels of energy and rearrange matter up to an unknown degree They can teleport themselves or vast objects across vast distances. If harmed they will regenerate near instantaneously. Capable of withstanding earth shattering blows and greater Celestials also possess the ability to generate energy blasts that are of an earth shattering level and higher. There is an unknown connection between the Celestials and Hyperspace. It is possible that Hyperspace is the origin of the Celestials vast powers. Oneg The Prober is charged with experimentation and examination.
