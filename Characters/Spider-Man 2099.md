## Origin

On Earth-928 the year is 2099, Miguel O'Hara worked as the head of the genetics department for the Alchemax Corporation, specializing in genetic coding and splicing. He toyed with the idea of recreating the powers of the original Spider-Man from years ago. Miguel's boss, [[Tyler Stone]], secretly got him addicted to the drug Rapture. This highly addictive drug couldn't be worked out of the body. It was Stone's way of controlling his employees by offering them the expensive drug in return for their loyalty. Miguel decided to try to genetically cure himself by rewriting his genetic code from a sample he had taken previously. He was being watched by a competitive co-worker Aaron Delgato who attempted to sabotage his experiment by adding a random code into the process. The results cured Miguel but also gave him spider-like powers. Aaron tried to shoot him but he fell from the Alchemax building despite Miguel trying to save him. Soon Tyler sent out a cyborg bounty hunter named [[Venture]] to capture him. Miguel fled to his home to escape Venture. Grabbing a costume from a Day of the Dead celebration he fought Venture and won. Miguel began fighting crime and the evil corporation Alchemax as Spider-Man.

## Creation

Spider-Man 2099 was created by Peter David and Rick Leonardi and first appeared in Spider-Man 2099 issue 1 (1992). He had however appeared before in previews in Marvel Age and Amazing Spider-Man 365.

In the year 2099, reality began to slowly warp, getting exponentially worse. Miguel confronted Doom, but Doom took no responsibility. Instead, he sent Miguel back through time to get help. This would have normally caused the timeline to collapse after his last time travel adventure, but the collapse was already happening.

Miguel fell through time and landed back on Earth-616 in the present yet again. He landed on an offshore oil platform belonging to Roxxon during a disaster bigger than Deepwater Horizon. He couldn’t remember how he got to the present or how to use his spider-powers. He only knew he needed Peter’s help. While being chased by Roxxon goons, Miguel eventually catches up to Peter and informs him of what’s going on before disappearing from the timeline.

Once Miguel disappears from the timeline, the resulting temporal energy surge powers a probability device created by an ESU student. Spider-Man is able to use the device to predict the best course of action to sway the timeline damage, which was caused by Doctor Doom overreacting to an assassination attempt on one of his Doombots.

Once the timeline stabilized, Miguel reappeared and washed up on an island. While searching the beach town, he found Tempest with his son, given a second chance at having a family. Meanwhile a variant of himself was reborn in a new 2099.

## Earth-2099

### Origin

*Manipulating his genetics*

In the new 2099 future, Miguel was an up and coming geneticist with Alchemax. He was sent to an area called The Ravage, where prolonged exposure causes fatal mutations. He was looking for a useful mutation when he found a spider humanoid mutate. He took a number of tissue samples during an autopsy and isolated the genetic sequence. Miguel was racked with guilt because his work wasn’t more helpful to those who needed it.

After trying to score drugs in the boroughs and arguing with his bleeding heart brother, Miguel ran into an old man who claimed to be the previous version of Miguel. The older him filled Miguel in on the Forgotten Age (the present fo Earth-616) about noble heroes standing between destruction and everyone else. They were memories stolen by Doom, and old Miguel believes young Miguel was the key to fixing it. He needed to use the spider humanoid genetics to become Spider-Man

Miguel rejected it at first, but his brother was the victim of Alchemax. He had broken into Miguel’s apartment to steal confidential files about Alchemax’s secret business, and the company responded by blowing up Miguel’s apartment, killing Gabriel in the process. This was the trigger than pushed Miguel into willingly becoming the Spider-Man of 2099.

### Exodus

*Spidey protects the garden*

When an Illuminati type group called The Cabal kills a Celestial, it crashes near Nueva York. Its body causes a new Garden of Eden to spring up. Miguel was monitoring chatter from The Cabal that was interested in securing the garden for their own needs. Led by [[Norman Osborn]], who learned to extend his life, The Cabal has placed a bounty on the garden, and Miguel is afraid it will cause chaos as civilians compete to secure it for the prize.

Miguel collected a group of allies that could assist him in protecting the garden. Ghost Rider 2099 got him in touch with Winter Soldier 13. She helped take down the Murmurnet satellite, shutting down the gig economy that was offering the bounty. When Osborn called in his Masters of Evil, Miguel convinced[[Moon Knight 2099|Moon Knight]], the surviving Avengers, to bring together a new roster of Avengers to stand against them. It wasn’t until the X-Men tried to make a home out of the Garden that Osborn finally showed his face.

With the X-Men and Avengers as back up, Miguel fought this Galactic Goblin. He swiped his black car, the key to his wealth and power, and slipped it to the mutant technopath, [[Nostromo]]. With Ghost Rider’s help, Nostromo hacked the card and shut the whole network down, cutting The Cabal off from its resources. No longer above the law, Osborn was arrested, and the Garden was given to the X-Men.

### Dark Genesis

*Trapped by Carnage*

After Miguel managed to shut down Osborn, his “black carders,” Cabal members who were above the law, protests broke out against the next group of elites trying to take their spots. Miguel, with his new partner, Spider-Woman, had to walk a thin line of allowing them to speak but stopping them before they got violent. This was made more difficult by Carnage, who joined the protests for the sole purpose of instigating riots. He was able to stab Miguel in the leg causing the wound to immediately fester.

He needed the help of Ghost Rider and [[Blade 2099|Blade]], who tended to his wounds and helped him and Spider-Woman strategize against this version of Carnage, which shows signs of vampirism. Their plan was to deoxygenate Carnage’s blood making it useless to him and then lock him in Rider’s Ghost Room. Carnage had new allies though, specifically [[Halloween Jack]], who was posing as a member of the Alchemax board, and a spider-android called Flipside that Alchemax had developed. He has also used his blood to put part of the protesters under his thrawl as his “hive.”

Thankfully, more allies come to Miguel's side. Punisher, Daredevil, Moon Knight, and a new Venom all join him against the hive. Together, they were finally enough to overtake Carnage, whom Venom threw into a building fire to finally be rid of.

## Powers

*Web-swinging!*

Spider-Man 2099 has powers similar to the modern day Spider-Man. He has superhuman strength, agility, durability, speed, reflexes, eyesight. His healing abilities are also heightened. He has spinnerets in his forearms that allow him to shoot organic webbing out from the top of his wrist.

Miguel also has retractable talons that come out of his fingers and toes that allows him to climb any surface, and can also be used offensively. They've been shown to be able to cut and grab most objects, but were ineffective against Thanatos' armor.

He also has fangs that secretes poison if he bites someone. The poison of these fangs appear paralyzes Spider-Man's "victims". The poison has been shown to be powerful enough to affect people like the Sub-Mariner, Risque, and even Thanatos.

Miguel also has genius-level intelligence and is especially gifted in genetics.

*Fangs!*

He doesn't have any extra sense like Spider-Man's danger sense, but makes up for it with all his enhanced senses, and doesn't web-swing as much as Spider-Man either, but is able to travel as fast as the Original Spider-Man is. Instead he uses a web cape for gliding. His "web cape" was given to him by a "Thorite". One of Thor's worshipers. This Thorite believed that Spider-Man is the Harbinger of Thor, and gave it to him, because, as the Thorite so rightly put it, "The Spider-Man must be masked".

His costume is composed of Unstable Molecule Fabric (UMF). This allows Miguel free use of his talons without the risk of damaging his suit, and protects him from slashing damage as seen when Superior Spider-Man takes a swipe at him with his claws but fails to cut the suit. It appears the UMF also protects against acid which Miguel notes in his battle against Venom 2099 who was secreting a skin-eating acid.

Miguel received a new high-tech outfit, courtesy of Peter Parker that improved upon his original suit. This time the Unstable Molecules were bonded to kevlar, greatly increasing its durability. It features on-board sensors for complete vicinity readings, better Lyte Byte Material for improved gliding, and internal boot thrusters for launch.

Not much was heard from Miguel untill a few months later, when he noticed, thanks to some Exiles equipment he took with him, that a group of beings known as the Inheritors had been killing Spider-like heroes through the multiverse.
