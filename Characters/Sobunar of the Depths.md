Sobunar is an Omega-level mutant from the island of Arakko and a member of the Great Ring . His body contains an entire oceanic ecosystem which he can release from himself; he used this power to create oceans and lakes on Mars during the terraforming of the planet.

## Major Story Arcs

### Morlocks

Following the Fall of Krakoa, Sobunar has left Arakko to lead the Morlocks, mutant outcasts who live beneath the city of New York. Alongside the mutant Caliban, Sobunar has created a miniature paradise for those with physical mutations, and invite Anole to join them in their cause.
