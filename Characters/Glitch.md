## Origin

Glitch is a member of the techno-organic alien race, The Technarchy. He rejected his culture's hivemind, disconnecting himself. Exiled, he floated around space alone for a few hundred years. Eventually, he came across Xanto Starblood, where he volunteered for Starblood's experiments. Starblood had him turned into a supersuit. 

## Origin

Glitch is revealed in *The Amazing Spider-Man (2025) #15* by Joe Kelly and Emilio Laiso, however, the supersuit that resulted from Xanto's experiments on him first appeared in Issue #11 of the same series.
