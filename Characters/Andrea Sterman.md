> ###### Andrea Sterman
> **First appearance** The Wild Horses #2
> **Appearances** 66
> **Also known as** Andie Sterman

> [!abstract] In brief
> Superhero biographer and therapist

Andrea Sterman was a professional writer and psychiatrist who crafted a book about the urban hero Nomad by looking into his past as Jack Monroe.

Her next book involved a deep probe into government conspiracies and nearly got her killed. She was taken in by the V-Battalion for her own protection after an assassination attempt, and became something of a voluntary prisoner among the organization for many months.

Years later, she was asked by the Avengers to become a psychiatrist to Moon Knight.

She claims that her specialty is "superhuman menticide (brain-washing".
