## Brief History

White Cloak is a character from Earth-295 who encountered the X-Force team when they went to the Age of Apocalypse to try and find a "Life Seed" to cure [[Angel|Archangel]], from his counter-part personality, Death.He is the teleporter of the Black Legion and uses his powers to travel across Earth-295. His identity is unknown, but he seems to be a combination of [[Cloak]] and [[Dagger]].
