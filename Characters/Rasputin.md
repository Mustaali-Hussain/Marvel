## Creation

Rasputin IV was created by Jonathan Hickman and R. B. Silva in Powers of X #1 - The Last Dream of Professor X as part of the Dawn of X relaunch. Rasputin is a mutant "chimera" from the future, cloned from the DNA samples of [[Colossus]], [[Kitty Pryde|Shadowcat]], [[Quentin Quire|Kid Omega]], [[Unus the Untouchable]] and X-23.

## Powers and Abilities

### Chimera Mutant Physiology

- Rasputin is a chimera mutant and possesses a wide array of powers including: Organic Metal Skin
- Intangibility
- Force Field
- Superhuman Strength
- Telepathy
- Healing Factor
- Rasputin can also manifest a fiery version of the Soulsword.

Her strength level is most likely similar to her father's, placing her around 70-tons in lifting power. She would also most likely share his weaknesses.

## Weaknesses

Colossus' metal skin is vulnerable to the anti-metal Vibranium, as evidenced when Xraven stabbed his arm with a Vibranium blade which instinctively turned him back into his normal form.

Colossus can be damaged when attacked by something made from or bonded with Adamantium (e.g. Wolverine's Adamantium-bonded claws).
