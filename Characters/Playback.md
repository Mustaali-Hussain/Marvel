> [!abstract] In brief
> Playback is a paranormal in Marvel's New Universe title Justice.  She uses her postcognition to assist law enforcement agencies.

Playback uses her postcognition to assist police and national security agencies in their investigations. Playback is notable for being one of the few paranormals positively judged by Justice, and playing a key role in revealing his identity to the public. Initially terrified of Justice and disapproving of his methods, Playback and Justice eventually form a working relationship, and she assists him in his failed attempt to hunt down a hired killer.
