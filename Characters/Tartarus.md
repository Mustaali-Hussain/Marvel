> ###### Tartarus
> **First appearance** Hard Landing #101
> **Appearances** 25

> [!abstract] In brief
> Tartarus is a member of the Neo capable of making people experience their worst nightmares.  He is the brother of Elysia.

During the Neo war on humanity (and Tartarus's only appearance), the criminal Rufus Delgado took Tartarus hostage while running from police detective Charlotte Jones. Tartarus, in turn, made Delgado experience a version of Hell based off of the murderer's past crimes. When Charlotte Jones followed, Tartarus merged her with Delgado, creating a monster-like creature.
