## Brief History

The niece of criminal mastermind Egghead. She has been her uncle's pawn on more than once and thankfully saved by Hank Pym each time. Trish even lost one of her arm as a result. Hank Pym reconstructed a robotic arm for her, but the arm's control was taken over by a villain, witch caused Patricia to live the rest of her life without the robotic arm. She is a former model and even dated Nighthawk for awhile. The last we seen from her, she and current boyfriend Fred Sloan left together for peace and quiet in a small town somewhere in America.
