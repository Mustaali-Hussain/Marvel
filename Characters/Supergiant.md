> ###### Supergiant
> **First appearance** Infinity #1
> **Appearances** 63

> [!abstract] In brief
> A mentally unstable telepathic member of the Black Order who preys on the intellect of her victims.

Supergiant was found as a child in an orphanage for the mentally unstable by [[Thanos]]. As she watched him kill one of his children, she asked for the same reward. Thanos said he would if she helped him find and kill his remaining children. Supergiant is the most psychotic of the Black Order. An omnipath and telepathic vampire, Supergiant feeds off of others' intelligence. Any intelligence and information Supergiant knows, Thanos knows.

Supergiant alongside [[Corvus Glaive]] was sent to the Jean Grey School for Higher Learning as part of Thanos' invasion. When they reached the school, after assessing the abilities of the mutants, Supergiant took over the mind of [[Iceman]] and used him to attack other mutants until she was forced out by [[Rachel Summers|Rachel Grey]].

She was next used by Thanos in Necropolis, where they found the Illuminati's world destroying bombs (designed for Universal Incursions). She also took control of [[Black Bolt]], who was defeated by Thanos, to learn the bombs secrets and use him as a weapon and trigger.

She planned to detonate the device and kill Thanos' last remaining offspring [[Thane]], as well as herself and Earth in the process. It was [[Maximus]]that detonated the device, but right before explosion [[Lockjaw]]teleported it and Supergiant into space, killing her.

## Powers and Abilities

**Omnipath**: Supergiant has the ability to read and control the minds of people of any race. She is a mental parasite who devours the mind of her victims. 

**Psychic Possession: **Supergiant has the ability to project a mental energy surge that overwhelms her victims' conscious minds, making them more subservient to her will.

**Psionic Entity:** As a psionic entity, Supergiant has the following powers:

- **Intangibility:** Supergiant can phase through almost anything, including opponents and obstacles.
- **Damage Immunity:** Supergiant is immune to all forms of physical damage.
