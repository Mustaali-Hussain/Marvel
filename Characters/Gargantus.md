> [!abstract] In brief
> Gargantus is an Iron Man foe. He is an alien robot in the form of a Neanderthal Man. He has superhuman strength, agility and can hypntotize people through "his" eyes.

Years after being defeated by Iron Man, Gargantus' race sent an army of robots as means to hypnotize Iron Man to their side, the golden avengers broke free of their control with Captain America's help, Steve sent an ultimatum to the aliens stating for them to never return.
