## Origin

Cynthia was a part of a clan of gypsies who dwelt within Latveria. She married Werner von Doom and gave birth to a son they named Victor. She always believed he was a special boy and encouraged him greatly. She was skilled in witchcraft and powered by a demon named Mephisto whom she called upon for power. It was under his influence that she murdered many people. Eventually she was shot and killed by a soldier.
