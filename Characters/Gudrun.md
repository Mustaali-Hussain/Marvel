## Origin

Gudrun is married to Volstagg and has at least eight children, most notably is Hildy

She seems to be named differently in every story, but Gudrun seems to be the most common name.

## Major Story Arcs

### Lost Gods

Gudrun was expelled from Asgard by The World Ash, losing his powers and memories. He was sent to Midgard, impersonating **Gerty ****Starwit**, Vince Starmit's wife managin a hotel in Malibu.

### Fear Itself

When Volstagg returns after helping Loki stop the Serpent, he wants to "ravage" his wife Hide but thier many children jump him first. Volstagg is only able to give his wife a quick peck before he is forced to tell his children the tale of how HE stopped the Serpent single handed. After Volstagg spins his yawn and all the children go to bed Gudrun finds her husband moping outside. She give him a brief pep talk and heads to bed, expecting his company soon.

## Powers

Gudrun is an Asgardian goddess, the wife of Volstagg. Like all the Asgardians, Gudrun is extremely long-lived, aging at an extraordinarily slow rate since reaching adulthood. Asgardian flesh and bone is about three times denser than similar human tissue, contributing to the gods' superhuman strength and weight. An average female god can lift about 25 tons. Gods are immune to all terrestrial diseases and resistant to conventional injury. The metabolism of the gods gives them superhuman endurance in all physical activities.
