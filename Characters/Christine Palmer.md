## Nurses, Heroine

Christine Palmer studied with Linda Carter, the first Night Nurse, and was one of her friends. Christine Palmer work at Metropolitan General Hospital and highly skilled, and she is willing to serve people even on her day off. She even went out with Nightcrawler and befriended the X-Men.
