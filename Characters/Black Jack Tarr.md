## Origin

Jack Tarr was a man whom grew up during the cold war and became a member of the British Army, MI-6, and Freelance Restorations. He became romantically involved with a fellow MI-6 member named Anna, but the two broke up when Jack's sense of duty came before his own priorities. He was heartbroken by this, but remained a member of the special forces and joined the CIA. He later rejoined MI-6 as one of the leaders, along with his good friend [[Clive Reston]].

## Creation

"Black" Jack Tarr was created by Steve Englehart and Jim Starlin.and first appeared in Master of Kung Fu issue 17 (1974). Black Jack Tarr is sometimes portrayed as a bigot because of his politically incorrect manners but is truly a good man and a good friend to [[Shang-Chi|Shang Chi]].
