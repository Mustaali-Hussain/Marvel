> ###### John Ogord
> **First appearance** Let My Planet Go! #29
> **Appearances** 11

> [!abstract] In brief
> Starhawk's son

John is the middle child and only son to [[Starhawk|Stakar]]and [[Aleta Ogord|Aleta]]. Born with latent psychic abilities, he was kidnapped by his grandfather, [[Ogord]]of the Reavers of Arcturus, and mind controlled into attacking his father. His powers were unlocked as well, making him a psychic vampire.

When the Guardians of the Galaxy removed the children from mind control, they instantly aged and died, their powers having reversed from the loss of control.
