> ###### Goro
> **First appearance** Killing Zone #31
> **Appearances** 14

> [!abstract] In brief
> Goro was a Yakuza agent and the right-hand man  of Dai-Kumo. He could only be killed by the blade of a dead man. He managed to kill Patch/Wolverine and buried him. But the dead man revived and his blades did kill Goro.

Goro was an assassin for the Yakuza, who always boasted that only the blade of a dead man could kill him. He eventually came upon a young girl name [[Reiko]]; whose father owed Goro numerous debts. He took the young girl to be his property until the day he died; making her his pupil/slave.

 Goro was eventually hired by a drug lord, [[Dai-Kumo]], to kill [[Tyger Tiger|Tyger Tiger's]] body guard, Patch (aka, [[Wolverine]]). Goro managed to "kill" Patch with a sword through the chest. However, he was not prepared for Patch to return and indeed kill him.
