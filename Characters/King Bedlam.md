## Brief History

King Bedlam is the brother of Jesse Bedlam and transmits a psionic scrambler field that can disorient others and protects him from telepathy.  He was a very powerfull being and fought X-Force and his brother during one occasion. 

## Creation

King Bedlam was one of the few characters to first appear outside regular 616 Marvel continuity. He first appeared in Factor X issue 1 (1995) as part of the Age of Apocalypse crossover. He first appeared in 616 continuity in X-Force 83.  

## Alternate realities

### Age of apocalypse

In the Age of Apocalypse, Christopher is a member of Sinister´s Elite Mutant Force.
