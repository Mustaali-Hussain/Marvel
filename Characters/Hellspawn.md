> ###### Hellspawn
> **First appearance** Chthonic Maneuvers #1
> **Appearances** 21
> **Also known as** Red Man

> [!abstract] In brief
> Evil demonic-looking doppelganger of Daredevil.

During the Infinity War crossover  , a number of heroes and even a few villains had doppelgangers made of themselves by the [[Magus]], much like [[Spider-Man|Spider-Man's Doppelganger]]. Daredevil's doppelganger, Hellspawn, looked more literally like Daredevil's demonic namesake. Later, when the creature died during the Fall from Grace story arc, it turned into Matt Murdoch's human form, albeit with horns. [[Daredevil]] used this as a way to fake the death of Matt Murdoch, and took on the new alias of Jack Batlin.
