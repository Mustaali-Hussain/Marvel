> ###### Valkin
> **First appearance** The Origin of the Red Raven #1
> **Appearances** 31
> **Also known as** General Vulcanin

> [!abstract] In brief
> Valkin is an Eternal and was the leader of their Polar clan. He later became the leader of all the Eternals and left Earth when they joined the Uni-Mind.

*"Once more, you are a disappointment, Druig. Once more. I would rather the Celestials had carved me a statue in dung than given you to me"* -- Valkin.

Valkin opposed Thanos leadership and was imprisoned into The Exclusion.
