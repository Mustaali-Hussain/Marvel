> [!abstract] In brief
> A pastiche of DC Comics' Lois Lane. She is the paramour of Hyperion.

A journalist for the Cosmopolitan, **Lonni Lattimer** was a woman who had a semi romantic relationship with the hero[[Hyperion]].

When the Squadron Supreme choose to change its modus operandi, Hyperion finished the relationship with Lonni, after revealing her his secret identity.

During the Heroes Reborn saga (2020), Lonni reappered as an inquisitive journalist who questioning the actions of Senator [[Nighthawk|Kyle Richmond]].
