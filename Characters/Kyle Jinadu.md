> Kyle is Northstar's husband. He doesn't have any powers, and is supportive of Northstar's heroic lifestyle.

## Brief History

Kyle has been friends with Aurora for years. She asked him to become the manager of " Team Northstar Extreme Snowsports" to keep an eye on her brother. This led to Kyle and Northstar's relationship. Kyle has continued to manage Jean-Paul's brand and coordinate shows, audio-visual and personal relations.

### **Fear Itself**

When Gary Cody betrays Alpha Flight, Kyle gets kidnapped by Department H and Northstar abducts one of their agent to get the location of where Kyle is being held. Northstar and Aurora manage to rescue Kyle along with Marrina. Kyle and Jean-Paul are currently still together and will get married in Astonishing X-men issue 52.
