Shades was a Dominican criminal that Luke Cage had once befriended while incarcerated. He was killed during an explosion caused by Bullseye, after attempting to turn his life around. Shades is the father of [[Power Man (Alvarez)|Power Man]] and frequent partner of [[Comanche]].

## Creation

Shades was created by Archie Goodwin and George Tuska in 1972 and first appeared in Hero For Hire #1.

## Other Media

Theo Rossi will play Shades as part of Marvel's Luke Cage series on Netflix.
