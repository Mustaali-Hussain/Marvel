> ###### Gammadroid
> **First appearance** Deadly Anniversary! #257
> **Appearances** 18

> [!abstract] In brief
> One of the Mad Thinker's creations

The Gammadroid is similar to the [[Mad Thinker|Mad Thinkers]] other android creations, except is built with technology discovered after he had become a part of the Intelligencia. The Gammadroid is powered by gamma and cosmic energies. Like his other androids, the Mad Thinker can control the Gammadroid with his mind and it is used to do heavy lifting or to protect him.
