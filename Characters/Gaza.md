## **Bio**

 

Gaza is a mutate, a human that was changed by [[Magneto]], that lived in the Savage Land, a tropical preserve hidden in Antarctica. Gaza was one of Magneto's first Savage Land Mutates, and has been involved in all the Mutates' subsequent activities.  

## Creation

Gaza was created by Roy Thomas and Neal Adams in 1969 and first appeared in X-Men # 62.  

## **Powers**

Gaza is blind, but possesses psionic abilities that enable him to “see” mentally.
