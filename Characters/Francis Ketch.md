## History

Francis Ketch's husband was a shop owner until he was murdered by an armed robber. She and her husband had been unable to have kids of their own, so when a man came to her with [[Barbara Ketch|Barbara]] and [[Ghost Rider (Ketch)|Daniel]] after their mother was killed in a car crash, she adopted them.
