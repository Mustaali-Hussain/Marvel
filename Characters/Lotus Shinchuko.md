## Brief History

Lotus Shinchuko had been enslaved by the Silent Ones. However, she broke free of their control and with the help of the Sons of the Tiger, defeated her captors.She then joined the Sons of the Tiger and entered into a relationship with Bob Diamond. However, as time passed, she fell in love with Lin Sun. This caused Bob to attack Lin in a jealous rage. After this, the Sons of the Tiger disbanded. She now runs a martial arts school with Lin and Abe Brown.
