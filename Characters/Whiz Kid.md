> Whiz Kid is a member of the Pennsylvania's official Initiative team, the Liberteens.

## Brief History

Whiz Kid started out working as a courier for Goodman, Lieber, Kurtzberg & Holliway. She was employed at this law firm until it was destroyed by [[Titania]].

When the 50-State Initiative was made, she joined the Liberteens who were based in Philadelphia. She was killed during the Secret Invasion of the Skrulls.
