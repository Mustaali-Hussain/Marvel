## Origin

A young Chinese girl changed by the terrigenesis cloud who was saved by Reader shortly after getting her powers.

## Creation

Iso was created by Charles Soule and Ryan Stegman and first appeared in Inhuman issue 4 (2014).

## Major Story Arcs

### Joining the Inhumans

After journeying overland from China to Venice with the Reader, Iso is targeted by the Ennilux as the new host body for their leader. The Reader helps her escape after he discovers her fate. Reader and Iso have gone their separate ways and she has joined Medusa on Attilan with the rest of the NuHumans.

## Powers

Iso has the power to control pressure able to create pressure on anything within her range that she focuses on.
