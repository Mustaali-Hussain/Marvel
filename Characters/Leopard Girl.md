## Origin

The secretary for a scientist who works in the middle of the jungle because civilization is too noisy for him. Gwen is given a mystical formfitting leopard outfit by a witchdoctor which grants her enhanced agility, knowledge of the jungle and the ability to summon leopards to aid in times of trouble.

## Creators

Leopard Girl was created by Don Rico and Al Hartley, she first appeared in Jungle Action Issue #1. Gwen is one of the few characters from Marvel’s history with more than 3 appearances that has not been brought back yet.
