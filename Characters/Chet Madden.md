> [!abstract] In brief
> Chet Madden was the former director of A.I.M. who oversaw projects involving Cache and Protocide and was, coincidentally, legally represented by Steve Rogers' then girlfriend Connie Ferrari.

## Creation

Chet Madden was created by Dan Jurgens and Andy Kubert and first appeared in *Captain America* #28.

## Character Evolution

Chet Madden was featured as the director of A.I.M. starting with *Captain America* #28, but wasn't introduced as Chet Madden until *Captain America* #35 when he appeared as one of [[Connie Ferrari]]'s clients, leading many resources to incorrectly identify his first appearance as being *Captain America* #35. His identity as director of A.I.M. was revealed in *Captain America* #39.
