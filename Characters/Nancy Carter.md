> ###### Nancy Carter
> **First appearance** The Beginning Of The Two-Gun Kid #60
> **Appearances** 49

> [!abstract] In brief
> Girlfriend of Matt Hawk, the second Two Gun Kid.

A school teacher who became romantically involved with the lawyer Matthew Hawk.

Contrary to other western romantic interests, she loved the kind Hawk and hated his alter ego, Two-Gun Kid (who was responsible for the killing of her outlaw brother).

Later, it was revealed that she married Hawk and had a daughter with him, but died due to birthing complications.
