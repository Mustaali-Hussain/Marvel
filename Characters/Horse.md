## Brief History

Horse is the powerhouse of Sovel's Shi'ar Salvagers crew. Horse has need for medication, called his "Happy pills", for his somewhat unstable psychic nature. Horse and his crew encountered the X-men a few times. First when they came to earth, where some of his teammates wanted to kill Rogue. On a different occasion, Horse and his team teamed up with Rogue, Rachel, Magneto and Gambit in order to save the X-men's lost teammates, Havok and Polaris. This was not voluntarily, but because Sovel had lost his leadership against Rogue during a duel when he had chosen Horse as his representative to fight Rogue.
