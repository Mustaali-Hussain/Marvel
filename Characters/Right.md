## Creation

Right was created by Evan Skolnick and Richard Pace in 1994 and first appeared in New Warriors # 54.     

## Powers & Abilities

Right has one enlarged, right arm that is at least four times larger than his normal, left arm. Right has a degree of superhuman strength.
