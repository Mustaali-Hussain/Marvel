> [!abstract] In brief
> Rapture is an artificial copy of the winged, blue-skinned mutant nun known as Sister Joy which was created by Cerebro.

## Character History

 Rapture was a part of Cerebro's team of X-Men created in order to "catalog and store" all mutants on Earth. In addition to being able to fly on account of her wings, Rapture wielded a sword.

As a synthetic creation of Cerebro, Rapture was a combination of mutants [[Nightcrawler]], [[Mystique]] and [[Angel]].
