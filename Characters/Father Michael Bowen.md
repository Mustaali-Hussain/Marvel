## Origin

Father Michael Bowen was a priest whom wanted to make the world a better place. He had a favorite niece named Tandy Bowen, a girl he would later again meet when she became the superhero known as Dagger.

## Creation

Father Michael Bowen was created by Chris Claremont and Frank Miller and first appeared in Marvel Team-Up issue 100 (1980).

## Major Story Arcs

### Meeting the New Mutants

In his duty as a priest, Father Michael Bowen ran a self-help relief agency for immigrants. He helped Xi'an Coy Manh (Karma) gain employment in the United States. He was also a paternal uncle to Tandy Bowen (Dagger). He kept in touch with both women over the years.
