> ###### Yvette Avril
> **First appearance** No S.H.I.E.L.D. To Protect Me! #119
> **Appearances** 20

Yvette Avril was operations manager for the Parisian division of Stark Industries. She was a close confidante of Tony Stark (Iron Man) and resigned when Obadiah Stane took over the company. She was reinstated when Stark regained his corporate holdings.
