## Overview

Daughter of Eitri, the master Forger and king of the dwarves, Kindra is an honest and caring young dwarf girl. When the New Mutants first visited Asgard, Kindra nursed [[Cannonball]] back to health. She quickly developed a crush on the handsome and tall mortal boy.

Kindra has been a bargaining tool for [[Hela]] on a later occasion. In a similar manner to when [[Loki]] blackmailed Eitri into forging a powerful hammer that gave [[Storm]] powers over the weather (see Giant-Sized Uncanny X-Men Annual #9) in exchange for Cannonball's life, Hela threatened to kill Kindra and the other captive New Mutants if Eitri didn't create an Uru sword that was capable of killing [[Odin]].
