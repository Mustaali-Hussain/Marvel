## Known History 

 

 He is the first born child of Nightcrawler of the X-men and Kymri  an alternate dimension princess. He was born after his father gained fame as a world class actor in Hollywood. His dad is arguably the most famous mutant actor of his time staring in such films as the mutant James bond flick 'Dying is forever". He was named TJ after his alternate dimension sister Talia Josephene AKA Nocturne  who at the time was believed dead by his father. 

 

 When the Skrulls and Shi'ar attacked and his father temporarily disappeared he was kept safe by his mother.
