> [!abstract] In brief
> Tito is an X-Man in the future as seen in Here Comes Tomorrow.

Tito Bohusk Jr., is the son of [[Tito Bohusk]]. therefore the grandson of [[Beak|Barnell Bohusk]] and [[Angel Salvadore]]. He took the codename his grandfather had out of respect. (Beak must have done something spectacular because Tito thinks he was a great hero). Unlike his grandfather, Tito is a musclebound and very capable fighter. He looks less like a chicken and more like an eagle. In Here Comes Tomorrow, Tito stood alongside [[Wolverine]] and the rest of the X-Men against Sublime and his Crawlers. He died, but before he did, he got to do the Fastball Special with Wolverine, something he had been waiting his whole life for.
