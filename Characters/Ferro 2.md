> ###### Ferro 2
> **First appearance** Sid's Story #4
> **Appearances** 60

-Ferro-2 is a replacement for his brother, who was killed in "Excalibur: The Sword is Drawn" by the Warwolves because his "Warranty Expired".

A furry, extradimensional warrior with a Cockney accent, he had four arms, with a sword in each, possibly the most bad-ass combo ever. He was a minor backgrounder, though.

When Technet returned to Excalibur, Ferro2, right along with the rest of his group, formed the hero group N-Men.

## Powers and Abilities

Ferro was a talented swordsman, and given he had four arms, an effective hand to hand combatant.

### Weapons

Ferro customarily carried four swords that he wielded with great skill using all four of his arms.
