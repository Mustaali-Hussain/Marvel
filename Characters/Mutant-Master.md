> ###### Mutant-Master
> **First appearance** We, the Jury... #37
> **Appearances** 32

> [!abstract] In brief
> The leader of Factor Three evil organization, he is actualy an alien from the Sirin race, and wants to destroy the Earth.

The Mutant-Master was an alien from star-sun Sirius  that intended to cause nuclear war between the US and the Soviet Union, which would wipe out the human race, and he uses his Factor Three organization to try it. After Changeling discovered his plans to destroy mutantkind as well as humankind, he helped the X-Men to defeat him. Banshee destroys Mutant-Master's cloaking device, and reveals his alien appearance. Then the X-Men and the remainder of Factor Three joined forces in attacking Mutant Master, who committed suicide to avoid capture, during X-Men #39. 

 

The Mutant Master had no powers of his own, relying on equipment. His weaponry included lasers, explosives, sonic weapons and a force field.
