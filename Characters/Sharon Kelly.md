> Sharon Kelly was the wife of Robert Kelly. She was killed in a fight between Rogue and Master Mold/ Nimrod.

## Origin

Sharon was a former waitress in the Hellfire Club from Sebastian Shaw. There she met and started dating Senator Robert Kelly. The two where married not much later.

## Creation

Sharon Kelly was created by Chris Claremont and Marc Silvestri and first appeared in The Uncanny X-Men Vol. issue 246 (1989)

## Major Story Arcs

### Death

The two were quickly married, but Sharon Kelly was soon killed while facing a combination of Master Mold and Nimrod. This made Senator Kelly's hatred towards mutants grow stronger.
