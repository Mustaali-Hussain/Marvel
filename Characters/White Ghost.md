## Creation

McLeish was created by Warren Ellis, Leinil Francis Yu, and Edgar Tadeo. He first appeared in Wolverine #119, acting as the main antagonist for the story of *Not Dead Yet*, which lasted until Wolverine #122.

## Major Story Arcs

Became and enemy of [[Wolverine]] after he killed Wong Tak-Wah, the father of Ai-Chia, Wolverine's girlfriend at the time.
