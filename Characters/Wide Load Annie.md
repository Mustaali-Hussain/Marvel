> ###### Wide Load Annie

> [!abstract] In brief
> Wide Load Annie is a former trucker and the current proprietor of the Star Stop.

Before marrying and opening the Short Stop with her husband, [[Poppa Wheelie]], Anne Wheelie was a trucker. Annie has a fiery temper and loves to fight. Annie once tossed Phil LeGreed out of a window when he threatened to foreclose on the Wheelie's mortgage. She afterwards hid out with [[Ulysses Archer|U.S. Archer]] on a haul until things cooled down.

During that haul, she raced and defeated Iron Mike, a biker who had been terrorizing a small town. When it was discovered that Iron Mike had been defeated by a middle aged woman, his reign or terror came to a close.

## Creation

Wide Load Annie was created by Al Milgrom and Herb Trimpe in 1983 and first appeared in U.S. 1 # 1.
