## Origin

Rambo was the pet dog of [[Rick Sheridan]]. He lived together with his owner in an apartment in New York City. Rambo also became friends with Sleepwalker, which resided inside Rick.

## Creation

Rambo the Dog was created by Bob Budiansky and Bret Blevins in 1991 and first appeared in [[Sleepwalker]] # 1.
