> [!abstract] In brief
> Son of Wolverine & Maureen Logan.

Scotty is the son of Logan and Maureen and the brother of Jade. He lived with his family in California, where they were constantly terrorized by the Hulk Gang for paying rent. Scotty was a brave kid and risked defending his father against the Hulk Gang, who spared him at the time. Unfortunately, the Hulk Gang later returned while Logan was away with Hawkeye and killed his entire family.
