> ###### King Lau
> **First appearance** Afraid of the Dark #1
> **Appearances** 11

> [!abstract] In brief
> Supporting character in Elektra (vol 1)

Grown in Hong Kong, he was friend with the local governor and becoming a martial art movie superstar, until the day some chinese mafia men from the Triad showed up to extort money and got all killed by King Lau. He immediately flew off to NYC, U.S.A.

Here he opened a dojo to keep children safe from the streets, and house troubled figures.

He keeps order with his friend McKinley Stewart, ex-fighter, and both they help Elektra when she needs more.
