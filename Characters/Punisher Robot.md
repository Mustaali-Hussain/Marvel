> ###### Punisher Robot
> **First appearance** If This Be Doomsday! #49
> **Appearances** 45
> **Also known as** Punishers

> [!abstract] In brief
> These half robot, half organic creatures hail from the outer rim of the universe. They are both ruthless and merciless fighting machines that were introduced to humanity by Galactus.

The Punisher Robots are the armed forces of the world devourer [[Galactus]]. The Punisher Robots reside on Galactus' Ship and answer directly to Galactus' mental whims, attacking any enemy Galactus cannot be bothered with. It is uncertain how many Punishers Galactus has, sometimes only calling one and other times calling a multitude.  It would also seem that they are obedient to any current Herald of Galactus as well. 

 

The Punishers have incredible strength and speed, capable of inflicting a barrage of physical attacks at super speeds. Even with near invulnerability, the Punishers have displayed the ability to create force fields.
