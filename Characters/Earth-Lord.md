## Creation

Earth-Lord was created by Tom DeFalco and Ron Frenz in 1988 and first appeared in Thor # 395. 

## Powers & Abilities

Earth Lord has tremendous superhuman strength which enables him to lift up to 100 tons, and he is highly resistant to injury. Earth Lord can also draw upon the mass of the earth to increase his height to that of a giant.
