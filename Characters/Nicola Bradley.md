## Powers & Abilities

A genius inventor, Bradley made a battery far more advanced than any before in his time period and he also seemed to have some form of technopathic ability to allow him to understand a machine in a mental map he could generate; it's not specified if this is a mutant power or not though he does specify understanding/seeing very complex things a normal person could not.
