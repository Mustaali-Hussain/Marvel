> Sando is a Nazi spy who acted as a fortune teller during World War II.

## Origin

Sando hired [[Omar]] to form a fortune telling double act during WW2. His plan was to instill fear in the hearts of the American public by predicting disasters before they occurred. The predictions were actually projections that appeared on a fake crystal ball and the actual disasters were planned Nazi terrorist attacks.

## Creation

Sando was created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby.
