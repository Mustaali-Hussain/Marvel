## Origin

Sindr was born as the daughter of Surtur, just like all of his children. When she turned three, she was subjected to a test. She went 13 days without eating. Then she was brought to the throne room in front of her father, where she had to make a choice: on one side, a pile of food the size of a troll; on the other side, a pile of flames and screaming souls.

Sindr was the first child to choose the flames.
