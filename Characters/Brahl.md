## Origins

Brahl is an Achernonian alien and an enemy of the Guardians of the Galaxy ([[Charlie-27]], Nikki, [[Starhawk]], [[Martinex|Martinex T'Naga]], [[Yondu|Yondu Udonta]], [[Major Victory|Vance Astro]]).Brahl became a member of Force, but ultimately betrayed them to [[Malevolence]]. He was willing to make a deal with her in order to get his revenge on the Guardians of the Galaxy.

## Powers & Abilities

Like some Achernonians, Brahl can turn intangible, allowing him to avoid conventional assaults. He can rematerialize at will, performing his own attacks. In addition, he can also project blasts that turn other people and/or things intangible. He possesses purple-grey skin, and his two toes ending in sharp talons. He sometimes uses an energy blaster.
