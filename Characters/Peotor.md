## Origin

Peotor was an orangutan who was purposefully exposed to cosmic rays by the Red Ghost. This granted Peotor higher intelligence as well as magnetic and gravitational powers.

## Creation

Peotor was created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby in 1963 and first appeared in Fantastic Four #13.
