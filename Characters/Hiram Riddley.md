## Origin

Hiram "Ram" Riddley was a computer hacker who hacked into Captain America's Hotline and began filtering calls for him based on their relevance, urgency and importance.

## Creation

Hiram Riddley was created by Mark Gruenwald and Paul Neary and first appeared in Captain America #313 and made sporadic appearances afterwards.

## Character Evolution

Ram eventually gave up on his hero when his mother was shot during a car-jacking and Captain America and his friends had done absolutely nothing to prevent these types of crimes.
