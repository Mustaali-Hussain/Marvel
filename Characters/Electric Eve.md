## Origin

Eve was a high school student that was once addicted in using heroin. She then worked as a prostitute due to the influence of her boyfriend named Ricky. Because of this, her parents kicked her out of the family. Her powers first manifested during her work when she accidentally electrocuted a John. After the incident, she joined the Postman's Morlocks. 

 

## Creation

Electric Eve is a Morlock and a Marvel comics character. The Morlocks are underground group of mutants who are disaffected and feel shunned by society. Their name is derived from the H. G. Wells novel The Time Machine, which also features a group of beings who live underground. Electric Eve is from the Chicago Morlock community and was created by Geoff Johns, Shawn Martinbrough and Gregory Wright. Electric Eve first appears in Morlocks #1 released in 2002. 

 

## Major Story Arcs

 

### **Morlocks**

Eve is one of the mutants who go in search for food while other members remain in the tunnels. Once, Eve encountered a young mutant named Cell. She protected him but refuse to declare that she fought to defend him. Postman then said that every Morlock will have a chance for their wish before leaving their life in the tunnel.

 

Angel Dust and Electric Eve fought, leading to an incident in which Angel Dust believed she had seriously harmed Shatter. When Trader confronted her, Eve said she was just trying to toughen Angel Dust up. Once Shatter's wish to be reunited with his dog, Hank, was fulfilled, it was Eve's turn. The Morlocks fled back to their tunnels, where Postman proceeded to chastise Electric Eve for her actions. Electric Eve reminded him he'd given no conditions on the last request. Trader stood up for Electric Eve, telling Postman he had a similar wish, to get the guy who outed him as a mutant. After Postman, in a fit of rage, erased the remaining policemen's minds, Electric Eve went over to Trader's body. Electric Eve commented that Litterbug seemed to know a lot about Sentinels, and he stated he'd helped build them.

 

In the ensuing battle, Eve fried the main controls, and all the connected Sentinels with it. During the decimation when above 90% of the mutants were depowered, Eve is one of the mutants whose status being depowered, lost, dead or active is unconfirmed.

 

## Powers and Abilities

Electric Eve for her name's sake was an electrokinetic who have the ability to generate electric fields that results to small electrical bursts that is enough to cause problems to machines or even kill an average human by being short circuited.
