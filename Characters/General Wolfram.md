## Origin and History

**General Wolfram** is a "rehabilitating" criminal who joined the Redeemer in taking down the Sinister Spider-Man, Venom (Mac Gargan).

Wolfram first encountered Gargan when he committed a theft in the middle of New York. Although no one was harmed, Gargan took great pride in "saving the day", humiliating him and eventually chewed off one of his limbs while running away with the cash Wolfram intended to steal.

As Wolfram recovered from his injuries in a local hospital, the Redeemer gave him a visit to take down Spider-Man in an attempt to kick the super-villain habit.

## Powers and abilities

General Wolfram claims to be the result of Nazi genetic experiments, but because his accent was proven to be faked the origin of his abilities are put into question.

Wolfram appears to be covered in brown fur and possesses razor sharp talons and fangs.
