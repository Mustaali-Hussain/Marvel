## Creation

Smoke Eater debuted in Blood Hunt #1, and was created by Jed MacKay and Pepe Larraz.

## Major Story Arcs

Following the Blood Hunt, the Bloodcovern goes into hiding in New York. [[Bloodstorm|Bloodstorm One]] observes newly-sired vampires being cured by the Blood Hunters, and sends Smoke Eater after them alongside [[Cruel]] and [[Unusual]]. The trio ambush monster hunter [[Elsa Bloodstone]] and briefly torture her, before [[Miles Morales]] and the rest of the team free her and join the fight. Smoke Eater responds by summoning a legion of civilian vampires to his side, who easily overpower the Blood Hunters until [[Dagger]] unleashes a purifying light that forces the Bloodcoven to retreat. Weakened and injured, Dagger is taken by Miles to the hospital, but the Bloodcoven intercept and capture Tandy for her mutate blood.
