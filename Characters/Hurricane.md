## Origin

*Huricane in his prime*

Harold Kane was an outlaw in the wild west. He was greatly wounded in a shoot out with Western hero the [[Two-Gun Kid|Two Gun]] during their first encounter. He was able to escape and was saved by an Indian medicine man who used magic to heal Kane. The medicine man also gave him "the speed of a cougar". Harold then challenged Two Gun again and this time won. Later Two Gun came back and won. Kane was defeated and headed for the hills.

Kane met up with the future once, when a band of the superheroes known as the Avengers came back in time to the Wild West era of America. Later in 1876, the old medicine man who had originally given Kane his powers requested a favor. He asked Hurricane to escort the Native maiden Little Cloud to Canada where she was to join her parents. In the travel, both became close.

## Alternate Version

### Marvel Zombie

*Hurricane as an old man *

The Hurricane of Earth-483 is an elderly drunk. He has a daughter with Little Cloud (now dead) named Swift Cloud . The two run a failing saloon in Rango, a small western town out side of Boot Hill Cemetery. Hurricane still has his quick draw and sharpshooting powers but they only work in a fight. Years ago he mistakenly shot Little Cloud, his Indian wife, during a stunt show.

During a Zombie attack of all of Hurricane's dead friends and foes, Hurricane is overwhelmed and passed his powers onto his daughter. Moments later Swift Cloud uses the power to stop the zombie hoard, with some help from [[Machine Man]] and [[Howard the Duck]]. One of her last acts to shoot the now zombie Hurricane in the head putting him to rest.

## Powers

Harry was superhumanly fast, allowing him to draw his gun at high speeds. His aim sucked though.
