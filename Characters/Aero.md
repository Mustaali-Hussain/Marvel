> An architect by day, Lei Ling is the superhero protector of Shanghai known as Aero. Using her mastery over the element of wind, Aero is an Avenger-Adjacent and member of the New Agents of Atlas.

## Origin

*Lei Ling, architect and superhero.*

Lei Ling is a young professional, working as a high-paying architect at the Sacred Tree Design Studio in the city of Shanghai. The origins of her powers are a mystery even to her, but one day Ling realized she could fly, and decided to protect the city as the superhero Aero. Her powers, status as a superhero and skills as an architect allow Ling the privilege of truly being one with the city.

## Creation

Aero was created for the Chinese market by the writer and artist team of Zhou Jiajun and Xu Ming and first appeared in the Chinese Marvel comic Aero (Chinese version) issue 1 (2018). Her first American debut was in War of the Realms: New Agents of Atlas issue 1 (2019). The original Chinese Aero comic would later be published or "reprinted" in the American Aero series.

## Character Evolution

*A mentor to younger Asian heroes*

Lei Ling is a polite and hard-working architect, while as Aero she exhibits a more confident demeanour. Although the hero Aero is considered to be somewhat of a celebrity in Shanghai, Ling is very humble and does not let the fame affect her personality. She has also displayed reverence towards American superheroes, being extremely excited to meet Tony Stark and knowing about Black Knight's past from reading about him online.

While she can feel like a newcomer amongst Western heroes, Ling has taken younger Asian heroes under her wing and helped them embrace their powers and destiny with a bit of tough love, such as with Wave and Lin Lie.

At times there is an air of cockiness to Ling's mindset. She is professionally very successful, meaning she has no financial troubles and has a personality of assuredness that reflects that.

## Major Story Arcs

### Protector of Shanghai

While balancing her work, superhero persona and social life is taxing, Ling is enjoying her life and being part of her city. While she knows that other superheroes struggle leading a double life, with their loved ones being targetted as a result, Lei insists that won't happen to her and the man she has been seeing recently, Zou Yu.

At an indeterminate time in the present, Aero is fighting for her life against several possessed skyscrapers that she helped to design. She cannot help but sense a magical disturbance in the air, which is confirmed when a vortex opens in the sky above and an old city begins descending over Shanghai. The city, however, is a living biorganic behemoth, half city half monster.

### The New Agents of Atlas

*Aero and Luna Snow form an ice tornado*

During her patrol of the Shanghai harbour, Aero detects abnormalities in the air currents at sea and flies out thousands of miles across the Pacific Ocean to discover Filipina hero Wave, who has sensed an equal disturbance in the water flow. Beneath them a volcano land mass explodes out of the ocean and the two are attacked by Fire Demons of Muspelheim, before being rescued by Tutu Pele. The trio travel to Shanghai to fight back Sindr's forces and, alongside fellow Shanghainese hero Sword Master, they beat back the invasion before being teleported to Seoul by the Agents of Atlas. There, Pele reveals that Sindr is planning to melt the polar ice caps, which would raise the Earth's temperature to a level that she could reside there as Queen. Aero agrees to go to the Arctic alongside Wave and Luna Snow to reverse the damage done by forming a massive tornado of ice that rapidly cools the climate. Though Sindr sends a demon of Muspelheim to prevent the heroines' progress, Aero successfully fends off the creature and the trio (now dubbed the Elementals) succeed in restoring the ice levels and cooling the Earth's temperature. Back in China north of Beijing, Aero is teleported with her allies to the Great Wall of China and defeats Sindr for good.

### Mentoring Wave

*Aero consoling the new hero Wave*

Following the War of the Realms, Aero notices Wave's absence at a ceremony celebrating the heroes of Asia. She tracks Pearl down to a beach Northeast of Manila and enquires why she was not present for the award of valorous defence. Pearl reveals that she has been kicked out of Triumph Division for abandoning her post during the conflict which is confirmed by Red Feather, who interrupts their talk to reclaim the swords and armor Triumph Division supplied Wave with. Pearl resists Red Feather's orders and Aero blocks his attacks, lifting Wave out of the battle with air currents. Pearl is enraged at Ling ending the fight, but Ling wisely reminds her that Red Feather is not the enemy and fighting him will further incriminate her.

Aero infiltrates a high security prison with Wave to clear her name with Triumph Division, interrogating the doctor who ran the AlonTech experiments Pearl participated in. Fleeing the prison by air, the two are caught in a trap by Sea Hunter, a former employee of AlonTech who went rogue after learning the company wanted to colonize the mythical underwater race of sirenas living in the Sulu Sea. Pearl and Ling are welcomed into the home of Sea Hunter and the sirenas' leader, Carina, and offer to help them defend their community from AlonTech's assault. While Wave is eager to assist Aero is suitably cautious of both sides, reminding Pearl that they have only heard a biased account of history. Nonetheless Aero decides to aid the sirenas by forming an underwater whirlwind tunnel that carries them to safety, before they are all ambushed by AlonTech's boss: Reyna Sirena, Queen of the mermaids. Aero is imprisoned in a cell that electrocutes her when she uses her powers, however she manipulates the currents enough to break the sensors and rescue Pearl, who is being prepped for dissection by the sirenas. Together Aero and Wave destroy the underwater lab. Back in Manila at Triumph Hall, Red Feather commends the two heroines for uncovering the secrets of Alontech and formally invites Wave to rejoin Triumph Division. Pearl thanks Ling for her trust and friendship, and Ling is invited to Mactan Island to celebrate with Wave's community.

### Incoming

Aero and Wave are patrolling the shores of Pan when Jimmy Woo holo-projects in front of them, asking the whereabouts of Amadeus Cho. Aero expresses her dissatisfaction with Woo's abandoning of the team, insisting that herself and the other Agents have been dealing with crisis after crisis for weeks without any sign of their "leader." Regardless, Aero acquiesces and sends out wind currents to track Amadeus.

### One World Under Doom

*Joining Doom's Tiger Division*

After Victor Von Doom announces a state of Pax Victoris, the Korean superhero team Tiger Division are ordered to comply with Doom's Pan-Asian expansion protocol. As part of this Aero, Wave and Karma are recruited as new members of the team. With the regional alliance comes rebranding, and Tiger Division are renamed Doom's Division. The team are ordered to aprehend the renegade Sunfire, who defies Doom's world order. The team follow Director Han's intel to Hokkaido, however Wave and Karma attack the team revealing themselves to be allies of Sunfire. Aero manages to protect both herself, Gun-R II and The General from Wave's assault.

Aero confronts her longtime ally Wave, angry that Pearl didn't feel it pertinent to confide her doubts about Doom's Division in Ling. She asks Pearl to free Taegukgi from her waterspout, but Wave refuses, and the two clash. Sunfire escalates the situation and puts human soldiers at risk, which causes Aero to distill his fires and put an end to the conflict. Taegukgi and Sunfire agree to a ceasefire, however White Fox intervenes and arrests the entire team on behalf of Doom. The team are captured, but with the help of Luna Snow they escape prison and confront White Fox, who reveals she had been working with Sunfire the entire time to feed him intel. Now that her cover is blown, Director Han suggests everybody go into hiding and operate outside the law until Doom falls.

### Invasion of the Seven Capital Cities of Heaven

*Aero the Inhuman*

In the time since Emperor Doom's rule of Earth, Aero learns that her powers' long-shrouded origin is that of Terrigenesis. Ling is training under the tutelage of the Inhuman Karnak, when Iron Fist's brother Lin Feng invades Earth. Reunited with her old Agents of Atlas teammate Lin Lie, Aero joins forces with him, Elektra, and White Tiger to fly up to the portal connecting K'un-Lun to New York City, and close it before Lin Feng finds the last orb of Chiyou. The team makes it through the portal, however Lin Feng ambushes them, rips the shards of the Sword of Fu Xi from Lin Lie's arm to forge the new Sword of Chiyou, and chops Lie's right arm off.

Overwhelmed by enemy forces, Ling creates her air avatars to defend Elektra and White Tiger until the group could defeat Neon Dragon and Tiger's Faithful Daughter, thereby closing the portal to Earth and ending the mind control of the citizens of K'un-Lun. In a last desperate attempt at victory, Lin Feng retrieves the last Orb of Chiyou from the Darkforce Dimension, once more uniting all three within the Sword of Chiyou. Aero fires a concentrated wind bullet to destroy the last orb, but Tiger's Faithful Daughter moves to protect her god and Aero accidentally kills her. As Feng transforms into the physical avatar of Chiyou, Lin Lie channels the chi of every citizen of K'un-Lun to send everyone back to safety on Earth, including Aero. Following Chiyou's temporary defeat, Aero resumes her training with Karnak.

## Powers and Abilities

The source of Aero's powers were initially a mystery tied to her solo series which was never answered, it was eventually revealed that Ling was in fact an Inhuman. Though this has never been shown on panel, it is assumed Ling underwent Terrigenesis, possibly during the Infinity event.

**Air Constructs: **Aero is capable of generating high-powered bursts of wind strong enough to smash through concrete, as well as concentrating these currents of air to deliver as punches. Aero's most distinctive use of power is in the ability to generate solid air constructs, which take on various shapes and forms. Her preferred use of this is in the form of swords sharp enough to cut through stone, and these air blades are her most-oft wielded weapon. As a defensive power, she has generated forces of wind that can act as physical barriers against most forms of damage. She can manipulate air currents to determine the path of objects both as a means of tracking and as a way to move people out of the object's trajectory.

**Heightened Senses: **Ling can use air currents to sense the presence of certain individuals, such as casing a building for civilians who require help, and the wind can carry voices to her ears. She has even displayed the ability to sense/hear a character's inner monologue, as she did with the Black Knight.

**Air Avatars: **Once she honed the ability to generate constructs, Ling was even able to create clones of herself made out of solid air. These "Air Avatars" are extensions of Ling herself, and she is able to control each one in battle or rescue scenarios to enact her will. Unfortunately they require constant concentration to maintain, meaning they dissipate if Aero's concentration is broken in battle. She can even create air avatars of other people, seen when she creates multiple version of Elektra to fight in battle.

**Flight: **Aero can also fly at high speeds on carry others on her wind currents. She is also able of using wind "springboards," solid air discs that can launch her at great speeds.

### Video Games

### Marvel Future Fight

*Aero in Marvel Future Fight*

Aero was added to the mobile game Future Fight in October 2019 alongside fellow Shanghainese hero Sword Master. Aero is a Speed type character that offers speed boosts to her companions. Her bio reads:

Lei Ling, a prominent Chinese architect, defends Shanghai with her the powers of aerokinesis. When not constructing skyscrapers, she controls the air, flying through the sky by creating powerful wind gusts. As a new agent of Agent of Atlas, Aero works to protect the world from any threat to Shanghai and beyond.

### Marvel Snap

Aero was added to the mobile card game Marvel Snap at launch with the ability to move every enemy card played to her location. Aero was considered toward the top of Snap's meta, consistently ranking at the top of tier lists and being used at a competitive level.
