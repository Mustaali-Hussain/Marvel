> ###### Mrs. Camacho
> **First appearance** Deadpool, Power Man and Iron Fist #13

> [!abstract] In brief
> Widow and owner of Camacho's Bodega.

## Origin

Mrs. Camacho and her husband ran Camacho's Bodega until he is killed being shook down by the White Man's men for protection money.

## Creation

Written by Gerry Duggan and Brian Posehn and drawn by Scott Koblish.

## Major Story Arcs

When the cops do nothing about her husband's death, Mrs. Camacho turns to the Heroes for Hire to fix her problems with the White Man. [[Luke Cage]] and [[Iron Fist]] hear her out but are interrupted by the arrival of [[Deadpool]]. Despite the confusion, Mrs. Camacho is at least happy that something will be done to protect her and her very attractive daughter [[Carmelita Camacho|Carmelita]] from the White Man. The White Man is dealt with and Mrs. Camacho and her daughter presumably continue to run the bodega.
