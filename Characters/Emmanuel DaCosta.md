## Brief History

He was the White Rook of Hellfire Club, during Sebastian Shaw's first Inner Circle.

DaCosta died when [[Gideon]]'s accomplice, Eve, drugged him during his afternoon coffee.  His death was a power play by Gideon to get [[Sunspot]] aligned as heir to the DaCosta fortune, which Gideon hoped to use/abuse for his own gain.
