Origin and History

======

Grace is a young teenage girl with a knack for technology. She has had a rough childhood, and uses slang terms as a means to try and find herself.

She was located and recruited by the mysterious Night Thrasher in order to help run the New Warriors from behind the scenes.

It has been said that she was once a former child prostitute, until Thrasher intervened and beat up her boss.
