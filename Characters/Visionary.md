## Origin

Visionary gained his powers when he was caught in a flood of ionic energy that [[Wonder Man]] had inadvertently released when his powers flared out of control. He was only one of eight individuals that gained powers in this manner. All eight of these individuals would join forces and become the team known as The Crazy 8.

## Powers

Visionary has the powers of Super Vision. Though the full scope of his powers were never explored he has shown a number of ocular-based super powers. He is able to see great distances as if they were in front of him. He is able to also distinguish between reality and illusions and has 3-D vision that can even allow him to see through solid surfaces, like concrete.
