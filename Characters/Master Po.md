> ###### Master Po
> **First appearance** Part Two: Black Dragon Death Squad to the Edge of Panic #2
> **Appearances** 28

> [!abstract] In brief
> An enigmatic master who taught Wolverine how to fight as a warrior again.

## Origin

He describes himself as a ''humble master of 82 Chinese martial arts".

## Creation

Master Po is created by Jason Aaron and Stephen Segovia.

## Major Story Arcs

### Manifest Destiny

He is able to embarrass Wolverine in hand-to-hand combat and help Wolverine relearn the basics of fighting which he had forgotten due to his over-reliance on his healing factor. With this knowledge, Wolverine is able to defeat the Black Dragon Death Squad.

### Goodbye, Chinatown

Master Po helps Wolverine, Gorilla-Man and Fat Cobra take down the drug operation of the Jade Claw.
