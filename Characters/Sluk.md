## Origin

Not much is known about Sluk's history, other then his real name, Byron Spencer. He had joined the tv sensation known as X-Force and quickly had become one of it's most popular members.

## Creation

Sluk was created by Peter Milligan and Mike Allred and first appeared in X-Force #116.

## Major Story Arcs

### Exit Wounds

Sluk joined his team on a mission to North Africa where they battled an oppressive military group.

Sluk was only really useful at close range, with his creepy face things.

It was during this battle that Sluk was mortally wounded, becoming one of the first fatalities in the team. Before his death, Sluk was one of the fan-favorites of the TV watching audience. His death brought a shock to the fanbase. His character-toys sold even better after his untimely death.
