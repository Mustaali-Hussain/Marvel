## Origin

Brickbat was a mutant that was infected by the Brood when they attempted to invade Earth. A paramedic named [[Harry Palmer]]was implanted with a [[Brood Queen|Queen]] egg and started infecting mutants to create a Brood force with abilities to rival the X-Men. Numerous mutants were infected including Brickbat, [[Tension]], [[Temptress]], [[Blindside]], [[Dive-Bomber]], [[Lockup]], [[Spitball]]and [[Whiphand]].

Brickbat was killed in battle by [[Colossus]]and the rest of his infected teammates were slain by the X-Men because they resolved that the Brood's human hosts were beyond saving.

## Creation

Brickbat was created by Chris Claremont and Marc Silvestri in 1988 and first appeared in *Uncanny X-Men* #232.

## Powers & Abilities

Brickbat was a mutant with superhuman strength and durability strong enough to go up against Colossus. After becoming a Brood, he gained fangs and claws like all Brood.
