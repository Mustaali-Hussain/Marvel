## Origin

Preston Hardwick, the first Gunhawk used the alias "Red Larabee" for a while. He was the son of a brave miner called james Hardwick, who was killed by a shot in the back. Preston decided to avenge his death, and adopted the alias "Gunhawk".

He travelled across the west on his horse named Terror and earned his reputation as a deadly gunslinger.
