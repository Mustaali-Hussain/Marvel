## Origin

Hired at first by the Mys-Tech with the purpose of tracing and finding the Knights of Pendragon, once found she's brought to the Green Chapel in Avalon, the base of the Green Knight and the Pendragons.

There, she's given the power of the Pendragon by the Green Knight himself. As a Knight, Breeze gain super speed (the Pendragon spirit basically enchances the base personal abilities). As weapon, her armor is provided of a single scythe on each wrist.
