## Origin

Nathaniel Carver is a mutant who came to the Xavier School to better understand and control his powers.

## Creation

Hindsight was created by Christina Strain and Amilcar Pinna and first appeared in Generation X Volume 2 issue 1 (2017).

## Major Story Arcs

### Generation X

After only being in the institute for a few hours, Nathaniel wanted to leave already, thinking a superhero school would not fit him. It was however when he came into contact with the school's janitor Andre Mexer (whose powers had left him lobotomized) that he decided to stay, not wanting to become a slave of his own mutant powers.
