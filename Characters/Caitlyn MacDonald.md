## Brief History

Caitlyn MacDonald was the secretary of [[Frederick Hudson]] and the mother of his son, Frederick Hudson II, who she named after her father. She had a secret relationship with her boss who was a married man and named her son after his father to spite him. She committed suicide 2 days after giving birth.
