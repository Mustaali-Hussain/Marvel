> ###### Tricephalous
> **First appearance** The Fantastic Four! #1
> **Appearances** 51

> [!abstract] In brief
> The Tricephalous was genetically engineered by Deviant scientists on Monster Island, where it was intended to serve as part of Warlord Kro's monstrous invasion forces on the surface world.

This monster appears in the legendary Fantastic Four #1 (1961). It goes after the Susan Storm (then the Invisible Girl), but Mr. Fantastic tells Sue to turn invisible. He then uses his long arm to snare the beast and throw it into the ocean.
