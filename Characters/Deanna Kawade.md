## Origin

Deanna is an American woman who is married to Minouru Kawade. Together they have a child named Kei.

## Creation

Deanna Kawade was created by Cullen Bunn and Greg Land and first appeared in Monsters Unleashed! Vol. 1 issue 2 (2017). Her voice was however already heard in issue 1 of Monsters Unleashed!.
