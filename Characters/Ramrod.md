## Origin

Ramrod was a member of the group known as The Nasty Boys whose first and only mission was against the government sponsored team X-Factor. 

## Creation

Ramrod was created by Peter David and Larry Stroman in 1992 and first appeared in X-Factor # 75.

## Major Story Arcs

### Versus X-Factor

During their battle against X-Factor, Ramrod used his powers to great effect against the team but he didn't quite enjoy Mr. Sinister's agenda. He enjoyed drinking beer along with fellow teammate Ruckus. He disappeared after the Nasty Boys disassembled and hasn't been seen since. 

### M-Day

Ramrod was one of the few mutants to retain their powers after M-Day.

## Powers & Abilities

He has the power to warp, expand or contract anything made of wood or plant fibers, and normally carries a wooden staff for this purpose.
