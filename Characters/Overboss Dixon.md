> ###### Overboss Dixon
> **First appearance** Paradigm Lost #-1
> **Appearances** 18

> [!abstract] In brief
> Head of a division of Landau, Luckman, and Lake that was working on the Mithras Directive without the knowledge of his superiors.

Overboss Dixon was in charge of a section of Landau, Luckman, and Lake and responsible for the Mithras Directive. He originally employed Deadpool for the directive, but eventually started feeding him misinformation and brought in Captain America to act as the hero needed to usher in the arriving Messiah. 

Dixon grew increasingly violent over time, even slapping Zoe Culloden, who was supposed to be on his side. His encounter with the Messiah left him insane and he was sent to a mental insttution. Deadpool ended up killing the so-called Messiah.
