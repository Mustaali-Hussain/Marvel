> The fifth in the long line of Red Guardians. Little about his personal life is known.

Krassno Granitsky was the fifth person to take up the mantle of Russia's symbolic hero the Red Guardian after Josef Petkus had renamed himself the Steel Guardian. 

 

After teaming up with the vigilante Maverick, Granitsky was caught and executed years later by Aleksander Lukin.
