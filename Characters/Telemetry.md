## Brief History

*Along with the Heavy Hitters, Telemetry lives in the Heavy Hitters Hotel & Casino in Las Vegas. *

Telemetry is a member of  The Heavy Hitters, the Nevada branch of the Initiative. Along with the Arizona based team, The Desert Stars she succesfully took on Zzzax at the Hoover Dam. While Gravity, Supermax, Two-Gun Kid, and Johnny Cool kept the monster occupied, Telemetry used her abilities access the internet and find information on the villain. After the headstrong Supermax attacks Zzzax before receiving orders, Telemetry used her abilities once again but this time to create an arrow out of psionic arrow. As she hurls the spear down towards the monster, Two-Gun Kid also attacks the opponent, unfortunately their attacks met eachother and not the foe. With the rest of the team having difficulties,   Komodo and Hardball  finished the job, while Hardball prepared to use his energy balls to disrupt Zzzax, Komodo distracted him in the river bed. Combining their abilities they successfully defeated the villain. Leaving Telemetry and fellow Heavy Hitter, Non Stop to tease the couple (Komodo & Hardball). 

Soon after, Komodo calls in the Heavy Hitters to help defeat the Hydra leader, Senator Woodman & Hardball. Gravity quickly uses his powers to throw the cars into the air, while Telemetry quickly took care of two Hydra Agents. While the action goes on around them, Woodman quickly injects himself with Dr. Curt Connor's serum turning him into a reptilian monster. With the rest of the team unable to defeat him they had to rely on Telemetry to defeat him. Unfortunately, Woodman had focused all his attention on her, leaving her to have to defend herself with a psionically constructed force field. As the fight ensued, Hardball quickly took to action as he used his abilities to create energy fields around Gravity, Telemetry, and Komodo. Realizing that the only way to stop the Hydra from leaving his friends and family alone, Hardball creates an energy field around Woodman and quickly shrinks it until the senator is squished. Now with his new status as Hydra leader, Hardball bids farewell before he gets into one of the Hydra jets and speeds off, leaving Telemetry and the rest of the Heavy Hitters to sit in silence. 

### Secret Invasion

When the Skrull sleeper agents were activated in Secret Invasion, Telemetry along with the Heavy Hitters helped the Skrull Kill Krew  defeat one of the Skrull's agents. Much to her chagrin, Telemetry was injured during the battle forcing Non Stop to use her super speed to take her to the E.R. 

### Fear Itself

Commander Steve Rogers informs Prodigy of the unknown meteors that have fallen from the heavens and mass panic is on the horizon. Rogers asks Prodigy to gather heroes on a volunteer basis to help keep the peace and lead a new Initiative where there is no forced conscription or registration. Prodigy agrees to become the leader of the new Initiative and most of the former Initiative members including Telemetry gather in Washington DC. Telemetry and the rest of the Heavy Hitters try to stop the Juggernaut in Las Vegas after he obtains a mystic hammer and is transformed into one of the Worthy as Kuurth: Breaker of Stone. Kuurth is truly unstoppable and smashes through Telemetry's psionic constructs with ease. Telemetry and the Heavy Hitters are forced to retreat when their leader, Hardball generates a powerful energy ball and appears to sacrifice himself to stop Kuurth. 

## Powers & Abilities

Telemetry is a talented young woman with the ability to create psionic based constructs. However the source of her abilities is not clear at this point, she has been shown to be very resourceful with her powers. The feats that she has performed include: Creating insect-like wings to fly, creating a keyboard and utilizing the internet, creating force fields, harnessing the power as psionic spears, and making a make-shift chair for her to hover above the Hoover Dam with. Most recently she has been shown to keep up with fellow Heavy Hitter, Non-Stop during usage of super-speed.
