## Origin

Acts as [[Gideon]]'s personal assistant, alerting about upcoming events, as well as announcing visitors to the home.

## Creation

Created alongside Gideon by writer Fabian Nicieza and artist Rob Liefeld.

## Major Story Arcs

Adam helped coordinate the death of [[Emmanuel DaCosta]] at the hands of [[Eve]]. He also supported Gideon in acquiring [[Sunspot]] as the new, hopeful External.

A few years later, when [[Cable]] comes to Gideon's lair, we discover that Adam and Eve have been killed, but have been "cyber-upgraded"; their consciousnesses have been programmed into the A.I. of robots.
