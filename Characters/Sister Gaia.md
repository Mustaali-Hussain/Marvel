> [!abstract] In brief
> Sister Gaia hails from Earth-9111 and serves as her reality's Captain Britain.

Serena Foster (also known as Sister Gaia) is her world's representative to the Captain Britain Corps. Her world is one where the mere act of picking a flower is considered an act of gross brutality.

Sister Gaia first appeared when Brian Braddock of Earth-616 was put on trial for breaching the Corps Code of Conduct. She was also present to attend the wedding of Meggan and Brian Braddock.
