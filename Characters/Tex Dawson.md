> ###### Tex Dawson
> **Real name** Tex Dawson
> **First appearance** Meet Tex Dawson! #1
> **Appearances** 94
> **Also known as** Tex Dawson, Western Kid, Gun-Slinger

> [!abstract] In brief
> One of Marvel's many Cowboy heroes during the late 1950's. Notable for his use of a dog.

Tex Dawson was the star of his own magazine in "Western Kid" in the 50s.

Later the character was revived in the 70s as "Gun-slinger". But those stories were only reprints from the 50s stories. So, he was a cowboy in the time of other famous Marvel cowboys such as Rawhide Kid and Kid Colt.

He was pals with a dog called Lightning and a horse called Whirlwind, who help him in his adventures.
