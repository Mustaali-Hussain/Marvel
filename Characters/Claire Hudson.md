## Brief History

Claire was born a few months before her parents James and Heather, as part of Alpha Flight, faced the entity known as the Collective. Both Claire's parents apparantely died during this battle and left Claire in custody to family. Luckily, both James and Heather came back from the dead, only to find out in court that they would not get the custody of Claire back. Whether Claire wil be returned to her parents or not remains to be seen.
