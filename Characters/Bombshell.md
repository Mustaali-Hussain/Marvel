> Lana Baumgartner is known as Bombshell with her mother when they rob banks. She, along with Miles Morales where transported from the Ultimate Universe into the prime Marvel Universe 616 after Secret Wars.

## Origin

Lana robs banks with her mom [[Lori Baumgartner|Lori Baumgartner;]] it seems that she does all the work with her blast power. When they were arrested last time, Lana was separated from her mother, who was imprisoned. The judges believed that her mother influenced her to rob banks, as she's still under-age.

## Creation

Bombshell was created by Brian Michael Bendis and David Lafuente and first appeared in Ultimate (Comics) Spider-Man #2 (2009). She was part of the Ultimate Universe, but was transported to the Prime Marvel Universe (616) after the events of Secret Wars.

## Major Story Arcs

### Going to School

Bombshell, under her human name Lana Baumgartner went to the same school as [[Spider-Man|Peter Parker]] for a short while, where she is recognized - but convinces Peter and his friends to keep quiet about her super-villainous exploits. For some time, Lana kept committing crimes with her mother, and occasionally fighting Spider-Man. But because of her contact with Spider-Man, she starts seeing the errors of her ways and slowly changed her attitude.

### All-New Ultimates

Years later, after Peter Parker's death, Lana becomes part of the All-New Ultimates, alongside [[Miles Morales|The New Spider-Man (Miles Morales)]], [[Kitty Pryde]], [[Black Widow (Clone)|Spider-Woman]], [[Cloak]] and [[Dagger]]. This is the first time she actually starts acting heroically, in honor of the original Spider-Man of her universe.

### Coming to Earth 616

After the events of Secret Wars in which all of reality was destroyed and reshaped once more, Lana is transported to the main Marvel universe (Universe 616). Somehow unknown that she transported universes, she attended the same school as Miles Morales (whom was also transported to this universe). She was seen teaming up with Miles Morales and Goldballs. Eventually, she also joined Miles' superhero team, the Champions.

## Powers and Abilities

Lana can shoot projectile energy beams from her hands for explosive effects or shockwaves, both of which emit loud booming sounds. By directing her blast emissions she is capable of short-ranged flight.
