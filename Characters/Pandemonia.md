> ###### Pandemonia
> **First appearance** The Fire Within #80
> **Appearances** 13

> [!abstract] In brief
> Sorceress who came to blows with X-Force and Jannifer Kale

Pandemonia is the succubus daughter of a lesser chaos lord that use to terrorize Central Europe during the Middle Ages when she would drain the life force of her servants to provide her strength and sustenance. Pandemonia would be exiled and imprisoned on Mount Sorcista in Latveria by a gypsy sorcerer for nearly six centuries. used both Skids and Locus in an attempt to enslave X-Force so that she could tap their powers.

With the combined efforts of Dani Moonstar and Jennifer Kale, she was sent to a different plane of existance.
