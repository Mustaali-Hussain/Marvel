Insane inventor and creator of [[Brynocki]] and a former member of MI-6.

Mordillo was incinerated in the solar-chute beam, and only skeleton remains of him.

## Powers & Abilities

Death-Hand possesses a cybernetic right arm, allowing for some measure of enhanced strength (at least with that arm). He is proficient in martial arts though not to the level of people like [[Shang-Chi]] or [[Domino]].
