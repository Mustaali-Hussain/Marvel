## Origin

Virtually nothing is known about the life of Amanda McGill. Whether she was a S.H.I.E.L.D. agent who transferred to H.A.M.M.E.R. or a new recruit is not revealed.

## Creation

Amanda McGill was created by Marjorie Liu and Giuseppe Camuncoli and first appeared in *Dark Wolverine* #75.

## Character Evolution

Amanda McGill was a minor background character who was really only brought to the forefront in one issue where we saw her have a one-night stand with the Dark Avengers' Daken. Her name wasn't revealed until 2 issues later when Norman Osborn mentioned her. Agent McGill subsequently appeared in the background of certain scenes among various other H.A.M.M.E.R. agents but never had any lines.

## Major Story Arcs

### The Prince

One day in Avengers Tower, Daken was walking through the corridors when he walked by Agent McGill. Liking what he saw, he decided to enduce an attraction to him within her using his control over pheromones. As the day continued on, McGill's craving for Daken was undeniable. The two's eyes meeting amidst long and dull meetings held by their mutual boss Norman Osborn. When Daken provoked his team-mates into a fight, McGill found this irresistible. However, when Daken was shot in the shoulder with an arrow courtesy of "Hawkeye", McGill rushed to Daken's side to provide aid. The two left the room and when alone, McGill could no longer contain her burning passion for Daken and the two almost immediately slept together.

That night, after the two had spent some time together, Daken told her to get out. McGill's final words were how cool it was to sleep with Wolverine.

### Siege

Agent McGill was one of the many H.A.M.M.E.R. agents who were sent into battle against the Asgardian Gods.

## Powers and Abilities

Amanda McGill has no superhuman powers but is trained in armed and unarmed combat courtesy of her H.A.M.M.E.R. training. Her main weakness is she is prone to falling hard for Daken.
