> ###### Mermaid
> **First appearance** #52
> **Appearances** 28

> [!abstract] In brief
> She is a member of the flagship team of X-Men in her reality, Earth-9997 (Earth-X). Though she can fly through and breathe air, she is also able to breathe and swim through water. She is an ichthysanthrope (Were-Fish) or a fish/human hybrid.

Mermaid is a member or Mr. S's X-Men. Mermaid is a former member of Daredevil's travelling thrill show (a circus). After seeking out Mr. S and joining the X-Men, Mermaid aided the worlds heroes in beating the Skrull and his armies. Years later Mermaid was among the X-Men who escorted the Wakanda civilization to their new home in the Savage Land. Mermaid then helped defend the new New York Torch from the church of Immortus.

Mermaid has a fish like tail instead of legs, making her unable to walk. However she can fly, "swimming" in the air.
