## Brief History

Domini** **was born in the 1950's and sent to a convent by her father who never loved her. She desperately wanted a man who would love her. She soon met **[[Anton Lupeski]]**. He was the leader of a satanic cult that preformed many rituals that she opposed. She remained with them because she couldn't leave. Lupeski had perforned a ritual to try to summon a demon and was about to sacrifice Domini, but instead **Dracula** interfered and posed as a demon. Dracula told Anton to release her not wanting to suffer the "demon's" wrath. Anton did and offered Domini to him. Dracula accepted and had a black wedding. The cult performed a spell on Domini so Dracula could impregnate her. She gave birth to **[[Janus]]**.
