> ###### Rose Wu
> **First appearance** I am Lady Mandarin #257
> **Appearances** 36
> **Also known as** Rose Carling (maiden name)

Rose Carling-Wu was a long-time operative of Landau, Luckman & Lake and friend of Wolverine. She was a hybrid, with her father being an Earth human and her mother a humanoid from the Dimension of Shadows. Rose had minor shape-shifting powers. She was killed in her old age by orders of General Nguyen Ngoc Coy and Prince Baran. She was survived by her granddaughter Ruth.
