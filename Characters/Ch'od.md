> Ch'od is a big reptilian alien and a member of the Starjammers.

## Origin

 

Ch'od is one of the founding members of the Starjammers. Despite his appearance he is usually friendly. He was once a slave of the Shi'ar Emperor [[D'Ken|D'ken]] until [[Corsair]] led a rebellion. Ch'od has been with the Starjammers as they helped [[Professor X]] gain a new body and while they fought the Brood. The Starjammers have the trust of the X-men and the Avengers.  

## Creation

Ch'od was created by Chris Claremont and Dave Cockrum in 1977 and first appeared in X-Men # 104. 

 

## Powers and Abilities 

 

Ch'od  has superhuman strength and his thick, scaly hide provides him with enhanced durability. Ch'od  has claws. Additionally, his saurid brain provides him with a degree of resistance to telepathic attacks. Ch'od is an experienced starship pilot and mechanic, hand-to-hand combatant, marksman and swordsman. He wields a variety of extraterrestrial weapons including energy rifles, swords and battle-axes.
