> [!abstract] In brief
> Universal Inhuman member of the Light Brigade

Prax Ord Metallic Titan is a Alpha Centaurian of the Universal Inhumans. He was one of six warriors chosen for the Light Brigade. A group that was meant to sacrifice themselves by going through Eldrac for "a battle they cannot win". Imprisoned by [[Annihilus]] in the Negative Zone he was forced to fight in gladiatorial matches to the death. He would then be resurrected and forced to repeat the cycle over and over for the Negative Zone inhabitants enjoyment. He along with the rest of the Light Brigade were led by the [[Human Torch|Johnny Storm]] to not only escape imprisonment but defeat Annihilus and gain control of the Annihilus Wave by taking the Cosmic Control Rod.
