> ###### Armageddon
> **First appearance** #1
> **Appearances** 12

> [!abstract] In brief
> Armageddon is the son of Apocalypse in Earth-2182 - Nocturne's home reality.
He is a member of X-Men in his reality.

Armageddon was created using the combined genetic code of Apocalypse and Jean Grey.

He would however turn against his father and join the X-Men, with the resulting battle leading to Apocalypse's death.

Armageddon also aided the X-Men in combating Cyclops Brotherhood. He knocked himself unconscious when he ruptured Radioactive-Man's containment suit and blew up, resulting in Radioactive-Man's death. 

Nocturne would then take possession of Armageddon's unconscious body to use his telekinesis and force Wolverine to stab Cyclops in the chest.
