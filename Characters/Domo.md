## Origin

Domo was an Eternal and the administrator of affairs on Olympia. He answered directly to the elder Eternal Zuras and dispensed Zuras' commands to the other Eternals.

## Creation 

Domo is a Marvel comics published character written and created by Jack Kirby. The character first appears in the first Eternals series, specifically The Eternals #1 - The Day of the Gods.
