## Brief History

A Morlock who became part of the Gene Nation but found himself unable to collaborate with the team due to his physical body being too weak. He later joined [[Havok|Havok’s]] Brotherhood where he planned to finally use his powers, however upon seeing several mutants being killed he ran to the Morlock tunnels to hide himself, until the [[Dark Beast]] found and killed him.

## Powers & Abilities

Ever's body is composed primarily of compacted gray matter, making him physically weak but in return giving him vast psionic abilities; such as telepathy, telekinesis, astral projection, mind control, and mental scanning of areas.
