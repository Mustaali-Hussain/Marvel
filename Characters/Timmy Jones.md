Timmy is son of police officer & single-parent Charlotte Jones.  When Archangel dated Charlotte, Timmy grew fond of the blue-skinned mutant.  However, because of his mother's association with the X-Men, Timmy was targeted by kidnappers.

### Operation: Zero Tolerance

* Kidnapped*

 Due to his mother´s connections with the X-Men, he was kidnapped by Bastion and put under Rose Gilberti (Bastion´s foster mother) personal care. An X-Men team (Cecilia Reyes, Marrow and Sabra) leaded by Iceman was entrusted by Charlotte to rescue Timothy from Bastion. At the end Timothy was rescued and he returned with her mother.
