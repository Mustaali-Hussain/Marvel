## History

Maxwell Max Taylor was a Guardsmen for The Vault. Maxwell joined The Jury to target and kill Venom, after that, he and the Jury members went after Spider-Man. More recently, the Jury has battled against the new Thunderbolts.

Maxwell Taylor is Orwell Taylor's second son.

## Creation

Screech was created by David Michelinie and Mark Bagley in 1993 and first appeared in * Venom: Lethal Protector *#2.

## Powers & Abilities

Screech wears an armored suit based on the design of the Guardsman armor that provides him with enhanced strength, flight, and various weapons. Screech is equipped with a sonic gun that emits powerful sound-waves and small hovering discs that attach to his feet to provide flight.
