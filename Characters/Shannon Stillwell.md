> Shannon Stillwell is the assistant of Regent. She is also Madame Monstrosity's daughter as well sister to both Farley and Harlan Stilwell.

## Powers & Abilities

Shannon is the sister to Harlan Stillwell and [[Dr. Stillwell]], all three are the children of [[Madame Monstrosity]] (Melodia Stillwell) and Jeremy Stillwell. Unlike her brothers, she chose not to follow in her mother's footsteps of human-animal hybrids/experiments. Her work seems to largely involve genetic augmentation for the purpose of gaining superpowers.

## Major Story Arcs

### The Web-Less Wonder

**For more information see: Spider-Boy: The Web-Less Wonder**

At some point Shannon takes a job working for the insanely rich influencer Killionaire. He hires her and [[Taskmaster]] to steal the [[Super-Adaptoid]] and make the want-a-be villain a super armor that replicates the powers of the Avengers. 

Taskmaster fails to bring the whole Super-Apaptoid, due to the interferance of [[Captain America]] and [[Spider-Boy (Briggs)|Spider-Boy]] and is only able to get the hand. Fortunately, that is enough for Shannon to make The Toy Soldier. 

Killionaire takes his toy and causes chaos on the streets of New York City and Shannon is assumingly paid for her work.
