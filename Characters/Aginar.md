## **Origin**

Aginar was an Eternal of the Polar Mountains. He was a military leader and he posed as the assistant to [[Valkin|Valkin/General Vulcanin]]. He later left Earth as part of the [[Uni-Mind]].

## Creation

Aginar was created by Jack Kirby to be used in Marvel Comics, and he first appeared in Eternals #11 which was released in May 1977.

## **Powers and Abilities**

**Passive:** Superhuman Strength, Speed, Stamina, Durability, Agility, Reflexes, and a Healing Factor.

**Energy Manipulation:** Aginar has the ability to manipulate cosmic energy, granting him virtual invulnerability. Aginar also has the ability to project cosmic energy from eyes or hands in the form of heat, light, or concussive force.

**Telepathy:** Low-level psychic abilities, enabling him to cast illusions disguising his appearance and to read the mind of anyone with lesser psychic ability.

**Telekinesis:** Aginar has the ability to levitate himself and thereby fly at superhuman speed; psionic ability to rearrange the molecular structure of objects.

**Immortality:** Like all Eternals, Aginar is functionally immortal.
