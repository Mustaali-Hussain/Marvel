## Brief History

It has been implied, though never stated directly for copyright reasons, that Clive Reston is a descendant of both James Bond and Sherlock Holmes.He is a spy who worked for MI-6 and Freelance Restorations. He now leads MI-6 with his good friend Black Jack Tarr. It should also be noted that Clive is an alcoholic, though is currently sober.
