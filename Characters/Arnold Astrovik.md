> ###### Arnold Astrovik
> **First appearance** Eelar Moves In Mysterious Ways! #5
> **Appearances** 25

> [!abstract] In brief
> Father of Justice.

Arnold Astrovik was the abusive father of [[Justice]] and husband to [[Norma Astrovik]]. Arnold hated his son because he was a mutant. Arnold was killed after attacking his son who used his powers in self-defense. Some time later when Justice was sent back in time by the Sphinx he discovered Arnold's father Jerry used to beat a teenage Arnold because Arnold was gay.
