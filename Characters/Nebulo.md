> ###### Nebulo
> **First appearance** A Refuge Divided! #1
> **Appearances** 14
> **Also known as** Shadow Uncast

> [!abstract] In brief
> An Inhuman who is invisible for all but his shadow.

Nebulo is an invisible Inhuman and a supporter of Maximus. The only visual sign of his presence is his shadow on nearby objects.

He was accused of high treason.

## Creation

Nebulo was created by Gary Friedrich and Marie Severin in 1968 and first appeared in The Incredible Hulk Annual # 1.
