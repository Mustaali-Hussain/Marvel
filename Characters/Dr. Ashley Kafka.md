> Ravencroft Sanitarium for the Criminally Insane psychiatrist. She has been the Doctor to Carnage, Shriek, Carrion, Vermin, Chameleon, Massacre and Venom to name a few. Later she becomes the villain called Goblin Queen.

## Origin

Studied psychology at Empire State partially inspired to do so, after negative childhood events, Ashley went on to become a professional psychologist. During her time as a psychologist she has helped and aided such persons of note, as Spiderman and Vermin. Never willing to give up on patients, Kafka has tried but as so far been unsuccessful to rehabilitate patients such as Carnage and Carrion. She was killed by Massacre who took one of her eyes to get past a retinal scanner.

## Creation

Ashley Kafka was created by JM DeMatteis and Sal Buscema in 1991 and first appeared in The Spectacular Spider-Man # 178.

## Powers and Abilities

Ashley Kafka possesses no superhuman abilities or powers. She is intelligent, and is a psychologist.
