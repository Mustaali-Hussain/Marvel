> Miguel is the mage of the Spider Society

*"Dark Lord of the infinite Earth... Weaver of all life, lend me your strength. Listen to me... Listen carefully... I need something from you. The most precious thing in your possession. Because life is precious, and it demands the same in token"* -- Miguel (Spider Society Mague).

His mission was search and protect new Spider Society initiates. For this he uses magical barriers to slow their enemies down. He need all the advantages he can get.

He's guardian of the Spider Society initiate. He will protect Anya Corazon as a new initiate, giving her guidance. He saved her life because she accepted fight for Spider Society as their chosen.

In his card it says he works for Web Corps.
