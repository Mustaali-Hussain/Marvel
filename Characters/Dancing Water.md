> A member of the Red Skull's S-Men. She claims to be the daughter of Avalanche.

## Creation

Dancing Water was created by writer Rick Remender and artist John Cassaday. She first appeared in the story "New Union" in October 2012.

## Major Story Arcs

### Uncanny Avengers

Dancing Water and the other S-Men attacked [[Scarlet Witch]] and [[Rogue]] with a surprise attack at [[Professor X]]'s grave, capturing the two and stealing Professor X's corpse for [[Red Skull]] in the process. Ironically it was access to her water based powers that allowed Rogue to escape from her bonds and find Scarlet Witch.
