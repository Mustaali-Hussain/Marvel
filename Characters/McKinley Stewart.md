> ###### McKinley Stewart
> **Real name** McKinley Stewart
> **First appearance** Afraid of the Dark #1

> **Also known as** McKinley Stewart

> [!abstract] In brief
> Supporting character in Elektra Vol. 1.

Former heavyweight boxing contender, after retiring from the ring he returned to New York City where he opened a dojo with his friend and colleague [[King Lau]]. He and King provided moral support to [[Elektra]] as well as helping with her training. He eventually became romantically involved with Elektra but his close relation with her made him a target of the Hand. When last seen he was in a coma due to injuries sustained battling the Hand.
