## Origin

Omega Black was built from the remains of the original [[Omega Red]] by an organization called the White Sky. She, along with her "siblings", was implanted with false memories that result in a strong grudge against X-Force who, as they believe, was hired by some pharmaceutical company to kill their father who discovered a cure for autoimmune diseases.

## Creation

Omega Black was created by writer Rick Remender for the current Uncanny X-Force story arc, Final Execution. She made her first appearance in Uncanny X-Force #25.

## Major Story Arcs

### Final Execution

Upon receiving a distress call from [[Deadpool]], [[Wolverine]] and Earth-295 [[Nightcrawler]] embark on a rescue mission. They track him down to a cloud floating above the Pacific Ocean. Seeing no better solution, they decide to teleport inside of it, only to find themselves ambushed by the assassins of the White Sky and the Omegas. The latter easily overpower X-Force's depleted ranks, claiming they will suffer for their parents' death.

## Powers and abilities

Omega Black's chest is implanted with five retractable tendrils through which she can inject lethal carcinogens into select adversaries.
