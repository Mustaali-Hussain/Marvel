> Hobgoblin of 2211 is the daughter of Spider-Man 2211.

Robin Bourne Felt being neglected by her father, [[Spider-Man 2211]], because of his career of being a superhero, Robin turned to being a villain by destroying any alternate universe by overlapping it with other universes. She was arrested by his father and was sent into a jail of virtual reality. When her boyfriend tried to break her out by introducing a virus in the virtual reality, it caused her mind to go completely insane. She tried to kill her father by throwing him a "retcon bomb" but it hit her boyfriend instead, erasing him from existence. She entered the mainstream universe and fought the original [[Spider-Man]], she tried to throw a retcon bomb at him, but Spider-Man threw it back to her and she was erased from existence.
