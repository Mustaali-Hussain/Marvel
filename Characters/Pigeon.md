## Brief History

Pigeon was a harmless pickpocket that was taken down by Spider Man and sent to prison. Pigeon would meet three other prisoners named Sugar Face, Gripes and Honcho. All four men befriended the Vulture during their stay in prison. Honcho was an engineer that attained the information on the Vulture's flying suit and created their own vulture wings after all four men were released early for being model prisoners. All four men were given a suit and called themselves the Vulturions. They performed a successful bank robbery with their wings but Honcho had to make some repairs and adjustments a few days later. Honcho wanted Pigeon and the rest of the Vulturions to test out their suits one day and ordered them to not engage Spider Man if they see him because it was just a scouting expedition. However, Gripes saw Spider Man and decided to take him on before his back up arrived. Spider Man would eventually engage all four Vulturions but Pigeon and the rest of the gang were easily defeated.    

## Creation

Pigeon was created by Louise Simonson and Greg Larocque in 1985 and first appeared in Web of Spider-Man # 1.
