> ###### Debbie Harris
> **Real name** Deborah Harris
> **First appearance** While the City Sleeps! #10
> **Appearances** 79
> **Also known as** Deborah Harris, Deborah Nelson, Deborah Harris Nelson

> [!abstract] In brief
> A socilaite and former wife of Foggy Nelson.

Deborah Harris was introduced in "Daredevil" vol. 1 #10 (October, 1965) as a socialite with criminal connections and a love interest to Foggy Nelson. She was arrested in #11 (November, 1965) and then imprisoned. She was released in #36 (January, 1968), deciding to reform and date Foggy. Their relationship had its ups and downs until they got married in #166 (September, 1980). Their marriage became increasingly troubled and ended in a divorce with #222 (September, 1985).
