> A mutant Skrull who is a member of Cadre K. He has the ability to absorb, store, and redirect energy.

## Creation

Spunje was created by Kurt Busiek and Jerry Ordway in 2000 and first appeared in Maximum Security Dangerous Planet # 1.

## Powers & Abilities

Spunje has the ability to harmlessly absorb various forms of energy directed at him. He can then store this energy to augment his physical abilities, or redirect it and fire it off as a blast with twice the original force/power.

He also possesses the shape-shifting abilities inherent to all Skrulls.
