## Origin

Esteban,"Stevie" Beach gained his powers and became Stat after he was caught in a flood of ionic energy that [[Wonder Man]] had inadvertently released when his powers flared out of control. He was only one of eight individuals that gained powers in this manner, including his sister, [[Snap|Ginger Beach (Snap)]]. All eight of these individuals would join forces and become the team known as the Crazy 8.

## Powers

Stat has the power to manipulate portions of the electromagnetic spectrum allowing him to create up to six duplicates of himself and anything he is currently holding.

Limitation:

He has only demonstrated the ability to make up to six dupes of himself.
