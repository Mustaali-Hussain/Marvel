## Red Feather

Red Feather was the leader of the Triumph Division. He was among those members that were recognized during a speech in Manila. [[Iron Man]] then said that the Philippines is in good hands. Red Feather and his teammates were killed by [[Ezekiel Stane]]'s suicide bombers that were disguised as monks at that speech. 

## Creation

Red Feather was created by Matt Fraction and Salvador Larroca in 2008 and first appeared in Invincible Iron Man # 2.
