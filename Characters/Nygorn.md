> ###### Nygorn
> **First appearance** When Strike The Monitors! #3
> **Appearances** 13
> **Also known as** The Last Monitor  ,Nygom

> [!abstract] In brief
> Semi-humanoid alien with psionic abilities.

Nygorn is a member of the extra-dimensional race known as Marvanites. He was a member of the Starblasters. Nygorn claimed to have slain prime U`lula`n and outmaneuvered the Axi-Tun aggregate. When he returned home he was captured by the [[Stranger]] and taken as one of the Stranger`s Prisoners. There he had found out that he was the last monitor due to all were vivisected by the Stranger.When the [[Overmind]] assaulted the Stranger, Nygorn was released and attacked not only the Stranger but many others there. He was enraged not only being the last of his kind but also being caged next to so many inferior creatures. [[Hyperion]] and [[Krimonn|Dr. Spectrum]] brought him down .
