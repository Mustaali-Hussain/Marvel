## Brief History 

* Josey's human form*

 

Paramedic Josey Thomas became a Brood Queen after being infected with a Brood egg by her partner Harry Palmer. She later implanted Hannah Connover with a Brood Queen egg and used her congregations to recruit more Brood. Oddly enough, Josey Thomas stated she should have been the Queen since Queen's are the only one's who are capable of implanting eggs into others. Josey was killed by the Firstborn as they believed as Hannah Conover's creator she must have been "contaminated" like her.
