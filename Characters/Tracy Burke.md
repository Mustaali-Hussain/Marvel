> [!abstract] In brief
> Editor-in-chief of Woman Magazine and friend of Carol Danvers.

Theresa 'Tracy' Burke was a famous photographer and journalist but problems dealing with alcohol led to her leaving that career. She was later brought out of retirement by [[Carol Danvers]] to become co-editor of Woman Magazine. Years later, now working at Tech Support magazine, Tracy encountered Carol again and noted she herself had developed a problem with alcohol and drinking. She confronted her over this issue, but was unsuccessful and tried to have [[Iron Man|Tony Stark]] talk to Carol about it.
