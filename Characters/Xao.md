> ###### Xao
> **First appearance** The Last Iron Fist Story, Part 1 #1
> **Appearances** 21

> [!abstract] In brief
> Conspired with Hydra to break the barrier to K'un-Lun. The Immortal Weapons traced Xao's family lineage back to The Lightning Lords of Nepal. Dog Brother #1 and Orson Randall tangled with them in the past.

Xao is part of the Xao Family Dynasty. His lineage was traced back to The Lightning Lords of Nepal.  

 

The Lightning Lords of Nepal were three Xao brothers who could combine their chi to create a kind of Beast made of lightning. Orson killed their father and stole one of the brothers horses. 

 

The three brothers could combine their powers to form an electric being known as the Super Lightning Lord. The being is also known as the "Electric Demon of Manaslu".

 

 It is the family's destiny to destroy K'un-Lun.
