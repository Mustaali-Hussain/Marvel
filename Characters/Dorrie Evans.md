## Brief History

Doris "Dorrie" Evans was once a reluctant girlfriend of the Human Torch, she would constantly take second place to Johnny's adventures with the Fantastic Four. Three months after their break-up, Evans met a man identified only as Sam, whom she eventually married. She had a dark-haired boy and a daughter who looked more like her. She appeared to Johnny Storm in Fantastic Four #526 - Dream Fever, Part 2 in a dream alongside other former romantic interests of the Torch.
