## San Francisco's X-Men

When the X-Men arrived in San Francisco, Mayor Sinclair welcomed [[Emma Frost|Emma]], [[Cyclops|Scott]], [[Wolverine|Logan]], [[Storm|Ororo]], [[Angel]], and [[Beast|Hank]]. She toured them in their headquarters that was being built during that time. She also revealed that San Francisco has nothing against mutants, and she had some sort of romantic relationship with Angel in the past.
