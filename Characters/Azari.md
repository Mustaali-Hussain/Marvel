## History

Azari is the son of the [[Black Panther]] and the [[Storm]] . He was raised by Iron Man alongside his friends [[James Rogers]], Henry Pym Jr., and Torunn. Together they formed the Kid Avengers and later added Hawkeye to their team. The Kid Avengers brought an end to Ultron's tyranny together with the help of Iron Man, Vision, and the Hulk.

### Personality

Azari is calm, level headed and the most mature of the children and is closest to Pym despite (or because of) there opposite personalities. Unlike his father who always argued with Tony, Azari always does what stark says and has great respect and trust in him. Due to being connected with the panther spirit through birth, Azari often moves in a catlike way and often sits in a crouched position.

## **Powers**

*Prince Azari is born.*

Azari inherited the abilities of Heart-shaped Herb from his father [[Black Panther|The Black Panther]]. Azari also inherited the mutant powers and abilities from his mother [[Storm]].

**Peak Human Condition**: Azari bodily functions are at the maximum limit of human condition. His natural capabilities are near-superhuman.

**Peak Human Strength** : Azari is considerably stronger than the average human. His strength is at the peak of human potential.

**Peak Human Stamina: **Azari's possess a highly developed musculature, and as a result, his musculature generates less fatigue toxins than the bodies of most humans. He can exert himself for hours, before fatigue starts to set in.

**Peak Human Endurance**: Azari can endure more physical stress than the average human.

**Peak Human Durability**: Azari's skin, bone, and muscles are harder and stronger than normal humans. He can withstand tremendous impact force, without showing signs of injury or discomfort. His ability to resist and recover from physical injury or disease is also at the pinnacle of human capability.

**Peak Human Healing Factor:** Azari's natural healing capabilities are at the highest levels of human potential. His white blood cells fight off microbes, infections, diseases, disorders, wounds, etc. Additionally, their healing time would very short: cuts/wounds would be healed in minutes and broken bones healed in hours.

**Peak Human Reflexes**: Azari's reaction time is superior to that of any Olympic athlete that has ever competed. His reactions are superior to those of normal humans and are near-superhuman.

**Peak Human Agility**: Azari's agility, balance, and bodily coordination are at the pinnacle of human development. His agility is superior to any Olympic level gymnast that has ever competed.

**Superhuman Acute Senses** : Inherited from his father, Azari possess extremely accurate senses. He can see in total darkness, see over hundreds of feet away as if it is right in front of his face. He can ear with amazing clarity, distance, and even frequencies outside normal range.

**Enhanced Speed**: Azari is able to run and move at speeds superior to those of any Olympic athlete, and is capable of out-running moving vehicles. His top speed isn't known.

**Flight:** Azari can fly by charging his lower body with electric energy.

**Electrokinesis**: Azari can generate and manipulate electricity, this power he seems to have inherited from his mother [[Storm]].

- **Electricity Projection**: Azari can project electricity through his hands and body.

*Azari projects a electrical force field. *

- **Formulated Electricity Blast:** Azari can create and project blasts of electricity that take the form of a Panther, and make it seem like it's alive.
- **Force Fields**: Azari can produce highly concentrated electric force

## Abilities

**Expert Tracker & Hunter**: Azari is a master tracker and hunter.

**Combat:** Azari inherited all his father's combat skills. This makes him an expert in practically all forms of armed and unarmed combat, and a master of all martial arts including African and unknown forms.
