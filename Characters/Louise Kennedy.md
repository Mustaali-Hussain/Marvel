Louise Kennedy was a Salt Lake City cop, on the take. One night she bumped into the mysterious Kaine in a bar, following an argument between Kaine and another patron. The two dwelved into a romance, that was doomed to fail. After Kaine found himself betrayed by Louise, he lashed out by scarring her with the "Mark of Kaine" and then killing her.
