> Ultimate Universe Abomination.

Abomination is a Chinese scientist named Chang Lam he was working on creating a better version of the Hulk and when he thought his work was a success he used it on himself and permanently transformed into the Abomination, but he maintained his mental intellect. He is a member of the Liberators, the evil counterpart of the Ultimates. He was later decapitated, mutilated and eaten by the Hulk. In this version, Abomination was huge (25 feet tall), had scaly shoulders and a tail. He looked like a cross between a gorilla and a lizard.
