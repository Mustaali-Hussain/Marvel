## Character History 

* Drache*

 

Madam Drache was a mutant and house-mother to a brothel called the X-Ranch. Her mutant powers where the ability to breathe fire. She lived a relative happy life until the mutant-hating group of soldiers named the Church of Humanity came and attacked the X-Ranch. Madam Drache was the first of the recidents to confront the soldiers, using her fire powers. The soldiers however managed to outwit her and she was attacked and killed by them.
