## Brief History

Bulls-Eye was hired by HYDRA to assassinate [[Nick Fury]]. Bulls-Eye succeeded in killing his target but was abandoned by HYDRA and forced to fend for himself against swarms of SHIELD agents. This encounter cost Bulls-Eye his own life as he was killed in the assault that followed. It was later revealed that the Nick Fury he killed was a Life Model Decoy.

Despite the numerous similarities, there are no known connections between the original Bulls-Eye and the [[Daredevil]] enemy [[Bullseye]].
