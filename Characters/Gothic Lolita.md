> ###### Gothic Lolita
> **First appearance** Part 1 of 6: See These Eyes So Red #1
> **Appearances** 12
> **Also known as** Lol, Gothlol

> [!abstract] In brief
> The strongest member of the Livewires, a team of robots who serve the Marvel Comics spy agency, S.H.I.E.L.D.

Gothic Lolita is a member of the Livewires and she is the heavy artillery of the group. Lolita is an android with a superhuman chassis and her body is a massively hyperdense assembly of smartware tailored to rumble and tumble with the best of them (her weight = 500 lbs). The Livewires dress up and wear disguises or cosplay a lot in their line of work and one day Lolita dolled herself up like a certain Japanese pop-culture fashion trend called the Gothic Lolita style. Lolita loved the style and dubbed herself Gothic Lolita, the powerhouse in black babydoll lace.
