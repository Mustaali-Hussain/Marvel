## Origins

Jennifer Starkey was an ordinary woman till one day she was abducted by 3K, who experimented on her awakening a mutant gene in her. After that, her memory of the abduction was erased and she was released, returning to her daily life like if nothing happened to her.

## Story Arc

Jennifer soon became the target of Tervor Fitzory and his new team of Upstarts who started to film videos of them murdering mutants for the likes. As she tried to escape, she thought of swimming to get to safety, which made her manifest her mutant powers.

Luckily she was rescued by Cyclop's X-men who were there to stop and arrest the Upstarts. Although they escaped, they saved Jennifer's live and took her with them to their base in Alaska. There she started to form a friendship with Beast and started to learn more about the nature of her mutant powers.

## Powers and Abilities

Jennifer has the power to adapt to her environment, transforming herself and adopting animalistic features, like developing fins to swim or wings to fly.
