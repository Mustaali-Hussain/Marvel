> There have been two Marvel characters going by the name Piper. One is a mutant and a member of the Morlocks, the other is a mutate of the savage land.

## Brief History

Piper was among the first Savage Land natives to be mutated in order to serve Magneto. The Savage Land Mutates were a villain team made by Magneto based in the Savage Land. Piper and his team opposed the X-men  on one ocassion, as well as other superhero teams such as the Avengers and Power Pack.  

## Creation

Piper was created by Roy Thomas and Neal Adams in 1969 and first appeared in X-Men # 62. 

 

## Powers and Abilities

Both Pipers have the ability to psionically control animals by using his flute. 

 

## Othert Versions

The second Piper was a Morlock and was living in the Morlock Tunnels.
