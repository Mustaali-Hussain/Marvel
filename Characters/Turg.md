## Origin

Turg was born in the Forgotten City of the High Evolutionary and was outcast for not being the same as the devolved residents of the city. He was saved by the Thing and taken in by the Fantastic Four. Turg carries a younger Moloid named Korr with him until it can walk on it's own.

## Creation

Turg was created by Jonathan Hickman and Dale Eaglesham in 2010 and first appeared in Fantastic Four # 575. In his original appearances, he was called Tong, yet later the name got switched with the moloid Turg.
