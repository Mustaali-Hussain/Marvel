> A female criminal who adopted her codename in Stilt-Man's honor. Participated in Purple Man's Villains for Hire.

## Creation

Lady Stilt-Man was created by Joe Kelly and Eric Canete. She first appeared in The Amazing Spider-Man #611.

## Major Story Arcs

### This Man, This Expletive Deleted

In the middle of New York City, Lady Stilt-Man was introduced by attacking [[Spider-Man]] with her armor similar to the original [[Stilt-Man]]. When questioned why she chose the name, she replied that it's an homage to the original. During their battle, Spider-Man dodged her attacks, which eventually forced her robotic stilt into a high heel ornament on a truck. [[Deadpool]] then removed a sewer cap, and after Lady Stilt-Man lost her balance, she stuck her stilt inside the sewer. She fell to the ground, defeated, and began to sob.

### All the World's A Stage

[[Mary Jane]] and her agent Stella are taking a limousine ride to an audition when they hit some traffic when her husband, Spider Man webs up Lady Stilt-Man onto a building wall. Mary Jane wonders how Lady Stilt-Man could stand in those things and continues to her audition.

### A Council Of Masters

[[Max Fury]], the leader of the Shadow Council has assembled a new Masters of Evil in the sovereign nation of Bagalia where Lady Stilt-Man is currently a member. Lady Stilt-Man is present when Max Fury and the Masters of Evil capture [[John Steele]] after he attempts to escape Bagalia with the Serpent Crown and the Crown of Thorns.

## Powers and Abilities

Lady Stilt-Man utilizes a suit that is very similar to that used by the original Stilt-Man. This suit provides full-body armor and has hydraulic legs that can extend to immense lengths, allowing her to propel herself into the air. The legs have also been shown to be quite flexible, unlike those of Stilt-Man. There have been some improvements to the suit and now she is capable of extending her arms due to limb extensors and hydraulics. Her strikes are more powerful and her arms can snare and coil around her enemies as well.
