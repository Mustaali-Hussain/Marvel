> [!abstract] In brief
> Death Shield was trained by Taskmaster to become sort of a rival of Captain America.

Death Shield was trained by the [[Taskmaster]]. He also trained two more students to become a nasty version of [[Hawkeye]] and [[Spider-Man]] and they are named the Jagged-Bow and Blood Spider. Taskmaster was given the order to test his special students against Spider-Man and [[Solo]] who infiltrate an estate to go after the [[Red Skull]]. The evil imposters are eventually defeated and Death Shield's current whereabouts are unknown.
