## Major Story Arcs

### Joining The League of Realms

In his bid to start the War of the Realms, Malekith kidnapped the queen of Alfheim and forced her to become his wife. In response, Ivory Honeyshot called together the League of Realms to reunite, including new recruits, like Ro. Without the support of the Congress of Worlds, they were forced to keep their mission to liberate the queen a secret.

Unfortunately, it was a trap. The queen had been moved, and Malekith sent his new Kurse after them, revealed to be their former teammate, Lady Warizia, with an army of dark elves. They also found the explosives Malekith rigged on the castle. They managed to save the queen, just as Malekith and his forces move on to their next target. Jane Foster than uses Mjolnir to manifest rain to put out the flames of the castle.

### War of the Realms

Malekith's war across the realm has continued undeterred with only one realm left targeted by his wrath: Midgard. After his initial attack, he assigned continents to the allies he had made across the realms. In turn, Thor assigned his own allies across the globe to fight on multiple fronts. Ro and the rest of the League of Realms were assigned to Africa, which had been given to Malekith loyalists from Heven. They were joined by a new representative of Midgard: Spider-Man.

A few others didn't like that Spider-Man refused to kill despite being at war, but the last straw was when he allied himself with Fernande, the angel lieutenant who had taken over Lagos, against the rest of the angels. Screwbeard, Ivory, and Ud abandoned Thor's appointed leader, while Ro and Titanya remained. As a show of good faith, the angels release their prisoners, and then Fernande orders her angels to fight alongside the Nigerian army to take the fight to the angel queen, while Fernande represents her realm with The League.

Ro and the others catch up with their former teammates at The Vatican, where they were being overwhelmed by Kurse. Spidey and Fernande are able to distract Kurse long enough for Ro to counterspell the armor and free Warizia, reuniting her with her old team.
