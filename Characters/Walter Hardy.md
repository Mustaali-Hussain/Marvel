## Variants

### Earth-6160

On the Earth that was manipulated by The Maker, Walter Hardy joins the Sinister Six, Kingpin's council of crime bosses from every borough. He supposedly retired from being a cat burglar when he bought up most of The Bronx' criminal enterprises.

## In Other Media

 

- Walter Hardy appears in the Spectacular Spider-Man (TV Series) voiced by James Remar. This version of Walter was the man who killed Uncle Ben. After robbing the place and escapes in the elevator he soon after shots Uncle Ben. Later in "Opening Night" Walter was shown as a prisoner in The Vault.
