## Brief History

Almost nothing in known about the Dwarf. He appeared out of nowhere handing out Darkhold pages to innocent people who would invoke the page (each time causing [[Victoria Montesi]] a blinding vision). He appears to have some sort of teleporting ability since he is able to appear and disappear from just about any situation (including a flying airplane). And he isnt limited to just innocents since he has also been seen giving pages to Midnight Son members [[Blade]] and Victoria Montesi. He was last seen at the grave site of Louise Hastings right after the disbanding of the Darkhold Redeemers.
