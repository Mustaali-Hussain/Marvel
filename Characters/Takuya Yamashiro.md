## Origin

Takuya Yamashiro is the [[Spider-Man]] of Earth-51778. He was a motocross driver and son of the astrophysicist Dr. Hiroshi Yamashiro. In one of his races, Takuya received telepathic messages from the alien Garia, that lead him to investigate a crashed Ufo, the Marveller.The incident attracted the villain Dr. Monster and his Iron Cross Army. Takuya recieved a transfusion of blood from the alien Garia, and recieved spider-like powers to began a fight against the Iron Cross Army.

Creation

The character debuted in 1978 in the live-action tokusatsu TV series Spider-Man produced by the Toei Company under license from Marvel Comics.

## Major Story Arcs

### The Lost Boy

**For more information see: The Lost Boy**

While battling King Bowler Takuya is teleported out of Leopardon to to Earth 616. There [[Julia Carpenter|Madame Web]], [[Silk]] and [[Spider-Girl (Corazón)]] explain that he and [[Spider-Boy (Briggs)|Spider-Boy]] switch places.

Always ready to help, Takuya is able to use Silk's link to the Life Web to communicate to Spider-Boy on how to pilot Leopardon, and Spider-Boy is able to defeat King Bowler. Spider-Girl uses her powers to then send Takuya back to Earth-51778. This sends Spider-Boy to another Earth, and Takuya wishes him good luck.

## Powers

In addition to the standard spider-powers he utilizes a variety of gadgets, a flying car, and a spaceship called the Marveler that transforms into a giant robot called Leopardon.
