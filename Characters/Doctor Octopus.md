> Doctor Octopus is a supervillain originating from Marvel Comics. A former nuclear physicist and atomic researcher, Otto Octavius' life changed forever when one of his experiments went horribly wrong involving his four mechanical appendages used to assist him in his experiments. From then on, Octavius soon after his initial encounter has become one of the most dangerous supervillains in the Marvel Universe.

## Origin

Otto Octavius was raised in an oppressive household, with an overprotective [[Mary Octavius|mother]], and a harsh, abusive [[Torbert Octavius|father]]. His mother encouraged the young Otto, already a good student, to push himself in school, in the hopes that he would have a better life than his factory worker father. Otto was awarded a scholarship for university, shortly before his father died in a workplace accident. Becoming obsessed with schoolwork after his father's death, Otto applied himself even harder to his studies in science. He graduated and got work with an engineering firm, soon becoming a respected nuclear physicist and research consultant, as well as working as an inventor and lecturer. He soon developed a remarkable invention- a chest harness that connected the wearer to four mechanical arms. This invention earned him a new nickname: Doctor Octopus. Around this time, he became involved with a fellow researcher, Mary Alice Anders, to whom he eventually proposed marriage. However, the happy couple was broken up by Otto's mother, who disapproved of the young bride-to-be. Otto broke off the engagement at his mother's behest, but she died soon after in the middle of an argument with her son. Having lost all of his social ties, Otto became more withdrawn and sullen, and grew obsessively dedicated to his work while simultaneously throwing professional and operational caution to the wind. This recklessness with his personal safety lead directly to an accident that would change his life forever. A radiation leak in the lab caused an explosion, which fused the harness and limbs onto his body. The radiation simultaneously caused mutations in his brain, allowing him to have direct control over the arms through telepathy. Driven insane by the accident and the damage it had caused to his body and mind, Doctor Octopus became dedicated to a life of crime.

## Creation

Doctor Octopus was created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko. He first appeared in "Spider-Man Versus Doctor Octopus" in The Amazing Spider-Man #3 in July 1963.

## Character Evolution

When first introduced, his powers, including the telepathic control, were explained as a direct result of the accident that fused the harness to his body in the first place. Later stories hinted that he may have possessed low-level telepathy from a young age, which was in turn implied to have caused a brain aneurysm in his father.

He briefly abandoned the Doctor Octopus identity, creating the identity of the "Master Planner," a criminal mastermind, to orchestrate his plots. However, he eventually resumed the Doctor Octopus identity.

### Sinister Six

*Formation of the Six*

Doctor Octopus is the impetus for the formation of the original Sinister Six, which occurs following a series of defeats at the hands of [[Spider-Man]]. He gathers [[Vulture]], [[Electro]], [[Kraven the Hunter]], [[Mysterio]], and the [[Sandman]], all who have faced, and been defeated by, Spider-Man. Though he tells the other villains that together they will be able to defeat Spider-Man, he secretly plans to wait until all the other villains have tried and failed to kill their foe, at which point he will step in and defeated the greatly weakened hero. He kidnaps [[Betty Brant]] and [[Aunt May|May Parker]] to bait Spider-Man into the fights and when, as predicted, his teammates fail, he steps in and battles Spider-Man in a large fishbowl-like tank, planning to drown the hero. He is ultimately defeated.

### Gang War

*Hammerhead vs Doc Ock*

Doctor Octopus becomes embroiled in a gang war against [[Hammerhead]] following the retirement of the [[Kingpin]]. The two wage an extremely destructive war throughout the streets of New York City in dual, competing bids to gain control over the vast criminal empire left behind. Spider-Man ultimately defeats both men, and Octavius is sent to prison.

*Aunt May and Uncle Otto?*

Octavius attempts to marry May Parker, in the hopes of acquiring a Canadian nuclear power plant that she has inherited. However, it turns out that he actually survived by transferring his consciousness into the "Proto Clone" the Jackal had created. With his new, more handsome body, Octavius returns to one of his old lairs, only to find it occupied by HYDRA agents. After a battle, Octavius and [[Arnim Zola]] come to a truce, and agree to work together to bring down Parker Industries. They create a new uniform and harness for Octavius, who now dubs himself the Superior Octopus.

During HYDRA's takeover of the United States, Octavius becomes part of [[Captain America|Steve Rogers]]' new team of HYDRA Avengers (which includes [[Deadpool]], [[Eric O'Grady|Black Ant]], [[Moon Knight|Taskmaster]], [[Thor|Odinson]], a brainwashed [[Vision]]and a [[Chthon|Cthon]]-controlled [[Scarlet Witch]]).

### Elliot Tolliver

With Peter's memories still influencing his decisions to be a hero, Otto would remake himself as Elliot Tolliver, a Horizon University employee, who was able to work side by side with Anna Marconi and try to reclaim his status as the Superior Spider-Man.

As Tolliver, he would combine Jackal and [[Jennix]]'s technology to create an army of duplicate bodies he could upload his consciousness too in case of injury or death. This got the attention of the Inheritors, who used these blank bodies for themselves. Thus, Otto would fall in with the other Web Warriors to face off against the Inheritors.

After the Web Warriors were victorious, Otto continued fighting crime as Superior Spider-Man, eventually being found out by a suspicious Anna Marconi. She wanted to go to the cops, but she settled on letting Otto continue being a hero as long as she was able to keep an eye on him.

### Return of Doc Ock

Otto would be targeted by a variant Norman Osborn, who had become Spider-Man on his Earth and feuded with Otto on the Web Warriors. [[Mephisto]] made a deal with Otto that would allow him to overcome Osborn.

Mephisto would return Otto's mind back to how it was before Otto combined himself with Peter and his body to before all the trauma from fighting Spider-Man. This would allow for a duplicate body to act as a deceased Elliot Tolliver, convincing variant-Osborn that there was no one to kill. Anna pleaded with Otto not to do it, but he did it anyway.

Mephisto altered some of his memories. Otto remembered his heroics in the cloned body, but he doesn't remember any of his heroics in Peter's actual body. Without Peter's influence, Otto returned to villainy. While trying to fill the missing gaps in his memory, he would fall under the influence of the demon, Kindred, who snuck one of his centipedes into his ear and pushed him to reform the Sinister Six.

### Sinister War

Dr. Octopus's new Sinister Six was made up of:

- Sandman, who Otto rescued from his island

- Kraven, who found in the Savage Land and given an offer he couldn't refuse
- The Lizard, who Otto separated from Dr. Connors with an Isotope Genome Accelerator

However, he also finds that this company is owned by the Beyond Corporation, and that they were outfitting their new Spider-Man with Doc Ock's tech that Beyond received from absorbing Parker Industries, angering Otto.

### Devil's Reign

*Superior Four*

Doc Ock was hired by Mayor [[Kingpin|Wilson Fisk]] to be part of his new Thunderbolts, a task force to weed out the superhuman vigilantes in New York City. Doc Ock was specifically chosen to ensure the proper containment of the Fantastic Four and their laboratory. There, Ock made sure to contain the Purple Man for Fisk's future plans.

In exchange, Doc Ock was given free reign of the Baxter Building using the Forever Gate to bring forth 3 variant Doc Ocks, whom appeared combined with Hulk, Wolverine, and Ghost Rider, resembling the New Fantastic Four. He regretted giving up his memories as the Superior Spider-Man in a demonic pact, so he wanted versions of himself who understood being a superior counterpart.

In addition to searching the multiverse, Ock's plans involved campaigning against Fisk in the mayoral race. He conspired with Chameleon to pose as Tony Stark. However, the heroes out-voted Stark and went with Luke Cage as their candidate, ruining Ock's plans. So, he pivoted to plan B, which was to release crime drones of his own design, keeping the crime rate down and getting Fisk under his thumb.

When Fisk grew impatient, he turned on Ock. Ock had a neural blocker that protected him from Purple Man's powers, but his Superior Four variants did not. They turned against Otto and even though he joined the Avengers in a fight against those under Purple Man's influence, Iron Man still blasted him for his involvement.

### Sinister Adaptoid

After going their separate ways, The Living Brain kidnapped Doc Ock and five other foes of Spider-Man's. He was using them to program a new Super Adaptoid to hunt down and defeat Spider-Man so that the Living Brain could fulfill its original purpose, answer the question "Who is Spider-Man?"

In a fight with thisSinister Adaptoid in the Living Brain's lab, Spider-Man uncovered his villains in shackles. Against his better judgment, he decided to release them so that they would aid him in defeating both the Super Adaptoid and the Living Brain. When they found the Living Brain's control center, the Sinister Six wanted to kill it.

Believing it to be alive, Spider-Man defended the Living Brain's life. Seeing Spider-Man put his life on the line for an enemy whose life Spider-Man considered equally valuable satisfied the "Who is Spider-Man?" question. Thus, the Living Brain trapped the Sinister Six and agreed to Spider-Man's demands that it be temporarily taken offline and given to proper authorities.

### The Missing Piece

*Otto's realization*

Otto's inability to figure out the missing piece of his memory began to weigh on him. He became more and more obsessive like his older self. When he saw that Norman Osborn was able to put his villainous past behind him and work with Spider-Man for the greater good, Otto grew resentful. He attacked Norman and attempted to reinfect him with his goblin formula. However, Norman was able to fight off its effects, leaving Otto with no excuse for his own inability to become a hero.

Otto got a break when Spider-Man was attacked by [[Supernova]], Otto's former lab assistant. She was granted superhuman abilities accidentally by Otto when he was possessing Spider-Man. She was too powerful, and he was forced to lock her up. When she was finally freed, she attacked Spider-Man believing him to be her true captor. Over the course of the fight, Spider-Man removed his mask and was spotted by one of Otto's drones. Once Peter's identity as Spider-Man was revealed to Otto, his memories from that period of his life came flooding back to him.

Otto planned on trying to usurp Spider-Man's body once again, but Supernova targeted his former love, Anna Maria. Instead, he decided he needs Spider-Man's help. He brought Spidey to one of his old caches, so he could equip Spidey with his old gear. He also claimed to be working on a cure, but he was actually working on a new prison for Supernova that would be capable of channeling her energy as a power source. After using Spidey in Otto's suit as bait, he managed to trap her.

He was specifically going to use her as a power source to send billions of copies of his brain patterns around the world linked by Peter's genetically modified spiders that would act as a conduit, effectively turning the entire human race into a Doc Ock hive mind. Doc had a change of heart when he saw the Anna Maria had also been infected. Sickened by the idea of a world of just him, Doc Ock wiped his mind from Peter's spiders. This also caused him to lose the memories of his time as Spider-Man again, this time without the realization that something was missing. Doc Ock had regressed to a time before he was ever Spider-Man.

### S.C.A.R.

After the Venom War, Doc Ock joined a new government agency, S.C.A.R. Officially, it stood for Symbiote Containment And Rendition, with a mission to find a habitable planet to deport symbiotes too. After losing the symbiotes, Doc Ock was promoted to leader, and his role was made public. He outfitted the field agents with robotic arms like his own.

## Powers and Abilities

### As Doctor Octopus:

Doctor Octopus has a genius-level intellect related to the field of atomic physics and radiation. He holds a PhD in nuclear science, and is skilled as an inventor and an engineer. He is an excellent tactician, as well as being a charismatic leader. He is able to effectively segment his attention in such a way as to engage multiple people in battle while simultaneously performing some other, more delicate task.

*Ock Arms*

He has telepathic control over four prehensile, electronic octopus arms that are attached to his torso. Even when the harness is detached from his torso, he maintains a level of telepathic control over the arms, even over great distances. The arms, which have pincers at the end, can extend to 24 feet at the maximum, and are capable of superhuman strength and enhanced reaction time. He can use them to lift a maximum of three tonnes per arm, though at least one arm must support his body, and he can use them similarly to legs, to lift his body and move rapidly over a variety of terrains, and also to scale walls. He wears an armored body suit because, while capable of dishing out an enhanced beating, his body is that of a regular human, and has become increasingly worn down by the constant fighting and injuries he has sustained. This suit adds an additional four cybernetic arms. His telepathic control also extends over an army of octobots, small, eight-limbed robots.

### As Spider-Man:

*Superior Spider-man*

Due to him having Spider-Man's body Otto has access to all of [[Spider-Man|Peter Parker's]] abilities and standard gear however has yet to show knowledge of the Way Of Spider fighting style. He also has some new technology integrated into his suit such as claws that can inject nanite spider-tracers allowing him to follow and hear anyone who's is injected with them, computer integrated into the lenses that have allowed him to see in multiple forms of vision (night-vision, infrared, etc.) as well as showing him data on the opponents he faces such as powers. The Superior Spider-Man also has 4 mechanical appendages that can retract from the suit akin to the arms he had as Doctor Octopus. These arms have shown considerable amounts of strength being able to lift and throw cars with ease. Otto also uses these arms to impale his opponents. Due to the amount of money he amassed as a villain Otto has also built his own base of operations with large amounts of tech including giant spider-like robots as well as an army of mecernaries under his command. The amount of new gear he has and what it is actually used for has yet to be fully revealed.

### Equipment:

**Doctor Octopus' Arms**: four mechanical appendages that he uses as his primary weapon and is able to control each of them through a special link. They are capable of both great strength and delicate procedures.

**Octobot**: a metal ball of varying size with four/eight tentacles controlled mentally by Otto via a remote control. They can be used to attack and to perform different tasks.

**[[Spider-Bot]]**: when Otto took over the role of Spider-Man he redesigned his robot to fit his new persona.

**Superior Spider Suit**: Upgraded version of The Spider-Man costume.

**Superior Octopus Suit**: Using Hydra resources Otto created The Superior Octopus suit, a fusion of his Superior Spider Suit and Doctor Octopus' Arms.

However, he left the company after Norman began engaging in dangerous and unethical genetic experiments, including one that resulted in the death of[[Mister Negative|Martin Li]]'s parents. After leaving OsCorp, Octavius started his own company, but quickly ran out of money, resulting in him not becoming as famous as his former friend. Despite his cheerful and polite demeanor, Otto secretly harbors intense bitterness over this perceived betrayal, leading to a decades-long hatred of Osborn.

In the present, he and Peter are working on a special project to create advanced cybernetic limbs, which will hopefully be used to help people with physical handicaps. As the game progresses, Otto reveals that he also has a personal stake in the experiment, as he is suffering from a degenerative disease that is slowly destroying the motor functions in his body. He believes that the prosthetic limbs he and Peter are developing will be able to replace his arms and legs after the disease progresses. Otto has seemingly has a breakthrough after Peter inspires him to create a neural interface that allows him to directly control the prosthetics, but the imperfect system turns out to have dangerous psychological effects. The brain implant begins to drive Otto insane, and causes his deep seated resentment towards Osborn take hold. Octavius becomes Doctor Octopus, and sets out to destroy Norman's empire and expose his crimes to the world, hoping to ruin his life before ultimately killing him.

Doc Ock stages a massive breakout at the Raft, and gathers a group of Spider-Man's enemies together to form the Sinister Six. He then releases the Devil's Breath, a dangerous disease that Osborn had accidentally developed, into New York City, infecting countless citizens. With the city in chaos, the Sinister Six begin targeting various locations connected to Osborn, but are defeated by Spider-Man. Octavius kidnaps Norman and takes him to the top of the OsCorp building, hoping to force a confession out of him for the whole world to see. The plot is foiled when Spider-Man arrives and saves Norman, leading to a final confrontation between mentor and pupil. Spider-Man pleads with Octavius to stop this madness and surrender the cure for Devil's Breath, hoping to appeal to the goodness still inside Otto. However, the implant has caused too much damage to Otto's mind, and the two engage in a fierce battle. Spider-Man ultimately emerges victorious, and removes the implant from Otto's skull. Powerless, Otto pleads with his former pupil and begs him to join forces, but a heartbroken Spider-Man leaves him for the police.
