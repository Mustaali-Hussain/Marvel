> [!abstract] In brief
> He is a superhero from Delhi, the capital of India. He was recruited into the Champions by Ms. Marvel, his addition served to make the Champions global.

## Powers and Abilities

The origin of his power has never been confirmed to date.

**Gate Creation:** He has the power to create green discs that serve as portals to any place on the planet. So far, no limit has been shown, either to keeping the portals open or to the number of people.

### Weaknesses.

Aside from his ability to create portals, he appears to be a completely normal human.
