## History

 Principal Harrington hired Midtown High School alumni Peter Parker to be the new Science teacher and Flash Thompson to be the new school coach

## In Other Media

### Spider-Man Homecoming

In *Spider-Man: Homecoming*, Roger Harrington is not a high school principal nor is he a faculty member of Midtown High School. Instead, he is a STEM school academic decathlon coach at Midtown School of Science and Technology. Harrington is portrayed by Martin Starr.
