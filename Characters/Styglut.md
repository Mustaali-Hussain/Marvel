> ###### Styglut
> **First appearance** "Bishop to King's Five" #287
> **Appearances** 15

> [!abstract] In brief
> Styglut was a vicious mutant from the future era that spawned Bishop and the X.S.E.

After escaping to the present era via [[Trevor Fitzroy|Fitzroy]]'s time portal, Styglut was the last of the future criminals to escape [[Bishop]]'s justice. He acted as somewhat of a leader, with the other criminals following his lead. 

 

Due to his recent membership in the X-Men, and their differing methods to the X.S.E., Bishop chose to spare his life rather than terminating him as he had the rest of the criminals from his era. 

 

What became of Styglut after he was captured is unknown.

  

 

## Powers

 

Similar to [[Trevor Fitzroy|Fitzroy]] , Styglut possessed the ability to drain a person's lifeforce, killing them.  He would then convert that energy into powerful bio-blasts.
