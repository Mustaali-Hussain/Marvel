> Catwalk was a supermodel turned shape-shifting Skrull killer.

Catwalk was a supermodel and discovered her powers while her flight was being hijacked by agents of Hydra. She single-handedly disposed of the terrorists and was soon recruited into the Skrull Kill Krew. 

## Powers

Though a Skrull-DNA infected shape-shifter, Catwalk seems to prefer the form of a feline creature, complete with claws, fangs, and enhanced athletic abilities.
