> ###### Dangard Ace
> **First appearance** Raydeen! #1
> **Appearances** 25

> [!abstract] In brief
> Dangard Ace along with Raideen and Combattler were taken from Japanese cartoons and turned into a comic book to go along with the Shogun Warriors toy line and the Force five cartoon series.

## Creation

Dangard Ace was created by Leiji Matsumoto and Dan Kobayashi for the anime series *Wakusei Robo Danguard Ace* (1977-1978), arrived in America in Mattel's Shogun Warriors toy collection and was adapted into Marvel comics by Doug Moench and Herb Trimpe in 1979 and first appeared in Shogun Warriors # 2.
