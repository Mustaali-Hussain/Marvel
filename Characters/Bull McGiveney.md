## Origin

Sergeant Bull McGiveney was a U.S. soldier that lead a group of commandoes known as the Maulers. He and Nick Fury clashed on a number of ocassions due to competitveness between the two groups.

## Creation

Bull McGiveney was created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby and first appeared in Sgt. Fury issue 7 (1964).

## Major Story Arcs

### After World War II and Death

Bull survived World War Two returned to the United States once agian. He often met his old soldiers during Veteran meetings.
