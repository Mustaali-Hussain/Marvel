> [!abstract] In brief
> Victor Hudson is Romulus' blind and mute right-hand man.

Victor Hudson was born blind and mute, for this very reason [[Romulus]] chose him to be his bodyguard and right-hand man. 

Victor is the son of Bernice and Frederick Hudson Jr., who is [[Wolverine]]'s cousin. His mother shot herself after proclaiming in church that she had given birth to the son of the devil. Romulus then took Victor and raised him. He is also the half-brother of [[The Professor|Truett Hudson]].
