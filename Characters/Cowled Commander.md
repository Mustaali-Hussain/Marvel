> [!abstract] In brief
> This New York City police Sergeant turned to a life of crime. Taking command of a large group of super-villains and assuming the guise of the hooded "Cowled Commander", he became fascinated with destroying Captain America. He hired the Manipulator android to kill the hero.

## Origin

Brian Muldoon was created by Stan Lee and John Romita in 1971 and first appeared in Captain America # 139.

## History

Sergeant Brian Muldoon was initially one of the best men of New York's Comissioner. He was suspended by the Comissioner pending further investigation.

He is considered a man-eater by other police officers.
