> [!abstract] In brief
> Miss Saccharine is an elderly mutant living among the mutant outcasts in the sewers of London, England.  Her sweat tastes as sweet as sugar.

Miss Saccharine was created by Joe Casey and Ian Churchill in 2001 and first appeared in Uncanny X-Men 395.

## History

Miss Saccharine was one of the London based Morlocks, a community of mutants who lived in the sewers. The community was being hunted down by Mr. Clean, an agent of the Church of Humanity, who was genetically engineered to murder mutants.

## Powers

Her sweat produces something akin to sugar.
