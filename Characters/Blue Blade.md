## Brief History

Blue Blade, otherwise known as Roy Chambers, is a member of the superhero group known as The Twelve. Despite having no powers and his origin being a mystery, Chambers joined The Twelve as the superhero, Blue Blade in order to fight the Nazis during World War Two until they were trapped and placed in stasis. 60 years later, Chambers as well as the rest of the team were revived and he has attempted to get his own television show which flopped due to its "retro" nature. He is unliked by the rest of the group and is found dead several months after they have been revived with The Phantom Reporter finding his body.
