> Also known as "Pizza Dog". He was owned by a member of the Tracksuit Mafia. After Hawkeye fed him some pizza, which wasn't even that good, he bravely came to Hawkeye's rescue during a fight.

## Origin

[[Hawkeye|Clint Barton]] wasn't too crazy about the name "Arrow". He changed it to Lucky as seen at the end of the second issue.

## Creation

Lucky was created by Matt Fraction and David Aja and first appeared in Hawkeye #1 (2012)
