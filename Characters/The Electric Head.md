## Background

**The Electric Head** was part of the Lethal Legion version inspired in DC comics team the Fatal Five. The Electric Head was an analog to Validus.

## History

The Electric Head was a member of the version of the Lethal Legion created by [[Henry Gyrich]], who stole the name for a hit squad he formed as a mole for Orchis on the Alpha Flight Space Program.

Along with his teammates, they were infinitely replaceable cyborg clones created by [[Orbis Stellaris]]. If a team fell, a new team was activated and sent to replenish the ranks. Gyrich sent them to Planet Arakko, the new name for Mars since mutants terraformed it. Arakko was hosting a Shi’ar diplomatic party with [[Xandra|Empress Xandra]] as part of their move to become the galactic capital of the solar system.

They failed to assassinate Xandra, but Orbis Stellaris still considered this field test to be a success. Killing Xandra was an impossible task, and they got closer than he expected.
