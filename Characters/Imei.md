> ###### Imei
> **Real name** Imei Chang
> **First appearance** Souls in Torment! #75
> **Appearances** 46
> **Also known as** Imei Chang, Xaos

Imei was betrothed to Doctor Strange's ally and manservant, Wong. She had apparently been transmogrified into a demon.

By the time Strange could attempt a rescue, it was too late... Imei had been dead for some time and all that remained was the demon who called itself Xaos.
