## Origin

Nell Ruggles is a psychopathic teenager with a history of murder. She one day went into her home room and killed her classmates with an automatic rifle. She later obtained strange powers which made her body turn red with black lines all over her like webs, and a strange webbing material leaving her body. She is first seen helping a girl from being raped by three guys, she came out of nowhere to attack them resulting in two injured and one dead.

## Powers

Arachnophilia possesses abilities similar to [[Spider-Man]] and other arachnid-based superhumans. Her body generates and secretes some sort of biological suit that gives her that unique appearance and also a form of unlimited mucous-like webbing. She appears to have superhuman strength, agility, stamina and leaping ability.
