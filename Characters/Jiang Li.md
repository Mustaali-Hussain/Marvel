## Origin

Jiang Li is a mortal born in Ta-Lo and daughter of [[Chieftain Xin|Chieftain Xin.]]

Jiang Li was a wife of [[Zheng Zu]], mother of [[Shang-Chi]]and [[Sister Hammer|Shi-Hua]].

Shang-Chi thought his mother was dead, but she was living in the Negative Zone.

## Creation

*Ying Li marrying Wenwu in the movie and Jiang marrying Zheng Zu in the comics.*

Jiang Li was created specifically for the 2021 live action motion picture, Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings from the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

The character was introduced into Earth-616 canon with Shang-Chi #4 (2021), written by Gene Luen Yang and illustrated by Dike Ruan. The name Jiang Li was used promotional material from the film, however, when it was released, the character was called Ying Li, Gene Luen Yang opted for the originally featured name.

At the origin of Shang-Chi, he was half Caucasian, the son of an unidentified American woman.

## Other Media

*Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings*

Actress Fala Chen played Jiang Li in the movie Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings.

Jiang Li or Ying Li was a guardian of Ta Lo and wife of [[Mandarin|Xu Wenwu]], with whom he had two children: Shang-Chi and Xialing.
