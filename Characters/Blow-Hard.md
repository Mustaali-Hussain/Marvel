## Origin

Shot and killed by a gang after fleeing to the surface world.

 

Member of the group called Morlocks

 

Outcasts from society,the Morlocks were a community of mutants who lived in tunnels beneath New York City and its vicinity.

 

The unthinkable happened when a group of mercenaries named the Marauders who had been hired by [[Mr. Sinister|Sinister]]and lead by [[Gambit]],to break into the tunnels and murder the Morlocks.Most had been killed some escaped,a few with the help of the X-Men and original X-Factor. 

## Creation

Blow-Hard was created by Louise Simonson and Walter Simonson in 1986 and first appeared in X-Factor # 11.   

## Powers & Abilities

Blow-Hard was able to create powerful gusts of wind with his wind pipe.
