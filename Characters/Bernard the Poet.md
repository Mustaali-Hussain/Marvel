Bernard is a poet that is always at Cafe a-go-go, a bar in Greenwich Village, where Iceman and Beast usually go to hang around and date Zelda and Vera, their girlfriends. 

 

## Other Versions

### X-Men First Class

According to "X-Men: First Class Special" #1 (July, 2007), Bernard was a latent mutant whose powers manifested in the presence of other mutants. His words could alter the perceptions of his listeners. An unusual take on a classic character.
