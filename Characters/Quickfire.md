## Origin

After the destruction of Attilan, Barbara McDevitt discovered she was an Inhuman descendant. Her powers became active after she was exposed to the Terrigen Mists, a rite of passage central to Inhuman Culture and remained in a cocoon for weeks.

## Powers & Abilities

### Chronokinesis

Barbara's most prominent power is the ability to slow down time around a single target or object. She believes that with more focus and experience these new-found abilities could expand into slowing down time itself, freezing entire groups of people and multiple objects.

### Espionage

Prior to obtaining her superhuman abilities McDevitt was a well trained corporate spy for Cortex Incorporated.
