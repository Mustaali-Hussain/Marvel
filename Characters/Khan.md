> ###### Khan
> **First appearance** Keys of the Kingdom #10
> **Appearances** 70

> [!abstract] In brief
> Ruler of an alien invasion force from another dimension that invaded the 616 dimension in Madripoor. He was a master strategist and tactician, and was highly skilled in many forms of hand to hand combat.

Khan was the ruler of a massive armada that has conquered multiple dimensions. When his forces invaded the 616 dimension, he was opposed by Storm and her team of X-Men that were in search of Destiny's Diaries. After a difficulty battle, he and his entire armada was defeated, seemingly destroyed or trapped in a collapsed dimensional riff.
