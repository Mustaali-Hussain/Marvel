> ###### Halflife

> **First appearance** Grave Circumstances #334
> **Appearances** 14
> **Also known as** Anthony Masterson, Tony Masterson

> [!abstract] In brief
> Halflife was an English professor who was exposed to gamma radiation. He can absorb energy from an enemy.

After he was transformed into Halflife he began working for [[Leader]]and battled the [[Hulk]]. In order to survive he had to absorb the life force of a living person every few hours. During one of his battles with the Hulk he tried to absorb all of the his radiation and ended up killing himself in the process.
