> Bernie Rosenthal is the former girlfriend of Steve Rogers and well-known glass blower in New York City.

## Brief History

Bernie met [[Captain America|Steve Rogers]], also known as [[Captain America]], and the two started dating. She eventually discovered his secret identity. After the two continued dating for quite some time, Bernie eventually moved away to pursue a degree in law. 

 

She moved back later, and had trouble with an old friend who had joined the Watchdogs. Steve had already been dating [[Diamondback|Rachel Leighton]] who was also the costume heroine [[Diamondback]], yet Bernie attempted to renew their love, finally deciding to stay friends instead.

She is apparently also a well-qualified attorney as she defends Bucky during the Winter Soldier Trials.

## Creation

Bernie Rosenthal was created by Roger Stern and John Byrne in 1980 and first appeared in Captain America # 247.
