> ###### Ashley Barton
> **Real name** Ashley Barton
> **First appearance** Old Man Logan: Part 2 #67
> **Appearances** 71
> **Also known as** Ashley Barton, Kingpin, Spider-Bitch, Spider-Girl

> [!abstract] In brief
> In the alternate reality of Old Man Logan, she is the daughter of Clint Barton (Hawkeye) and Tonya (youngest daughter of Peter Parker, Spider-Man). During the events of Spider-Verse is officially named Spider-Girl.

When readers first met her she's jailed by Kingpin and is being forced to do battles in a gladiatorial style along with other fallen heroes. Hawkeye eventually rescues her with Logan's help. Once free, she murders the original Kingpin and reveals her original intentions. She then gains his criminal control and territory.
