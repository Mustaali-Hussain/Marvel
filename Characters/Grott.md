The [[Grandmaster]] abducts the Defenders and [[Daredevil]] to play a game against the [[Prime Mover]] with the Earth as the grand prize.

## Creation

Grott was created by Steve Gerber and Jim Starlin in 1975 and first appeared in Giant-Size Defenders # 3.

## Powers & Abilities

Grott is able to absorb energy from the sun through his antennae and turn his body into a living solar-cell. Grott is able to transmute that energy to form an impenetrable shield or convert it into psychokinetic force.
