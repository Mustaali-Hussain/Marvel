> [!abstract] In brief
> The Monstroid are combat automatons created by the Skrull civilization.

A Skrill Warscout robot.

Crashed onto Earth right outside the beach home of the Puppet Master who then used it to attack the Baxter Building. Was defeated by Spider-Man while Vision defeated Puppet Master. Was placed in SHIELD storage.

Was later used by Master Khan to attack Iron Fist who destoryed it by smashing it in the head with his chi fist.
