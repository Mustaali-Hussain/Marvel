> ###### Henry Sutter
> **Real name** Henry Sutter
> **First appearance** Innocence Lost, Part 5 #5
> **Appearances** 18
> **Also known as** Henry Sutter

> [!abstract] In brief
> Henry is the biological son of Zander Rice and Rachel Sutter, but the legal son of Martin and Rachel Sutter. He was a target of X-23 and one of the few that she spared.

Henry was conceived during an affair between [[Rachel Sutter]] and [[Dr. Zander Rice|Zander Rice]], a man who her husband thought of like a son. When she found out she was pregnant Zander told her to "tell [her husband] it's his." She did and Henry was raised as the son of [[Martin Sutter|Martin]] and Rachel Sutter. He appeared to have a happy childhood until Zander Rice, his biological father, in a quest for power ordered the assassin [[X-23]] to kill him and his parents. She came and killed both of his parents. When she found him hiding in a closet she ended up just cutting the head off his Spiderman action figure and letting him live. He was later found in the woods outside his flaming house.
