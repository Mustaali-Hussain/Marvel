## Powers And Abilities

Genesis is an Omega-level mutant.

- **Chlorokinesis:** She could mentally control all plant life within her vicinity. While using her powers, she manifests the ancient Egyptian emblem known as The Eye of Horus, formed by a green aura, from each of her hands. 

It also allows her to control and listen to plants and fungi, even the fungi inside people's bodies.

- **Advanced Longevity:** Like her family, Genesis is several thousands of years old.
