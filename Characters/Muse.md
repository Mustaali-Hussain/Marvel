> A serial killer who turns his victims into art.

## Major Story Arcs

Muse committed numerous heinous crimes, including killing newly made Inhumans following the Terrigen Cloud Bomb. He regularly evaded capture by Daredevil and Blindspot while doing so. Prior to being captured by the authorities, he would catch Blindspot and pierce both of his eyes with his fingers (Blindspot would later regain his sight through a mystical process). Blindspot would seek revenge and the pair would fight with Muse succumbing to fire damage during their fight.

## Powers & Abilities

While Muse doesn't seem to demonstrate any superhuman abilities, his skills are nearly on par with master martial artists, such as Daredevil. He is shown to have agility and reflexes capable of snatching Daredevil's baton (tossed by the same hero) out of the air and tossing a knife from across the room to perfectly strike a target in the throat. Along with his complex art made of formerly living beings, he has also shown an intellect with strategy as he has planned public crimes as well as the means to evade capture following them.

Muse was later revealed to have some superpowers.

**Imperceptibility**: Muse can absorb all sensory information into his body like a blackhole making him difficult to be perceive even for someone with enhance senses.

**Enhance Strength**: Muse posses low superhuman strength, being capable of ripping a car door off, snapping Daredevil's billy clubs and seemly dismember people with his bare hands.
