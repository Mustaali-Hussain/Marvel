## Origin

Very little is known about Mikka. She matriculated to Kinney University in Venice, California. There, she began working for the student newspaper and writing a popular blog, which soon attracted harassment, causing her to abandon her work for some time. 

## Creation

Mikka was created by Kelly Thompson and Leonardo Romero. She made her first appearance in *Hawkeye* #1.

## Major Story Arcs

### Hawkeye

Mikka approaches [[Kate Bishop]] seeking help with the mysterious stalker who resurfaced when she began writing again. When Kate apprehends fellow student Larry Gort, Mikka assumes she is safe. However, she is kidnapped by the enigmatic hate group Take Back Control, and taken to their headquarters, where she is brainwashed. She is kissed by her ex-girlfriend [[Alloy|Ramone]], which snaps her out of her brainwashing. After Aggregate leaves the party to chase Kate, Mikka helps to take the mind-controlling stickers off of the partygoers.
