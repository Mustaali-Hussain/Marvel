> [!abstract] In brief
> Jacques Moreau was a rowdy, violent person and was changed into the Brawler.

### Biography:

During an environmental survey Jacques Moreau was turned into the Brawler. He lost his powers and abilities and is now a normal human again. A former member of the now defunct Berzerkers.

### Powers:

Jacques Moreau as Brawler had superhuman strength, resiliency, and he was physically superior to normal humans in all respects. Brawler was also a master in many forms of combat.

### Creation:

Brawler was created by Chris Claremont and Paul Smith and first appeared in X-Men and Alpha Flight #1 in December 1985.
