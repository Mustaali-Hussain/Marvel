> ###### Willie Lincoln
> **First appearance** Brother, Take My Hand! #47
> **Appearances** 12

> [!abstract] In brief
> A former US Soldier who was blinded during the Vietnam War. Willie Lincoln idolises Daredevil and is a friend and former client of Matt Murdock.

The criminal Biggie Benson fromed polive officer Willie Lincoln on the charges of accepting a bribe, Shortly thereafter, while serving in Vietnam, Lincoln jumped on a live grenade to save his fellow soldiers. The blast blinded him. Daredevil met Willie while on a tour of the war-torn country, entertaining and boosting the morale of the troops.

When Willie returned home, he enlisted Matt Murdock's help to help clear his name . The tow became friends as Willie struggled to deal with his new impairment. Willie assisted Daredevil against Crime-Wave and his agent, the Torpedo. Later, he was one of the vetereans targeted by Bengal.
