## Brief History

Donna was raised as the daughter of a wealthy coffee industrailist named [[Zombie|Simon Garth]]. Her father raised Donna in wealth and spoiled her very much, but he had a stone-cold heart himself. Eventually, Donna's father was killed by his former gardner, but was also ressurected and became known as [[Zombie|the Zombie]]. Donna encountered her undead father on a few ocassions. Years later, Donna came into the possesion of her father's living corps thanks to Spider-man. She used him to avenge the death of one of her best friends and classmates, whom was the victim of a Voodoo doctor whom had slipped her a leathal potion. Donna vowed to stop every walking dead from that moment on, but she has not been seen again since.
