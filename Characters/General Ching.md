> ###### General Ching

> **Also known as** Jun Ching

> [!abstract] In brief
> Chinese member of the villainous Exiles. He has great skill with firearms.

General Ching is a Chinese member of the Exiles. While the rest of Exiles use exotic weaponry, Ching trusts in his regular handguns in combat. He was the only Exile to question Red Skull's de facto leadership, finding the Skull's ambitions in conflict with his own. Finding no support from the rest of the Exiles however, Ching had no choice but to accept Red Skull's rule. He later attempted to shoot Captain America in the back but was prevented from doing so by the Red Skull.

## Creation

General Ching was created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby in 1968 and first appeared in *Captain America *#103.
