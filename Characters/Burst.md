> A disgraced Asian Kung-Fu speedster.

## Origin

Burst appeared along with several other super powered "villians" (their true intentions are not yet known). The team lead by [[Lobe]], including [[Bouncing Betty]], [[Thug]] and [[Verre]], claims to want to save all of the world's mutant population.

## Creation

Burst was created by Matt Fraction and first appeared in Uncanny X-Men #515.

## Character Evolution

Both of Burst's appearances have been alongside Lobe and the rest of the team and in both instances has been defeated by the X-Men.

## Major Story Arcs

Nation X

Burst and the rest of his team kidnapped a clone of the marauder, [[Scalphunter]], and forced him to deliver 5 [[Predator X|Predator Xs]], that Lobe had recreated and modified. Scalphunter does in fact deliver the Predators, but they are all killed by the X-Men.

[[Wolverine]], [[Colossus]] and [[Betsy Braddock|Psylocke]] are sent to find where the Predators came from. Wolverine and the team are able to find the group, after a brief encounter with [[Fantomex]], and Wolverine stabs Lobe in the chest.

Quarantine

Burst appears again alongside his team as they stand against [[Angel]], [[Storm]], [[Northstar]], [[Dazzler]] and [[Pixie]] at Lobe's unveiling of his artificially powered X-Men. They are defeated off panel.
