> [!abstract] In brief
> Pisces is an aquatic member of the Zodiac.

Noah Perricone is a founding member of the Zodiac and his base of operations was in Miami. The Zodiac Cartel was founded by Taurus, handpicking the eleven other members; van Lunt concealed his own identity, while he was the only one who knew the identities of the others. Each member was based in a different American city as part of his nationwide criminal network, with the ultimate goal of world economic and political domination.

## Creation

Pisces was created by Roy Thomas and Sal Buscema in 1969 and first appeared in The Avengers # 72.

## Powers & Abilities

Pisces is a skilled underwater combatant, and uses his webbed hands and feet to swim at great speeds.
