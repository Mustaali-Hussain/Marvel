> ###### Lord Tyger
> **First appearance** The Beast In Me #1
> **Appearances** 25

> [!abstract] In brief
> Evolved tiger.

Lord Tyger is one of the [[High Evolutionary]]'s first New Men and was later placed as the leader of the Knights of Wundagore. Lord Tyger is very noble yet an open warrior when in combat. He has clashed with the likes of [[Wolverine]] and [[Spider-Man]] over the years. He fought for leadership of the Knights from Man-Wolf at one point when the mutant, Exodus tried to take over Wundagore mountain. He continues to serve as the Knights' leader.
