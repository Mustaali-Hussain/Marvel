## Origin

Aundray Phelps gained his powers and became the hero known as Attractive Lad after he was caught in a flood of ionic energy that [[Wonder Man]] had inadvertently released when his powers flared out of control. He was only one of eight individuals that gained powers in this manner. All eight of these individuals would join forces and become the team known as The Crazy 8.

## Powers

Attractive Lad had the ability to manipulate magnetic forces around his body. This would allow him to repel or compel magnetic objects to and from him. He is also able to create Force Fields, is able to fly and can create magnetic force blasts.
