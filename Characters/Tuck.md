*Death's Head II & Tuck*

A synthetic being created on a planet where this was highly illegal, she became a freedom fighter and joined Death's Head on his journeys through space, time, and other dimensions.

Tuck is a product of genetic engineering. Her body is organic but her physical abilities are enhanced.

Tuck has traveled through time alongside Death's Head II and Evelyn Necker but is not actually carrying her own time-traveling equipment.
