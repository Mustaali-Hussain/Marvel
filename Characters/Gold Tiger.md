## Creation

Gold Tiger debuts canonically in *New Champions (*2025) #1 by Steve Foxe and Ivan Fiorelli. She originally appeared on a variant cover for *Black Panther *in October 2023 drawn by Ernanda Souza. This cover was part of the "New Champions" variants depicting original sidekicks for Marvel's title characters.
