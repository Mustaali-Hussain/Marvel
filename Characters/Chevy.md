> ###### Chevy
> **First appearance** Racing the Night #1
> **Appearances** 32

> [!abstract] In brief
> One of the humans involved in the killing of Dennis Hogan. To make up for it, since he feels terrible about it, he was sent to the Massachuttes Academy by an unknown individual.

Chevy worked as a handyman, for free, at the Massachutts Academy for Gifted Youngsters. Having disappeared from the series long before the Massachutts Academy was closed in Generation X #75, it can be assumed that at some point Chevy's services were no longer needed.

Chevy made his first appearance in the pages of Generation X in issue #16 and was seen meeting with his mysterious blackmailer in Generation X #20.
