Red Army Colonel who served with the United Nations forces attached to SHIELD. His son was killed whilst serving aboard the submarine *Rodina*, which was sunk by Magneto. During a mission in the Savage Land, he joined forces briefly with Zaladane, after an attempt to kill Magneto, in revenge for his son`s death...

He was later killed by Magneto, electrocuted to death when the Master of Magnetism used a power-cable to defend himself in Zaladane`s citadel.
