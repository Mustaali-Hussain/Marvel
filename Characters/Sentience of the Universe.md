## Origin

The Sentience of the Universe was the embodiment of life in the previous universe (similar to [[Eternity]] in the new universe). This was revealed to be the sixth personification of the multiverse. He is stated as the inventor of science and the builder of the junction to everywhere (Overspace).

As the universe collapsed on itself, the Sentience found one of the last sentient beings alive. His name was Galen from the planet Taa.

Though their deaths were inevitable, the Sentience joined with Galan so they could be reborn in the new multiverse They formed a cosmic egg that would become the new universe. Upon it's expansion, they were reborn as [[Galactus]] along with Eternity (the seventh personification of the multiverse), [[Death]] and the other cosmic abstracts.

The Sixth Cosmos then died as he was consumed by the cosmic entity known as [[Black Winter]]. During the Eternity War, the Sixth Cosmos was summoned alongside his counterparts to combat the First Firmament, who sought to reclaim his position as everything that is.
