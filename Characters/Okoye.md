## Origin

Okoye was a member of the Dora Milaje, part of an order of wives-in-training for the King of Wakanda, and served as Tchalla's bodyguard and chauffeur. Each member of the Dora Milaje were chose from a different tribe of Wakandans , a political tradition to ensure peace among the tribes as each held a role in the palace and a chance to become the next queen.

Rather than seeing Okoye and Nakia as "wives in-training", T'challa seen them as daughters, and treated them as such. Unlike Nakia, Okoye understood that Tchalla loved Nakia and her as daughters and nothing more. Okoye remained loyal to T'challa and continued to serve as his chauffeur and bodyguard.

## Creation

Okoye first appeared in Black Panther (1998) #1, and was created by writer Christopher Priest and artist Mark Texeira.

## Alternate Realities

### Ultimate Universe (Earth-6160)

*Ultimate Okoye*

In the new Ultimate Universe, Okoye is the former leader of the Dora Milaje, wife of King T'Challa and Queen of Wakanda. In a time of strife for Wakanda which faces potential war with the Upper and Lower Kingdoms, Queen Okoye shared many heated debates with both her King and T'Challa's sister, Princess Shuri.

### Venomverse

*Okoye bonds with The Companion*

On an alternate Earth, both vibranium and a symbiote called "The Companion" fell from the heavens and crashed into Wakanda. The vibranium was harnessed as a weapon of the Black Panther, however the symbiote resisted subjugation until a woman called La'Dana prayed to Bast and discovered companionship was the key. By bonding with the alien, La'Dana became the first Queen of Wakanda, and tradition began where once the Queen ages she passes the Companion on to a chosen Dora Milaje. Okoye competes for the honour of becoming the next host of the Companion, and succeeds in the bonding process. She now protects the nation as its Queen.

## Other Media

### Films

*Danai Gurira as Okoye*

- Okoye appears as a major character in the 2018 *Black Panther* film, portrayed by *The Walking Dead* star Danai Gurira. Unlike in the comics, this version of Okoye is an adult woman and married to [[W'Kabi]], as well as the late [[T'Chaka|King T'Chaka]]'s former bodyguard. As the leader of the Dora Milaje, she is a fierce supporter of T'Challa, and ends up at odds with her husband after he supports [[Erik Killmonger]]. After Killmonger defeats T'Challa in a ritual duel and becomes the new ruler of Wakanda, Okoye reluctantly follows him. When T'Challa returns to overthrow his usurper, Okoye leads the other Dora Milaje in a rebellion against Killmonger, which eventually leads to her facing off against W'Kabi.
- Danai Gurira reprises her role in* Avengers: Infinity War*. She appears when the forces of [[Thanos]] invade Wakanda seeking the [[Vision]], who has the Mind Stone, the last remaining Infinity Stones. During the final battle, she teams up with [[Black Widow]] and [[Scarlet Witch]] to kill [[Proxima Midnight]], the leader of the Outrider army.
- Okoye appears in *Lego Black Panther: Trouble in Wakanda*, voiced by *Community* star Yvette Nicole Brown.

### Black Panther: Wakanda Forever

*Okoye in Wakanda Forever*

Danai Gurira reprises her role of Okoye in the 2022 film Black Panther: Wakanda Forever. The state of Wakanda is still reeling by the passing of its King T'Challa, and Okoye is tasked by [[Queen Mother|Queen Ramonda]] to keep a grieving [[Shuri|Princess Shuri]] safe. During a mission in the United States they are ambushed by an Atlantean hit squad and, despite her best efforts, Okoye is not able to stop them from kidnapping Shuri and [[Ironheart|Riri Williams]]. Okoye returns to Wakanda and confesses her failure to Ramonda who, in an emotional rage driven by the notion of losing both of her children, discharges Okoye from her position as leader of the Dora Milaje.

*Marvel's Avengers: War For Wakanda*

She is loyal to the King, however, and Okoye trusts T'Challa's judgment of the outsiders as correct.
