> [!abstract] In brief
> The White Wolf was the leader of the Hatut Zeraze, the secret police of Wakanda. As a baby, he survived a plane crash that plummeted onto Wakandan land. He was then given Wakandan citizenship by King T'Chaka. White Wolf became T'Chaka's most loyal servant doing any and everything, no matter how brutal, to appease him.

## Origin

The White Wolf is the adopted son of T'Chaka, making him the brother of [[Black Panther|T'Challa]]. Their relationship is not a good one though. Hunter was adopted before T'Challa was born and was raised as T'Chaka's true son. When T'Challa was born, Hunter became the "other" child. He always resented T'Challa for this and even blamed him for their father's death. Despite his feelings for T'Challa, he dutifully serves the Black Panther, leading the Hatut Zeraze in protecting the interests of Wakanda.
