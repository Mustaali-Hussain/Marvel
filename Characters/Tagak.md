> ###### Tagak
> **First appearance** Lo! The Lord Of The Leopards! #72
> **Appearances** 20
> **Also known as** The Leopard Lord, Leopard Lord, Brother In Sight

> [!abstract] In brief
> Tagak is a member of an extradimensional blind humanoid race. To see, members of his race bond with large jaguars or leopards.

Tagak first appeared battling Daredevil in a classic case of don't judge a book by its covers.

Tagak was actually pursuing a criminal from his own dimension, possibly losing his seeing eye leopard Opar in the process when a night guard or cop (not clear which) shot it.

Tagak also joined up with the Defenders (for a whole day).

His status is currently undetermined according to the best investigations of Tony Stark and the Initiative.

Although originally blind he is now able to see.
