> Sadurang is a dragon from Jotunheim and former student of Dormammu.

## Creation

Sadurang debuts in Tony Stark: Iron Man #13 by Gail Simone and Paolo Villanelli

## Major Story Arcs

### War of Realms

Sadurang is a dragon and former student of [[Dormammu]], awoken from hibernation by [[Malekith]]. Malekith needs Sadurang's help conquering the Nine Realms. He sics Sadurang after [[Iron Man|Tony Stark]], calling him the "Golden Avenger." Sadurang reluctantly agrees, reminding Malekith that he is in possession of the Second Eye of Agamotto. If Malekith betrays him, Sadurang will use the Second Eye against him.

Sadurang shows up to Stark Unlimited HQ in human form. He quickly grows impatient with Stark's security team, so he transforms into his dragon form and attacks the building. Stark and his armored friends are able to beat the dragon into retreating, but Sadurang vows to return more prepared.

### Eyes of Agamotto

When a savage team of Avengers were assembled by chance, they found themselves up against the sorcerer, Kulan Gath. Gath escaped these Avengers with the Third Eye of Agamotto in his possession. Elektra brought this information to Doctor Strange, keeper of the First Eye of Agamotto. They would reassemble the team to split into two war parties: one to go after Gath and the Third and one to retrieve the second eye from Sadurang.

Magik led a team consisting of Conan, Black Knight, and Juggernaut after the dragon in Bruges. Magik's team would first try brute force, but eventually Magik decided to negotiate. She used her Soulsword to revert him to his human form. She then trapped him in Limbo, promising to return him Earth with the eye once the team was done with the talisman.

Magik was true to her words.

### Eyeless

Sadurang took the form of a winged serpent of Aztec lore. He slept on top of the Temple of the Sun in Mexico. Rogue, Gambit, and Wolverine decided to investigate fearing for the nearby town. Gambit managed to steal the Second Eye from the dragon. Gambit bargained with the dragon, agreeing to return the eye to him in a year if he simply left Mexico. Gambit suggested Antarctica, and Sadurang was interested in being an "Ice Drake" again.

However, Sadurang returned for a new bargain with Gambit. He offered to take the Eye back before it started to have a psychological effect on Remy. He warned Gambit that the Eye is insatiable and could drive him to cannibalism. Despite this warning, Gambit chose to continue carrying the eye, which Sadurang respected.

## Power and Abilites

**Shapeshifter: **Sadurang can shapeshift from a human form to a dragon form. As a dragon, he can fly and has sharp teeth and claws.

- **Size Changing: **When he shapeshifts into a dragon, he becomes much larger
- **Culture Mimicry: **Sadurang is able to shapeshift into culturally or environmentally indigenous dragons. In Mexico, he became a feathered serpent, and in Antarctica, he became a pale and smooth ice drake.
