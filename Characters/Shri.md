## Creation

Shri was created by Matt Kindt and Steven Sanders, and first appeared in Infinity: The Hunt #1.

## Major Story Arcs

### Pan-Asian School

*Defending her fellow students*

Shri is one of three students contending in the global school contest, representing the Pan-Asian School for the Unusually Gifted. When the forces of Thanos invade Earth, Headmasters [[Jimmy Woo]] and [[Death (Javeed)|Sanjar Javeed]] place the students in a Panic Room escape pod that blasts into the planet's upper atmosphere. They receive a distress call from Atlantis, calling for all aid in response to the Black Order's assault. Shri's teammate [[Gang]] is able to override the parental controls and crash the Panic Room into Wakanda, where they encounter students from the Wakandan School of Alternative Studies. The Pan-Asian students convince their Wakandan counterparts that Atlantis requires help, and as they travel over the ocean to the distress beacon, the Panic Room is hit by a damaged Avengers Quinjet, carrying the combined students of Avengers Academy, the Jean Grey School, Braddock Academy and Future Foundation. A large scale battle erupts but they are too late to save Atlantis, so Latverian student [[Demona]] teleports the entire group to the Thanos' next target: Wakanda. There [[Shuri|Princess Shuri]] is fighting a losing war against an alien leviathon, but with the combined help of every student the leviathon is defeated and Wakanda's safety is secured.

## Powers and Abilities

*Being a young goddess*

Being the reincarnated avatar of Durga, Shri is able to generate each of the goddess' divine weapons with her eight energy arms.
