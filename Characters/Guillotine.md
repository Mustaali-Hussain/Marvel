## Origin

The seventh Guillotine of her bloodline. Due to her ancestor's bargain she is forced to wield the sword that grants her the powers of Guillotine. Although the sword tempts her to kill, she withstands the temptations and uses the sword to do good.

## Major Story Arcs

### Secret Empire

*Champions of Europe*

During Hydra's global move to power with [[Captain America (HYDRA)|Hydra Supreme]] at the helm, Guillotine is seen defending Paris as a member of the Champions of Europe. When Avengers members [[Squirrel Girl]] and [[Enigma]] are transported to the Parisian streets they are assaulted by Hydra forces, until Guillotine and her teammates intervene and save them. Jeannine joins her fellow Contest of Champion survivors Ares and Outlaw and, along with [[Captain Britain]], [[Faiza Hussain|Excalibur]] and [[Peregrine]], and the team breaches Hydra's Parisian armory.
