## **Origin**

 

Aminedi is part of the Iraqi mercenary team called Desert Sword. Freedom Force, led by Crimson Commando, travels to the Middle East on a mission and ends up in a confrontation with Desert Sword. Aminedi decapitated Super Sabre and cut off the Commado's right arm. Desert Sword lost team member Veil and the scientist Kurtzmann, both of whom were killed by Pyro. Sirocco and Aminedi trapped Avalanche and Commando in a mine field and as Commando's condition worsened, Avalanche trapped the two under a pile of rock, though they later escaped. Blob and Pyro were later captured by Desert Sword. Freedom Force's devastating failure soon led to the team's abandonment.

 

At an unknown point, Aminedi contracted the Legacy Virus, and died from it.

 

## **Powers and Abilities**

Aminedi possessed extremely superhuman speed.
